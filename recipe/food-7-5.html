Menemen

Türkiye

The Turkish breakfast scramble: eggs barely swirled through a buttery tomato-pepper base with Aleppo heat — soft, glossy and scoopable with bread, served in the pan. (The onion question divides households; both camps are delicious.)

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, diced (optional-but-loved)
  • 2 green peppers, diced
  • 400 g ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Aleppo pepper + oregano + salt
  • 4 eggs; parsley; bread for serving

Method

  1. Soften onion and peppers in oil 5 minutes without coloring.
  2. Add tomatoes, butter and spices; simmer 5–8 minutes to a thick, hot base.
  3. Crack the eggs over; wait 1 minute, then swirl yolks through once or twice — streaky, not scrambled.
  4. Cook 2–3 minutes more to soft-set whites and runny-glossy yolks.
  5. Shower with parsley and serve in the pan with bread.

Chef tips

  • Under-stir on purpose — swirls of white and gold, not uniform curds.
  • Pull it early; residual heat finishes the eggs.
  • Ripe tomatoes only — pale winter ones need a spoon of paste.
