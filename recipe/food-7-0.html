Döner Kebab

Türkiye

Meat roasted on the vertical spit and shaved to order — at home replicated with a tightly packed, roasted and chilled loaf, shaved thin and crisped. Yogurt-onion marinade and cumin-oregano warmth define the flavor.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground lamb + 500 g ground beef (20% fat)
  • 2 tbsp yogurt + ½ grated onion + 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp each salt, cumin; 1 tsp each paprika, oregano, black pepper
  • To serve: pide/flatbread, tomato, red onion, sumac, parsley
  • Sauces: garlic yogurt + hot sauce

Method

  1. Knead the meats with marinade ingredients until smooth and tacky; pack into a tight loaf (or freeze in a can for density).
  2. Roast at 180–200 °C until cooked through; chill completely — cold meat shaves thin.
  3. Shave thin slices and crisp them hard in a hot oiled pan.
  4. Warm the bread; massage onion with sumac, salt and lemon.
  5. Build: bread + meat + salad + sauces; wrap tight and serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Density is everything — a loose loaf crumbles instead of shaving.
  • The chill-then-shave step is what mimics the spit.
  • Crisp the shavings; soft steamed slices taste nothing like döner.
