Stuffed Cabbage

Hungary

Töltött káposzta: pork-and-rice rolls in wilted cabbage leaves, nestled between layers of sauerkraut with smoked ribs, simmered in tomato-tinged broth until melting — served with sour cream and bread. Hungary's Christmas-and-wedding comfort pot.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cabbage (blanched leaves) + 1 kg sauerkraut, rinsed
  • Filling: 500 g pork (+ beef) + 100 g rice + onion + garlic + egg + paprika
  • Smoked ribs/bones + tomato juice + water + bay + caraway; sour cream

Method

  1. Blanch cabbage; peel 12–16 whole leaves; shave thick ribs.
  2. Mix filling; roll 2–3 tbsp per leaf, sides tucked.
  3. Bed half the kraut in a pot; arrange rolls; smoked meat among; cap with kraut.
  4. Add tomato juice + water; simmer 1.5–2 hrs (or bake).
  5. Serve with sour cream and crusty bread.

Chef tips

  • Rinse sharp kraut — unrinsed can overpower.
  • Low and slow — rushing leaves crunchy rice.
  • Better reheated next day — plan for it.
