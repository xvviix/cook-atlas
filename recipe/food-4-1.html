Bacalhau

Portugal

Portugal's 'faithful friend': dried salt cod, desalted over 1–3 days and cooked in hundreds of ways — the saying goes there is a recipe for every day of the year. This card covers desalting plus the classic Porto bake Bacalhau à Gomes de Sá (cod, potatoes, onion, egg, olives, olive oil).

Ingredients

  • 1 kg thick salt-cod loins
  • Cold water for soaking (changed 3–4× daily)
  • Gomes de Sá: 1 kg potatoes, 3 onions, 4 hard eggs, 20 black olives
  • 150–200 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • Garlic, parsley, pepper
  • (Optional: 500 ml hot milk to mellow the cod)

Method

  1. Rinse the cod, cover with cold water and refrigerate 24–72 hours depending on thickness, changing the water 3–4 times a day.
  2. Taste a flake: it should be pleasantly briny, never harshly salty; drain and pat dry.
  3. (Optional mellowing step: soak the flaked cod in hot — not boiling — milk 1–2 hours.)
  4. Boil sliced potatoes until just tender; soften sliced onions and garlic in olive oil.
  5. Layer potatoes, flaked boneless cod and onions in an oiled dish with generous oil; bake covered at 180 °C ~30 minutes, then uncovered 10.
  6. Garnish with egg, olives and parsley; serve with more olive oil and crusty bread.

Chef tips

  • Under-soaking ruins the dish — when in doubt, soak longer; you can add salt, never remove it.
  • Thick loins (lombos) are worth the price; thin tail pieces over-salt and shred.
  • Bacalhau improves with olive oil generosity — Portuguese recipes are unapologetic about it.
