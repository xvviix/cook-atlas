Haneeth

Saudi Arabia

Haneeth is southern Arabia's ancient pit-roast: lamb rubbed with turmeric, cumin and black pepper, slow-roasted for hours (traditionally in a tannour earth oven) until it surrenders at a glance, served over spiced rice with the drippings. Simpler-spiced than kabsa or mandi — haneeth is about lamb purity, time and fire. Low oven, tight foil, patience: 4 hours minimum for shoulder or leg.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg lamb shoulder/leg + turmeric + cumin + black pepper + salt + ghee
  • Basmati rice + whole spices + raisins
  • Daqqus + yogurt to serve

Method

  1. Rub lamb all over with turmeric, cumin, pepper, salt and ghee.
  2. Rest marinated 4+ hours or overnight.
  3. Roast tightly foiled at 150–160 °C for 4–5 hours.
  4. Uncover; blast hot 15 minutes to bronze.
  5. Cook fragrant rice; mound; crown with lamb and drippings.
  6. Serve with daqqus.

Chef tips

  • Low and long — rushing toughens the lamb.
  • Tight foil — escaping steam dries the meat.
  • Simple spices — haneeth honors the lamb, not the masala.
  • Rest before carving — the juices redistribute.
