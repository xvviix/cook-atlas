Bulgogi

Korea

'Fire meat': paper-thin beef (ribeye/sirloin, sliced semi-frozen) marinated in soy, grated Asian pear, garlic, sesame and sugar — the pear's enzymes tenderizing as they sweeten — then seared fierce-hot to caramelized edges. Wrapped in lettuce with ssamjang, or over rice.

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef ribeye/sirloin, sliced 2–3 mm (semi-freeze to slice)
  • Marinade: soy + grated Asian pear + brown sugar + garlic + sesame oil + pepper
  • Onion + scallion + mushrooms; sesame seeds; lettuce + ssamjang

Method

  1. Semi-freeze the beef 2 hours; slice against the grain, paper-thin.
  2. Marinate 2 hours to overnight (pear over-tenderizes past 24 hrs — watch it).
  3. Sear in a screaming-hot pan in a single layer — no crowding, no stewing.
  4. Toss through onion and scallion at the end; shower sesame.
  5. Serve sizzling with rice, or wrap in lettuce with ssamjang.

Chef tips

  • Thin slicing is half the recipe — semi-frozen meat slices cleanly.
  • Single layer + fierce heat = caramelized; piled meat = grey and steamed.
  • Don't marinate past a day — pear enzymes turn beef mushy.
