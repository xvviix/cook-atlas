Lanzhou Lamian

China

Lanzhou's hand-pulled beef noodle soup: crystal-clear spiced broth, springy alkaline noodles pulled to order, tender beef, soft daikon and a spoon of chili oil. Judged on 'one clear, two white, three red, four green, five yellow'.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef bones + 500 g brisket/shank
  • Daikon chunks; ginger + scallions
  • Spice bag: star anise, cinnamon, fennel, Sichuan + black pepper, cloves, cardamom
  • Noodles: 500 g high-gluten flour + water + salt + peng hui/alkaline
  • Chili oil + cilantro + scallions to finish

Method

  1. Blanch bones and meat; rinse; simmer with ginger, scallion, daikon and spices 2–4 hours, skimming to crystal clarity.
  2. Lift out the beef; slice or cube. Season the broth with salt and white pepper.
  3. Knead a firm noodle dough; rest oiled 1+ hours.
  4. Pull: stretch, fold and slap into strands; boil 30–60 seconds until chewy.
  5. Bowl: noodles + broth + beef + daikon + chili oil + herbs; serve scalding.

Chef tips

  • Clarity demands a bare simmer and relentless skimming — boiling clouds it.
  • The alkaline (peng hui) is what makes noodles pullable and springy.
  • Pull to order; pulled noodles wait for no broth.
