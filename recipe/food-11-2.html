Enchiladas

Mexico

Listed here as enchiladas rojas: corn tortillas dipped in a guajillo-ancho chile sauce, lightly fried, filled with chicken, cheese or potato-carrot, and finished with onion, queso fresco, crema and cilantro. San Luis Potosí style — rolled, not baked in a casserole.

Ingredients

  • 14–18 corn tortillas
  • Sauce: 6–8 guajillo + 1–4 ancho, tomato, onion, garlic, marjoram, stock
  • Filling: 2 chicken breasts shredded (or potato-carrot, or cheese)
  • Queso fresco + white onion + crema + cilantro; oil for frying

Method

  1. Toast and soak the chiles; blend with charred tomato, onion, garlic and stock; strain.
  2. Fry the sauce in oil 10 minutes until it darkens and thickens; salt boldly.
  3. Pass each tortilla through hot oil seconds to soften, then through the warm sauce.
  4. Fill, roll snugly, and arrange seam-down on the platter.
  5. Blanket with remaining sauce; finish with onion, cheese, crema and cilantro.

Chef tips

  • The oil-dip stops tortillas cracking and drinking the sauce to mush.
  • Strain the sauce — chile skins are the grit in lazy enchiladas.
  • Serve the moment they're assembled; enchiladas wait for no one.
