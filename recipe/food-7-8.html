Dolma

Türkiye

'Stuffed' — vine leaves (sarma) or pepper shells filled two ways: zeytinyağlı (olive-oil rice with pine nuts, currants and cinnamon, served cool) or etli (meat-rice, served warm). Finger-thin rolls are the mark of skill.

Ingredients

  • 40 vine leaves or 8 pepper shells
  • 200 g rice, rinsed
  • 2 onions, minced; 100 ml olive oil
  • Pine nuts + currants; mint, dill, parsley; cinnamon, allspice, sugar
  • Lemon juice + water; salt, pepper

Method

  1. Sweat onion in half the oil; add rice 2 minutes; stir in nuts, fruit, herbs, spices and a splash of water; par-cook 5 minutes and cool.
  2. Fill leaves finger-thin (or peppers ¾ full — rice swells).
  3. Pack seam-down in a lined pot; pour over lemon, remaining oil and water to barely cover.
  4. Weight with a plate; simmer 40–60 minutes until tender.
  5. Cool in the pot; serve oil-dolma at room temperature with lemon.

Chef tips

  • Three-quarters full, never more — rice needs room to swell.
  • The plate weight stops rolls unraveling in the simmer.
  • Zeytinyağlı improves overnight as the oil and lemon marry.
