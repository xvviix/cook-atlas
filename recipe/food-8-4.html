Mapo Tofu

China

Sichuan's ma-la legend: silken tofu braised with beef mince in Pixian doubanjiang, glossed with chili oil and dusted with ground peppercorns. Numbing-hot, slick, and spooned over rice — soupy, never gravy-thick.

Ingredients

  • 450 g soft/silken tofu, 2 cm cubes
  • 100 g minced beef
  • 1½ tbsp Pixian doubanjiang, chopped
  • 1 tbsp douchi (fermented black beans); chili flakes
  • Garlic + ginger + scallion whites; 150 ml stock
  • Cornstarch slurry; 1 tsp toasted ground peppercorns

Method

  1. Blanch tofu cubes 1 minute in salted water; drain — this firms them and removes beaniness.
  2. Crisp the beef in oil; remove, keeping the fat.
  3. Fry the doubanjiang low 1 minute until the oil turns brilliant red; add douchi and aromatics 30 seconds.
  4. Add stock and tofu; simmer 5 minutes, pushing the pan (never stirring) to keep cubes whole.
  5. Gloss with slurry to a soupy sheen; scallions in; plate and dust with peppercorns.

Chef tips

  • Pixian doubanjiang is the soul — substitutes flatten the dish.
  • Push, don't stir: broken tofu sinks the texture.
  • The peppercorn dust goes on at the table for maximum tingle.
