Montreal Smoked Meat

Canada

Montreal's legendary deli meat: brisket cured 7–10 days in peppery, coriander-flecked brine with pink curing salt, hot-smoked, then steamed until it surrenders — sliced thick, piled indecently high on rye with yellow mustard at institutions like Schwartz's. Distinct from pastrami (different cut, cure and spice), it's pepper-crusted, juicy and hand-cut thick because thin slices would dry. A two-week project at home: cure, rest, smoke 6–9 hours, steam 2–3 hours.

Ingredients

  • 5–7 lb brisket (point end) + curing brine: kosher salt + pink cure #1 + garlic + pickling spice
  • Rub: cracked black pepper + coriander + mustard seed + paprika + garlic
  • Rye bread + yellow mustard + pickles to serve

Method

  1. Cure the brisket refrigerated in brine 7–10 days, turning daily.
  2. Rinse, rest uncovered overnight to form the pellicle.
  3. Crust thickly with cracked pepper and spices.
  4. Hot-smoke at 110–120 °C for 6–9 hours to ~70 °C internal.
  5. Steam 2–3 hours until probe-tender (~93 °C).
  6. Rest; slice thick against the grain; pile on rye with mustard.

Chef tips

  • Pink cure #1 is mandatory — it keeps long-cured meat safe and pink.
  • Point end over flat — fat is what survives the smoke-and-steam.
  • Slice thick and against the grain — thin slices dry in minutes.
  • Steam, don't skip — steaming is what makes it deli-tender.
