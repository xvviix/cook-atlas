Mechouia Salad

Tunisia

Slata mechouia — grilled salad: peppers, tomatoes, onions and garlic charred directly over flame until collapsing, then chopped, dressed with olive oil and caraway, and crowned with tuna, hard-boiled eggs, olives and capers. Smoky, sweet, tangy and substantial, it's Tunisia's favorite starter — the char is the entire point, so a pale oven-roast is no substitute for real flame. Serve at room temperature with bread.

Ingredients

  • 4 peppers + 4 tomatoes + 2 onions + 1 garlic head
  • Tuna + 3 eggs + olives + capers
  • Olive oil + caraway + lemon + salt

Method

  1. Char everything over open flame (or fiercest grill), turning, until blackened and soft.
  2. Steam covered 10 minutes; peel, seed and squeeze dry.
  3. Chop to a coarse, juicy relish — not a purée.
  4. Dress with olive oil, caraway, lemon and salt.
  5. Mound on a platter; crown with tuna, eggs, olives and capers.

Chef tips

  • Real flame or bust — pale vegetables make a different salad.
  • Squeeze out excess juice — watery mechouia is dilute mechouia.
  • Chop, don't blend — texture is the pleasure.
  • Room temperature serving — cold mutes the smoke.
