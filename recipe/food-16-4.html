Bún bò Huế

Vietnam

Huế's imperial beef noodle soup — phở's fiery sibling: a lemongrass-and-shrimp-paste broth stained sunset-red with annatto-chile sate, loaded with beef shank, pork hock, thick round noodles and sausage. Bold, spicy, funky, royal.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank + 1 kg pork hocks
  • Broth: lemongrass forest + onion + shrimp paste + rock sugar + fish sauce
  • Sate: annatto seeds + chile flakes + lemongrass + garlic + shallot fried in oil
  • Thick round bún noodles + chả sausage; banana blossom + herbs + lime

Method

  1. Parboil meats; rinse; simmer with lemongrass and onion 2–3 hours to tender.
  2. Strain; season with shrimp paste, sugar, fish sauce and salt.
  3. Fry the sate to a brick-red oil; swirl into the broth.
  4. Blanch thick noodles; bowl with sliced meats and sausage.
  5. Ladle broth; crown with sate oil; serve with blossom, herbs and lime.

Chef tips

  • Shrimp paste + lemongrass define it — timid amounts taste like generic beef soup.
  • The sate oil goes in the bowl, not just as garnish.
  • Thick round noodles only — flat phở noodles are a different dish.
