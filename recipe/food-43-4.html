Syrnyky

Ukraine

Ukrainian cheese pancakes: dry farmer's cheese (tvorog) mixed with egg, a little flour, sugar and vanilla, shaped into patties and fried golden — crisp outside, molten-creamy within — served for breakfast with sour cream, honey or berry compote. The cheese must be dry: wet tvorog needs extra flour, and extra flour makes dense, bready syrnyky instead of creamy ones. Press and drain the cheese, or blitz it smooth for the most delicate result.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dry farmer's cheese/tvorog (pressed)
  • 1 egg + 3–4 tbsp flour + 2 tbsp sugar + vanilla + pinch of salt
  • Butter-oil for frying + sour cream + honey/berries to serve

Method

  1. Press the cheese dry (cheesecloth, 30+ minutes); blitz smooth if desired.
  2. Mix with egg, flour, sugar, vanilla and salt to a soft, shapeable mass.
  3. Chill 20 minutes; shape patties with floured hands.
  4. Fry in butter-oil over medium heat 3–4 minutes per side until gold.
  5. Serve warm with sour cream, honey or berries.

Chef tips

  • Dry cheese is everything — wet cheese forces floury, dense syrnyky.
  • Minimal flour — just enough to hold the shape.
  • Medium heat — fierce heat burns the outside raw inside.
  • Shape with floured hands — the mix is supposed to be soft.
