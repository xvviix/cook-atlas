Kotlet schabowy

Poland

Poland's Sunday cutlet — bigger, juicier cousin of Wiener schnitzel: pork loin brined overnight in milk and onion, pounded plate-size, breaded in fine crumbs (never panko) and pan-fried gold. Served with potatoes and mizeria (cucumber salad).

Ingredients

  • 4 pork loin chops (~2 cm), pounded thin
  • Overnight brine: milk + sliced onion (+ garlic, bay, allspice)
  • Breading: flour + beaten egg + fine dry breadcrumbs; salt + pepper + marjoram
  • Lard/oil for frying

Method

  1. Brine the pounded cutlets in milk and onion overnight (minimum 2 hours).
  2. Pat very dry; season with salt, pepper and marjoram.
  3. Bread flour → egg → fine crumbs, pressing firmly; rest 10 minutes.
  4. Pan-fry in hot fat 3–4 minutes per side to deep gold.
  5. Drain briefly; serve with potatoes and mizeria.

Chef tips

  • Fine crumbs, not panko — the tight crust is the tradition.
  • The milk brine is what separates good from great schabowy.
  • Pat dry after brining or the breading slides off.
