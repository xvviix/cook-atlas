Tzatziki

Greece

The cool cucumber-yogurt sauce of Greek tavernas: thick strained yogurt with grated drained cucumber, garlic, olive oil, vinegar or lemon, and dill. Ten minutes of work plus chilling; served with grilled meats, gyros, or simply bread.

Ingredients

  • 500 g thick full-fat strained Greek yogurt
  • 1 large English cucumber, grated
  • 1–3 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar or lemon juice
  • 2–3 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Grate the cucumber, salt lightly, rest 10–15 minutes, then squeeze very dry in a clean towel — this is the decisive step.
  2. Combine yogurt, garlic, oil, vinegar/lemon and salt; refrigerate at least 30 minutes (overnight best) so flavors meld.
  3. Fold in the drained cucumber and dill; adjust salt, acid and garlic.
  4. Chill again briefly and serve cold with a thread of olive oil on top.

Chef tips

  • Watery tzatziki is always under-squeezed cucumber — wring it hard.
  • Whole-milk strained yogurt is essential; low-fat or unstrained yogurt goes thin.
  • Keeps 3–4 days refrigerated; stir before serving.
