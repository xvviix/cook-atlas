Syrniki

Russia

Farmer's-cheese pancakes: dry tvorog mixed with yolk, sugar, semolina and a whisper of flour — shaped into thick patties, floured, and fried low and slow to crisp gold with custardy middles. Served with smetana and jam; the Soviet breakfast that never left.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dry farmer's cheese/tvorog (drained, sieved)
  • 2 yolks + 3 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp semolina + 2 tbsp flour + vanilla + salt
  • Flour for dusting; butter/oil; smetana + jam

Method

  1. Sieve/blend cheese smooth and dry.
  2. Mix with yolks, sugar, semolina, flour; rest 15 min.
  3. Shape a log; cut; form thick patties; dust flour.
  4. Fry low-medium 4–5 min per side to deep gold.
  5. Serve warm with smetana and jam.

Chef tips

  • Dry cheese or disaster — drain wet tvorog overnight.
  • Low heat — fierce heat burns raw middles.
  • Rest the mix — semolina needs time to hydrate.
