Couscous

Morocco

Friday's sacred dish: fine couscous grains steamed three times over the simmering seven-vegetable stew (carrot, zucchini, turnip, cabbage, pumpkin, onion, chickpeas + lamb) — rolled with oil between steamings for separate, fluffy pearls. Mounded high, vegetables arranged, broth ladled over at the table.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fine couscous + oil + salt + water for steaming
  • Lamb/beef + onion + tomato + ginger + turmeric + pepper
  • Seven veg: carrot + zucchini + turnip + cabbage + pumpkin + chickpeas + (potato)

Method

  1. Brown meat with onion and spices; add water; simmer.
  2. Add hard veg first (carrot, turnip, pumpkin); simmer 15 min.
  3. Add soft veg (zucchini, cabbage) + chickpeas; simmer to tender.
  4. Steam couscous 3× ~15 min, rolling with oiled hands between.
  5. Mound grains; arrange meat + veg; broth alongside.

Chef tips

  • Three steamings = fluffy; one steaming = clumps.
  • Roll with oil between steams — dry grains stick.
  • Friday couscous is communal — make plenty.
