Pierogi

Poland

Listed here as pierogi ruskie: silky hot-water dough around mashed potato, twaróg (quark) and caramelized onion — boiled until they float, then fried in butter to crisp edges. Served with sour cream and more buttery onions.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 250 ml hot water + 1 tbsp butter/oil + salt; rest 30 min
  • Filling: 700 g russets mashed + 250 g twaróg + 2 caramelized onions + salt + pepper
  • Butter for frying; sour cream + onions to serve

Method

  1. Mash potatoes smooth; blend with twaróg and buttery onions; season boldly; cool.
  2. Knead a soft elastic dough; rest covered 30 minutes.
  3. Roll 3 mm; cut rounds; fill, fold and pinch thin and tight.
  4. Boil in batches until they float plus 1–2 minutes; lift out.
  5. Fry in butter to golden-crisp spots; serve with sour cream and onions.

Chef tips

  • Pinch the seam thin — thick seams stay doughy.
  • Cool filling seals cleanly; warm filling steams the seam open.
  • Flour the tray generously; fresh pierogi glue themselves to everything.
