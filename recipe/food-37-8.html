Qidreh

Palestine

Qidreh (qidra) is southern Palestine's festive urn-cooked feast: lamb and rice with chickpeas, whole garlic heads and warming qidreh spices, traditionally sealed in a hand-turned clay urn and slow-baked in a communal oven until the meat surrenders and the rice drinks the fat. At weddings and aqeeqas the urn is cracked open tableside, releasing a cloud of cinnamon-cardamom steam. At home, a heavy Dutch oven reproduces the magic — the keys are searing the lamb hard and never skimping on samneh.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg lamb (shanks or shoulder chunks) + 3 cups basmati
  • Chickpeas + 2 whole garlic heads + onions
  • Qidreh spice (turmeric, cardamom, cinnamon, allspice, black lime) + samneh + almonds + parsley

Method

  1. Sear lamb in samneh until deeply browned; build a spiced broth and braise 1.5 hours.
  2. Toast rice with qidreh spice; add chickpeas and whole garlic heads.
  3. Combine lamb and its broth with the rice; seal tightly.
  4. Bake low (160 °C) 60–90 minutes until the rice is separate and fragrant.
  5. Rest 15 minutes; fluff; garnish with toasted almonds and parsley.

Chef tips

  • Sear hard — pale lamb makes pale qidreh.
  • Whole garlic heads melt into sweet cream — don't substitute chopped.
  • Seal the pot tightly — escaping steam means drying rice.
  • Rest before fluffing — the grains finish setting off the heat.
