Fricassé

Tunisia

Tunisia's beloved fried sandwich: small yeasted buns enriched with egg, fried golden, split and stuffed while warm with tuna, harissa, potato, egg, olives and capers — sometimes with a spoon of mechouia for good measure. A Tunis street-cart icon and beach-day essential, the bun should be light and hollow-ish inside to cradle the filling. Fry at the right temperature and they stay feather-light, not greasy.

Ingredients

  • Buns: 500 g flour + yeast + 1 egg + 100 ml oil + warm water + salt + pinch of sugar
  • Filling: tuna + harissa + boiled potato + eggs + olives + capers
  • Frying oil at 170–175 °C

Method

  1. Knead a soft enriched dough; rise 1 hour.
  2. Shape small oval buns; proof 30–40 minutes until puffy.
  3. Fry at 170–175 °C, turning, until golden all over.
  4. Drain; split while warm.
  5. Stuff generously: harissa first, then potato, tuna, egg, olives.

Chef tips

  • Proof fully — under-proofed buns fry dense.
  • 170–175 °C exactly — cool oil greases; hot oil burns raw middles.
  • Stuff while warm — cold buns tear.
  • Harissa inside, not optional — plain fricassé is just a roll.
