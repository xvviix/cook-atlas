Chả giò

Vietnam

Southern Vietnam's fried spring rolls: pork-and-shrimp filling with glass noodles, wood ear, carrot and taro in rice-paper (or wheat) wrappers, fried to blistered gold. Wrapped in lettuce with herbs and dunked in nước chấm — crunch outside, juicy within.

Ingredients

  • Filling: 500 g pork + 250 g shrimp + glass noodles + wood ear + carrot + taro + onion + egg
  • Seasoning: fish sauce + sugar + pepper + bouillon
  • Rice-paper or spring-roll wrappers; flour-water glue; oil at 165–175 °C
  • Lettuce + herbs + nước chấm

Method

  1. Soak noodles and wood ear; chop everything fine; mix gently (don't overwork).
  2. Roll tight in wrappers, sealing with glue; rest seam-down.
  3. Fry at 165–175 °C 6–8 minutes to deep blistered gold; drain upright.
  4. Serve hot with lettuce, herbs and nước chấm for wrapping.

Chef tips

  • Tight rolling prevents oil-logging; air pockets fry greasy.
  • Squeeze carrots and taro dry — wet filling bursts wrappers.
  • Fry in batches; crowding drops the temperature to soggy.
