Changua

Colombia

Bogotá's milky breakfast soup — the 'poor man's soup' that comforts like riches: milk and water simmered with scallion and cilantro, stale bread melted in, eggs poached directly in the bowl of soup, cheese cubes over. Five ingredients, pure Andean morning.

Ingredients

  • 1 l milk + 500 ml water
  • 4 eggs + 150 g queso fresco, cubed
  • Stale bread chunks + scallions + cilantro + salt

Method

  1. Bring milk + water to a bare simmer (never a hard boil).
  2. Add scallion, salt, pepper; 3 min.
  3. Add bread chunks; soak 2 min.
  4. Slip in eggs; poach 3–5 min to jammy yolks.
  5. Ladle egg + bread + broth; cheese + cilantro over; serve now.

Chef tips

  • Never boil hard — milk boils over in a blink.
  • Stale bread is the point — fresh turns to paste.
  • Poach gently — broken yolks cloud the broth.
