BeaverTails

Canada

Ottawa's Rideau Canal classic: whole-wheat dough hand-stretched into a beaver-tail oval, fried until puffed and golden, then painted with butter and cinnamon sugar (the Original) or loaded with chocolate-hazelnut, maple or garlic-cheese toppings. Crisp-edged, chewy-centered and eaten walking, preferably while skating. The stretch — thin middle, thicker rim — is what gives the signature chew.

Ingredients

  • Dough: flour + whole wheat flour + yeast + sugar + warm milk + egg + butter
  • Frying oil at 180 °C
  • Toppings: butter + cinnamon sugar (classic); Nutella/maple optional

Method

  1. Knead an enriched dough; rise 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Divide and stretch each piece into a thin tail-shaped oval.
  3. Fry at 180 °C, about 1 minute per side, until puffed and gold.
  4. Drain briefly; brush with butter.
  5. Shower with cinnamon sugar; serve immediately, walking optional.

Chef tips

  • Stretch thin — thick centers fry doughy.
  • 180 °C oil — cooler oil soaks; hotter burns the sugar coating later.
  • Top while hot — toppings slide off cold tails.
  • One per person minimum — halves cause disputes.
