Ajiaco

Colombia

Bogotá's highland chicken-and-potato soup: three potatoes (papa criolla dissolving to gold, sabanera and pastusa holding shape) simmered with chicken, corn and guascas herb — served with cream, capers and avocado stirred in at the table. Thick, golden, comforting.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken (~1.6 kg) + 2 l stock
  • 3 potato types: criolla + floury + waxy (~1 kg total)
  • Dried guascas + corn + scallion + cilantro; cream + capers + avocado to serve

Method

  1. Poach chicken in stock 30 min; lift out; shred.
  2. Simmer all potatoes with guascas 20–30 min until the criolla melts.
  3. Add corn 10 min; scallion + cilantro last.
  4. Return chicken; season; rest 10 min.
  5. Serve with cream, capers and avocado at the table + rice.

Chef tips

  • Guascas is irreplaceable — order dried online if needed.
  • Three potatoes, three textures — one variety falls flat.
  • Cream and capers go in the bowl, never the pot.
