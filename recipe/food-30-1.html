Tagine

Morocco

Djej emshmel: Morocco's most famous tagine — chicken braised with ginger, turmeric, saffron, garlic and onion under the conical lid, finished with briny green olives and strips of preserved lemon. The lid's self-basting keeps everything silky; serve with bread for the sauce.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2 onions + garlic + fresh ginger + turmeric + saffron + paprika
  • 1 preserved lemon + 1 cup green olives; cilantro + parsley; broth

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in spices + garlic + herbs 2+ hrs (optional).
  2. Soften onions; bloom spices; nestle chicken in.
  3. Add broth; lid on; simmer low 40–45 min.
  4. Add olives + lemon strips; simmer 10–15 min more.
  5. Reduce sauce if thin; herbs over; serve with bread/couscous.

Chef tips

  • Low and slow — rushing toughens the chicken.
  • Preserved lemon rind only — the pulp is bitter.
  • No tagine? Dutch oven with lid works.
