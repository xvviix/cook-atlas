Pollo a la Brasa

Peru

Peru's rotisserie chicken, sold in thousands of pollerías: a whole bird marinated in soy, garlic, cumin, ají panca, vinegar, beer and herbs, then slow-roasted (traditionally over charcoal/wood on a spit) until the skin is crisp and smoky and the meat juicy. Always with ají verde sauce and fries.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (~1.5 kg)
  • Marinade: 5 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp each cumin, black pepper, oregano
  • 1 tbsp ají panca paste (or smoked paprika), 1 tsp rosemary, 2 tbsp salt
  • 100 ml beer + 2–3 tbsp vinegar + 1 tbsp mustard + oil
  • Ají verde: cilantro, jalapeño/poblano, mayo or egg yolk + oil, lime, garlic, salt
  • Fries and salad to serve

Method

  1. Blend the marinade and rub all over the bird, inside the cavity and under the breast skin; marinate refrigerated 3–24 hours.
  2. Bring to room temperature 30–45 minutes; truss the legs for even cooking.
  3. Roast on a spit/rotisserie if possible at ~200 °C, or oven-roast on a rack 1.25–1.5 hours, basting with drippings, until thigh reads 74 °C and skin is deep mahogany.
  4. Blend the ají verde until creamy; adjust heat, salt and lime.
  5. Rest the chicken 15 minutes, carve, and serve with fries, salad and generous ají verde.

Chef tips

  • Overnight marinating is where the deep pollería flavor comes from — don't rush it.
  • Under-the-skin marinade on the breast keeps the leanest meat seasoned.
  • Leftover marinade keeps a week refrigerated — enough for a second bird.
