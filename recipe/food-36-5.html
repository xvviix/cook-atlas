Kaiserschmarrn

Austria

The Emperor's mess: a thick, fluffy pancake enriched with separated whipped egg whites, studded with rum-soaked raisins, fried in butter until golden, then torn into bite-size shreds with forks, caramelized with a snowfall of sugar, and dusted with icing sugar. Legend says Franz Joseph's cook shredded a failed pancake and the Emperor loved it. Served with Zwetschkenröster (plum compote) or applesauce, it is Austria's most joyful dessert — equally valid as a ski-lunch main.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs, separated + 140 g flour + 200 ml milk + 2 tsp sugar + vanilla + lemon zest + salt
  • 50 g rum-soaked raisins + butter for frying + sugar for caramelizing
  • Icing sugar + plum compote or applesauce to serve

Method

  1. Soak raisins in rum 30+ minutes; separate eggs.
  2. Whisk yolks with sugar, milk, flour, vanilla, zest and salt until smooth.
  3. Whip whites to firm peaks; fold gently into the batter.
  4. Fry the batter thick in foaming butter, covered, until the base is gold.
  5. Flip in sections; tear into chunks with two forks; scatter sugar and caramelize.
  6. Dust with icing sugar; serve immediately with plum compote.

Chef tips

  • Whipped whites are the lift — flat batter makes a flat schmarrn.
  • Tear, don't cut — ragged edges catch the caramelized sugar.
  • Caramelize at the end over high heat — pale shreds taste merely of pancake.
  • Serve in the pan — kaiserschmarrn waits for no one.
