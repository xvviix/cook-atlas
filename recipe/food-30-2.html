Pastilla

Morocco

Chicken pastilla (bastilla): the sweet-savory wedding pie — saffron-ginger chicken shredded into egg-thickened onions, layered in buttered warqa with cinnamon-sugar almonds, baked shattering-crisp and dusted with powdered sugar and cinnamon lattice. (See also food-30-9 for the seafood bastilla.)

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken + onions + saffron + ginger + cinnamon + parsley
  • 4 eggs for thickening the juices
  • 1 cup almonds (fried, ground) + powdered sugar + cinnamon + orange-blossom water; 8–10 warqa/filo sheets + clarified butter

Method

  1. Poach chicken with onions and spices 45 min; shred; reduce juices.
  2. Scramble eggs into the reduced juices to a thick cream.
  3. Grind almonds with sugar + cinnamon + blossom water.
  4. Layer buttered warqa: egg cream, chicken, almonds; fold and seal.
  5. Bake 190 °C 30–45 min to gold; dust sugar + cinnamon; serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Crisp layers need generous buttering — stinginess sogs.
  • Dust just before serving — sugar melts on standing.
  • Traditionally pigeon — chicken is the modern standard.
