Fattoush

Lebanon

The bread salad of the Levant: romaine, cucumber, tomato, radish and purslane with toasted pita chips, dressed in lemon, olive oil, garlic and sumac. Stale pita reborn as crunch — toss the chips in at the last second so they stay shattering.

Ingredients

  • 2 pitas, torn, oiled and toasted crisp
  • Romaine + cucumber + tomatoes + radishes + green onion + parsley + mint (+ purslane)
  • Dressing: lemon + olive oil + garlic + 2 tbsp sumac + salt

Method

  1. Toast the pita to deep gold; cool completely.
  2. Chop all vegetables bite-size; herbs rough-chopped.
  3. Whisk the sumac-lemon dressing boldly tart.
  4. Toss vegetables with dressing.
  5. Add pita chips last; toss once; serve immediately.

Chef tips

  • Pita goes in last — dressed pita turns to mush in minutes.
  • Sumac is the signature tang — be generous.
  • Day-old pita toasts best — fresh pita steams.
