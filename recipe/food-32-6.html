Asado

Chile

Chilean asado: the weekend parrilla ritual — lomo vetado, entraña, asado de tira, chicken and longanizas over coals, starting with choripán and anticuchos. Thinner cuts and longer grilling than Argentina; pebre (fresh, always) and ensalada chilena on the side; red wine throughout.

Ingredients

  • Mixed grill: lomo vetado + entraña + tira + chicken + longanizas
  • Salt; choripán + anticuchos to start
  • Pebre + ensalada chilena + marraqueta + red wine

Method

  1. Light coals; wait for even gray heat.
  2. Salt meats simply; grill in waves (sausages first, steaks later).
  3. Rest each cut before slicing.
  4. Serve with pebre, salads, bread.
  5. 4–6 hours, unhurried — the gathering is the point.

Chef tips

  • The asador never leaves the grill — it's a post of honor.
  • Pebre fresh daily — bottled pebre is a compromise.
  • Waves, not piles — everything lands hot.
