Hamburger

United States

Listed here as the smash burger: loose 80/20 beef balls smashed paper-thin on a ripping-hot cast-iron surface for maximum Maillard crust, flipped once, crowned with American cheese, and stacked double on a toasted potato bun. Crispy lace edges, juicy middle — done in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef 80/20, in 4 loose balls (double up for doubles)
  • Salt + pepper
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 4 potato/brioche buns + butter
  • Pickles, onion, burger sauce, shredded lettuce

Method

  1. Toast buttered buns cut-side down on the hot plancha; set aside.
  2. Heat cast iron until smoking; place 2 balls spaced; smash hard with a heavy spatula 10 seconds to ~5 mm.
  3. Season; cook 2–3 minutes until the bottom is deep brown and edges grey.
  4. Scrape-flip (keep the crust!), cheese immediately, cook 1 minute more.
  5. Stack doubles on buns with pickles, onion and sauce; serve instantly.

Chef tips

  • Smash once, hard, in the first 30 seconds — never press after the flip.
  • 80/20 fat is the juiciness; lean beef makes hockey pucks.
  • Scrape, don't scoop — the stuck crust is the entire point.
