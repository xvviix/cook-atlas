Ciorbă rădăuțeană

Romania

Rădăuți's chicken answer to tripe soup — all the garlicky-sour-cream comfort, none of the tripe: shredded chicken in clear root broth, finished with the same smântână-yolk liaison, garlic and vinegar. Lighter than burtă, equally beloved.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast/thighs + roots (carrot, parsnip, celery, onion, pepper)
  • Liaison: 200 g smântână + 2–3 yolks (+ flour)
  • Garlic infusion + white wine vinegar; parsley

Method

  1. Poach chicken with veg to tender; skim for clarity.
  2. Shred chicken; strain broth.
  3. Whisk liaison; temper with hot broth.
  4. Stir in + garlic + vinegar; warm through (no boil).
  5. Serve with parsley, extra garlic and vinegar.

Chef tips

  • Clear broth matters — skim diligently.
  • Temper, never dump — curdled soup can't be saved.
  • Vinegar to taste at the table — sourness is personal.
