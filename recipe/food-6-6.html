Okonomiyaki

Japan

Osaka-style savory pancake — 'grilled as you like it': cabbage folded through a dashi-nagaimo batter with pork belly, griddled thick and finished with okonomi sauce, mayo, aonori and dancing bonito flakes.

Ingredients

  • 80 g flour + 100 ml dashi + 50 g grated nagaimo + 2 eggs (batter for 2)
  • 200 g cabbage, 1 cm dice; green onion; tenkasu; pickled ginger
  • 160 g thin pork belly
  • Toppings: okonomi sauce, Japanese mayo, aonori, katsuobushi

Method

  1. Rest the flour-dashi-nagaimo batter 30 minutes refrigerated; whisk in eggs.
  2. Fold in cabbage, onion, tenkasu and ginger — chunky, not soupy.
  3. Griddle a thick round on medium heat; lay pork slices on top.
  4. Flip once after ~5 minutes; cook 5 more until springy and cooked through.
  5. Slide to a plate; paint with sauce, crosshatch mayo, and snow with aonori and bonito.

Chef tips

  • One flip only — pressing or flipping squeezes out the steam that cooks the cabbage.
  • Thick and patient beats thin and fast; low-medium heat cooks the center.
  • Nagaimo (or extra egg) gives the signature fluffy-grated lift.
