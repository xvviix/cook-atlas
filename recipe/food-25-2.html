Lechon

Philippines

Cebu's whole roast pig — the fiesta centerpiece: a 20 kg pig stuffed with lemongrass, scallion, garlic, onion and bay, sewn shut, glazed with evaporated milk and slow-turned over charcoal 4–5 hours to shatteringly crisp red skin and lemongrass-perfumed meat. Served with liver sauce (mang Tomas style).

Ingredients

  • 1 whole pig (~20 kg), cleaned
  • Stuffing: 10–20 lemongrass bundles + 500 g scallion + 1 kg garlic + 1 kg onion + bay
  • 2–3 cups salt + pepper; evaporated milk glaze; liver sauce to serve

Method

  1. Rinse cavity with boiling water; drain; salt inside and out.
  2. Pound aromatics; stuff the belly; sew shut; skewer on bamboo.
  3. Glaze skin with evaporated milk.
  4. Roast over charcoal 4–5 hrs, turning constantly, re-glazing.
  5. Rest; chop; serve with liver sauce and puso (hanging rice).

Chef tips

  • Constant turning = even crisp; still spots burn.
  • Milk glaze builds the signature red crackling.
  • Cebu lechon needs no sauce — but serve it anyway.
