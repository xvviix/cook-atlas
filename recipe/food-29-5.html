Beef Rendang

Malaysia

Minang slow-cooked glory: beef braised hours in coconut milk with lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves and toasted spice paste — until the liquid evaporates and the meat fries in its own spiced oil to dark, caramelized, flossy tenderness. Dry rendang (not a curry!) with kerisik depth.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef chuck, large chunks
  • Paste: shallot + garlic + ginger + galangal + lemongrass + chiles + turmeric + coriander
  • 800 ml coconut milk + lime leaves + cinnamon + star anise + tamarind + kerisik (toasted coconut paste)

Method

  1. Blend the paste; fry in oil to dark and fragrant.
  2. Add beef; coat; add coconut milk + aromatics.
  3. Simmer 2–3 hrs, stirring increasingly, until liquid gone.
  4. Fry in the rendered oil 15–20 min to dark and caramelized.
  5. Stir in kerisik + tamarind; rest; serve with rice/lemang.

Chef tips

  • Dry, not saucy — wet rendang is kalio, a different dish.
  • Large chunks — small pieces shred to floss.
  • The final fry makes rendang — don't stop at braised.
