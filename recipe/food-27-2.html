Schnitzel

Germany

Schweineschnitzel: pork loin pounded to 5 mm, floured, egged (with a squeeze of lemon in the wash), crumbed — never pressed — and fried floating in hot fat to a puffed, golden, souffle-like crust. Lemon wedges only; sauce on a schnitzel starts arguments.

Ingredients

  • 4 pork loin steaks, pounded 5 mm even
  • Flour + 2 eggs with lemon juice + plain breadcrumbs (no pressing!)
  • Plenty of oil/butter at 170 °C; lemon wedges

Method

  1. Pound even between film; season.
  2. Flour (shake off), egg, crumbs — lay crumbs on, don't press.
  3. Fry floating 2–3 min per side, swirling the pan, to puffed gold.
  4. Drain on a rack (not paper — steam softens).
  5. Serve instantly with lemon; potato salad alongside.

Chef tips

  • Never press the crumbs — pressing kills the souffle puff.
  • Swirl the pan — moving fat lifts the crust.
  • Fry in plenty of fat — shallow-fried schnitzel fries flat.
