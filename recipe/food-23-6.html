Tamales

Colombia

Tamal Tolimense: corn masa spread on plantain leaves and loaded with chicken, pork ribs, belly, rice, peas, potato, carrot and egg — folded into parcels, tied, and steamed 2–3 hours. Eaten for breakfast with hot chocolate and arepa.

Ingredients

  • Masa: masarepa + hot broth + lard + achiote + salt
  • Per tamal: chicken + pork rib + belly + rice + peas + potato + carrot + egg slice
  • Plantain leaves (flame-softened) + string

Method

  1. Flame-soften and grease the leaves.
  2. Marinate meats with cumin, garlic, salt.
  3. Spread masa on each leaf; layer rice, meats, veg, egg; cap with masa.
  4. Fold and tie parcels firmly.
  5. Steam 2–3 hrs; rest 10 min; unwrap at the table.

Chef tips

  • Tie firmly — loose parcels flood with water.
  • Keep the steamer topped up — boiling dry scorches.
  • Rest before unwrapping — the masa sets as it stands.
