Hot Dog

United States

Listed here as the Chicago dog — 'dragged through the garden': an all-beef frank (Vienna Beef) in a steamed poppy-seed bun with yellow mustard, neon-green relish, onion, tomato, pickle spear, sport peppers and celery salt. Ketchup is a disqualifying offense.

Ingredients

  • 4 all-beef franks (Vienna Beef / Hebrew National)
  • 4 poppy-seed buns, steamed
  • Yellow mustard + neon-green relish
  • Diced white onion + tomato wedges + dill pickle spear
  • Sport peppers + celery salt

Method

  1. Steam or boil the franks; steam the buns until soft and warm.
  2. Nestle each frank in its bun.
  3. Build in order: mustard zigzag, relish, onion, tomato wedges one side, pickle spear the other.
  4. Crown with 2 sport peppers; dust the whole garden with celery salt.
  5. Serve immediately with fries and a cold drink.

Chef tips

  • Order and completeness matter — seven toppings or it's not a Chicago dog.
  • No ketchup. Ever. This is a civic law.
  • Pepperoncini + regular relish substitute acceptably outside Chicago.
