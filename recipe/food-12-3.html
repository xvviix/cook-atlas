Karađorđeva šnicla

Serbia

Named for revolutionary Karađorđe: a pounded veal or pork cutlet rolled around kajmak (and ham), breaded and fried to deep gold — the molten center flowing like the river of legend. Serbia's most theatrical schnitzel.

Ingredients

  • 4 veal/pork cutlets (~150 g each), pounded 5 mm thin
  • 150–200 g kajmak (or cream cheese + sour cream)
  • 4 slices smoked ham; (swipe of ajvar, optional)
  • Flour + salted beaten eggs + breadcrumbs; oil/lard for frying; lemon

Method

  1. Pound cutlets thin and even; season both sides.
  2. Lay ham, spread kajmak generously, and roll tightly from the short side; tuck ends; pin with toothpicks.
  3. Bread flour → egg → crumbs, pressing firmly; rest 10 minutes to set the crust.
  4. Fry in 170 °C oil 5–7 minutes per side until deep gold and cooked through.
  5. Rest, remove pins, slice to reveal the flow; serve with lemon and potatoes.

Chef tips

  • Tight rolling + set crust = sealed molten center; loose rolling = leaked filling.
  • Don't skimp the kajmak — the flood is the drama.
  • Rest the breading before frying or it slides off in the pan.
