Croissant

France

Three days to honeycomb: yeasted dough laminated with a butter block through single and double folds, shaped into crescents, proofed to a jiggle and baked to deep mahogany. Eighty-one layers of patience.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g T55/bread flour + 150 ml milk + 100 ml water + 60 g sugar + 10 g salt + 7 g yeast + 30 g butter
  • Butter block: 250 g+ European-style butter (82%+ fat)
  • Egg wash; flour for rolling

Method

  1. Day 1: knead a smooth dough; chill overnight. Pound butter into a flat square; chill.
  2. Day 2: enclose the block envelope-style; roll and give one single fold; chill 1 hour. Roll and give one double fold; chill overnight.
  3. Day 3: roll to 4 mm; cut tall triangles; stretch, notch and roll to crescents.
  4. Proof 2 hours until wobbly and doubled; egg-wash twice.
  5. Bake at 200 °C 18–22 minutes to deep golden-brown; cool on a rack.

Chef tips

  • Butter and dough must share one firm-pliable texture — warm butter leaks, cold butter shatters.
  • Underbaking leaves a doughy core; push past golden to mahogany.
  • A cool kitchen is an ingredient — heat melts lamination.
