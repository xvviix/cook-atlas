Clam Chowder

United States

New England's creamy chowder: salt pork rendered down, onion and celery softened in the fat, potatoes simmered in clam broth, fresh clams added last, and heavy cream to finish. Thick with potatoes, never with flour paste — oyster crackers mandatory.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg fresh clams (quahogs/cherrystones) + their liquor + bottled clam juice
  • 100 g salt pork or bacon, diced
  • 1 onion + 2 celery ribs, diced; thyme + bay
  • 3–4 potatoes in 2 cm cubes; 500 ml heavy cream; white pepper; parsley + oyster crackers

Method

  1. Steam the clams just until open; strain and reserve the liquor; chop the meats.
  2. Render salt pork; soften onion and celery in the fat without browning.
  3. Add potatoes and clam liquor (+ water to cover); simmer 20–25 minutes to tender.
  4. Add clams 5 minutes; pour in cream; warm gently — never boil.
  5. Season with salt, white pepper and celery salt; serve with oyster crackers.

Chef tips

  • Clams go in last and briefly — rubber comes from overcooking.
  • Never boil after the cream — it splits and toughens everything.
  • Salt pork, not smoked bacon, keeps the clam flavor pure.
