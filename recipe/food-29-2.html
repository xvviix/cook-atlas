Char Kway Teow

Malaysia

Penang's wok-breath noodles: fresh flat rice noodles seared seconds in a smoking wok with prawns, cockles, lap cheong, egg, sprouts and chives — sauced with light/dark soy, chile and a touch of sugar. Wok hei is the ingredient you can't buy; small batches and violent heat fake it best.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh kway teow (room temp, separated)
  • Prawns + cockles + lap cheong + 2 eggs (duck eggs = luxury)
  • Sauce: light + dark soy + fish sauce + sugar + sambal; sprouts + chives + garlic; pork lard

Method

  1. Mix the sauce; everything within arm's reach.
  2. Smoking wok + lard; garlic 5 seconds; prawns + sausage.
  3. Eggs in; scramble half-set; noodles in — don't stir 20 seconds (char!).
  4. Sauce around the hot edges; toss 30 seconds.
  5. Sprouts + chives + cockles; 30 seconds; plate immediately.

Chef tips

  • One portion per batch — crowding kills wok hei.
  • Sauce on the wok edge — it caramelizes before touching noodles.
  • Eat instantly — CKT waits for no one.
