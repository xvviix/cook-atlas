Shchi

Russia

Russia's thousand-year cabbage soup: fresh and sauerkraut cabbages simmered with potato, carrot and porcini in meat broth — soured, hearty, restorative. 'Schi da kasha' (soup and porridge) sustained Russia; a dollop of smetana and dill finishes every bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 l meat/chicken broth + dried porcini (soaked, liquor saved)
  • 2 cups sauerkraut + 1 cup fresh cabbage + 3 potatoes + carrot + onion
  • Meat pieces + bay + dill + smetana

Method

  1. Simmer broth with porcini + liquor 30 min.
  2. Add potatoes, kraut, cabbage; simmer 30–45 min to tender.
  3. Soften onion-carrot; stir in (or cook directly in soup).
  4. Add meat; season.
  5. Serve with dill, green onion and smetana.

Chef tips

  • Rinse fierce kraut — unrinsed can overpower.
  • Porcini deepen everything — don't skip the liquor.
  • Better the next day — like all honest soups.
