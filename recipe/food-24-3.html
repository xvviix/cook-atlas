Massaman Curry

Thailand

The Persian-tinged royal curry: beef braised melting-tender with potatoes and onion in coconut milk with massaman paste, toasted whole spices, peanuts, tamarind and palm sugar — mild, sweet-sour, aromatic. Famously crowned 'world's most delicious food' in polls.

Ingredients

  • 700 g stewing beef + 400 ml coconut milk + stock
  • Massaman paste + cinnamon + cardamom + star anise + bay
  • Potatoes + onion + peanuts + tamarind + palm sugar + fish sauce

Method

  1. Braise beef in stock/coconut 1–1.5 hrs to near-tender.
  2. Toast whole spices fragrant.
  3. Fry paste in coconut oil; add coconut milk + tamarind.
  4. Add beef + stock + potatoes + onion + peanuts + spices.
  5. Simmer to tender; season sweet-sour-salty; rest; serve with rice.

Chef tips

  • Low and long — massaman can't be rushed.
  • Tamarind + palm sugar balance is the signature — taste and adjust.
  • Next-day massaman is even better.
