Sinigang

Philippines

Filipino sour soup: pork belly simmered with onion and tomato, soured with tamarind (fresh pulp or mix), loaded with radish, eggplant, sitaw, okra and kangkong — seasoned with patis. Mouth-puckering, savory, deeply comforting; the sourness should make you blink.

Ingredients

  • 900 g pork belly/ribs + 2.5 l water
  • Tamarind: 250 g fresh (boiled, mashed, strained) or 40 g mix
  • Onion + tomato; radish + eggplant + sitaw + okra + kangkong + siling haba; patis

Method

  1. Simmer pork with onion and tomato 50–60 min, skimming, to tender.
  2. Stir in tamarind; taste — boldly sour.
  3. Add hard veg (radish, taro) 10 min; then eggplant, beans, okra.
  4. Kangkong + chiles last 2 min; season with patis.
  5. Serve steaming with rice and patis-calamansi on the side.

Chef tips

  • Sour first, salt second — patis at the end.
  • Layer vegetables by density or all turns to mush.
  • Fresh tamarind beats mix; mix beats no sinigang.
