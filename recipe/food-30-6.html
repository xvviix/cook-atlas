Maakouda

Morocco

Casablanca's potato fritters: cumin-turmeric mashed potato with garlic and herbs, bound with egg, shaped into patties — dipped in a yeast batter (street style) or flour — fried gold. Stuffed into bread with harissa as the classic sandwich, or served with mint tea.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, boiled + mashed smooth
  • Garlic + cumin + turmeric + parsley/cilantro + egg + salt
  • Yeast batter (flour + yeast + water, risen 1 hr) or flour dust; oil at 175 °C

Method

  1. Boil + mash potatoes lump-free; cool 10 min.
  2. Mix in garlic, spices, herbs, egg.
  3. Shape patties; chill 20 min to firm.
  4. Dip in yeast batter (or flour).
  5. Fry at 175 °C to deep gold; drain; salt; serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Chill before frying — warm patties fall apart.
  • Drain potatoes well — wet mash = soggy fritters.
  • Don't crowd the oil — temperature crashes.
