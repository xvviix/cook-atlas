Köfte

Türkiye

Hand-kneaded Turkish meatballs/patties: beef or lamb worked springy with onion, breadcrumbs and cumin, grilled (ızgara) or pan-fried. Hundreds of regional versions — İzmir, İnegöl, içli — share the knead-and-rest method.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef/lamb (or mix)
  • 1 onion, grated and squeezed dry
  • 50 g breadcrumbs or soaked bread
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • Parsley + mint; cumin, paprika, allspice, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Knead everything by hand 5+ minutes until tacky and springy — this builds the signature bite.
  2. Rest covered in the fridge 30+ minutes to firm and meld.
  3. Shape into oval patties of even thickness.
  4. Grill or pan-fry over medium heat 4–5 minutes per side to browned and just cooked.
  5. Rest 5 minutes; serve with bulgur, salad and sumac onions.

Chef tips

  • Long kneading = springy köfte; brief mixing = crumbly burgers.
  • Squeeze the onion dry or the mixture loosens and splits.
  • Even thickness cooks evenly — ragged shapes burn at the edges.
