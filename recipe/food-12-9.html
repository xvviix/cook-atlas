Leskovačka mućkalica

Serbia

Leskovac's BBQ-born stew — mućkati means 'to mix/shake': grilled pork (traditionally leftover roast) shaken together with onions, peppers and tomatoes into a smoky, juicy sač-style stew. Grill smoke is the seasoning no spice can replace.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork (shoulder/leg), grilled first
  • 500 g onions + 4 red peppers + 3 tomatoes
  • Oil/lard; hot paprika; salt + pepper + parsley (+ garlic, optional)

Method

  1. Grill the seasoned pork 10 minutes for char and smoke; chop bite-size.
  2. Soften onions in oil; add peppers, then tomatoes; dust with paprika.
  3. Add the grilled pork and a splash of water/stock.
  4. Stew 20–30 minutes until the vegetables melt into a sauce around the meat.
  5. Shower with parsley; serve with bread or potatoes.

Chef tips

  • Pre-grilling is the dish — ungrilled pork makes a different stew.
  • Mućkalica means mixed: shake and stir, don't baby it.
  • Leftover roast or BBQ meat is the authentic, thrifty base.
