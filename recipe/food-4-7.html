Arroz de Marisco

Portugal

Portuguese seafood rice — soupier than paella, closer to a risotto: carolino rice simmered in shellfish broth with a tomato-onion sofrito and white wine, loaded with prawns, clams, mussels (and crab in the north), finished wet and glossy with cilantro.

Ingredients

  • 300 g carolino/Arborio rice
  • 1.2 l fish/shellfish stock + shellfish liquor
  • 200 g prawns, 300 g clams, 300 g mussels (scrubbed); crab (optional)
  • 1 onion + 3 garlic cloves + 200 g ripe tomatoes (sofrito)
  • 150 ml dry white wine; 60 ml olive oil; bay, paprika
  • Big handful of cilantro; salt, pepper, lemon

Method

  1. Steam open the clams and mussels in the wine; strain and reserve all liquor; shell half, keep half in shell for garnish.
  2. Build the sofrito: soften onion and garlic in oil, add tomato and paprika, cook to a thick jam.
  3. Add the rice, toast 1–2 minutes, then pour in hot stock + shellfish liquor (roughly 3:1 liquid to rice).
  4. Simmer ~15 minutes without much stirring; fold in prawns and shellfish for the last 4–5 minutes.
  5. Rest 2 minutes — the rice must arrive at the table moist and slightly soupy — then shower with cilantro and serve immediately.

Chef tips

  • Wet is correct: arroz de marisco should glisten and pool, never stand dry like paella.
  • Every drop of shellfish liquor goes back in — that is the flavor of the sea.
  • Serve the instant it's ready; the rice keeps drinking broth off the heat.
