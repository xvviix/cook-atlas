Colombia

Colombia's daily corn cakes: masarepa (pre-cooked cornmeal) hydrated to a smooth dough, patted into discs and griddled to spotty gold — plain as bread, or stuffed molten with cheese (arepa con queso). Crack-free edges and a hollow tap-tap mean done.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups masarepa (P.A.N.) + 1 tsp salt + 1 tbsp butter
  • 1.5 cups warm water/milk; queso fresco + mozzarella for stuffed version

Method

  1. Mix masarepa, salt, butter and warm liquid; rest 10 min.
  2. Knead smooth; form balls; flatten to 1 cm patties (or stuff with cheese and reseal).
  3. Griddle on medium-low 4–5 min per side to golden spots and firm.
  4. Tap — hollow sound means cooked through.
  5. Rest 3 min; serve hot with butter.

Chef tips

  • Medium-low heat — fierce heat burns raw middles.
  • Rest the dough — hydration prevents cracks.
  • Expel air when sealing cheese — pockets burst the cheese out.
