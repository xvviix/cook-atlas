Mango Sticky Rice

Thailand

Khao niao mamuang: glutinous rice soaked overnight, steamed and folded hot with sweet-salted coconut milk — rested to drink it all — served with ripe honey mango, extra coconut drizzle and sesame. Warm rice, cool mango; salt in the coconut is mandatory.

Ingredients

  • 250 g glutinous rice, soaked 4+ hrs/overnight
  • 400 ml coconut milk + 100 g sugar + ½ tsp salt
  • 3 ripe mangoes + sesame; (extra salted coconut drizzle)

Method

  1. Steam soaked rice 25–40 min to translucent and tender.
  2. Warm coconut + sugar + salt to dissolved (don't boil).
  3. Fold hot rice with most of the coconut; cover; rest 20–30 min.
  4. Slice mango; mold rice; drizzle salted coconut.
  5. Sesame over; serve warm/room-temp — never fridge-cold.

Chef tips

  • Hot rice drinks the coconut; cold rice repels it.
  • Salt in the coconut — unsalted tastes flat-sweet.
  • Ripe fragrant mango only — firm mango ruins it.
