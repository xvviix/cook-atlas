Shashlik

Russia

Soviet-style pork kebabs: shoulder cubed and layered overnight with masses of onion, vinegar, bay and pepper — threaded with onion rings and grilled over embers to charred and dripping. Served on lavash with scallions; the dacha weekend's entire purpose.

Ingredients

  • 2.5 kg pork shoulder, 4 cm cubes
  • 1.5 kg onions, half-rings + 60 ml vinegar + bay + peppercorns + salt
  • Lavash + scallions to serve; skewers (shampury)

Method

  1. Layer meat + onion + vinegar + spices; press; refrigerate 6+ hrs (vinegar: shorter).
  2. Thread meat with onion rings, spaced.
  3. Grill over embers 12–20 min, turning, to charred and 70 °C+.
  4. Rest on lavash 5 min.
  5. Serve with scallions, lavash and adjika/tkemali.

Chef tips

  • Vinegar briefly (1–2 hrs) — overnight vinegar pickles the meat.
  • Onion juice does the tenderizing — massage it in.
  • Embers, spaced skewers — crowding steams.
