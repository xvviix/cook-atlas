Pizza Napoletana

Italy

⭐ 5.0  1 community vote  join them

The Naples original protected by AVPN rules and EU TSG status: a soft, elastic disc with a puffy, leopard-spotted rim (cornicione), thin center, and minimal topping — classically Marinara (tomato, garlic, oregano, oil) or Margherita (tomato, fior di latte or buffalo mozzarella, basil, oil). Authentic dough contains only flour, water, salt and yeast — no oil or sugar.

Ingredients

  • 500 g “00” flour (or half 00 / half bread flour)
  • 325 ml lukewarm water (~65% hydration)
  • 10 g fine sea salt
  • 3–7 g fresh yeast (or ~2 g instant dry yeast)
  • 400 g canned San Marzano tomatoes, crushed by hand + pinch of salt
  • 250 g fior di latte or buffalo mozzarella, torn and well drained
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra-virgin olive oil

Method

  1. Dissolve salt in the water, then mix with flour and yeast into a shaggy dough; knead 10–20 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  2. Bulk-ferment covered at room temperature ~2 hours, then divide into 250–280 g balls and cold-proof 24–48 hours (or at least 8 hours) for flavor.
  3. Bring dough balls to room temperature 2 hours before baking. Place a pizza stone/steel on the top rack and preheat the oven at its maximum (275–300 °C / 525 °F+) for at least 45–60 minutes.
  4. On a floured surface stretch each ball from the center outward, leaving a 1–2 cm airy rim untouched; never use a rolling pin.
  5. Spread a thin layer of crushed tomato, add torn mozzarella sparingly, and slide onto the hot stone/steel (a wood-fired oven needs only 60–90 seconds; a home oven 6–12 minutes).
  6. Finish with basil and a drizzle of olive oil and serve immediately.

Chef tips

  • Less is more: too much sauce or cheese makes the center soupy.
  • Drain fresh mozzarella ahead so it does not flood the pizza.
  • Use the hottest oven possible — heat is the main substitute for a wood fire.
