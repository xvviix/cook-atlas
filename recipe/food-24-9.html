Satay

Thailand

Thai chicken satay: turmeric-gold strips marinated in coconut milk, lemongrass, garlic and spices — grilled over charcoal to charred edges, served with sweet-savory peanut sauce and ajaad (cucumber-vinegar relish). Zigzag threading grills evenly.

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken, thin strips
  • Marinade: coconut milk + turmeric + coriander + cumin + lemongrass + garlic + fish sauce + sugar
  • Peanut sauce: peanut butter + coconut milk + red curry paste + sugar + lime; ajaad relish

Method

  1. Marinate 2–4+ hrs (overnight best); soak skewers.
  2. Thread zigzag on skewers.
  3. Grill 2–3 min per side to charred and cooked.
  4. Simmer the peanut sauce smooth; thin as needed.
  5. Serve with sauce, ajaad and toasted bread/rice cakes.

Chef tips

  • Thin even strips — thick chunks char raw.
  • Soak bamboo skewers or they ignite.
  • Char is flavor — don't fear the blackened edges.
