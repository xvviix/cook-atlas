Pulpo a la Gallega

Spain

Galicia's octopus feast (pulpo a feira at fairs): a whole octopus triple-dipped ('scared') then boiled tender, sliced over potatoes cooked in its water, and dressed with coarse salt, Pimentón de la Vera and olive oil — served on a wooden plate.

Ingredients

  • 1 octopus, 1.5–2 kg (frozen-then-thawed)
  • 1 bay leaf + ½ onion for the pot
  • 500 g potatoes (cachelos)
  • Sweet Pimentón de la Vera (2 tbsp) + pinch of hot
  • Coarse sea salt + generous extra-virgin olive oil

Method

  1. Bring a tall pot of water to a rolling boil with bay and onion.
  2. Grip the octopus and dip it in-and-out 3 times ('asustar' — this keeps the skin on and curls the tentacles).
  3. Submerge and simmer gently ~6 minutes per 500 g, until a skewer slides through the thickest part; rest in the water 15–20 minutes.
  4. Slice tentacles ~1 cm thick; boil potato rounds in the octopus water.
  5. Plate potatoes, then octopus; dress in order: salt, sweet paprika blanket, hot pinch, olive oil flood.

Chef tips

  • Previously frozen octopus is more tender — buy frozen on purpose.
  • Smoked La Vera paprika is mandatory; generic paprika misses the soul of the dish.
  • Resting in the hot water finishes the texture — don't skip it.
