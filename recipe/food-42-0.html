Chelo Kebab

Iran

Iran's national dish: charcoal-grilled koobideh (hand-kneaded minced lamb with grated onion) and/or barg (saffron-marinated tenderloin strips) laid over saffron-steamed basmati (chelo) crowned with a knob of butter and a grilled tomato — eaten with sumac, raw onion and doogh. The koobideh must be kneaded to a sticky paste so it clings to the flat skewer over fierce coals; falling kebab is a rite of passage. Simple, smoky, perfect.

Ingredients

  • Koobideh: 1 kg minced lamb (20% fat) + 2 grated onions (squeezed!) + salt + pepper
  • Barg: 600 g tenderloin + saffron + onion juice + yogurt + lemon
  • Chelo: basmati + saffron + butter; tomatoes for grilling; sumac + onion to serve

Method

  1. Squeeze every drop of juice from the grated onions — wet onion drops kebabs.
  2. Knead the mince 10+ minutes until sticky and pasty; chill 2+ hours.
  3. Mold firmly onto flat skewers, grooving with your fingers.
  4. Grill over fierce coals 4–5 minutes per side, turning once.
  5. Steam saffron chelo; butter generously.
  6. Serve kebabs on the rice with grilled tomato, sumac and onion.

Chef tips

  • Dry onions, sticky mince, cold rest — the trinity of clinging koobideh.
  • Flat skewers only — round ones spin the meat.
  • Fierce heat, fast cook — gentle heat dries kebabs out.
  • Saffron bloomed in ice water gives the brightest chelo.
