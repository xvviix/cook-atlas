Carbonada

Argentina

Carbonada en zapallo: beef stewed with sweet potato, corn, tomato and dried fruit (apricots/peaches) — served inside a roasted whole pumpkin, the flesh scooped with each ladle. Sweet-savory criollo comfort and the most dramatic presentation in Argentine cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole pumpkin (4–5 kg) + 800 g beef chuck
  • Onion + peppers + tomato + stock + wine
  • Sweet potato + potato + corn + dried apricots; oregano + bay + paprika

Method

  1. Cap and seed the pumpkin; oil; roast 180 °C 45–60 min to tender.
  2. Brown beef; soften veg; wine + stock in.
  3. Braise 1 hr; add roots, corn, fruit; 30–40 min more.
  4. Ladle stew into the pumpkin shell.
  5. Serve scooping pumpkin flesh with the stew.

Chef tips

  • Roast the pumpkin alongside — timing them together matters.
  • Dried fruit is traditional — don't skip the sweet note.
  • Thick stew — watery carbonada floods the shell.
