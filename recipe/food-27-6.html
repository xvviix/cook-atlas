Kartoffelpuffer

Germany

Rhine-style potato pancakes: russets grated fine, squeezed dry (saving the starch!), bound with egg and onion — fried lacy-crisp in shallow oil. Served with applesauce (the classic sweet-savory pairing) or sour cream; Christmas-market essential.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg russet potatoes, finely grated
  • 1 onion grated + 2 eggs + salt + pepper + nutmeg
  • Saved potato starch + oil for shallow-frying; applesauce

Method

  1. Grate potato + onion; squeeze bone-dry in cloth, catching liquid.
  2. Let starch settle; pour off water; return starch to the mix.
  3. Bind with egg + seasonings.
  4. Shallow-fry thin patties 3–4 min per side to lacy gold.
  5. Drain; salt; serve hot with applesauce.

Chef tips

  • Squeeze ruthlessly — wet batter fries soggy, never crisp.
  • Russets only — waxy potatoes go gluey.
  • Fry thin — thick puffers stay raw inside.
