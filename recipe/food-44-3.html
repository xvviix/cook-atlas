Kibinai

Lithuania

Kibinai are the Karaim pastries of Trakai: half-moon hand pies of rich sour-cream pastry wrapped around juicy minced lamb (or pork) with onion — brought by the Karaim people settled in Trakai by Grand Duke Vytautas in the 14th century. The pastry must be rolled thin but sturdy enough to hold the juicy filling; crimped edges seal in the steam that cooks the meat. Eaten hot from Trakai's kibinines with broth or beer, they're Lithuania's most storied snack.

Ingredients

  • Pastry: 500 g flour + 200 g butter + 200 ml sour cream + 2 eggs + salt
  • Filling: 500 g minced lamb/pork + 2 onions + pepper + salt + broth splash
  • Egg wash + (broth to serve)

Method

  1. Rub butter into flour; bind with sour cream and eggs; chill 1 hour.
  2. Mix mince with finely chopped onion, seasoning and a splash of broth.
  3. Roll thin rounds; fill one half generously.
  4. Fold, crimp tightly and egg-wash.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 25–35 minutes until deep gold.
  6. Rest 5 minutes (the filling is molten); serve with broth.

Chef tips

  • Chill the pastry — warm pastry shrinks and leaks.
  • Crimp hard — the juicy filling escapes weak seals.
  • Thin but sturdy — too thin bursts; too thick doughs the bite.
  • Rest before biting — kibinai lava is legendary.
