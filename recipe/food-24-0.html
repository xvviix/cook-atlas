Pad Thai

Thailand

Thailand's wok-tossed icon: rice-stick noodles in tamarind-palm sugar-fish sauce with shrimp, tofu, egg ribbons, pickled radish and chives — tossed over fierce heat, finished with peanuts, bean sprouts and lime. Sweet-sour-salty in balance; ketchup nowhere near it.

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice-stick noodles, soaked pliable (not boiled)
  • Sauce: tamarind + palm sugar + fish sauce (+ dark soy)
  • 300 g shrimp + firm tofu + 2 eggs + pickled radish + dried shrimp + chives + sprouts + peanuts + lime

Method

  1. Simmer the sauce to dissolved; set aside.
  2. Sear shrimp 1 min; push aside; scramble eggs in the wok.
  3. Add noodles + sauce; toss 2–3 min until supple and glazed.
  4. Fold in tofu, radish, shrimp, chives, sprouts.
  5. Plate with peanuts, sprouts, lime and chile flakes.

Chef tips

  • Soak, don't boil, the noodles — they finish in the sauce.
  • One or two portions per wok — crowding steams.
  • Taste the sauce first — it should sing sweet-sour-salty.
