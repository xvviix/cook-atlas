Adobo

Philippines

The Philippines' national dish: chicken (or pork) braised in cane vinegar, soy sauce, crushed garlic, bay and peppercorns until tender, then reduced to a glossy, tangy-salty glaze — often with a final crisp under the broiler. Every family has its ratio; the vinegar-soy debate is eternal.

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg bone-in chicken thighs
  • 180 ml cane vinegar + 120 ml soy sauce + 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed + 4 bay leaves + 2 tsp peppercorns

Method

  1. Marinate chicken 1–3 hrs in everything (optional but deeper).
  2. Sear skin-side down to gold; add garlic briefly.
  3. Pour in marinade; simmer covered 20 min.
  4. Flip; simmer 20 min more to tender.
  5. Uncover; reduce to a syrupy glaze (or broil skin crisp); serve over rice.

Chef tips

  • Don't stir the vinegar early — let it boil out raw harshness.
  • Balance is personal: more vinegar for Batangas style, more soy for Manila.
  • Leftovers improve overnight — adobo is meal-prep royalty.
