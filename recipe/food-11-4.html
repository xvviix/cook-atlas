Guacamole

Mexico

The real thing, built in layers in a molcajete: onion, serrano, cilantro and salt ground to a paste first (releasing their oils), then avocado mashed just to chunky, tomato folded in last — never ground. Five ingredients, zero packets.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • ¼ white onion + 1–2 serranos + cilantro + salt (the ground paste)
  • 1 tomato, diced; 1 lime; (garlic + cumin: traditional in some regions)

Method

  1. Grind onion, chile, cilantro and salt in the molcajete to a dark-green paste.
  2. Add avocado and mash to broken-up but distinctly chunky.
  3. Fold in tomato with a spoon — never grind it.
  4. Squeeze lime over, adjust salt, and serve in the molcajete with totopos.

Chef tips

  • Paste first, avocado second — the order is the technique.
  • Chunky, not baby food — over-mashing is the classic failure.
  • Make it at the last minute; lime slows browning but time always wins.
