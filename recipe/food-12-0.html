Ćevapi

Serbia

Serbia's national grill: hand-shaped skinless kebabs of beef (plus lamb and/or pork), worked sticky with baking soda and sparkling water, rested overnight and charred over fierce heat — stuffed into lepinja with kajmak, raw onion and ajvar.

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef + 250 g lamb + 250 g pork, ground
  • 2 tsp salt + 2 tbsp black pepper + garlic paste
  • 1 tsp baking soda + 2–3 tbsp sparkling water
  • Lepinja flatbread; kajmak; white onions; ajvar

Method

  1. Mix the meats vigorously by hand 5+ minutes with seasonings, soda and sparkling water until sticky and cohesive.
  2. Rest covered overnight in the fridge — flavor melds and texture sets.
  3. With wet hands, shape 8 cm × 2 cm cylinders.
  4. Grill over very high heat 3–4 minutes per side to charred outside, juicy inside.
  5. Warm lepinja on the grill; stuff with ćevapi, diced onion, ajvar and kajmak.

Chef tips

  • The overnight rest is structural — same-day ćevapi crumble.
  • Baking soda + sparkling water give the springy, juicy bite.
  • Fierce heat, quick turn — gentle grilling steams them grey.
