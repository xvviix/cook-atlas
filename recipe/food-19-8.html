Sundubu-jjigae

Korea

Silken-tofu stew served volcanic in a ttukbaegi: uncurdled sundubu ladled into a gochugaru-seafood (or pork-kimchi) broth with zucchini, mushrooms and an egg cracked in at the table. Spooned over rice, still bubbling — winter's answer.

Ingredients

  • 350 g sundubu (uncurdled silken tofu)
  • Broth: anchovy stock + gochugaru + garlic + soy + fish sauce + sugar
  • Clams + shrimp (or pork + kimchi); zucchini + enoki + onion
  • Sesame oil; egg per serving; scallions; rice

Method

  1. Sizzle aromatics and gochugaru in sesame oil 1 minute (don't burn).
  2. Add stock and seasonings; boil; add seafood/pork and vegetables.
  3. Ladle in big clouds of sundubu; simmer 2–3 minutes.
  4. Crack in the egg; scallions over; serve erupting with rice.

Chef tips

  • Gochugaru burns bitter in seconds — one minute, then liquid.
  • Sundubu, not firm tofu — the custardy curds are the dish.
  • Serve in the screaming-hot pot — the tableside bubble matters.
