Gado-Gado

Indonesia

Jakarta's 'mix-mix' salad: blanched vegetables, fried tofu/tempeh, potato, egg and cucumber under a thick sweet-spicy peanut dressing with tamarind and kecap manis — finished with fried shallots and krupuk. A complete vegetarian meal.

Ingredients

  • Vegetables: green beans, cabbage, carrots, bean sprouts, cucumber, tomato, boiled potato
  • 200 g fried tofu/tempeh + 2–3 boiled eggs
  • Sauce: 150 g roasted peanuts + garlic + shallot + chile + 2 tbsp kecap manis + tamarind + palm sugar + lime
  • Fried shallots + krupuk to serve

Method

  1. Blanch each vegetable separately to crisp-tender; shock in cold water to hold color.
  2. Fry tofu/tempeh golden; boil eggs and potatoes; slice everything bite-size.
  3. Blend the sauce with hot water to a thick, pourable dressing; balance sweet-sour-salty-hot.
  4. Arrange vegetables, tofu, potato and egg on a platter; blanket with sauce.
  5. Top with fried shallots and serve with krupuk.

Chef tips

  • The sauce is the dish — taste and balance it before anything else.
  • Blanch-and-shock keeps every vegetable vivid, not army-green.
  • Assemble just before serving; dressed vegetables wilt within the hour.
