Ají de Gallina

Peru

A comforting Creole classic: shredded poached chicken folded into a creamy golden sauce of ají amarillo, milk-soaked bread, walnuts and Parmesan, served over rice with boiled potatoes, egg and black olives. Mild heat, rich and nutty.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts (or 1 small hen), poached + shredded; reserve the broth
  • 1 red onion + 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 3–4 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 3 slices white bread (or 6 saltines), soaked in 250 ml evaporated milk
  • 50 g walnuts or pecans, ground; 50 g Parmesan, grated
  • ½ tsp turmeric (for color); oil, salt, pepper
  • To serve: white rice, boiled yellow potatoes, hard eggs, black olives, parsley

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in salted water 10–40 minutes (breast vs hen); cool, shred, and strain the broth.
  2. Soften onion and garlic in oil, stir in ají amarillo and turmeric until fragrant.
  3. Blend in the soaked bread and a cup of broth; simmer 10 minutes, then add walnuts and Parmesan until thick and velvety.
  4. Fold in the shredded chicken; loosen with broth to a spoonable, curry-like consistency; season.
  5. Serve over rice with potato slices, egg, olives and parsley.

Chef tips

  • The sauce thickens as it stands — keep extra broth handy to loosen.
  • Seeded fresh ajíes (blanched) give brighter flavor than paste alone.
  • Day-two leftovers are excellent; the sauce keeps 3 days refrigerated.
