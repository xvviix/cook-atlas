Chicken Inasal

Philippines

Bacolod's orange-glazed grill: chicken marinated in calamansi, vinegar, lemongrass, garlic and ginger — grilled over charcoal while basted with annatto-chicken oil to a glossy tangerine char. Served with garlic rice and sinamak (spiced vinegar). Paa (leg) or pecho (breast)? Choose sides.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken leg quarters
  • Marinade: calamansi + vinegar + lemongrass + garlic + ginger + salt + pepper (+ soda splash)
  • Baste: annatto oil + butter/margarine + marinade drippings; sinamak + garlic rice

Method

  1. Marinate 3 hrs–overnight.
  2. Render chicken skin with annatto for the basting oil (+ butter).
  3. Grill over medium charcoal, turning and basting constantly.
  4. Cook to 74 °C+ with charred, glossy skin.
  5. Serve with garlic rice and sinamak.

Chef tips

  • Baste constantly — the oil builds color and juiciness.
  • Medium heat — fierce coals char raw middles.
  • Overnight marinade penetrates; 1 hour only perfumes.
