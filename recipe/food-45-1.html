Shawarma

Syria

Syrian shawarma — Damascus street-food royalty: chicken (or lamb) marinated in yogurt, lemon, garlic and shawarma spice, roasted and shaved, rolled in thin markouk bread with toum (whipped garlic sauce), pickles and fries, then pressed on the grill until crisp. The Syrian signature is the toum — pungent, fluffy, mayo-free garlic emulsion — and the final press that fuses the wrap. (See food-48-2 for the Saudi saj-wrapped style.)

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg chicken thighs + yogurt + lemon + garlic + shawarma spice + paprika + oil
  • Toum: garlic + egg white/oil emulsion + lemon + salt (or yogurt-garlic shortcut)
  • Markouk/pita + pickles + fries to roll

Method

  1. Marinate chicken 4+ hours (overnight best) in spiced yogurt.
  2. Roast hot (220 °C) until charred at the edges; rest; shave thin.
  3. Crisp the shavings in a hot pan with their juices.
  4. Whip toum: garlic paste emulsified with oil drop by drop until fluffy.
  5. Spread toum on bread; add chicken, pickles, fries; roll tight.
  6. Press on a hot griddle until golden and sealed; serve with extra toum.

Chef tips

  • Thighs over breast — breast dries under the fierce heat.
  • Overnight marinade — hours make the char and tang.
  • Press the finished wrap — unpressed shawarma falls apart.
  • Toum drop by drop — rushing the oil splits the emulsion.
