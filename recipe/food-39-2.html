Tibs

Ethiopia

Tibs (siga tibs) are Ethiopia's sizzling stir-fry — chunks or strips of beef seared hard in niter kibbeh with onions, peppers, garlic, ginger, berbere and a spoon of fiery awaze paste, finished with rosemary and served still crackling. Unlike slow wats, tibs is fast food: high heat, minutes in the pan, eaten with injera to catch every drop of spiced butter. Variations run from mild (lec-lec tibs) to full-fire awaze tibs.

Ingredients

  • 750 g beef (sirloin/flank), cubed or stripped
  • 2 onions + 2 bell peppers + jalapeños + garlic + ginger
  • Niter kibbeh + 2 tbsp berbere + awaze paste + rosemary + lemon

Method

  1. Toss beef with berbere, salt and pepper; sear in batches over fierce heat.
  2. Set beef aside; soften onions, peppers, garlic and ginger in niter kibbeh.
  3. Return beef; stir through awaze and rosemary.
  4. Sizzle 2–3 minutes more; finish with lemon.
  5. Serve immediately on injera — tibs must arrive crackling.

Chef tips

  • Sear in batches — crowded beef steams grey instead of browning.
  • Fierce heat throughout — tibs is a sprint, not a braise.
  • Awaze to taste — its fire varies by batch.
  • Serve instantly — resting tibs overcook in their own heat.
