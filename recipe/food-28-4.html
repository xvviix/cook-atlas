Olivier Salad

Russia

Salat Olivye: Russia's New Year table anchor — potatoes and carrots boiled whole, diced with eggs, pickles, peas and ham/chicken, bound with mayo (plus a spoon of smetana). Every cube the same size; served cold in heroic quantities from December 31 to mid-January.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes + 2 carrots, boiled whole in skins
  • 4 eggs hard-boiled + 3 pickles (salted, not sweet) + peas + 200 g ham/chicken
  • Mayo (+ smetana) + salt + pepper

Method

  1. Boil potatoes/carrots whole to just tender; cool completely.
  2. Dice everything into even 7–8 mm cubes; squeeze pickles dry.
  3. Combine with drained peas.
  4. Fold in mayo gradually to coat, not drown.
  5. Chill 1+ hr; serve cold with bread.

Chef tips

  • Boil whole in skins — peeled potatoes go watery.
  • Even cubes — the texture depends on it.
  • Mayo to coat — soupy Olivier is a disgrace.
