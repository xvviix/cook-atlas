Kitfo

Ethiopia

Kitfo — from the Gurage people — is Ethiopia's celebrated minced-beef dish: the finest lean beef chopped by hand, warmed through with melted niter kibbeh, mitmita (scorching bird's-eye chili blend) and aromatic korerima (Ethiopian cardamom), served leb-leb (warmed rare) or fully cooked to order. Traditionally eaten on holidays with ayib (fresh cheese) and gomen (collards) to cool the fire, it demands the freshest meat handled scrupulously. The butter should glisten on every strand.

Ingredients

  • 900 g very fresh lean beef (top round/tenderloin)
  • 120 ml niter kibbeh + 2 tbsp mitmita + korerima + koseret + salt
  • Ayib cheese + gomen + injera to serve

Method

  1. Source the freshest beef possible; keep everything ice-cold and clean.
  2. Trim all fat and sinew; mince finely by hand (cleaner texture than machine).
  3. Melt niter kibbeh with mitmita, korerima and salt until fragrant.
  4. Fold in the beef off or on very low heat; warm 3–5 minutes to desired doneness.
  5. Serve immediately with ayib, gomen and injera.

Chef tips

  • Freshness is safety — buy from a trusted butcher and serve the same day.
  • Hand-mincing gives the prized texture — machines smear the fat.
  • Mitmita is fiercely hot — warn the table and serve cooling ayib.
  • Offer leb-leb and well-done — not everyone eats rare beef.
