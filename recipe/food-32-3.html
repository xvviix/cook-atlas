Cazuela

Chile

Chile's home-soup: beef short rib (or chicken) simmered with onion into a clear broth — then potato, pumpkin, carrot, corn and rice added in stages, finished with green beans and cilantro. Big recognizable pieces, bright broth; winter comfort meaning 'home'.

Ingredients

  • 800 g short ribs/chicken on the bone + 2 l water
  • Onion + garlic + oregano + cumin + bay
  • Potato + pumpkin + carrot + corn + rice + green beans; cilantro

Method

  1. Sear meat; add onion + spices + water; simmer 30 min, skimming.
  2. Add potato + carrot; 10 min.
  3. Add pumpkin + corn + rice; 15 min.
  4. Green beans last 5 min.
  5. Cilantro over; serve with pebre and bread.

Chef tips

  • Skim for clarity — cloudy cazuela looks tired.
  • Stage the veg — together they mush.
  • Bone-in meat — boneless broth tastes thin.
