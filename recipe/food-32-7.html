Chorrillana

Chile

Valparaíso's bar platter: a mountain of fries topped with seared beef strips, caramelized onions and fried eggs (yolks runny!) — seasoned with merkén, served on one long plate for the table to attack with forks. Born in bars; best with beer and friends.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, cut fries
  • 500 g beef (posta negra/sirloin), strips + merkén
  • 2 onions feather-cut + 4 eggs; oil; salt + pepper

Method

  1. Fry potatoes crisp; salt; keep hot.
  2. Sear beef hard with merkén; set aside.
  3. Caramelize onions; fry eggs sunny.
  4. Platter: fries, onions, beef, eggs on top.
  5. Serve instantly, forks in, beer alongside.

Chef tips

  • Runny yolks enrich everything — don't overcook.
  • Merkén is the Valparaíso signature — source it.
  • Serve on one platter — chorrillana is shared.
