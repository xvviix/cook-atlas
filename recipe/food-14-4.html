Gołąbki

Poland

'Little pigeons': pork-and-rice parcels in tender cabbage leaves, braised in tomato sauce until spoon-soft. Sunday-dinner Poland — humble, filling, and better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 1 large green cabbage, cored
  • 600 g pork (or pork-beef) + 1 cup par-cooked rice
  • Onion + garlic + marjoram + salt + pepper
  • Tomato passata + broth + pinch of sugar

Method

  1. Blanch the cored cabbage, peeling leaves as they soften; trim thick ribs.
  2. Mix meat with rice, onion and seasonings; roll firm parcels in the leaves.
  3. Line the pot with outer leaves; pack rolls seam-down.
  4. Pour over tomato + broth; cover and braise 1.5–2 hours at 170 °C.
  5. Rest; serve with potatoes or bread, spooning over the sauce.

Chef tips

  • Par-cook the rice — raw rice bursts the rolls; fully cooked turns to mush.
  • Pack snugly — loose rolls unravel in the braise.
  • Day-two gołąbki, reheated in their sauce, are the prize.
