Boerewors

South Africa

Farmer's sausage in its iconic coil: coarse beef-pork with toasted coriander, clove, nutmeg and vinegar — stuffed in hog casings, coiled, and grilled over coals (never pricked). Served with pap and sous; Braai Day runs on boerewors rolls.

Ingredients

  • 2.5 kg beef + 1.5 kg pork + 500 g pork fat, coarse ground
  • Toasted coriander + clove + nutmeg + allspice + pepper + salt + sugar
  • Vinegar (+ brandy); hog casings

Method

  1. Toast + grind coriander and spices.
  2. Mix meats + spices + vinegar; chill 2+ hrs.
  3. Grind coarse; mix to tacky.
  4. Stuff loosely into casings; coil.
  5. Grill over coals to 70 °C+ — never prick.

Chef tips

  • Coarse grind — fine mince isn't boerewors.
  • Never prick — juices stay in.
  • 90% meat minimum by law — no fillers.
