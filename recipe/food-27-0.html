Bratwurst

Germany

Beer brats, done right: raw bratwurst poached gently in beer with onions (never boiled, never pierced), then grilled to snappy, charred casings — served in rolls with the beer onions and sharp mustard. The simmer-then-sear keeps them juicy; the grill gives the snap.

Ingredients

  • 6 raw bratwurst (don't pierce!)
  • 2 bottles lager + 3 sliced onions + butter + thyme
  • Rolls + sharp German mustard

Method

  1. Caramelize onions in butter 20–30 min.
  2. Add beer; nestle brats; simmer gently 15 min to 70 °C (never boil).
  3. Lift brats; grill 3–5 min to charred and snappy.
  4. Toast rolls.
  5. Serve with beer onions and mustard.

Chef tips

  • Never pierce — juices escape, dryness enters.
  • Simmer, don't boil — boiling bursts casings.
  • Mustard sharp, not sweet — that's the German way.
