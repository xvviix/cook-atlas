New York Cheesecake

United States

The dense, tall, velvet New York slice: 1+ kg of full-fat block cream cheese beaten silky with sugar, eggs and cream, baked low in a water bath, cooled in stages, and chilled overnight. The jiggle-and-rest method defeats cracks.

Ingredients

  • Crust: graham crumbs + butter + sugar, baked 10 min
  • Filling: 900 g–1.1 kg block cream cheese (room temp!) + 300 g sugar + flour/cornstarch
  • 4–5 eggs + yolks + 180 ml sour cream/cream + vanilla + lemon

Method

  1. Beat room-temp cream cheese silky; add sugar; add eggs one at a time on low.
  2. Fold in cream, vanilla, lemon; pour over the crust in a leak-proofed springform.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 160 °C 60–90 min until edges set, center wobbles.
  4. Cool 1 hour in the turned-off oven (door ajar), then room temp, then chill overnight.
  5. Slice with a hot, wiped knife; serve plain or with berries.

Chef tips

  • Room-temperature everything — cold cream cheese lumps permanently.
  • Low speed, minimal air — whipped air rises then cracks.
  • The overnight chill is structural; same-day slices slump.
