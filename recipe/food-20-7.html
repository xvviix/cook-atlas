Baba ghanoush

Lebanon

The smoky eggplant purée: whole eggplants charred directly over flame until collapsed, drained of bitter juices, then whipped with tahini, lemon and garlic while streaming in olive oil to a pale, creamy emulsion. Finished with oil, pomegranate and parsley.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants (or 3 Italian)
  • 3 tbsp tahini + 2 tbsp lemon + 1–2 garlic cloves + salt
  • 80 ml olive oil + more to finish; parsley + pomegranate + sumac

Method

  1. Pierce eggplants; char over open flame (or broiler), turning, until collapsed (~20 min).
  2. Cool in a colander; peel; drain 15–30 minutes.
  3. Mash with lemon and garlic to a rough paste.
  4. Whisk in tahini, then oil in a thin stream to a pale cream.
  5. Swirl into a bowl; oil, pomegranate, parsley over; serve with pita.

Chef tips

  • Direct flame = smoke; oven-only = flat. Char aggressively.
  • Drain well or the dip turns watery and bitter.
  • Emulsify the oil in slowly — dumped oil pools.
