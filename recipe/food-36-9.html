Würstel

Austria

Vienna's Würstelstand culture runs on the Frankfurter — the slim smoked beef-pork sausage in natural casing that emigrated to become the world's 'wiener', here eaten standing up with mustard, kren (horseradish) and a Semmel, or as Bosna/Käsekrainer variations. Authentic preparation is poaching, never boiling: simmered water, sausages off the heat for 8 minutes, so the snap stays snappy and the skin never splits. With beer at midnight after the opera, it is Vienna in edible form.

Ingredients

  • Quality frankfurters in natural casing (2 pairs per person)
  • Water for poaching + crusty Semmel or rye bread
  • Mustard (sweet Bavarian or sharp) + kren + pickles

Method

  1. Bring a pot of water to a bare simmer — never a rolling boil.
  2. Take off the heat; slip in the sausages; cover 8 minutes to heat through.
  3. Warm the rolls; slice pickles; set out mustard and horseradish.
  4. Serve the Würstel whole in the roll or alongside, dunked freely.
  5. Eat standing, with beer, ideally after midnight.

Chef tips

  • Poach, never boil — boiling splits casings and leaches flavor.
  • Natural casing gives the snap — skinless sausages are a different food.
  • Mustard plus kren is the classic Viennese pairing.
  • Serve immediately — reheated Würstel turn rubbery.
