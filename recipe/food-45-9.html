Baklava

Syria

Syrian baklava — Aleppo's pistachio masterpieces (bülbül nests, mabroomeh rolls, baklawa diamonds): whisper-thin phyllo (or kataifi) layered with clarified butter and mounds of Aleppo pistachios, baked low until shattering, then flooded with orange-blossom or rose atr syrup. Less sweet and more buttery-nutty than many versions, Syrian baklava rests overnight so the syrup settles into every layer. Ghee (samneh), not plain butter, is the authentic richness.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack phyllo (or kataifi) + 400 g clarified butter/ghee
  • 500 g pistachios (Aleppo if possible), coarsely ground
  • Atr: 2 cups sugar + water + lemon + orange blossom/rose

Method

  1. Grind pistachios coarse (never powder); clarify the butter.
  2. Layer phyllo 8–10 sheets, buttering each; spread nuts; repeat.
  3. Top with 8–10 buttered sheets; cut diamonds before baking.
  4. Bake low (160–170 °C) 45–60 minutes until deep gold and crisp.
  5. Flood hot baklava with cool syrup; rest overnight.
  6. Serve at room temperature, garnished with ground pistachio.

Chef tips

  • Cut before baking — cutting after shatters the layers.
  • Hot pastry + cool syrup — the shock drives absorption.
  • Low and long — fierce heat browns raw middles.
  • Rest overnight — fresh baklava is good; day-two is transcendent.
