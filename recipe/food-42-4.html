Joojeh Kebab

Iran

Joojeh — saffron chicken kebab — is Iran's charcoal-grilled joy: bone-in chicken marinated overnight in saffron, onion juice, yogurt and lemon until sunset-yellow, grilled over coals and basted with saffron butter. Juicier than breast-only versions when made with cornish hen or leg quarters, it arrives on chelo with grilled tomato and lemon. The overnight saffron marinade is what separates restaurant-transcendent from merely good.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg bone-in chicken (or cornish hens, halved)
  • Saffron bloomed + 2 onions, juiced + ½ cup yogurt + lemon
  • Butter for basting + salt + pepper + tomatoes + chelo to serve

Method

  1. Bloom saffron; mix with onion juice, yogurt, lemon, salt and pepper.
  2. Marinate chicken overnight (minimum 4 hours).
  3. Skewer, spacing pieces for even heat; grill over medium-high coals.
  4. Turn often; baste with melted saffron butter.
  5. Grill tomatoes alongside until blistered.
  6. Serve on chelo with lemon, sumac and onion.

Chef tips

  • Overnight marinade — hours, not minutes, for saffron penetration.
  • Onion juice, not chunks — chunks burn on the grill.
  • Bone-in stays juicy — breast-only dries fast over coals.
  • Baste with saffron butter — it lacquers and perfumes.
