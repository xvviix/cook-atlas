Firfir

Ethiopia

Firfir (fit-fit) is Ethiopia's genius leftover-breakfast: torn injera tossed through sizzling berbere-tomato-onion sauce in niter kibbeh until every tangy shred is coated, sometimes enriched with egg or meat. The injera fries at the edges while staying chewy within — tangy, toasty, savory all at once. It rescues day-old injera (which fries better than fresh) and takes barely 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3–4 sheets day-old injera, torn
  • 1 onion + 2 tomatoes + 2 garlic cloves + 1.5 tbsp berbere
  • Niter kibbeh or oil + salt (+ egg or meat optional)

Method

  1. Soften onion in niter kibbeh until sweet and golden.
  2. Bloom berbere and garlic; add tomatoes and cook to a thick sauce.
  3. Add torn injera; toss to coat every piece.
  4. Fry 3–5 minutes until edges crisp but centers stay chewy.
  5. Serve immediately, optionally topped with fried egg.

Chef tips

  • Day-old injera fries best — fresh injera turns gummy.
  • Add injera last and briefly — long boiling makes dough.
  • Toss, don't mash — distinct shreds are the texture.
  • Egg on top turns it into a full breakfast.
