Macarons

France

Almond-meringue sandwich cookies: feeted, smooth-shelled and chewy-crisp, matured 24 hours with ganache or buttercream. Macaronage (folding to lava-ribbon) and resting to a dry skin decide success.

Ingredients

  • 100 g aged egg whites + 100 g almond flour + 200 g powdered sugar + 50 g granulated sugar
  • Gel color (never liquid); fillings: ganache/buttercream/jam
  • Scale (grams only), piping bag + round tip, silpat/parchment

Method

  1. Pulse and sift almond + powdered sugar 2–3 times; discard grit.
  2. Whip a stable French meringue on medium speed to stiff peaks.
  3. Macaronage: fold until the batter flows like lava and a figure-8 holds 10–15 seconds.
  4. Pipe straight down; bang the tray 4–5 times; pop bubbles with a toothpick.
  5. Rest 30–60 minutes until bone-dry to the touch; bake 140–150 °C (no fan) 12–16 minutes.
  6. Cool fully before peeling; fill; mature 24 hours refrigerated.

Chef tips

  • Weigh everything — cups kill macarons.
  • Vinegar-wipe every tool; a speck of grease collapses meringue.
  • Hollow shells = overmixed batter or underbaking; feetless = unrested skins.
