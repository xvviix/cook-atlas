Khachapuri

Georgia

Acharuli (Adjarian) khachapuri: the cheese boat — yeasted dough rolled oval, edges rolled inward and pinched to points, filled with mozzarella-feta (standing in for sulguni/imeruli), baked blazing-hot, then finished with a raw yolk and butter slab. Tear, stir, scoop. Eat immediately.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 220 ml warm water + 100 g yogurt + yeast + sugar + salt + oil
  • Filling: 420 g low-moisture mozzarella + 220 g feta + 1 egg + butter
  • 2 yolks + 30 g butter to finish

Method

  1. Knead a smooth, tacky dough; rise 60–75 min to doubled.
  2. Mix cheeses with egg and soft butter; chill.
  3. Roll 2 ovals; mound cheese center; roll edges in; pinch boat points.
  4. Bake at 250 °C (steel/stone) 12–15 min to deep gold and bubbling.
  5. Add yolk + butter to each boat; stir tableside; tear and scoop.

Chef tips

  • Blazing heat + steel = crisp bottom; cool ovens turn gummy.
  • Yolk goes in after baking — it cooks in the molten cheese.
  • Serve within minutes — congealed khachapuri is a tragedy.
