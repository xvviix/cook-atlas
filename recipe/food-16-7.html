Bánh xèo

Vietnam

The 'sizzling cake': a turmeric-gold rice-flour crepe (the name mimics its hiss in the pan), fried shatteringly crisp around shrimp, pork, onion and bean sprouts — torn, wrapped in lettuce with herbs, and dunked in nước chấm. Crispness is everything.

Ingredients

  • Batter: rice flour + cornstarch + turmeric + coconut milk + water/beer + scallions; rest 30+ min
  • Filling: shrimp + thin pork belly + onion + mung beans + bean sprouts
  • Plenty of oil; lettuce + mixed herbs + nước chấm

Method

  1. Rest the thin batter 30 minutes–overnight for maximum crisp.
  2. Sear pork, shrimp and onion in a hot oiled skillet.
  3. Ladle batter and swirl thin to lace the edges; scatter sprouts; cover 2–3 minutes.
  4. Uncover; fry to deep gold and crisp; fold in half.
  5. Serve instantly with lettuce, herbs and nước chấm for wrapping.

Chef tips

  • Thin batter + hot pan + enough oil = shatter; thick batter = pancake.
  • Beer or sparkling water in the batter boosts the lace.
  • Serve the second it leaves the pan — steam is crispness's assassin.
