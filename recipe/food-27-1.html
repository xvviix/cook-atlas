Sauerbraten

Germany

Germany's marinated pot roast: beef steeped 3–5 days in red wine-vinegar with pickling spices, juniper and bay — then braised tender and sauced with the strained marinade sweetened and thickened with crushed gingersnaps. Sour, sweet, spiced; served with red cabbage and dumplings.

Ingredients

  • 1.8 kg bottom round
  • Marinade: red wine + red wine vinegar + onion + pickling spice + juniper + bay + clove (3–5 days)
  • Gravy: strained marinade + ¾ cup crushed gingersnaps + sugar; red cabbage + knödel

Method

  1. Boil marinade; cool; submerge beef; refrigerate 3–5 days, turning.
  2. Pat dry; sear hard all over.
  3. Braise in strained marinade 2.5–3 hrs to fork-tender.
  4. Strain liquor; simmer with gingersnaps to a glossy gravy.
  5. Slice against the grain; gravy over; cabbage and dumplings alongside.

Chef tips

  • Full 3–5 day marinade — one day is just seasoned beef.
  • Gingersnaps thicken AND sweeten — add gradually.
  • Slice thin against the grain — round is lean.
