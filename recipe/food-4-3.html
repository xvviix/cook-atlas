Caldo Verde

Portugal

Portugal's national soup from the Minho: a silky potato purée laced with emerald ribbons of couve galega (or kale/collards) and coins of smoky chouriço, finished with a thread of olive oil. Humble, economical, and served everywhere from taverns to weddings.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 large onion + 3–4 garlic cloves
  • 2 l chicken stock or water
  • 400 g couve galega or kale, rolled and cut in hair-thin ribbons
  • 150–250 g chouriço/linguiça, thinly sliced
  • 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil + more to finish
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Sweat onion and garlic in oil; add potatoes and stock; simmer 15–20 minutes until falling apart.
  2. Blend or mash to a smooth, pourable purée; thin with water if needed — it thickens as it stands.
  3. Stir in the chouriço and simmer 5 minutes to perfume the soup.
  4. Add the shredded greens and cook just 3–5 minutes until vivid green and tender — never grey.
  5. Season boldly, ladle into bowls, and finish each with olive oil.

Chef tips

  • Slice the greens as thin as physically possible — thick ribbons turn leathery.
  • Greens go in last and briefly; overcooking kills the emerald color.
  • Serve with broa (cornbread) for the full Minho experience.
