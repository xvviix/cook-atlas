Francesinha

Portugal

Porto's legendary gut-buster (1950s, inspired by the croque-monsieur): steak, ham and sausages stacked between toast, smothered in melted cheese and drowned in a hot beer-tomato-port gravy, crowned with a fried egg and flanked by fries. Each house guards its own sauce recipe.

Ingredients

  • Per sandwich: 2 thick toast slices, 1 small steak, 2 sausages, 2 ham slices
  • 5–6 slices flamengo/Edam-style melting cheese; 1 fried egg
  • Sauce: 2 onions, 4 garlic cloves, butter + oil, bacon/lard
  • 250 ml tomato sauce, 500 ml lager, 150 ml beef stock, 60 ml port + 60 ml white wine/whisky
  • Worcestershire, chili, bay, milk-flour liaison
  • Fries to serve

Method

  1. Start the sauce: sweat onion, garlic, bay and bacon in butter/oil; add tomato and chili, then beers, wines, port and stock.
  2. Simmer 30 minutes, blend smooth, thicken lightly with the milk-flour liaison to a gravy consistency; keep hot.
  3. Grill sausages (split) and season and sear the steak medium-rare; toast the bread.
  4. Stack: toast, cheese, ham, steak, sausages, toast; drape with remaining cheese and melt under the grill/broiler.
  5. Top with the fried egg, ladle over abundant boiling sauce, and serve with fries for mopping.

Chef tips

  • The sauce makes or breaks it — rich, beer-forward, poured lava-hot and without restraint.
  • Assemble fast and serve immediately; a waiting francesinha goes soggy.
  • Lighter version: skip the egg and halve the cheese — Porto will forgive you.
