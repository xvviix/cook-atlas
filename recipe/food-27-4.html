Pretzel

Germany

Laugenbrezel: the mahogany-crusted icon — a lean yeasted dough rolled to ropes (fat belly, thin arms), twisted, chilled, dipped 5–30 seconds in food-grade lye, salted, slashed and baked blazing-hot to a chewy gloss. Lye makes the crust; soda is the timid substitute.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + 7 g yeast + 300 ml cool water + butter + salt + malt/sugar
  • Lye dip: 1 l cold water + 40 g food-grade lye (gloves + glasses!)
  • Coarse pretzel salt

Method

  1. Knead smooth; rise 1 hr; divide; roll ropes thick-center.
  2. Twist the classic knot; chill 30–60 min (cold dough + lye).
  3. Dip 5–30 sec in lye (gloves, ventilation, no aluminum).
  4. Salt + slash the belly deep.
  5. Bake 220–230 °C 12–15 min to deep mahogany.

Chef tips

  • Cold pretzels into lye — warm dough reacts badly.
  • Lye safety: gloves, glasses, cold water, ventilate.
  • Deep belly slash — shallow cuts seal shut.
