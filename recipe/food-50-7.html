Chicken Tikka Masala

England

Britain's adopted national dish — allegedly invented in Glasgow when a chef added tomato soup to dry tikka: charred yogurt-marinated chicken folded into a creamy spiced tomato-fenugreek sauce, served with basmati and naan. The char on the chicken (tandoor-hot grill) and the kasuri methi (dried fenugreek) in the sauce are the two non-negotiables. Curry-house Britain in a bowl.

Ingredients

  • Tikka: 800 g chicken + yogurt + tikka spices + lemon + garlic-ginger
  • Sauce: tomato + onion + cream + butter + garam masala + kasuri methi
  • Basmati + naan + coriander to serve

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in spiced yogurt 4+ hours.
  2. Grill fiercely until charred at the edges; rest.
  3. Soften onion; bloom spices; add tomato; simmer silky.
  4. Blend smooth; add cream, butter and fenugreek.
  5. Fold in chicken; simmer 10 minutes.
  6. Serve with basmati and naan.

Chef tips

  • Char the chicken — uncharred tikka is just curry.
  • Kasuri methi — the curry-house perfume.
  • Marinate long — hours, not minutes.
  • Rest the chicken — juices stay in.
