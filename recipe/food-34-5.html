Malva Pudding

South Africa

Cape Dutch sticky sponge: apricot-jam batter baked golden — drowned hot in buttery cream sauce that soaks clean through. Served warm with custard or ice cream; the sauce-pour while hot is the whole technique.

Ingredients

  • Batter: flour + sugar + egg + apricot jam + milk + vinegar + soda
  • Sauce: cream + butter + sugar + water (+ jam); custard to serve

Method

  1. Cream sugar + egg + jam; add milk-vinegar + flour + soda.
  2. Bake 180 °C 30–40 min to gold.
  3. Simmer sauce ingredients.
  4. Pour hot sauce over hot pudding; soak 10 min.
  5. Serve warm with custard/ice cream.

Chef tips

  • Hot-on-hot pouring — cold sauce sits on top.
  • Don't skimp the sauce — dry malva disappoints.
  • Serve warm — cold malva turns stodgy.
