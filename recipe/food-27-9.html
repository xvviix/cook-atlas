Schweinshaxe

Germany

★ Germany's added 10th dish — Bavaria's beer-hall anchor: pork knuckle simmered with aromatics (or brined), then roasted low and blasted hot — basted with beer — to shattering crackling over jellied, tender meat. Served with dunkel-beer gravy, Knödel and kraut; mustard and a Maß alongside.

Ingredients

  • 2 pork knuckles (~750 g–1.2 kg each), scored
  • Simmer: water/beer + onion + carrot + bay + juniper + caraway + salt
  • Beer for basting + gravy (stock + roux/beer); Knödel + kraut + mustard

Method

  1. Simmer knuckles with aromatics 60–90 min to tender; dry the skin thoroughly (fridge air-dry ideal).
  2. Roast at 160 °C 1 hr on a rack over veg + beer.
  3. Blast at 220–230 °C 20–30 min, basting with beer, to blistered crackling.
  4. Rest 15 min.
  5. Serve with beer gravy, dumplings, kraut and mustard.

Chef tips

  • Dry skin = crackling; wet skin = leather. Air-dry uncovered.
  • Score to (not through) the fat — even blistering.
  • Rest before carving — the juices settle into the meat.
