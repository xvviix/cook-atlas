Prebranac

Serbia

Serbia's baked beans: creamy white beans layered with a mountain of soft onions and sweet paprika, baked until the top dries to a crusty lid. Lenten when made with oil, richer with lard — thick and creamy, never soupy.

Ingredients

  • 500 g large dried white beans (tetovac/cannellini), soaked overnight
  • 4–5 large onions, sliced; 4 tbsp oil or lard
  • 2–3 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika (+ smoked pinch, optional)
  • Garlic + bay + salt + pepper; tomato paste (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the soaked beans (changing water once); simmer until just tender; reserve liquid.
  2. Soften the onions low and slow in fat until sweet and golden — volume is the point.
  3. Stir in garlic and paprika off the fiercest heat (paprika burns bitter).
  4. Layer beans and onions in a baking dish with a little bean liquid.
  5. Bake at 180 °C 30–45 minutes until the top dries and browns; rest before serving.

Chef tips

  • More onion than feels sane — it melts into the sauce.
  • Dry top, creamy middle: pour off excess liquid before baking.
  • Black pepper over the top at the table is traditional.
