Buffalo Wings

United States

Born at Buffalo's Anchor Bar in 1964: naked wings fried crisp, tossed in Frank's RedHot melted with butter, served with celery and blue cheese. No breading, no ranch-in-Buffalo — the vinegar-butter glaze is the whole recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg wings, split into flats and drumettes, patted very dry
  • Sauce: 120 ml Frank's RedHot + 80 g butter + splash of white vinegar + garlic powder + cayenne + Worcestershire
  • Celery sticks + blue cheese dressing

Method

  1. Dry the wings thoroughly (uncovered fridge time helps); season with salt.
  2. Fry at 200 °C 10–12 minutes (or bake 220 °C ~45–60 min on a rack) to crisp.
  3. Simmer the sauce ingredients to a glossy emulsion.
  4. Toss hot wings in warm sauce to coat every crag.
  5. Serve immediately with celery and blue cheese.

Chef tips

  • Dry skin crisps; wet skin steams — pat and air-dry aggressively.
  • Sauce hot wings in warm sauce — cold sauce slides off.
  • Frank's-style cayenne sauce is the flavor; thicker hot sauces unbalance it.
