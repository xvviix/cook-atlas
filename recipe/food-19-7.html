Kimchi Jjigae

Korea

Korea's weeknight stew: well-aged sour kimchi (+ its brine) simmered with pork belly, onion and tofu in anchovy stock, seasoned with gochujang and gochugaru — funky, porky, deeply comforting. Old kimchi is the ingredient; fresh kimchi makes a lesser stew.

Ingredients

  • 400 g aged sour kimchi + 60 ml brine, chopped
  • 250 g pork belly/shoulder
  • Anchovy-kelp stock; onion + garlic + tofu
  • Gochujang + gochugaru + sugar + salt; scallions + sesame oil

Method

  1. Sear the pork; add kimchi and fry 5 minutes until translucent and fragrant.
  2. Add stock, onion and seasonings; simmer 20–30 minutes.
  3. Lay in tofu slabs; simmer 10 minutes more.
  4. Finish with scallions and sesame oil; serve bubbling with rice.

Chef tips

  • Aged sour kimchi only — fresh kimchi lacks the funk.
  • Fry the kimchi first — raw-boiled kimchi tastes flat.
  • The brine is liquid gold — never discard it.
