Sarma

Serbia

Sauerkraut-leaf rolls of beef-pork and rice, studded with smoked ribs or bacon, baked low and slow on a bed of sauerkraut until melting. Serbia's Christmas and slava centerpiece — and unanimously better reheated the next day.

Ingredients

  • 1 head sauerkraut (whole fermented head) + 500 g shredded sauerkraut
  • 500 g beef + 300 g pork, ground
  • 150 g rice; smoked ribs/bacon; onion; paprika, pepper, salt
  • Water/broth to cover; tomato (optional)

Method

  1. Soak sauerkraut leaves to tame the salt; trim thick ribs.
  2. Mix meats with rice, onion and spices to a compact mass.
  3. Roll firm parcels, tucking the ends; layer over shredded sauerkraut with smoked meat.
  4. Cover with water; bake covered 2–3 hours at 160–170 °C.
  5. Rest; serve with mashed potatoes and sour cream. Reheat tomorrow for the true version.

Chef tips

  • Firm rolling survives the hours; loose rolls unravel to soup.
  • Sauerkraut strength varies — soak, taste, then season.
  • Next-day sarma is not leftovers; it's the destination.
