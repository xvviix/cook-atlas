Blini

Russia

Maslenitsa's sun-discs: thin yeasted (or quick) milk-and-egg pancakes poured whisper-thin and fried to lacy gold — stacked under a towel, served with smetana, jam, honey, or caviar and smoked fish for the savory feast. The first blin is always a sacrifice to the pan.

Ingredients

  • 500 ml warm milk + 3 eggs + 200 g flour + sugar + salt + oil/butter
  • (Yeasted version: + yeast, rise 1–1.5 hrs); butter for the pan

Method

  1. Whisk to a thin kefir-like batter; rest 20–30 min.
  2. Butter a hot pan; swirl a thin coat.
  3. Cook 1 min to gold; flip; 30 seconds.
  4. Stack under a towel.
  5. Serve with smetana, jam, honey — or salmon and caviar.

Chef tips

  • Thin batter = tender blini; thick batter = rubber.
  • Rest the batter — hydration prevents tearing.
  • First blin feeds the cook — pans need seasoning.
