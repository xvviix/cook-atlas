Germknödel

Austria

Austria's beloved alpine dessert: a giant steamed yeast dumpling with a molten heart of Powidl (thick plum butter), served drowned in vanilla sauce or brown butter and buried under poppy seeds and icing sugar. Ski-hut famous — one germknödel is practically a meal — it must be eaten the moment it arrives, the steam still rising. The dough should be pillowy and light; a dense germknödel betrays under-proofing or a lid lifted too soon.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 250 ml milk + 1 egg + 60 g butter + 20 g yeast + sugar + salt + lemon zest
  • Filling: 4 tbsp Powidl (plum butter)
  • To serve: vanilla sauce + ground poppy seeds + icing sugar + melted butter

Method

  1. Knead a soft enriched dough; proof 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Divide into balls; flatten, fill with Powidl and seal tightly.
  3. Proof the filled dumplings 20–30 minutes until puffy.
  4. Steam over simmering water 12–15 minutes with the lid firmly on.
  5. Serve instantly with vanilla sauce, poppy seeds, icing sugar and butter.

Chef tips

  • Seal the filling completely — leaking Powidl stains and deflates.
  • Never lift the lid while steaming — the dumplings collapse.
  • Full second proof — under-proofed dumplings turn dense.
  • Serve immediately — steamed dough firms as it stands.
