Locro

Argentina

Argentina's May-Revolution stew: white hominy corn and beans simmered hours with beef, pork belly, chorizo and pancetta — pumpkin melting in to thicken — finished tableside with quiquirimichi (paprika-chile oil with scallion). Thick enough to stand a spoon; patriotic to the last ladle.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dried hominy + 250 g white beans, soaked overnight
  • 500 g beef + 300 g pork belly + 200 g chorizo + pancetta
  • Onion + garlic + paprika + cumin + bay; 300 g pumpkin; stock
  • Quiquirimichi: oil + paprika + chile + scallion

Method

  1. Soak corn and beans overnight separately.
  2. Brown meats; soften onion; bloom paprika-cumin.
  3. Add corn, beans, stock; simmer 2 hrs, stirring, topping water.
  4. Add pumpkin; cook 45–60 min to dissolved and thick.
  5. Serve with quiquirimichi at the table.

Chef tips

  • Stir and scrape — hominy sinks and scorches.
  • Pumpkin late — early pumpkin vanishes entirely.
  • Quiquirimichi at the table — heat to taste.
