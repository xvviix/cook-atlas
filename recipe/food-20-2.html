Kibbeh

Lebanon

Kibbeh krass (fried torpedoes): a shell of lean meat kneaded with fine bulgur, onion and allspice, hollowed thin with a wet finger, stuffed with spiced meat, onion and pine nuts, pinched into footballs and fried golden. Lebanon's national dish in its crispest form.

Ingredients

  • Shell: 500 g lean beef/lamb + 200 g fine bulgur (soaked, squeezed) + grated onion + allspice + salt
  • Filling: 250 g meat + onion + 50 g pine nuts + allspice + cinnamon + pepper
  • Oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Soak bulgur 20–30 min; squeeze bone-dry.
  2. Process meat with onion and spices to a smooth paste; knead in bulgur.
  3. Cook the filling with pine nuts; cool completely.
  4. Hollow golf-ball shells thin; fill; seal and shape into torpedoes.
  5. Fry at 175 °C ~5 min to deep gold; drain; serve with yogurt and salad.

Chef tips

  • Wet hands constantly — the shell sticks otherwise.
  • Thin shells, cool filling — thick shells stay raw inside.
  • Chill shaped kibbeh 30 min so they hold in the oil.
