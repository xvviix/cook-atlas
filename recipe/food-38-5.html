Hamam Mahshi

Egypt

Stuffed pigeon is Egypt's delicacy of celebration: whole squabs filled with cinnamon-and-pepper spiced rice or freekeh, simmered gently in aromatic broth, then bronzed in samneh or roasted until the skin crisps and glistens. Nile Delta villages raise pigeons in dovecotes (borg) precisely for this dish, served at weddings and feasts with the cooking broth as soup. Delicate work — the skin tears easily — but the reward is meat of extraordinary tenderness wrapped around fragrant, meat-enriched rice.

Ingredients

  • 4 squabs/pigeons, cleaned + 1.5 cups rice or freekeh
  • Stuffing: onion + cinnamon + allspice + pepper + nuts + ghee
  • Broth: water + onion + cardamom + bay + mastic; ghee for bronzing

Method

  1. Clean squabs meticulously; rinse with salt, flour and lemon; pat dry.
  2. Parcook the rice with spiced onion in ghee until half-done and fragrant.
  3. Stuff loosely (rice swells!); truss or sew the openings closed.
  4. Simmer gently in aromatic broth 30–40 minutes — never a hard boil.
  5. Lift out carefully; bronze all over in foaming samneh or roast hot.
  6. Serve with the strained broth as soup, flatbread and salad.

Chef tips

  • Stuff loosely — swollen rice bursts tight birds.
  • Gentle simmer only — boiling shreds the delicate skin.
  • Don't overcook in broth — it finishes in the hot fat.
  • Sew or truss securely — escaping stuffing clouds the broth.
