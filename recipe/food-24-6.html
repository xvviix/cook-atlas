Khao Pad

Thailand

Thai fried rice: day-old jasmine tossed in a screaming wok with garlic, egg, protein and onion — seasoned simply with fish sauce, soy and sugar, finished with white pepper, scallion, tomato, lime and prik nam pla. Dry grains, smoky breath, zero mush.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups day-old jasmine rice, cold
  • 2 eggs + 200 g chicken/shrimp + onion + tomato + scallion
  • Fish sauce + soy + sugar + white pepper; lime + cucumber + prik nam pla

Method

  1. Cold rice, clumps broken; everything prepped (wok moves fast).
  2. Garlic + onion fragrant 1 min; protein seared; push aside.
  3. Scramble eggs in the wok; add rice; toss 3–4 min to separate and toast.
  4. Season fish sauce-soy-sugar around the hot wok edge.
  5. Tomato + scallion + pepper; serve with lime, cucumber, prik nam pla.

Chef tips

  • Day-old cold rice — fresh rice steams to mush.
  • Screaming wok, small batches — the smoky wok hei needs heat.
  • Sauce on the hot wok edge, not the rice — it caramelizes.
