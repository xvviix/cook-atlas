Balandėliai

Lithuania

Lithuania's 'little doves' — cabbage rolls of pork-and-rice (sometimes barley) braised in tomato-sour-cream sauce until silky, served with boiled potatoes. Smaller and more delicate than many neighbors' versions, balandėliai favor a lighter, tangier sauce where sour cream leads and tomato supports. Sear the rolls before braising for color and flavor that simmering alone can't build.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cabbage + 600 g pork mince + 1 cup rice, parboiled
  • Onion + carrot + dill + salt + pepper
  • Sauce: stock + tomato + generous sour cream + bay

Method

  1. Blanch leaves pliable; trim ribs.
  2. Mix mince with rice, sautéed onion-carrot and dill.
  3. Roll small parcels; sear golden on all sides.
  4. Nestle in a pot; pour over tomato-sour-cream sauce.
  5. Braise 45–60 minutes until silky; serve with potatoes and dill.

Chef tips

  • Sear before braising — color equals flavor.
  • Sour cream leads the sauce — Lithuanian, not Italian, balance.
  • Parboil the rice — raw rice bursts small rolls.
  • Small parcels — balandėliai means little doves.
