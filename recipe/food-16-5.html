Cơm tấm

Vietnam

Saigon's broken-rice plate: fractured grains (softer, sauce-hugging) with lemongrass grilled pork chop (sườn), steamed egg meatloaf (chả), shredded pork skin (bì), pickles, scallion oil and a flood of sweet nước chấm. Street luxury on one plate.

Ingredients

  • Broken rice (tấm), steamed soft
  • Sườn: pork chops + fish sauce + oyster + honey + lemongrass + garlic, grilled
  • Chả: pork mince + glass noodles + wood ear + eggs, steamed, yolk-glazed top
  • Bì + đồ chua pickles + scallion oil + nước chấm; tomato + cucumber

Method

  1. Marinate chops 3+ hours; grill to charred and juicy; slice.
  2. Mix and steam the chả 25 minutes; glaze with yolk; steam 5 more; cool and slice.
  3. Steam broken rice soft; fry scallion oil; mix nước chấm.
  4. Plate rice + chop + chả slice + bì + pickles + cucumber.
  5. Spoon scallion oil over; flood with nước chấm at the table.

Chef tips

  • Broken rice cooks softer and drinks sauce — long grain can't substitute.
  • Grill the chop last so it lands hot — everything else holds.
  • Nước chấm generously — a dry cơm tấm is a sad one.
