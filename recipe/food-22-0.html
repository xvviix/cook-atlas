Goulash

Hungary

Gulyásleves — Hungary's herdsman's soup (a soup, not a stew!): beef braised with a mountain of onions, sweet paprika bloomed off-heat in lard, caraway, peppers and tomato, then simmered with carrots and potatoes into a brick-red, brothy bowl. Csipetke pinched noodles are the classic finish.

Ingredients

  • 900 g beef chuck, 2 cm cubes
  • 700 g onions, chopped + lard + garlic
  • 60 ml sweet Hungarian paprika + caraway + bay
  • Peppers + tomatoes + carrots + potatoes + 1.5 l beef broth

Method

  1. Soften onions golden in lard (15+ min); garlic briefly.
  2. Off-heat: stir in paprika; add beef + splash of water; braise 5 min.
  3. Add peppers, tomato, caraway, bay, broth; simmer 1 hr.
  4. Add carrots, then potatoes; simmer 30–40 min to tender.
  5. Season; serve with bread and csipetke (pinched noodles).

Chef tips

  • Paprika off-heat — scorched paprika turns bitter and brown.
  • Onions nearly equal the beef — they melt into the body.
  • Real gulyás is brothy; thick 'goulash' is pörkölt territory.
