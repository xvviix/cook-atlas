Tempura

Japan

Lacy, shattering fritters: shrimp and vegetables in an ice-cold, barely-mixed batter, fried at 170–180 °C and served instantly with tentsuyu dip. Cold batter + hot oil + minimal gluten is the entire science.

Ingredients

  • Batter: ¾ cup cake flour + ¼ cup cornstarch (+ 1 tsp baking powder, optional)
  • 250 ml ice-cold water or sparkling water + 1 egg (optional)
  • Shrimp (straightened) + vegetables in 3–5 mm slices
  • Flour for dredging; neutral oil for deep-frying
  • Tentsuyu: dashi + soy + mirin; grated daikon + ginger

Method

  1. Chill everything; set the batter bowl over ice. Prep and thoroughly dry all ingredients.
  2. Whisk dry ingredients; add ice water and stir with chopsticks 5–6 strokes — lumpy is correct.
  3. Heat oil to 170–180 °C; dredge each piece in flour, dip in batter, lay away from you.
  4. Fry small batches 2–3 minutes until pale gold and quiet; drizzle extra batter for 'hana' crunch.
  5. Skim the oil between batches; drain vertically on a rack; serve within minutes with tentsuyu.

Chef tips

  • Overmixed batter = chewy pancakes; lumps = crisp lace.
  • Dry ingredients and steady oil temperature beat any recipe tweak.
  • Tempura steams itself soggy if stacked — single layer on a rack.
