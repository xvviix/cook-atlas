Pastel de Choclo

Chile

Chile's summer corn pie: pino (beef, onion, olive, raisin, egg) plus chicken buried under a thick blanket of fresh-ground sweet corn with basil and butter — baked in clay bowls until the top caramelizes, dusted with sugar. Sweet corn over savory meat: summer in a paila.

Ingredients

  • Pino: 450 g beef + onion + paprika + cumin + olives + raisins + eggs
  • Chicken pieces, poached
  • Topping: 1 kg fresh corn ground + basil + butter + milk + sugar

Method

  1. Make the pino; cool.
  2. Poach chicken; portion.
  3. Grind corn with basil; cook with butter + milk to a thick cream.
  4. Layer pino + chicken in clay bowls; cap with corn.
  5. Sugar over; bake 200 °C 30–40 min to caramelized.

Chef tips

  • Fresh corn grinds sweetest — frozen works, canned doesn't.
  • Thick corn cap — thin layers leak and burn.
  • Sugar the top — the caramel crust is the signature.
