Samboosa

Saudi Arabia

Saudi samboosa — Ramadan's essential crunch: triangular pastries of thin samboosa leaves wrapped around spiced minced meat (or cheese, or potato), sealed with flour paste and deep-fried to blistered gold. Iftar tables groan with them; home freezers stock them by the hundred through Ramadan. The seal must be complete and the oil hot — leaking samboosas burst and grease-log.

Ingredients

  • Samboosa leaves (thin pastry strips) + flour-water paste
  • Filling: 400 g mince + onion + parsley + cumin + black lime + chili
  • Oil at 175–180 °C

Method

  1. Brown mince with onion and spices until dry; cool completely.
  2. Fill each strip; fold tight triangles; seal with paste.
  3. Rest 15 minutes so seals set.
  4. Fry at 175–180 °C until blistered and gold.
  5. Drain; serve hot with chutney or daqqus.

Chef tips

  • Dry, cool filling — wet filling bursts the pastry.
  • Seal completely — gaps leak and explode.
  • Hot oil — cool oil makes greasy samboosa.
  • Freeze raw, fry from frozen — the Ramadan system.
