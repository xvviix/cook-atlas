Kanafeh

Palestine

Kanafeh in its classic shredded-pastry (khishneh) form: crisp buttered kataifi layered over molten sweet cheese, baked until the base shatters golden, flipped and drowned in orange-blossom or rose syrup (ater), then crowned with crushed pistachios. Served hot so the cheese stretches in long pulls, it is the Levant's most beloved dessert — celebration, comfort and theater in one pan. (See also food-37-7 for the Nablus na'ameh style with Nabulsi cheese.)

Ingredients

  • 500 g kataifi, shredded + 150 g ghee/butter
  • 500 g sweet cheese (akkawi desalted, or mozzarella + ricotta)
  • Syrup: 2 cups sugar + 1.5 cups water + lemon + orange blossom; pistachios

Method

  1. Desalt akkawi if using (soak, changing water); shred all cheeses and pat very dry.
  2. Work ghee through the kataifi until every strand glistens.
  3. Press three-quarters firmly into a buttered pan; spread cheese edge to edge.
  4. Cap with remaining pastry; bake at 200 °C for 20–30 minutes until deep gold.
  5. Flip onto a platter; flood with warm syrup; scatter pistachios; serve instantly.

Chef tips

  • Dry cheese is critical — wet cheese steams the pastry soggy.
  • Press the base firmly — a loose base crumbles on the flip.
  • Syrup warm, kanafeh hot — then serve within minutes for the cheese pull.
  • Salt-test your cheese — even slightly salty cheese ruins the dessert.
