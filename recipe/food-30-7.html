Sfenj

Morocco

Morocco's chewy ring doughnuts: an eggless, sticky, long-kneaded dough — risen twice, shaped by oiled fingers into rings, fried to blistered gold. Eaten hot dusted with sugar (or plain with mint tea) at breakfast from the neighborhood sfenj-maker.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + 2 tsp yeast + 1.5 cups warm water + salt + sugar
  • Oil for deep-frying at 180 °C; sugar for dusting

Method

  1. Knead a soft sticky dough 10–15 min to elastic.
  2. Rise 1–2 hrs to doubled and bubbly.
  3. Oil your hands; pull balls into rings.
  4. Fry at 180 °C, turning, to puffed gold.
  5. Drain briefly; dust sugar; eat hot.

Chef tips

  • Sticky dough is correct — floury hands, not more flour.
  • Double rise = airy chew; single rise = dense.
  • Eat within minutes — sfenj stales fast.
