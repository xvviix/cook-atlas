Vėdarai

Lithuania

Vėdarai — potato sausages — are peak Lithuanian potato devotion: raw grated potato mixed with bacon, onion and marjoram, stuffed into pork casings and baked until the skin blisters and the filling sets sliceable. Crisp-skinned, peppery and rich, they're served in coils with sour cream and applesauce. Prick before baking (burst vėdarai are a tragedy) and bake on a bed of onions for the full effect.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg potatoes, grated + 300 g bacon + 2 onions + marjoram + salt + pepper
  • Pork casings, rinsed
  • Onions for the tray + sour cream + applesauce to serve

Method

  1. Rinse casings well; grate potatoes fine; mix with bacon, onion and marjoram.
  2. Stuff loosely — the filling swells — twisting into coils.
  3. Prick all over with a needle.
  4. Bake on onions at 180 °C for 60–75 minutes until blistered and firm.
  5. Rest 10 minutes; slice; serve with sour cream and applesauce.

Chef tips

  • Stuff loosely — tight vėdarai burst.
  • Prick thoroughly — steam needs escape routes.
  • Bake until firm — a wobbly coil is raw inside.
  • Applesauce alongside — its sharpness cuts the richness.
