Pide

Türkiye

The boat-shaped flatbread: yeasted ovals with crimped edges, filled with spiced mince (kıymalı), cheese-spinach (or egg-crowned), and baked until the rim puffs golden. Butter-brushed, served blistering.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 375 g flour + 240 ml warm water + 2¼ tsp yeast + salt + oil
  • Mince filling: 400 g beef + onion + pepper + tomato + parsley + spices
  • Cheese filling: kaşar/feta + spinach (alternative)
  • Butter + optional egg per pide

Method

  1. Mix and knead a soft dough; ferment 1.5 hours (or cold-ferment overnight for flavor).
  2. Prepare fillings; keep them moist but never watery.
  3. Roll balls into 25 cm ovals; spread filling leaving a border.
  4. Fold the border over and pinch the ends into the boat shape.
  5. Bake at 220–230 °C 12–15 minutes; brush rims with butter (crack an egg in the last 3 minutes for effect).

Chef tips

  • Pinch the ends firmly or the boat opens mid-bake.
  • Restraint with filling — overloading splits the base.
  • A steel/stone under the tray crisps the bottom properly.
