Bunny Chow

South Africa

Durban's curry in a loaf: hollowed half-loaves of white bread filled to the brim with fiery mutton/chicken/bean curry — eaten with fingers using the scooped bread as scoop. Indian-South African street genius; no cutlery allowed.

Ingredients

  • 2 unsliced white loaves, halved + hollowed
  • Durban curry: 1 kg mutton/chicken + onion + masala + turmeric + cumin + garam masala
  • Tomatoes + potatoes + curry leaves + coriander; carrot salad + sambals

Method

  1. Bloom whole spices; brown onions deep gold.
  2. Add masala + meat; coat; tomatoes in.
  3. Simmer 1+ hr; potatoes to tender; thick gravy.
  4. Ladle into hollowed loaves.
  5. Garnish coriander; serve with scooped bread + sambals.

Chef tips

  • Thick curry — watery curry floods the bread.
  • Hollow leaving thick walls — thin walls collapse.
  • Eat with hands — cutlery misses the point.
