Lechona

Colombia

Tolima's celebration pig: a whole pork stuffed with rice, yellow peas, pork meat and potatoes, sewn shut and slow-roasted 10–12 hours in a brick oven to crisp skin and savory, dripping stuffing. Home version: a skin-wrapped pork roll with the same soul.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole pig (or 2 kg skin-on pork shoulder + skin sheet, home version)
  • Stuffing: rice (half-cooked) + yellow peas (soaked) + diced pork + potato + scallion + garlic + cumin
  • Salt + pepper + achiote; twine for sewing

Method

  1. Marinate the pig/skin with garlic, scallion, cumin and salt 12+ hrs.
  2. Mix half-cooked rice, peas, pork, potato and seasonings.
  3. Stuff tightly; sew shut with twine.
  4. Roast low (150–160 °C) 8–12 hrs (whole) or ~2 hrs covered + crisping (roll).
  5. Rest; carve; serve with arepa and lime.

Chef tips

  • Dry skin = crisp skin — pat thoroughly, salt well.
  • Pack the stuffing tight — air pockets collapse.
  • Rest before carving — juices redistribute into the rice.
