Crni rižot

Croatia

Dalmatia's dramatic black risotto: cuttlefish simmered with onion, garlic, tomato and wine, the rice cooked in its inky stock to a glossy jet-black cream. Briny, silky, unforgettable — and a bib is advisable.

Ingredients

  • 500 g–1 kg cuttlefish/squid + ink (sacs or 2–3 sachets)
  • 320 g arborio/carnaroli rice
  • Onion + garlic + tomato paste + white wine + warm fish stock
  • Olive oil + bay + salt + pepper; lemon + Parmesan to serve

Method

  1. Clean the cuttlefish; reserve ink; cut meat bite-size.
  2. Soften onion in olive oil; add cuttlefish 5 minutes; tomato paste + wine to evaporate.
  3. Add rice; toast; ladle in warm stock gradually, stirring.
  4. Stir in the ink midway for an even glossy black; cook 18–20 min to al dente.
  5. Rest 2 minutes; serve with lemon (Parmesan optional and debated).

Chef tips

  • Ink midway, not at the end — it needs cooking into the rice.
  • Warm stock always — cold stock shocks the starch.
  • Al dente and flowing (all'onda), never stiff.
