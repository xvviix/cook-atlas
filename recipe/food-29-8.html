Nasi Kandar

Malaysia

Penang's curry-flooded rice: steamed rice drowned tableside in a ladle-mix of fish, chicken, beef and prawn curries (kuah campur — 'mixed gravy') — with fried chicken, squid, okra, cabbage and bitter gourd on the side. Eat with your right hand, mixing as you go; Line Clear and Hameediyah are the legends.

Ingredients

  • Steamed rice (ghee optional)
  • 3–4 curries: fish + chicken + beef + prawn/dhal (spiced, thin gravies)
  • Sides: fried chicken + squid + okra + cabbage + bitter gourd + salted egg

Method

  1. Cook the rice fluffy.
  2. Simmer each curry separately — thin, pourable gravies.
  3. Fry chicken crisp; prep veg sides.
  4. Plate rice; ladle kuah campur generously over.
  5. Sides around; serve with teh tarik.

Chef tips

  • Kuah campur is mandatory — plain rice misses the point.
  • Thin gravies — thick curries don't flood properly.
  • Mix with your hand — cutlery is allowed, hands are better.
