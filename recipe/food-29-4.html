Hainanese Chicken Rice

Malaysia

The poached-chicken trinity: silky chicken poached at a trembling sub-simmer and iced for jelly skin — oily fragrant rice cooked in the poaching stock — and three sauces (ginger-scallion oil, chile-garlic, dark soy). Simplicity hiding total precision; the rice is the test.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (~1.7 kg), room temp
  • Poach: water + ginger + scallion + salt (+ pandan)
  • Rice: jasmine + chicken fat + garlic + stock; sauces: ginger-scallion + chile + soy; cucumber + coriander

Method

  1. Poach at 80–90 °C (trembling, never boiling) ~40–50 min to done.
  2. Ice-bath 10 min for tight jelly skin; rub sesame oil; rest.
  3. Fry rice in chicken fat + garlic; cook in poaching stock.
  4. Blend the three sauces.
  5. Chop chicken; plate with rice, cucumber, sauces + a bowl of stock.

Chef tips

  • Never boil — boiling toughens and clouds.
  • Room-temp bird in, ice bath out — the texture ritual.
  • Taste the stock — bland stock, bland rice.
