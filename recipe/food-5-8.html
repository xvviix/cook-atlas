Fabada Asturiana

Spain

Asturias' soul stew: buttery fabes de la Granja beans slow-simmered with the compango (smoked chorizo, morcilla, panceta/lacón) and saffron. Never stirred — the pot is shaken — and always better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fabes de la Granja, soaked 12+ hours
  • 2 Asturian chorizos + 2 morcillas + 150 g panceta + lacón piece
  • 1 onion + 1 garlic clove, whole
  • Pinch of saffron
  • Water to cover generously; salt at the end

Method

  1. Cover the soaked beans with cold water in a wide pot; add the whole compango, onion and garlic — cold start, always.
  2. Bring slowly to the barest simmer; skim diligently.
  3. Simmer 3–4 hours; 'scare' (asustar) twice with a splash of cold water to stop the boil and keep skins intact.
  4. Never put a spoon in — swirl by shaking the pot by its handles; broken beans thicken the broth.
  5. Add saffron late; salt to taste; rest 1+ hours (overnight ideal). Serve beans first, sliced compango after.

Chef tips

  • No stirring, ever — utensils shred the delicate beans.
  • The compango goes in whole and uncut; slice only to serve.
  • Next-day fabada, gently reheated, is the true version.
