Skilandis

Lithuania

Skilandis (kindziukas) is Lithuania's ancient cold-smoked sausage: coarsely ground pork, heavily garlicked and peppered, stuffed into a pig's bladder or large casing, cold-smoked for days and air-dried for weeks into a dense, sliceable, intensely savory salami. A Christmas-table centerpiece with Protected Traditional status in the EU, it's traditionally made after the autumn pig slaughter. Home curing demands curing salt, patience and cool humid air — or simply buy from a Lithuanian butcher.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg coarse pork (shoulder + belly) + curing salt (pink salt #2 for drying)
  • Garlic + cracked pepper + allspice + mustard seed + sugar
  • Large casings/bladder + string for hanging

Method

  1. Grind coarse; mix with salt, cure and spices until tacky.
  2. Stuff firmly into casings, excluding all air.
  3. Cold-smoke (under 25 °C) over alder/oak for several days.
  4. Hang at 12–15 °C, 75% humidity, for 3–6 weeks until 30% weight loss.
  5. Slice thin; serve with rye, pickles and beer.

Chef tips

  • Curing salt #2 is mandatory — dried sausage without it risks botulism.
  • Exclude all air — air pockets spoil from within.
  • Cool humid drying — warm or dry air ruins the cure.
  • When in doubt, buy authentic — curing is a craft.
