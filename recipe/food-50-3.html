Shepherd's Pie

England

True shepherd's pie is lamb (beef makes it cottage pie): minced lamb simmered with onion, carrot, peas, rosemary and Worcestershire in rich gravy, topped with buttery mash roughed up with a fork, baked until the peaks bronze and crisp. Comfort in a dish, best with extra gravy. The mash must be stiff enough to ridge — sloppy mash slides off and steams instead of crisping.

Ingredients

  • 700 g minced lamb + onion + carrot + peas + garlic
  • Rosemary + Worcestershire + tomato purée + lamb/beef stock + flour
  • 1.5 kg potatoes mashed with butter + egg yolk + cheddar (optional)

Method

  1. Brown lamb hard; add vegetables and rosemary.
  2. Stir in flour and purée; add stock; simmer to a thick gravy.
  3. Mash potatoes stiff with butter and yolk; season well.
  4. Spread lamb in a dish; pipe or fork-ridge the mash.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 25–30 minutes until bronzed.
  6. Rest 10 minutes; serve with extra gravy.

Chef tips

  • Lamb = shepherd's; beef = cottage — the law.
  • Stiff mash — sloppy mash won't crisp.
  • Fork ridges — peaks bronze, valleys stay creamy.
  • Thick filling — watery filling floods the mash.
