Varenyky

Ukraine

Ukraine's beloved dumplings: tender unleavened dough (flour, egg, water, sour cream for silkiness) wrapped around potato-cheese, sauerkraut, cherry or blueberry fillings, boiled until they float, then tossed in butter with crispy onions — sweet ones with sour cream and sugar. Shaped as half-moons and sealed tight, they're the centerpiece of Christmas Eve (Sviat Vechir) and Sunday tables alike. The dough must be soft and rested; stiff dough makes chewy varenyky.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 1 egg + 250 ml warm water + 2 tbsp sour cream + salt
  • Fillings: potato + farmer's cheese; or kraut + onion; or cherries + sugar
  • Butter + crispy onions + sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Knead a soft, smooth dough; rest covered 30–60 minutes.
  2. Prepare fillings: buttery mashed potato with cheese; squeezed kraut fried with onion; sugared pitted cherries.
  3. Roll thin (2–3 mm); cut rounds; fill modestly.
  4. Seal tightly, pressing out all air.
  5. Boil in batches in well-salted water until floating plus 2–3 minutes.
  6. Toss in butter with onions (savory) or sour cream and sugar (sweet).

Chef tips

  • Rest the dough — unrested dough springs back and seals poorly.
  • Press out air — trapped air bursts dumplings in the water.
  • Salt the water generously — unsalted water makes bland dough.
  • Don't overcrowd the pot — they stick and cool the boil.
