Fuži

Croatia

Istria's hand-folded quill pasta, served the classic way with truffles: fresh fuži tossed in butter-cream with tartufata or shaved black/white truffle and Grana Padano. The Motovun forest on a plate — simple because the truffle forbids complication.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh fuži (or pljukanci)
  • 80 g butter + 200 ml cream (optional but classic)
  • 80 g tartufata or 20 g fresh truffle + truffle butter
  • Grana Padano/Parmesan + salt + pepper + olive oil

Method

  1. Boil fuži in well-salted water to al dente; save pasta water.
  2. Melt butter low; add cream briefly (or skip for the purist version).
  3. Toss pasta through with a splash of water to gloss.
  4. Fold in tartufata off the fiercest heat (heat kills truffle perfume).
  5. Plate with grated cheese, truffle shavings and an oil drizzle.

Chef tips

  • Truffle goes in last and gently — boiling mutes its aroma.
  • Salt the water boldly; the sauce is barely seasoned.
  • Serve instantly — cream sauces seize as they stand.
