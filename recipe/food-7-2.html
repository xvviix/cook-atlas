Lahmacun

Türkiye

'Turkish pizza' — a cracker-thin flatbread with lamb mince pressed directly into the dough and blasted at fierce heat. Rolled with parsley, onion, sumac and lemon; eaten folded, always with lemon.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 350 g strong flour + 150 ml water + 1 tsp yeast + salt + oil
  • 225 g lean ground lamb
  • 1 onion + 2 tomatoes + 1 red pepper, blitzed and strained
  • Parsley bunch + 1 tbsp tomato paste + lemon juice
  • Cumin, paprika/isot, sumac, salt, pepper
  • To serve: parsley, sumac onions, lemon wedges

Method

  1. Make a soft dough; proof 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Blitz the vegetables, then strain hard — excess liquid is the sogginess culprit.
  3. Knead the lamb with paste, parsley, spices, lemon and the strained veg into a spreadable paste; chill.
  4. Roll dough balls paper-thin; spread the topping edge-to-edge, paper-thin.
  5. Bake at your oven's maximum (250 °C+) on steel/stone 5–7 minutes until crisp; serve with lemon and salad to roll.

Chef tips

  • Strain the vegetable pulp — wet topping steams the base.
  • Topping and base must both be thin; thick lahmacun is just pizza.
  • Maximum heat + preheated steel = the blistered edge.
