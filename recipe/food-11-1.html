Mole Poblano

Mexico

Puebla's legendary sauce of 20+ ingredients: three or more dried chiles, toasted nuts and seeds, stale tortilla and bread, plantain, spices, piloncillo and Mexican chocolate — each element toasted separately, blended silk-smooth, and simmered until the fat rises. Served over turkey or chicken with sesame.

Ingredients

  • 4–7 each dried ancho, mulato, pasilla + 2–3 chipotle
  • 1 onion + 3 tomatoes + garlic, charred
  • ⅔ cup almonds + ¾ cup peanuts + ⅓ cup pepitas + ⅓ cup sesame + raisins
  • Stale tortilla + bolillo slice; fried plantain
  • Cinnamon, clove, anise, peppercorns; piloncillo; 1 tablilla Mexican chocolate; stock

Method

  1. Toast chiles 5–10 seconds per side (never burn — bitterness is forever); soak 20 minutes.
  2. Toast nuts and seeds; char onion, tomato and garlic; fry tortilla, bread and plantain.
  3. Blend everything with stock in batches until completely smooth; strain.
  4. Fry the mole in oil, then simmer 20–30 minutes with chocolate and piloncillo until thick and the fat glistens on top.
  5. Serve over poached turkey/chicken; shower with toasted sesame.

Chef tips

  • Burnt chile = bitter mole with no rescue — toast pale and quick.
  • Blend longer than feels reasonable; silk is the standard.
  • Mole improves for days — make it ahead of the feast.
