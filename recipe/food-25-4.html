Lumpia

Philippines

Lumpiang Shanghai: the party egg roll — pork (and shrimp) with carrot, onion and celery bound with egg in whisper-thin wheat wrappers, rolled tight and deep-fried to blistered gold. Served with sweet-chili sauce or spiced vinegar; gone in minutes at every fiesta.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork mince (+ 250 g minced shrimp optional)
  • Carrot + onion + celery/kintsay + garlic + egg
  • Soy + oyster sauce + salt + pepper; 25–50 lumpia wrappers; oil at 175 °C

Method

  1. Mix filling; fry a tester spoon and adjust seasoning.
  2. Roll 1–2 tbsp per wrapper tight; seal with water/egg.
  3. Keep rolled lumpia covered (wrappers dry fast).
  4. Fry at 175 °C in batches to deep gold; drain upright.
  5. Serve hot with sawsawan (sweet chili or sinamak).

Chef tips

  • Test-fry a spoon of filling — raw tasting is out.
  • Roll tight — air pockets fry greasy and burst.
  • Squeeze carrot dry — wet filling sogs wrappers.
