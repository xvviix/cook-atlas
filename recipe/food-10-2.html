Satay

Indonesia

Sate ayam: turmeric-marinated chicken skewers grilled hot and fast over charcoal, served with a real ground-peanut sauce simmered until thick, glossy and split with oil — plus kecap manis, shallot, cucumber and lontong rice cakes.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken thigh, bite-size pieces
  • Marinade: shallot, garlic, turmeric, coriander, kecap manis, palm sugar, lime, oil
  • Sauce: 200 g roasted peanuts + garlic + sambal + kecap manis + tamarind + water
  • Soaked bamboo skewers; cucumber + shallot + lime to serve

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken 30 minutes to overnight.
  2. Grind peanuts to mostly-smooth with garlic and sambal; simmer with kecap manis, palm sugar, tamarind and water until thick and the oil separates.
  3. Thread 4–5 pieces per skewer, packed snugly.
  4. Grill over high heat 3–4 minutes per side, basting with marinade, to charred and just cooked.
  5. Spoon over peanut sauce, drizzle kecap manis, scatter shallots; serve with cucumber, lime and rice.

Chef tips

  • The sauce splitting (oil rising) is the sign it's done — not a flaw.
  • Thighs stay juicy over fierce heat; breast dries in minutes.
  • Soak the skewers or they become the fuel.
