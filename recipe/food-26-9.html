Alfajores

Argentina

Maicena alfajores: melt-away cornstarch shortbread rounds sandwiched with dulce de leche and rolled in coconut. Pale-baked (never browned), chilled to set, and eaten with mate — crumbly, creamy, dangerously stackable.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour + 300 g cornstarch + leaveners
  • 200 g butter + 150 g sugar + 3 yolks + vanilla + lemon zest
  • 350 g dulce de leche (chilled firm) + coconut for rolling

Method

  1. Cream butter-sugar; yolks + vanilla in.
  2. Fold in sifted drys; chill dough 1 hr.
  3. Roll 4–5 mm; cut rounds; chill 15 min; bake 175 °C 10–12 min pale.
  4. Cool fully; sandwich with dulce de leche.
  5. Roll edges in coconut; chill 15 min to set.

Chef tips

  • Pale, not brown — color means overbaked and dry.
  • Chill the dough twice — warm dough spreads.
  • Firm chilled dulce de leche — runny filling squirts out.
