Paška janjetina

Croatia

Pag island's EU-protected lamb: milk-fed lambs grazing salt-blown sage and immortelle pastures, producing pale, tender, subtly herbal meat. Cooked with radical simplicity — spit-roast, peka, or grill with Pag salt and olive oil. The rule: do as little as possible.

Ingredients

  • 1 milk-fed lamb (or shoulder/leg), at room temperature
  • Paška sol (Pag sea salt) + Lun olive oil (+ rosemary, optional)
  • Sides: potatoes, grilled veg, polenta with Paški sir, red onion

Method

  1. Spit: salt all over; turn slowly over embers to crisp gold and juicy (~2–3 hrs).
  2. Or peka: chunks with potatoes under the bell in embers ~1.5–2 hrs.
  3. Or grill (gradele): salted pieces over open fire, turned often.
  4. Rest the meat before carving.
  5. Serve hot with salt, olive oil, onions and wine.

Chef tips

  • Youth + pasture do the work — heavy marinades would bury it.
  • Salt is nearly the only seasoning — trust the meat.
  • Rest before carving or the juices escape.
