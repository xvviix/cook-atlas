Chakapuli

Georgia

Georgia's spring-Easter stew: lamb layered with absurd quantities of tarragon, cilantro, parsley, scallion and sour tkemali plums, braised in dry white wine until the meat surrenders. Green, tangy, winey — the taste of Georgian Easter.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg lamb (thigh/rib), bone-in chunks
  • 2 bunches tarragon + parsley + cilantro + scallions, rough-chopped
  • 150 g sour tkemali plums (or tomatillos/rhubarb + lemon) + 350 ml dry white wine + garlic + chile

Method

  1. Layer lamb, herbs, garlic and plums in a heavy pot; salt well.
  2. Pour over wine and a splash of water; cover.
  3. Braise low 45–60 min to fall-apart.
  4. Shower in the last third of fresh herbs; rest 5 min off-heat.
  5. Serve with bread to mop the green-gold liquor.

Chef tips

  • Tarragon by the bunch, not the sprig — it defines the dish.
  • Sour plums + wine = the tang; sweet plums miss entirely.
  • Final fresh herbs go in off-heat to stay vivid.
