Arroz con Camarones

Ecuador

Ecuadorian shrimp rice: achiote-gold rice tossed with plump shrimp, peppers, onion, tomato, peas and cilantro — coastal, colorful, one-pan comfort, often with a squeeze of lime and ají. The shrimp cook separately and join at the end so they stay snappy; the rice drinks a light shrimp stock for depth. Festive enough for Sunday, easy enough for Tuesday.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups rice + achiote + shrimp stock
  • 1 kg shrimp, peeled + peppers + onion + tomato + peas
  • Cilantro + lime + ají to serve

Method

  1. Simmer shrimp shells into a quick stock; strain.
  2. Cook rice in the stock with achiote until fluffy.
  3. Sear shrimp fast (2 minutes); set aside.
  4. Soften vegetables; fold through rice with peas.
  5. Fold in shrimp; steam 5 minutes; shower cilantro; serve with lime.

Chef tips

  • Shrimp last — overcooked shrimp are erasers.
  • Shell stock — free depth, don't skip.
  • Achiote gold — the coastal color.
  • Fluffy rice first — gummy rice ruins it.
