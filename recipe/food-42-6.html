Zereshk Polo

Iran

Zereshk polo is Persia's ruby-and-gold celebration rice: fluffy saffron chelo jeweled with barberries (zereshk) sautéed in butter with sugar to tame their electric sourness, served over saffron chicken. The barberries must glisten — bloomed in butter, glossed with sugar, scattered at the last moment so they stain the rice in jewel-toned streaks. Served at weddings and parties, it is as beautiful as it is delicious.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups basmati + saffron
  • 1 cup barberries, picked over and rinsed + 3 tbsp butter + 2 tbsp sugar
  • Saffron chicken (thighs braised with onion, saffron, lemon) + slivered pistachios

Method

  1. Braise saffron chicken until tender; keep warm.
  2. Cook fluffy chelo; crown with saffron rice.
  3. Rinse barberries; sauté 1–2 minutes in foaming butter with sugar.
  4. Toss some barberries through saffron rice for the jewel topping.
  5. Mound chelo; cascade the jeweled rice over; arrange chicken alongside.

Chef tips

  • Sugar-tame the barberries — raw zereshk is mouth-puckering.
  • Sauté briefly — scorched barberries turn bitter and black.
  • Rinse well — grit hides in the tiny berries.
  • Scatter at serving — early mixing bleeds the color.
