Bún chả

Vietnam

Hanoi's lunchtime legend (of Obama-Bourdain fame): caramel-glazed grilled pork patties and belly swimming in warm sweet-sour nước chấm with green papaya, served with vermicelli, lettuce and a mountain of herbs for wrapping and dunking. Lunch only, eaten at tiny plastic stools.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fatty ground pork + 300 g pork belly slices
  • Marinade: fish sauce + sugar + caramel sauce + shallot + garlic + lemongrass + pepper
  • Nước chấm: lime + sugar + water + fish sauce + garlic + chile + papaya/carrot
  • Vermicelli + lettuce + mint + cilantro + Thai basil + perilla

Method

  1. Make a dark caramel; mix into the pork marinade; rest 1–2 hours.
  2. Form small flat patties; grill patties and belly over charcoal to charred and juicy.
  3. Mix warm nước chấm; float the hot pork in it with papaya.
  4. Serve with noodles, lettuce and herbs alongside — never mixed in.
  5. Eat by dunking noodles and pork together, wrapping herbs in lettuce.

Chef tips

  • Charcoal char is the flavor — griddle second-best, oven last.
  • The dipping broth is served warm — cold mutes everything.
  • Fatty pork only — lean patties turn to corks.
