Fritule

Croatia

Dalmatia's festive doughnut bites: a yogurt-egg batter perfumed with lemon, orange and rakija, studded with rum-soaked raisins, dropped by spoonfuls into hot oil and dusted with powdered sugar. Christmas, carnival and feasts smell like these.

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour + 1–2 eggs + 150 ml yogurt
  • Sugar + baking powder + salt + vanilla
  • Lemon + orange zest + 1–2 tbsp rakija/rum; 50 g raisins
  • Oil at 170–175 °C; powdered sugar

Method

  1. Soak raisins in rakija; whisk wet ingredients with zests.
  2. Fold into dry to a thick sticky batter; rest 15–30 minutes.
  3. Fry spoonfuls at 170–175 °C, turning, to puffed deep gold.
  4. Drain; dust heavily with powdered sugar while hot.
  5. Serve warm — fritule stale within hours.

Chef tips

  • Rakija flavors and lightens — rum substitutes, omission weakens.
  • Steady oil temperature; test-fry one first.
  • Small spoonfuls cook through; golf balls stay raw inside.
