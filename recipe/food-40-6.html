Montreal-Style Bagels

Canada

Denser, sweeter, smaller-holed than New York's — and many say better: Montreal bagels are hand-rolled, poached in honey-sweetened water, crusted thickly with sesame and baked in a wood-fired oven. No salt in the dough (honey instead), malt-free, eaten warm from the bag at St-Viateur or Fairmount, often with cream cheese. The honey boil gives the signature chew and faint sweetness.

Ingredients

  • Dough: bread flour + yeast + honey + oil + egg (no salt!) + warm water
  • Poach: water + generous honey
  • Sesame seeds (poppy is heresy in Montreal)

Method

  1. Knead a stiff, smooth dough 10 minutes; rise 1 hour.
  2. Roll ropes; wrap around your hand and seal into rings.
  3. Proof 20–30 minutes until puffy.
  4. Poach 1–2 minutes per side in simmering honey water.
  5. Dredge in sesame; bake at 230 °C for 15–20 minutes until bronzed.

Chef tips

  • Stiff dough, tight roll — slack dough makes bready, not chewy, bagels.
  • Honey in the boil, not the oven — it sets the crust's sweetness.
  • Don't over-proof — puffy, not doubled, before poaching.
  • Eat the same day — Montreal bagels stale fast and proudly.
