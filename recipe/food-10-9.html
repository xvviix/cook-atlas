Ayam Goreng

Indonesia

Listed here as ayam goreng ungkep: chicken braised (ungkep) in spiced coconut water until the liquid vanishes and the spices lock in — then deep-fried to golden, shattering crisp. Flavor inside, crunch outside.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg chicken, cut in 8–10 pieces
  • Spice paste: shallots, garlic, turmeric, ginger, galangal, coriander
  • Lemongrass + salam + lime leaves; salt + pinch of sugar
  • 720 ml coconut water or water; oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Blitz the paste; fry with the leaves until fragrant and darkened.
  2. Add chicken; turn to coat every piece in the paste.
  3. Pour in coconut water; simmer covered 30–40 minutes until cooked and the liquid nearly vanishes.
  4. Drain and cool the chicken (reserve any paste clinging to it).
  5. Deep-fry at 175 °C 3–4 minutes per side until deep gold and crisp; drain and serve with sambal and rice.

Chef tips

  • The braise is the marinade — frying alone never reaches the bone.
  • Dry the braised chicken before frying or it spatters and steams.
  • Braised chicken keeps days refrigerated — fry to order for the crunch.
