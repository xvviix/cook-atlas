Tabbouleh

Lebanon

Lebanon's parsley salad (not a bulgur salad!): mountains of knife-minced flat-leaf parsley and mint with fine bulgur, tomato, scallion, lemon and olive oil. The herbs are the dish; the grain is a guest. Scooped with romaine leaves.

Ingredients

  • 3 large bunches flat-leaf parsley, hand-minced fine
  • 60 g fine bulgur soaked in lemon juice 15 min + drained
  • 2–3 tomatoes, small dice + 2–3 scallions, sliced
  • Small bunch mint, minced; 80 ml lemon juice + 80 ml olive oil + salt

Method

  1. Soak the bulgur in lemon juice; drain any excess.
  2. Mince all herbs finely by hand — no food processor (it bruises).
  3. Combine herbs, bulgur, tomato and scallion.
  4. Dress with lemon, oil and salt; toss; rest 15 minutes.
  5. Serve chilled with romaine hearts for scooping.

Chef tips

  • Parsley, not bulgur, is the star — 3 bunches minimum.
  • Always hand-chop — machines make green slurry.
  • Dress before serving; it weeps if it sits dressed for hours.
