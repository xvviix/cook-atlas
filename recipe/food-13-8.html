Rogan Josh

India

Kashmir's aromatic lamb braise: bone-in lamb slow-cooked with Kashmiri chile (color, not cruelty), fennel and dry ginger — the valley's signature pair — in a yogurt-ghee sauce. No onion-tomato excess; warmth and perfume over heat.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg bone-in lamb shoulder/leg, large pieces
  • 200 g whisked yogurt + 3 tbsp ghee
  • Kashmiri chile powder + fennel (seed + ground) + dry ginger + coriander + turmeric
  • Whole: black/green cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, cumin, bay; ginger-garlic paste; warm stock

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in ghee in batches; set aside.
  2. Bloom whole spices; add pastes and ground spices with a splash of water 1 minute.
  3. Lower the heat; whisk in yogurt spoon by spoon (hot yogurt splits).
  4. Return lamb with warm stock; braise covered 1–1.5 hours until yielding.
  5. Rest; finish with garam masala and saffron milk (optional); serve with rice or naan.
