Hummus

Lebanon

Hummus bi tahina: Lebanon's silken chickpea dip — tahini whipped first until pale and fluffy, then blended with lemon, garlic and skinless chickpeas, loosened with ice water to a mousse. Served swirled with olive oil, whole chickpeas, paprika and parsley. Smoothness is the entire art.

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooked chickpeas, skins rubbed off (baking-soda boil helps)
  • 60 g good tahini, well stirred
  • 60 ml lemon juice + 1–2 garlic cloves + ½ tsp salt + pinch of cumin
  • 60 ml ice water; olive oil + paprika + parsley to serve

Method

  1. Whip tahini with lemon and a splash of water 1 minute until pale and thick.
  2. Add chickpeas, garlic, salt and cumin; blend 2–3 minutes.
  3. Drizzle in ice water while blending to a silky mousse.
  4. Swirl into a shallow bowl; top with oil, chickpeas, paprika and parsley.
  5. Serve at room temp with warm pita — never fridge-cold.

Chef tips

  • Skins off = silk; skins on = grain. Rub in baking-soda water.
  • Ice water lightens the texture — add gradually.
  • Tahini quality decides everything — use a runny, nutty one.
