Kürtőskalács

Hungary

The chimney cake: sweet yeasted dough cut in ribbons, wrapped around a spit (or foil-wrapped cans/rolling pins at home), buttered, rolled in cinnamon sugar and baked to caramelized, pull-apart spirals — hollow, steamy, and best torn in the street.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 600 g flour + 250 ml warm milk + yeast + 2 eggs + yolks + 80 g butter + sugar + salt
  • Coating: melted butter + cinnamon sugar (+ walnuts optional)
  • Foil-wrapped cans or rolling pins as spits

Method

  1. Knead a soft enriched dough; rise 1 hour.
  2. Roll 5 mm; cut 2 cm ribbons.
  3. Oil the foil spits; wrap ribbons overlapping; roll to seal.
  4. Butter generously; roll in cinnamon sugar.
  5. Bake 180 °C 20–25 min, turning once, to caramel-gold; slide off warm.

Chef tips

  • Overlap the ribbons or they gap while baking.
  • Butter before sugar — dry dough won't hold coating.
  • Turn once mid-bake for even caramelization.
