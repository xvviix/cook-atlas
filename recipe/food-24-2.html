Green Curry

Thailand

Gaeng keow wan: green curry paste fried in cracked coconut cream until the oil separates and the perfume turns deep — then chicken, eggplant, bamboo, lime leaves and basil simmered in coconut milk, seasoned with fish sauce and palm sugar. Served over jasmine rice, naturally dairy-free.

Ingredients

  • 3–4 tbsp green curry paste + 400 ml coconut milk/cream + 250 ml stock
  • 500 g chicken thigh + Thai eggplant/bamboo + peppers
  • Fish sauce + palm sugar + lime leaves + Thai basil + lime

Method

  1. Boil down ¾ cup coconut cream; fry paste 3–5 min to oil separation.
  2. Add chicken; coat; 2 min.
  3. Add remaining milk + stock + lime leaves; simmer 10 min.
  4. Add vegetables; simmer to tender-crisp.
  5. Season fish sauce-sugar; basil in; serve over jasmine rice.

Chef tips

  • Fry the paste till oily and dark — raw paste tastes harsh.
  • Coconut cream first, milk after — the crack matters.
  • Basil last, off-heat — it blackens if boiled.
