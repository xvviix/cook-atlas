Prosciutto di San Daniele

Italy

A PDO dry-cured ham made only in San Daniele del Friuli from heavy Italian pigs' hind legs, sea salt, and the local microclimate (Alpine air + Adriatic breeze + Tagliamento river). No additives or preservatives. Recognizable by its guitar shape (with trotter) and sweet, delicate flavor. Minimum curing: 13 months / 400 days.

Ingredients

  • Fresh hind legs of Italian heavy pigs (12.5–17.5 kg with trotter)
  • Sea salt only
  • Time + San Daniele microclimate (natural ventilation)
  • To serve at home: the sliced ham, breadsticks/grissini, melon or figs (optional)

Method

  1. Select and trim heavy legs; weigh and salt for about one day per kilo of weight (traditional rule).
  2. Brush off the salt and press the legs 24–48 hours to give the characteristic guitar shape.
  3. Rest in cool rooms so the salt penetrates evenly to the core.
  4. Wash with warm water, dry, and coat exposed meat with sugna (lard-based paste); hang for curing with fully natural ventilation.
  5. Cure at least until the 13th month (400+ days), when aroma and sweetness develop; final quality checks, then Consortium branding.
  6. At home: buy freshly sliced, paper-thin; serve at room temperature (take out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead), never cooked.

Chef tips

  • Only three 'ingredients' exist: pork, sea salt, microclimate — any ham listing nitrates is not San Daniele PDO.
  • The branded guitar-shaped ham with trotter is the authenticity mark.
  • Pair with melon, figs, or plain bread; avoid strong condiments that mask its sweetness.
