Maqluba

Saudi Arabia

Saudi maqluba shares the Levant's upside-down drama — spiced rice, chicken or lamb, fried eggplant, cauliflower and potato, flipped tableside — with a Gulf accent: baharat and black lime in the rice, sometimes saffron, served with daqqus and yogurt. The flip is the test; the rest before it is the technique. (See food-37-1 for the Palestinian maqluba.)

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg chicken or lamb + 3 cups basmati
  • Eggplant + cauliflower + potatoes, fried + tomato slices
  • Baharat + turmeric + black lime + stock + nuts + parsley

Method

  1. Simmer meat into a rich stock; reserve.
  2. Fry all vegetables golden; drain.
  3. Line pot with tomato; layer meat, vegetables, spiced rice.
  4. Add stock to just cover rice; simmer covered 25–30 minutes.
  5. Rest 15 minutes; flip confidently onto a platter.
  6. Garnish with nuts and parsley; serve with daqqus.

Chef tips

  • Rest before flipping — the dome needs to set.
  • Fry vegetables golden — pale ones steam to mush.
  • Stock just covering — too much makes porridge.
  • One confident flip — hesitation sticks.
