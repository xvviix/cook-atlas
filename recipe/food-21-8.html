Churchkhela

Georgia

Georgia's 'candle candy' and ancient warrior ration: walnut halves strung on thread, dipped repeatedly in tatara (grape must thickened with flour to pudding), then hung to dry 1–2 weeks to a leathery, chewy, wine-dark sweet. Rtveli harvest season made portable.

Ingredients

  • 200 g walnut halves + sturdy cotton thread + needle
  • 1.5 l dark grape juice/must + 200 g flour + sugar to taste

Method

  1. Toast nuts lightly; string onto 45 cm threads; knot. Hang ready.
  2. Boil most of the juice; whisk flour into cool reserved juice; stream in.
  3. Cook ~25 min, stirring, to thick pudding.
  4. Dip each string; hang over a tray; dry; repeat 2–3 coats.
  5. Air-dry 5–14 days ventilated; wrap in cloth; store dark.

Chef tips

  • Tatara must coat thick — thin juice slides off.
  • Ventilated drying prevents mold — no damp corners.
  • Patience: under-dried centers ferment.
