Chechebsa

Ethiopia

Chechebsa (kita firfir) is Ethiopia's warming breakfast: torn pieces of fresh kita flatbread tossed in sizzling niter kibbeh bloomed with berbere, served with honey and yogurt to balance the heat. Made in about 30 minutes from flour, water and salt — the kita griddled like a thick tortilla, then shredded straight into the spiced butter — it is Oromo comfort food at its simplest and most satisfying.

Ingredients

  • Kita: 2 cups flour + ½ tsp salt + water to a soft dough
  • 3–4 tbsp niter kibbeh + 1–2 tbsp berbere
  • Honey + plain yogurt + (scrambled eggs optional) to serve

Method

  1. Mix flour, salt and water to a soft dough; rest 15 minutes.
  2. Roll thin rounds; griddle dry until golden both sides.
  3. Tear warm kita into bite-size pieces.
  4. Sizzle berbere in melted niter kibbeh until fragrant.
  5. Toss kita through the spiced butter until evenly coated.
  6. Serve immediately with honey, yogurt and eggs if desired.

Chef tips

  • Toss while warm — cold kita drinks butter unevenly.
  • Berbere to taste — start modest; the heat blooms in the butter.
  • Honey + yogurt aren't garnish — they balance the fire.
  • Thin kita tears best — thick kita turns bready.
