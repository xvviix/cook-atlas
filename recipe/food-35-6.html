Moussaka

Bulgaria

Bulgaria's moussaka will surprise anyone expecting the Greek eggplant version: here it is a potato-and-mince bake — cubed potatoes and seasoned pork-beef mince simmered in tomato, finished with a golden yogurt-egg topping (zastroyka) instead of béchamel. Chubritsa (summer savory) is the defining spice, and the whole dish bakes in one dish until the potatoes drink up the juices and the topping sets. Homestyle, economical and deeply comforting, it is a weeknight staple across Bulgaria.

Ingredients

  • 700 g potatoes, small cubes + 500 g pork-beef mince
  • 1 onion + 1 carrot + 100 ml passata + 1 tbsp chubritsa + parsley + salt + pepper
  • Topping: 400 g Greek yogurt + 2 eggs + 1 tbsp flour (+ 50 g yellow cheese optional)

Method

  1. Brown the mince; add onion, carrot, passata and chubritsa; simmer 10 minutes.
  2. Combine with raw potato cubes in a baking dish; pour over warm water to just cover.
  3. Bake at 200 °C for 40 minutes until potatoes are nearly tender.
  4. Whisk yogurt with eggs and flour; pour evenly over the top.
  5. Scatter cheese if using; bake 10–15 minutes more until golden and set.
  6. Rest 10–15 minutes so it slices cleanly; garnish with parsley.

Chef tips

  • Cut potatoes small and even — large chunks stay raw while the topping burns.
  • Rest before slicing — cutting too soon turns it to delicious rubble.
  • Room-temperature egg for the topping blends lump-free into the yogurt.
  • Chubritsa is the authentic note — oregano is only an approximation.
