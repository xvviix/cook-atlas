İskender Kebab

Türkiye

Bursa's 1867 masterpiece, named for İskender Efendi: thin döner lamb laid over buttered pide, drowned in tomato sauce, crowned with sizzling brown butter poured tableside — cool garlic yogurt alongside, never on top.

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb (thin slices or ground-lamb loaf, roasted)
  • 2 pide breads, cubed
  • Tomato sauce: 3 grated tomatoes + 1 tbsp paste + butter + paprika
  • 250 g thick yogurt + 1 garlic clove (room temperature)
  • 80 g butter + paprika/chili for sizzling
  • Grilled green peppers + tomato halves

Method

  1. Marinate/roast the lamb; shave or slice thin.
  2. Toast the pide cubes in butter until crisp-edged but soft inside.
  3. Simmer the tomato sauce to a thick, glossy nappe.
  4. Plate: pide → lamb → tomato sauce; yogurt quenelles on the side.
  5. Foam the butter with paprika until nutty and pour sizzling over at the table; add grilled peppers.

Chef tips

  • Yogurt stays cool and separate — heat would split it and dull the contrast.
  • The tableside butter sizzle is theater and flavor at once.
  • Slice the meat thin; thick chunks unbalance the soft-crisp architecture.
