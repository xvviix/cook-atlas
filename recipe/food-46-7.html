Mhajeb

Algeria

Mhajeb is mahjouba under another name — the same paper-thin semolina griddle parcel (see food-46-3) — here in its heartier stuffed style with spiced kefta-style minced meat folded through the onion-tomato-pepper filling. Doubly savory and satisfying, it's the meal-sized version of the Algiers street classic. As with mahjouba, dough elasticity and a nearly-dry filling decide success.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g fine semolina + salt + water + oil
  • Filling: 300 g minced beef + onions + tomato + peppers + cumin + paprika + harissa
  • Oil for griddling

Method

  1. Knead and rest the dough until very elastic (1+ hour).
  2. Brown mince; add vegetables and spices; cook until thick and nearly dry; cool.
  3. Stretch dough paper-thin; spread filling; fold into squares.
  4. Griddle until blistered and crisp both sides.
  5. Serve immediately with mint tea.

Chef tips

  • Nearly-dry filling — the golden rule of stuffed griddle breads.
  • Elastic dough or nothing — rest it fully.
  • Cool filling before folding — steam tears thin dough.
  • Same family as mahjouba — the names vary by region.
