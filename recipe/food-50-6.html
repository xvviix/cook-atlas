Bangers and Mash

England

Bangers and mash — sausages (proper pork bangers, slowly cooked so they don't burst, hence 'bangers'), buttery mash and rivers of onion gravy. Pub comfort at its simplest: the gravy, built from caramelized onions deglazed with ale or stock, makes or breaks it. Low heat on the sausages; the mash smooth with hot milk and butter.

Ingredients

  • 8 pork sausages + 1.5 kg potatoes + butter + hot milk
  • Onion gravy: 3 onions + flour + ale/stock + Worcestershire + thyme

Method

  1. Cook sausages low and slow, turning, 20–25 minutes.
  2. Caramelize onions deeply; stir in flour.
  3. Deglaze with ale; add stock; simmer to a glossy gravy.
  4. Mash potatoes with butter and hot milk until smooth.
  5. Plate mash, sausages, and floods of gravy.

Chef tips

  • Low and slow sausages — fierce heat bursts them.
  • Deep caramel onions — pale onions make pale gravy.
  • Hot milk in mash — cold milk makes glue.
  • Gravy floods — bangers swim, not perch.
