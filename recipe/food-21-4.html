Kharcho

Georgia

Mingrelia's beef-and-walnut soup: beef simmered to tender, enriched with ground walnuts, tomato, tkemali sour-plum sauce, rice, khmeli suneli and garlic — thick, sour-spiced, and better the next day. Soup so hearty it eats like a stew.

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef chuck/brisket, large pieces + 2 l water
  • 150 g walnuts, ground; 2 onions + tomato paste
  • 100 g tkemali + 100 g rice + khmeli suneli + coriander + fenugreek + garlic + cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer beef 1–1.5 hrs, skimming, to very tender.
  2. Golden the onions; add tomato paste + dry spices; bloom.
  3. Stir walnuts + onion base into the broth.
  4. Add rice, tkemali and garlic; simmer 20–25 min.
  5. Rest 10 min; serve with cilantro — next-day leftovers excel.

Chef tips

  • Ground walnuts thicken and enrich — don't skip or skimp.
  • Tkemali sourness is essential; lemon is a pale substitute.
  • Rest before serving — flavors marry off-heat.
