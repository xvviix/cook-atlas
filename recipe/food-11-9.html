Churros

Mexico

Mexico's beloved street churro — thicker and more cinnamon-forward than its Spanish cousin (see food-5-7): vanilla-scented dough piped through a star tip, fried crisp, rolled in cinnamon sugar and dunked in cajeta (goat's-milk caramel) or chocolate.

Ingredients

  • 250 ml water + 60 ml oil + 50 g sugar + vanilla + cinnamon + salt
  • 250 g flour
  • Oil for frying (175 °C)
  • 1 cup sugar + 1 tbsp cinnamon for rolling
  • Cajeta or dulce de leche to dip

Method

  1. Boil water with oil, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and salt; dump in the flour and beat to a smooth ball.
  2. Pipe 10–15 cm lengths through a large star tip.
  3. Fry at 175 °C 2–3 minutes per side until deep gold; drain briefly.
  4. Roll in cinnamon sugar while screaming hot.
  5. Serve with warm cajeta for dunking.

Chef tips

  • Mexican churros go heavy on cinnamon — don't be shy.
  • Fry temperature discipline: cool oil = greasy churros.
  • Pipe onto parchment and slide in for uniform shapes.
