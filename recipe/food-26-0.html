Asado

Argentina

Argentina's sacred barbecue: beef ribs (tira), flank (vacío), short ribs and more cooked low over wood embers — seasoned with nothing but salt — served with chimichurri and Malbec. The asador tends the fire for hours; guests wait, drink, and never rush the ritual.

Ingredients

  • Mixed cuts: tira de asado + vacío + entraña + matambre (~3 kg)
  • Coarse salt only; wood/charcoal embers
  • Chimichurri: parsley + garlic + oregano + vinegar + oil + chile

Method

  1. Build a wood fire; let it burn to mature embers.
  2. Salt the meat simply; arrange on the parrilla over gentle heat.
  3. Cook low and slow 1.5–3 hrs, turning occasionally — no flare-ups.
  4. Rest 10–15 min; carve against the grain.
  5. Serve with chimichurri, salad and Malbec.

Chef tips

  • Embers, never flames — flame is the enemy of asado.
  • Salt is the only seasoning on the meat — purists insist.
  • Patience is the recipe — rushing makes shoe leather.
