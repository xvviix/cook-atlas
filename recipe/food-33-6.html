Řízek

Czech Republic

Vepřový řízek: the Czech schnitzel — pounded pork in flour-egg-strouhanka (homemade roll breadcrumbs), fried in lard to pale gold, then finished in a low oven. Served with potato salad (Christmas Eve tradition), lemon and parsley; cold leftovers in rohlík are sacred.

Ingredients

  • 4 pork cutlets, pounded 5 mm
  • Flour + 2 eggs (unsalted!) + strouhanka breadcrumbs
  • Lard for frying; lemon + parsley + potato salad

Method

  1. Pound even; salt the meat (not the eggs).
  2. Flour, egg, crumbs — press gently.
  3. Fry in lard 3 min per side to pale gold.
  4. Finish 20 min at 120 °C.
  5. Serve with lemon, parsley, potato salad.

Chef tips

  • Salt the meat, never the eggs — salted eggs turn the crust rubbery.
  • Strouhanka from dried rolls — panko is a different crunch.
  • Lard fries best — oil is second choice.
