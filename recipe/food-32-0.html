Empanadas

Chile

Empanadas de pino — Chile's Fiestas Patrias icon: lard-enriched dough folded over juicy onion-beef pino with olive, raisin and egg quarter, baked to a burnished half-moon. Made a day ahead for deeper flavor; eaten with pebre and red wine in September.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 80 g lard + yolks + milk + salt
  • Pino: 500 g knife-cut beef + 2 onions + cumin + paprika + oregano
  • Olives + raisins + 2 eggs; egg wash; pebre to serve

Method

  1. Cook pino: onion + beef + spices + flour + stock; simmer 30 min; cool (overnight ideal).
  2. Knead dough; rest 30 min; roll 15 cm discs.
  3. Fill: pino + olive + raisins + egg quarter.
  4. Fold; seal; crimp; egg-wash.
  5. Bake 200 °C 20–25 min to deep gold.

Chef tips

  • Cool pino only — warm filling melts the dough.
  • Knife-cut beef — minced turns pasty.
  • Rest the dough — relaxed dough won't shrink.
