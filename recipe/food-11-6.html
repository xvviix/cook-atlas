Pozole

Mexico

Pozole rojo: the celebratory pork-and-hominy soup, its broth stained brick-red with guajillo-ancho chile sauce, finished at the table with shredded cabbage, radish, onion, lime, oregano and tostadas. Party food since pre-Hispanic times.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork shoulder (+ neck bones for body, optional)
  • 1 kg cooked hominy (pozolero corn)
  • Red sauce: 6–8 guajillo + 2 ancho + garlic + onion, blended and strained
  • Bay + cumin + oregano; salt
  • Garnishes: cabbage, radish, onion, lime, oregano, chile de árbol, tostadas

Method

  1. Simmer the pork with onion, garlic and bay 1.5–2 hours until spoon-tender; skim.
  2. Toast, soak and blend the chiles; strain into the pot.
  3. Add hominy; simmer 30 minutes more to marry.
  4. Shred the pork; return to the pot; season boldly.
  5. Serve with the full garnish array — the table finishes the soup.

Chef tips

  • The garnishes aren't optional — crunch and acid balance the rich broth.
  • Bones in the pot give the broth its lip-sticking body.
  • Pozole feeds a crowd and reheats gloriously — make the big pot.
