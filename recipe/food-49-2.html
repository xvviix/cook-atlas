Llapingachos

Ecuador

Llapingachos are Ecuador's cheese-stuffed potato cakes: seasoned mashed potato wrapped around queso fresco, griddled in achiote oil until crisp and gold, served with peanut sauce (salsa de maní), fried egg, chorizo, curtido and avocado — the full almuerzo plate. The peanut sauce makes them Ecuadorian; without it they're just potato cakes. Dry, seasoned mash and a hot griddle give the crust.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg potatoes, mashed dry + achiote oil + salt
  • 300 g queso fresco for stuffing
  • Peanut sauce: peanuts + milk + onion + achiote + cumin; egg + chorizo + curtido to serve

Method

  1. Mash potatoes dry and smooth; season; cool.
  2. Flatten patties; wrap around cheese cubes; seal.
  3. Griddle in achiote oil until crisp and gold both sides.
  4. Blend peanut sauce silky; warm through.
  5. Plate with egg, chorizo, curtido and avocado; spoon over sauce.

Chef tips

  • Dry mash — wet mash won't seal around cheese.
  • Seal completely — leaking cheese burns.
  • Achiote oil — color and flavor in one.
  • Peanut sauce silky — gritty sauce disappoints.
