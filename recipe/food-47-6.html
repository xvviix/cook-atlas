Haring

Netherlands

Hollandse Nieuwe — raw, lightly salt-cured herring (maatjes), gutted and frozen per EU rules, served cold with diced raw onion and pickles, eaten theatrically by the tail or sensibly in a soft roll (broodje haring). The season opens each June with Vlaggetjesdag; the fish must be silky, mild and cold — never fishy. At home, sourcing is the recipe: buy maatjes from a good fishmonger and serve within a day.

Ingredients

  • Fresh maatjes herring fillets from a trusted fishmonger
  • Raw onion, diced + pickles/gherkins + soft white rolls

Method

  1. Buy the freshest maatjes available; keep ice-cold.
  2. Rinse lightly if needed; pat dry.
  3. Dice onion fine; slice pickles.
  4. Serve fillets whole with onion and pickle, or in rolls.
  5. Eat ice-cold, the same day.

Chef tips

  • Fishmonger quality is everything — supermarket tubs differ.
  • Ice-cold serving — warm herring tastes oily-fishy.
  • Same-day eating — raw herring doesn't wait.
  • Onion + pickle mandatory — sweet-sharp balance.
