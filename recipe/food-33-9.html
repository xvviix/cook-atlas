Smažený sýr

Czech Republic

Smažák: the Czech fried-cheese institution — thick Edam/Gouda (or Hermelín) double-breaded and fried to molten-gold, served with tatarská omáčka and fries. Pub vegetarian classic; the double coat is structural, not optional.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices Edam/Gouda (~1 cm)
  • Flour + 2 eggs + breadcrumbs (double coat!)
  • Oil at 180 °C; tatarská: mayo + gherkins + onion + mustard + capers; fries

Method

  1. Chill cheese slices firm.
  2. Flour, egg, crumbs — then egg + crumbs again.
  3. Chill breaded 20 min.
  4. Fry at 180 °C ~1 min per side to gold.
  5. Serve instantly with tartar + fries.

Chef tips

  • Double-coat or leak — single coats burst.
  • Hot oil, fast fry — slow frying melts through.
  • Serve instantly — smažák waits for no one.
