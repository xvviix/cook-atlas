Halo-Halo

Philippines

'Mix-mix': the Philippines' shaved-ice sundae — sweetened beans, garbanzos, kaong, nata de coco, jackfruit, saba and gulaman layered under a mountain of shaved ice, drowned in evaporated milk, crowned with leche flan, ube halaya/ice cream and pinipig. Mix violently; that's the name and the instruction.

Ingredients

  • Shaved/crushed ice + evaporated milk
  • Jarred/canned: red munggo + garbanzos + kaong + nata de coco + langka + macapuno
  • Homemade: minatamis na saba + kamote; leche flan + ube + pinipig/cornflakes

Method

  1. Prep toppings: sweeten saba and kamote; cube gulaman.
  2. Layer sweets in tall glasses.
  3. Pack shaved ice high over.
  4. Pour over evaporated milk generously.
  5. Crown with flan, ube, pinipig; serve with long spoons — halo (mix)!

Chef tips

  • Shaved ice, not cubes — texture is everything.
  • Milk generously — dry halo-halo is just cold beans.
  • Mix before eating — layered bites miss the point.
