Quiche Lorraine

France

The queen of quiches from Lorraine: smoky bacon in a silky egg-cream custard (purists: no cheese; modernists: Gruyère) inside a crisp blind-baked shell. Baked to a trembling jiggle, served warm.

Ingredients

  • 1 pâte brisée shell, blind-baked
  • 200 g bacon lardons + 1 onion, softened
  • 3 eggs + 300 ml cream (+ 100 ml milk for lightness)
  • 80 g Gruyère, grated (optional-but-loved); nutmeg, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Blind-bake the shell with weights 15–20 minutes until set and dry.
  2. Crisp the bacon; soften the onion; cool slightly (hot filling scrambles custard).
  3. Whisk eggs, cream, nutmeg and seasoning until frothy.
  4. Scatter bacon, onion and cheese in the shell; pour over custard.
  5. Bake at 160–175 °C 30–40 minutes until edges set with a gentle center wobble; rest 15 minutes.

Chef tips

  • The jiggle is the target — fully firm means overbaked and grainy.
  • Cool the filling before the custard meets it.
  • Bake on a tray — custard leaks find every oven seam.
