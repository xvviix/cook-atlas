Beef Wellington

England

The showpiece: beef fillet seared and mustard-brushed, wrapped in mushroom duxelles (cooked bone-dry) and Parma ham, encased in puff pastry and baked until gold with a rosy medium-rare heart — Ramsay's restaurant signature, sold by the million. Soggy bottoms are the enemy: dry duxelles, ham barrier, chilled wrapping, hot oven, and resting before slicing. A thermometer is mandatory, not optional.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef fillet, even center-cut + Dijon mustard
  • Duxelles: 500 g mushrooms + shallots + thyme, cooked dry
  • Parma ham + 500 g puff pastry + egg wash

Method

  1. Sear fillet hard all over; brush with mustard; chill.
  2. Cook duxelles until the pan is dry; cool completely.
  3. Wrap: ham, duxelles, beef — roll tight in cling film; chill 30+ minutes.
  4. Wrap in pastry; seal; egg-wash; chill again.
  5. Bake at 200–210 °C to 52–54 °C internal (medium-rare).
  6. Rest 15–20 minutes; slice with a sharp knife.

Chef tips

  • Duxelles bone-dry — wet mushrooms = soggy pastry.
  • Chill every stage — cold layers bake crisp.
  • Thermometer mandatory — guessing ruins fillet.
  • Rest before slicing — juices redistribute.
