Maaqouda

Algeria

Maaqouda are Algeria's golden potato fritters — mashed potato with parsley, garlic, cumin and turmeric, sometimes studded with tuna or cheese, shaped into patties, dipped in flour-egg and fried crisp. Ramadan-table essential and sandwich filling (maaqouda baguette with harissa), they're creamy within and shattering without. Dry mash is the secret: wet potatoes need flour, and flour makes them bready instead of creamy.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes + parsley + garlic + cumin + turmeric + salt
  • Egg + flour for coating (some versions add tuna/cheese)
  • Oil at 175–180 °C + lemon + harissa to serve

Method

  1. Boil potatoes whole in skins; peel and mash while hot; cool and dry out.
  2. Mix with herbs, garlic and spices; chill 30 minutes.
  3. Shape firm patties; coat in flour, then beaten egg.
  4. Fry at 175–180 °C until deep gold both sides.
  5. Drain; serve hot with lemon and harissa, or in baguettes.

Chef tips

  • Dry mash, minimal flour — wet mix makes bready fritters.
  • Chill before frying — cold patties hold together.
  • 175–180 °C oil — cool oil greases, hot oil burns raw centers.
  • Flour-then-egg coating — the armor that crisps.
