Sarmale

Romania

Romania's Christmas rolls: pork-and-rice with onion, paprika and thyme wrapped in sour fermented cabbage leaves — nestled on chopped sauerkraut with smoked pork, simmered 2–3 hours in tomato-tinged broth. Served with mămăligă, smântână and hot peppers; small rolls are the pride.

Ingredients

  • 30 sour cabbage leaves (murături) + chopped kraut for bedding
  • 750 g pork (+ beef) + 100 g rice + onion + paprika + thyme + dill
  • Smoked ribs/bacon + tomato + broth; smântână + mămăligă + chiles

Method

  1. Rinse fierce kraut leaves; trim thick ribs.
  2. Mix meat + rice + onion + spices.
  3. Roll small and tight, tucking ends.
  4. Bed of kraut + smoked meat; rolls snug; tomato + broth to cover.
  5. Simmer 2–3 hrs low; serve with polenta + cream + chiles.

Chef tips

  • Small tight rolls — big loose ones burst.
  • Low and long — rushing leaves raw rice.
  • Rinse sour leaves or the tang overwhelms.
