Baklava

Greece

The Greek festival pastry: some forty buttered filo sheets layered with cinnamon walnuts, baked crisp, then drenched in honey-lemon syrup. The iron rule — hot pastry meets cool syrup (or vice versa) — keeps every layer shattering-crisp.

Ingredients

  • 450 g filo pastry, thawed
  • 200 g melted butter (or butter + oil)
  • 450 g walnuts, finely chopped
  • 1½ tsp cinnamon + pinch of cloves
  • Syrup: 400 g sugar, 250 g Greek honey, 300 ml water, lemon peel + squeeze, cinnamon stick (optional)

Method

  1. Make the syrup first and cool it completely (or bake first and pour hot syrup over cooled pastry — one must be cool).
  2. Mix walnuts with cinnamon and cloves; butter a ~33×23 cm dish.
  3. Lay 8–10 filo sheets, buttering each; spread a layer of nuts; repeat 5-sheet + nuts layers 3–4 times; finish with 8–10 buttered sheets.
  4. Cut into diamonds/squares with a sharp knife before baking (uncut baklava shatters).
  5. Bake at 160–175 °C (325–350 °F) 50–75 minutes until deep golden and crisp throughout.
  6. Pour the cool syrup evenly over the hot pastry; cool uncovered 4+ hours so layers absorb while staying crisp.

Chef tips

  • Keep unused filo under a damp towel at all times — it cracks within minutes.
  • Underbaking is the classic failure: the center must be fully crisp, not chewy.
  • Store uncovered at room temperature 1–2 days for maximum crispness; never refrigerate.
