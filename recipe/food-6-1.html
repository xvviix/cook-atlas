Ramen

Japan

Japan's noodle soup in five parts: broth (body), tare (seasoning), noodles (alkaline), toppings, and aroma oil. Shoyu, shio and miso tares each define a style; assembly order is tare → noodles → toppings → broth.

Ingredients

  • Tare (shoyu): 250 ml soy + 120 ml mirin + 60 ml sake + sugar + garlic + ginger
  • 2 l broth: chicken/dashi or vegetable stock
  • 4 portions ramen noodles (fresh or dried)
  • Toppings: chashu pork, 6.5-minute marinated eggs, nori, scallions, corn, bamboo shoots
  • Aroma oil: sesame or chili oil

Method

  1. Simmer the tare 10 minutes, cool and strain — this concentrated seasoning defines the bowl.
  2. Simmer the broth gently; keep it at a bare steaming heat, never a hard boil.
  3. Soft-boil eggs 6.5 minutes, chill, peel, and marinate in diluted tare.
  4. Cook noodles separately until just springy; drain hard — watery noodles dilute the bowl.
  5. Assemble: 1½ tbsp tare in each hot bowl, noodles, arranged toppings, then ladle over broth and serve instantly.

Chef tips

  • Tare seasons, broth nourishes — keep them separate until the bowl.
  • Noodles go from pot to bowl in seconds; ramen has a 4-minute life.
  • Slurping aerates and cools — it's technique, not rudeness.
