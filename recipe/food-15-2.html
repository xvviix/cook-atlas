Barbecue Ribs

United States

Baby backs via the 3-2-1 method: 3 hours of low smoke, 2 hours wrapped with butter, brown sugar, honey and juice, 1 hour unwrapped and lacquered with sauce. The wrap (Texas crutch) powers through the stall to bend-don't-break tenderness.

Ingredients

  • 2 racks baby back ribs, membrane removed
  • Dry rub: salt, paprika, brown sugar, mustard powder, pepper, garlic, cayenne
  • Wrap: butter + brown sugar + honey + apple juice
  • BBQ sauce for lacquering; mustard binder (optional)

Method

  1. Remove the membrane; slather mustard; coat generously with rub; rest 1+ hours.
  2. Smoke at 110–120 °C 3 hours, bone-side down, spritzing hourly.
  3. Wrap each rack tight in foil with butter, sugar, honey and juice; cook 2 hours.
  4. Unwrap; sauce both sides; finish 30–60 minutes to a tacky lacquer.
  5. Rest 15 minutes; slice between bones; serve with pickles and white bread.

Chef tips

  • Baby backs run leaner than spares — watch the wrap time to avoid mush.
  • Bend test: the rack cracks but doesn't break when lifted.
  • Rest before slicing or the juices flood the board.
