Seco de Chivo

Ecuador

Seco de chivo — slow-braised goat in a sauce of beer (or chicha), naranjilla juice, tomato, peppers, cilantro and achiote — is coastal Ecuador's celebration stew, served with yellow rice, avocado and sweet plantain. Despite 'seco' (dry), it's stewed in just enough liquid to braise, the sauce reducing to a glossy coat. The naranjilla's tart perfume is irreplaceable; beer adds the malty depth.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg goat, bone-in chunks + beer/chicha + naranjilla juice
  • Tomato + peppers + onion + cilantro + achiote + cumin
  • Yellow rice + plantain + avocado to serve

Method

  1. Marinate goat with garlic, cumin and beer 2+ hours.
  2. Brown hard in achiote oil; set aside.
  3. Soften vegetables; add tomato, naranjilla and beer.
  4. Braise covered 1.5–2 hours until falling apart.
  5. Reduce sauce glossy; shower with cilantro.
  6. Serve with yellow rice, plantain and avocado.

Chef tips

  • Brown hard — the fond is the sauce's foundation.
  • Naranjilla matters — no substitute tastes the same.
  • Reduce glossy — thin seco is just goat soup.
  • Bone-in — bones enrich the braise.
