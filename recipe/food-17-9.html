Farofa

Brazil

Brazil's indispensable sprinkle: coarse cassava flour toasted in bacon fat and butter with onion and garlic until nutty and gold — showered over feijoada, churrasco and moqueca for crunch and savor. Optional eggs, olives and herbs gild it further.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups coarse cassava/manioc flour (farinha)
  • 150 g bacon + 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion + 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped; salt + pepper
  • (Optional: diced boiled egg, olives, parsley)

Method

  1. Render diced bacon from a cold skillet to crisp and golden.
  2. Soften onion in the fat; add garlic 1 minute.
  3. Add the flour; toast stirring constantly 5–8 minutes to nutty gold.
  4. Season; fold in egg, olives and herbs if using.
  5. Serve warm alongside everything — farofa goes on, not beside.

Chef tips

  • Constant stirring — cassava flour scorches in seconds.
  • Coarse farinha, not fine tapioca starch — texture is the point.
  • Toast to gold, not brown — bitter farofa ruins the plate.
