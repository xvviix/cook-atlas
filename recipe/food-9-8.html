Bouillabaisse

France

Marseille's fisherman's stew, protected by charter: at least 4–5 Mediterranean rockfish simmered with saffron, fennel and orange, served as saffron broth over rouille-smeared toast plus the fish on a platter. Rouille is mandatory.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg mixed firm fish (snapper, sea bass, monkfish, cod) + shellfish
  • Stock: fish bones + leek + fennel + tomato + saffron + orange zest + wine
  • Potatoes; olive oil; thyme, bay, cayenne
  • Rouille: yolk + garlic + chili + saffron + breadcrumbs + olive oil
  • Baguette, toasted

Method

  1. Build the stock: sweat aromatics with saffron, add bones, tomato and wine; boil 30–60 minutes; strain and press.
  2. Simmer potatoes in the broth; add firm fish 10 minutes, shellfish the last 5.
  3. Whisk the rouille to a thick mayo; toast the bread.
  4. Serve traditionally: broth ladled over rouille toast in bowls, fish and potatoes on a platter (or rustic-combined).
  5. Extra rouille on the table, always.

Chef tips

  • Fish variety builds the depth — single-fish bouillabaisse falls flat.
  • Saffron and fennel are the Marseille signature; skip neither.
  • The rouille-toast-broth bite is the dish's thesis statement.
