Patacones

Colombia

Twice-fried green plantains: thick rounds fried pale, smashed flat, dunked in salted water (or garlic water), and fried again to crisp gold — served with hogao, guacamole or ají. Crunchy outside, fluffy within.

Ingredients

  • 2 large green plantains + oil for frying + salt
  • Salted/garlic water for dunking; hogao or guacamole to serve

Method

  1. Peel; cut 4 cm rounds.
  2. First fry at 170 °C 3–4 min to pale gold; drain.
  3. Smash to 1 cm between plastic/plates.
  4. Dunk in salted water; pat.
  5. Second fry to deep crisp gold; salt immediately; serve with hogao.

Chef tips

  • Green plantains only — ripe ones caramelize instead of crisping.
  • The water dunk seasons deeply — don't skip it.
  • Salt the second they leave the oil.
