Papanasi

Romania

Romania's cheese doughnuts: cow-cheese dough shaped into rings with little 'hats', fried gold — stacked ring + cream + jam + hat + more cream and jam. Sour-cherry or blueberry jam against tangy smântână; crisp outside, pillowy within.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dry cottage/cow cheese, drained
  • 2 eggs + 100 g sugar + vanilla + lemon zest + 250 g flour + baking powder
  • Oil at 170 °C; smântână + sour-cherry jam

Method

  1. Drain cheese well; mix dough soft.
  2. Shape rings + small balls (hats).
  3. Fry low-medium 3–4 min per side to deep gold.
  4. Drain on paper.
  5. Stack: ring, cream, jam, hat, cream, jam. Serve warm.

Chef tips

  • Drain the cheese — wet dough absorbs oil.
  • Low-medium oil — fierce heat burns raw middles.
  • Serve warm — cold papanasi turn heavy.
