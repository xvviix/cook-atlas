Chiles en Nogada

Mexico

Puebla's baroque masterpiece in flag colors: roasted poblanos stuffed with sweet-savory picadillo (pork, apple, pear, plantain, nuts, spices), cloaked in cool walnut-crema nogada, scattered with pomegranate and parsley. August–September only, when walnuts are fresh.

Ingredients

  • 6 large poblanos, roasted and peeled
  • Picadillo: 500 g pork/beef + onion + tomato + apple + pear + plantain + almonds + raisins + cinnamon + clove
  • Nogada: 200 g fresh peeled walnuts + crema + queso fresco/cream cheese + milk + sherry + sugar + salt
  • Pomegranate arils + parsley

Method

  1. Slit and roast the poblanos until blistered; steam, peel and seed carefully.
  2. Brown the meat; add onion, tomato, fruits, nuts and spices; simmer to a juicy-sweet picadillo; cool.
  3. Blanch and peel the walnuts; blend with crema, cheese, milk, sherry and sugar to a thick pourable sauce.
  4. Stuff each chile generously; lay on the platter.
  5. Blanket with cool nogada; scatter pomegranate and parsley. Serve at room temperature.

Chef tips

  • Fresh walnuts peel cleanly; old ones turn the sauce grey and bitter.
  • Room temperature, never hot — heat splits the nogada.
  • The sweet-savory-cool-crunch balance is the entire point — don't simplify it.
