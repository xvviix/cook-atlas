Xiaolongbao

China

Shanghai's soup dumplings: delicate pleated purses of pork and molten aspic that steams into drinkable broth. Eighteen pleats is the master standard; black vinegar and ginger are the classic dip.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 250 g flour + 130 ml hot water (or thin round wrappers)
  • 250 g ground pork + Shaoxing + soy + ginger + scallion
  • 150 ml rich stock + gelatin, chilled to aspic and cubed
  • Napa leaves or perforated parchment liners
  • Black vinegar + julienned ginger to serve

Method

  1. Make the aspic a day ahead: season stock, bloom gelatin, chill firm, cube.
  2. Mix the pork one direction until tacky; fold in aspic cubes last and keep cold.
  3. Roll 8 cm wrappers, edges thinner than centers.
  4. 1 tbsp filling each; pleat 12–18 folds rotating upward; twist and pinch the crown shut.
  5. Steam spaced over boiling water 8–10 minutes until translucent; serve instantly.

Chef tips

  • Cold filling + warm hands management — melting aspic leaks before steaming.
  • Seal the crown: any gap becomes a burst dumpling.
  • Eat properly: nip, sip the soup, then dip and devour.
