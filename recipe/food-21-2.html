Mtsvadi

Georgia

Georgian pork shashlik: marbled shoulder or neck cubed and marinated overnight in onion and pomegranate juice (or white wine), threaded loosely on skewers and grilled over vine-clipping embers to charred and dripping. Finished with pomegranate seeds and onion rings at the supra feast.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork shoulder/neck, in 4 cm cubes
  • Marinade: 2 onions sliced + 500 ml pomegranate juice (or white wine) + salt + pepper
  • Pomegranate seeds + onion + cilantro to serve

Method

  1. Cube pork evenly; mix with onion and juice; marinate overnight.
  2. Thread loosely on metal skewers — air between pieces.
  3. Grill over mature embers (gray ash, no flame) ~25 min, turning often.
  4. Rest 5 minutes.
  5. Scatter pomegranate and onion; serve with bread, tkemali and wine.

Chef tips

  • Embers, not flames — flame equals soot and raw centers.
  • Even cubes cook evenly; mixed sizes don't.
  • Don't press the skewers — dripping juice is lost juiciness.
