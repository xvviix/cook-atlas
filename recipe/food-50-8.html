Steak and Kidney Pie

England

The pub classic: braised steak and ox kidney in a rich ale-and-thyme gravy under a suet-crust (or puff) lid — deep, beefy, old-fashioned comfort. The kidney, soaked in milk to tame it, melts into the gravy; the suet crust steams soft below and crisps above. Fill the pie generously and bake until the gravy bubbles through the vent.

Ingredients

  • Filling: 800 g braising steak + 200 g ox kidney + onion + ale + stock + thyme
  • Suet crust: 300 g flour + 150 g suet + cold water (or puff pastry)
  • Egg wash

Method

  1. Soak kidney in milk 1+ hour; trim and dice.
  2. Brown steak and kidney; add onion, ale, stock and thyme.
  3. Braise 2+ hours until melting; cool completely.
  4. Line a pie dish (or fill under a lid only); add cold filling.
  5. Cap with suet crust; seal, vent, egg-wash.
  6. Bake at 190–200 °C for 35–45 minutes until gold and bubbling.

Chef tips

  • Cold filling — hot filling melts the pastry raw.
  • Soak the kidney — milk tames the tang.
  • Braise until melting — chewy pie is a failure.
  • Vent the lid — steam needs escape.
