Wagyu

Japan

A premium ingredient, not a recipe: Japanese beef graded on yield (A–C) and quality (1–5) for marbling, color and texture — A5 with BMS 8–12 is the summit. Intensely rich, served in small portions, cooked rare and simply.

Ingredients

  • A5 wagyu steak (ribeye/strip/filet), 100–120 g per person
  • Flaky sea salt only
  • Cast-iron or carbon-steel pan (no oil — the fat provides it)
  • Rice + simple sides; nothing that competes

Method

  1. Temper 20 minutes at room temperature (or partially freeze 30 minutes for thin slicing).
  2. Heat a dry pan to medium-high/high; no oil needed.
  3. Sear: 60–90 seconds per side for a ¾-inch steak, or 10–30 seconds per side for ¼-inch yakiniku slices.
  4. Target 115–125 °F internal — rare to medium-rare, never beyond.
  5. Rest briefly, slice, salt, and eat in small, slow bites.

Chef tips

  • 3–4 oz per person satisfies — richness overwhelms in steakhouse portions.
  • Past medium-rare the marbling renders out and the magic is gone.
  • Salt only; sauces and marinades mask what you paid for.
