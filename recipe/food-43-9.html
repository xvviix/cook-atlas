Kutia

Ukraine

Kutia is Ukraine's sacred Christmas Eve wheat pudding: whole wheat berries simmered tender, folded with ground poppy seeds, honey, chopped walnuts, raisins and a splash of uzvar (dried-fruit compote) — the first of twelve Sviat Vechir dishes, blessed and shared from a common bowl. Each ingredient symbolizes: wheat for eternity, honey for sweetness, poppy for abundance. The wheat must be tender but intact; the poppy ground to a milky paste.

Ingredients

  • 250 g wheat berries, soaked overnight
  • 100 g poppy seeds + honey + walnuts + raisins
  • Uzvar/compote + pinch of salt

Method

  1. Simmer soaked wheat 1.5–2 hours until tender but intact; drain and cool.
  2. Scald poppy seeds; grind to a grey, milky paste.
  3. Fold poppy, honey, walnuts and raisins through the wheat.
  4. Loosen with uzvar to a spoonable, glistening consistency.
  5. Chill; serve as the first Christmas Eve dish.

Chef tips

  • Tender but intact wheat — mushy wheat makes porridge.
  • Grind the poppy fine — whole seeds taste gritty.
  • Honey to taste — kutia should gleam, not swim.
  • Serve first — tradition places kutia at the head of Sviat Vechir.
