Cochinita Pibil

Mexico

Yucatán's pit-roasted pork: shoulder marinated in achiote and bitter orange, wrapped in banana leaves and slow-cooked until it shreds with a spoon — served on corn tortillas with pickled red onions and habanero salsa.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork shoulder/butt
  • 100 g achiote paste + 2 cups bitter orange juice (or orange + lime/vinegar)
  • Garlic + cumin + allspice + Mexican oregano + salt
  • Banana leaves (softened over flame); lard
  • Pickled red onions + habanero salsa + corn tortillas

Method

  1. Blend achiote with citrus, spices and garlic to a smooth recado; coat the pork; marinate 4+ hours or overnight.
  2. Line the pot with banana leaves; add pork + lard; wrap fully; cover tight.
  3. Roast 150–175 °C 3–4 hours (or slow-cooker low 8 hours) until collapsing.
  4. Shred into its juices; adjust salt and citrus.
  5. Serve on warm tortillas with pickled onions and habanero salsa.

Chef tips

  • Bitter orange is the soul — sweet orange alone misses the tang.
  • Seal the wrap tight; the trapped steam is the 'pit'.
  • Pickled onion acidity is structural, not garnish.
