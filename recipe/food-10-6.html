Bakso

Indonesia

Indonesia's beloved meatball soup: springy, elastic beef balls (the bounce comes from ice-cold processing with tapioca and baking powder) floating in a clear garlicy beef broth with noodles, greens, fried shallots, sambal and a squeeze of lime.

Ingredients

  • Balls: 500 g very cold lean beef + 75 g tapioca starch + 120 ml ice water + baking powder + fried shallots + garlic + salt + white pepper
  • Broth: 1 kg beef bones + 2.5 l water + garlic + shallots + scallions + celery
  • Egg noodles + rice vermicelli + choy sum/bok choy
  • Kecap manis, sambal, lime, fried shallots to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the bones 2+ hours with aromatics; skim to a clear broth; season with salt, pepper and sugar.
  2. Process the cold beef with seasonings 60 seconds; add tapioca, baking powder and ice water; process 90 seconds to a sticky elastic paste.
  3. Squeeze paste through fist-and-thumb; scoop balls with a wet spoon into warm (not boiling) water.
  4. Poach until they float plus 5 minutes; chill in ice water for maximum bounce.
  5. Blanch noodles and greens; bowl them with bakso; ladle broth; garnish generously.

Chef tips

  • Cold is the texture — warm meat makes dense, crumbly balls.
  • Warm poaching water first; a hard boil bursts them before they set.
  • Store-bought bakso is a legitimate shortcut — the broth and toppings carry the bowl.
