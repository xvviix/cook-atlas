Tonkatsu

Japan

Panko-crusted pork cutlet fried to a shattering gold: juicy loin, airy crumb, served in strips with shredded cabbage, rice, miso soup and tonkatsu sauce. The national comfort plate.

Ingredients

  • 4 pork loin chops, ~1.5 cm thick
  • Salt + pepper
  • Flour + 2 beaten eggs + 2 cups fresh panko
  • Neutral oil, 170–180 °C
  • Cabbage, tonkatsu sauce, rice, karashi mustard

Method

  1. Snip the fat-meat membrane in 3–4 places so the cutlet lies flat; pound to even thickness.
  2. Season, then flour → egg → panko, pressing the crumbs on firmly; rest 5 minutes to set.
  3. Fry at 170–180 °C ~3–4 minutes per side until deep gold and 63–70 °C inside.
  4. Drain vertically on a rack 2–3 minutes — lying flat steams the crust.
  5. Slice into strips, plate over cabbage, and sauce at the table.

Chef tips

  • Fresh coarse panko makes the craggy crust; fine crumbs go sandy.
  • Oil temperature is everything — a thermometer beats guessing.
  • Rest before slicing or the juices flood the board.
