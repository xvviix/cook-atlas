Cozido à Portuguesa

Portugal

The Portuguese boiled dinner: beef, pork, chicken and smoked sausages (chouriço, morcela) simmered into a rich broth, with cabbage, kale, carrots, turnip and potatoes cooked in stages, plus mint-scented rice. A feast dish for cold days and gatherings — broth first, meats and vegetables after.

Ingredients

  • Beef: shank/brisket ~1 kg; pork: ribs/shoulder ~700 g + bacon piece
  • 4 chicken thighs; 1 chouriço + 1 morcela (in cheesecloth)
  • 1 cabbage (wedges), kale bunch, 4 potatoes, 3 carrots, 1 turnip, 1 onion, garlic, bay
  • Rice + mint sprigs (in a pierced bag or 'rice ball')
  • Salt, pepper; crusty bread

Method

  1. (Night before, optional: salt the pork and beef to deepen flavor.) Blanch all fresh meats 5 minutes; discard the water and start clean.
  2. Cover meats with fresh water, bring to a boil, skim, then simmer gently 1 hour.
  3. Add sausages and hard vegetables (carrots, turnip, potatoes, onion); simmer 30–40 minutes.
  4. Add the rice bag, cabbage and kale last; simmer 20–30 minutes until everything is tender.
  5. Lift out meats and vegetables to a platter; slice; serve the strained broth first with bread, then the feast with broth spooned over.

Chef tips

  • Staged cooking is the whole technique — everything tender, nothing mushy.
  • Wrap morcela in cheesecloth so it doesn't burst and cloud the pot.
  • Low and slow only; a rolling boil turns the broth greasy and the meats stringy.
