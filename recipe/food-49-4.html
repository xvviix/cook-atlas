Encebollado

Ecuador

Encebollado — Guayaquil's legendary tuna-and-yuca soup, hangover cure and breakfast of champions: albacore tuna simmered with yuca in a cumin-tomato broth, loaded with pickled red onion (the encebollado), cilantro and lime, served with chifles, popcorn and ají. Hearty, tangy, onion-heavy — the pickled onion goes on by the handful, not as garnish. Firm tuna that holds its chunks is essential.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg albacore tuna steaks + 1 kg yuca
  • Tomato + onion + cumin + achiote + cilantro + stock
  • Pickled red onion (lime-cured) + lime + chifles + ají to serve

Method

  1. Simmer yuca in stock until tender; lift out.
  2. Poach tuna in the broth 10–12 minutes; flake into chunks.
  3. Build the broth with tomato, cumin and achiote.
  4. Lime-pickle heaps of sliced red onion.
  5. Bowl yuca and tuna; ladle broth; bury in onion and cilantro.
  6. Serve with lime, chifles and ají.

Chef tips

  • Firm tuna — flaky fish disintegrates.
  • Onion by the handful — timid onion misses the point.
  • Lime generously — acidity is the engine.
  • Chifles for crunch — texture contrast matters.
