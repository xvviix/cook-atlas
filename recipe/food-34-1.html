Biltong

South Africa

Air-dried spiced beef — South Africa's iconic snack: thick strips cured in vinegar, salt, toasted coriander, pepper and brown sugar, then hung to dry 3–5 days to chewy (or dry, or wet — the eternal debate). Silverside/topside, cut with the grain.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg silverside/topside, 2–3 cm strips with the grain
  • Cure: coarse salt + toasted cracked coriander + pepper + brown sugar
  • Malt/cider vinegar (+ Worcestershire optional)

Method

  1. Toast + crack coriander; mix cure.
  2. Dip strips in vinegar; coat in cure.
  3. Cure refrigerated overnight.
  4. Pat (don't rinse); hang with airflow 3–5 days.
  5. Slice against the grain; store cool and dry.

Chef tips

  • Airflow prevents mold — still air is the enemy.
  • Thick strips — thin ones over-dry to jerky.
  • Cut with the grain to dry, against to eat.
