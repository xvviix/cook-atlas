Erwtensoep

Netherlands

Erwtensoep (snert) is Holland's winter armor: split peas simmered with pork hock, bacon, celeriac, leek, carrot and potato until thick enough to stand a spoon in — finished with sliced rookworst (smoked sausage) and served with rye bread and bacon. Traditionally better on day two, when it sets almost solid overnight and is thinned back to glory. Thickness is law: thin erwtensoep is just pea soup.

Ingredients

  • 500 g split peas + pork hock + 200 g bacon
  • Celeriac + leek + carrot + onion + potato + bay + thyme
  • Rookworst + rye bread + celery leaf to serve

Method

  1. Simmer peas with hock and bacon 1+ hour, skimming.
  2. Add vegetables; simmer 45 minutes more until collapsing.
  3. Lift out meats; shred; return to the pot.
  4. Mash coarsely — thick enough to stand a spoon.
  5. Warm sliced rookworst through; season boldly.
  6. Rest overnight if possible; serve with rye and bacon.

Chef tips

  • Spoon-standing thick — thin it never.
  • Day two is best — the flavors marry overnight.
  • Smoked meats build it — unsmoked makes bland soup.
  • Rookworst warmed, not boiled — boiling splits it.
