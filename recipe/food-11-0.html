Tacos

Mexico

Listed here as tacos al pastor: chile-and-achiote marinated pork shaved off the trompo (or griddled at home), crowned with charred pineapple, onion, cilantro and lime on warm corn tortillas. Lebanese technique, Mexican soul.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork shoulder, thinly sliced
  • Marinade: guajillo + ancho + chipotle, achiote, tomato, pineapple juice, orange juice, vinegar, garlic, cumin, oregano, cloves
  • Fresh pineapple rings; white onion; cilantro; limes
  • Corn tortillas; salsa

Method

  1. Blend the soaked chiles with all marinade ingredients to a smooth adobo.
  2. Coat the pork and marinate 4 hours to overnight.
  3. Sear hard on a grill pan 4–5 minutes per side to charred edges; chop bite-size.
  4. Char the pineapple rings; dice. Warm tortillas until pliable.
  5. Assemble: pork + pineapple + onion + cilantro + lime; salsa on the side.

Chef tips

  • Thin slices + fierce heat mimic the trompo's crust at home.
  • Don't marinate past 24 hours — the acids turn the texture mushy.
  • Double-tortilla street-style if your tortillas are thin.
