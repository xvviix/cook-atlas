Spanakopita

Greece

Spinach-and-feta pie in shattering filo pastry: wilted spinach mixed with crumbled feta, herbs, egg and nutmeg, layered between buttered/oiled filo sheets (or coiled into a spiral) and baked golden. Served warm or at room temperature.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh spinach (or 300 g frozen, thawed)
  • 200 g feta, crumbled
  • 2 red onions + 2 spring onions, chopped; 2 garlic cloves
  • Fresh dill + parsley, chopped; pinch of nutmeg; 2 beaten eggs
  • 250–400 g filo pastry
  • 100 ml olive oil or melted butter for brushing
  • Salt, pepper; sesame seeds (optional)

Method

  1. Sauté onions until soft, add garlic, then wilt the spinach in batches; cool and squeeze out every drop of liquid.
  2. Mix spinach with feta, herbs, nutmeg, eggs and seasoning — taste for salt (feta varies).
  3. Layer half the filo in an oiled pan, brushing each sheet; spread the filling; top with remaining filo, brushing each sheet.
  4. Score into portions, sprinkle with water/oil and sesame, and bake at 180 °C (350 °F) 35–45 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
  5. Cool at least 15 minutes before cutting along the scores.

Chef tips

  • Wet filling = soggy pie: squeeze the spinach ruthlessly.
  • Keep filo covered with a damp towel while working — it dries in minutes.
  • Olive oil between layers is the traditional village style; butter is richer.
