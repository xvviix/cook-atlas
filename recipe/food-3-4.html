Anticuchos

Peru

Peru's iconic street skewers: cubes of beef heart marinated in ají panca, vinegar, garlic, cumin and beer, grilled over charcoal and basted with an oily ají glaze applied with a corn-husk brush. Tender with a steak-like chew, served with corn and potato slices.

Ingredients

  • 1 beef heart (~1 kg), trimmed of fat, silver skin and vessels, cut in 3 cm cubes
  • Marinade: 4 tbsp ají panca paste, 120 ml vinegar, 250 ml dark beer
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic, 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp oregano, salt, pepper
  • Basting glaze: 250 ml oil + 2 tbsp ají panca + pinch of salt
  • Wooden skewers; boiled corn + potato slices to serve

Method

  1. Trim the heart meticulously — all fat and connective tissue must go for tenderness.
  2. Marinate the cubes 4+ hours, ideally overnight, refrigerated.
  3. Thread 3–4 pieces per skewer with a little space between for even charring.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal 2–3 minutes per side, basting continuously with the ají oil.
  5. Serve 2–3 skewers per portion with grilled corn, potato rounds and extra ají sauce.

Chef tips

  • Trimming matters more than marinating time — untrimmed heart stays chewy whatever you do.
  • Heart cooks fast; overcooking toughens it — aim for browned outside, juicy inside.
  • The corn-husk brush (panca de choclo) is traditional but a silicone brush works.
