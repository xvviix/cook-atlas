Mămăligă

Romania

Romania's cornmeal soul — once peasant staple, now national pride: coarse mălai rained into salted boiling water, stirred to a thick porridge, cooked until it pulls from the ceaun. Turned out whole, sliced with string; the bed under sarmale, tochitură and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 250 g coarse cornmeal (mălai) + 1 l salted water
  • Butter/smântână + brânză to serve

Method

  1. Boil salted water; rain in cornmeal whisking (no lumps).
  2. Stir 20–40 min on low until thick and pulling away.
  3. Rest 5 min; invert onto a board.
  4. Slice with string or thread.
  5. Serve hot with butter, cheese, smântână.

Chef tips

  • Rain gradually while stirring — dumping makes lumps.
  • Coarse mălai only — fine meal turns gluey.
  • String-sliced is traditional — knives drag.
