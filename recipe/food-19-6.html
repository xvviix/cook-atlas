Korean Fried Chicken

Korea

Yangnyeom-tongdak: shatteringly crisp chicken from the double-fry (first to cook, second to glass), glazed in sweet-spicy gochujang-honey-garlic sauce and scattered with sesame — over pickled radish (chikin-mu) and beer. Chimaek culture in a basket.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg wings/drumettes
  • Coating: potato starch + flour + baking powder/soda + egg
  • Sauce: gochujang + ketchup + honey/rice syrup + soy + garlic + vinegar
  • Sesame; pickled radish + beer to serve

Method

  1. Coat seasoned chicken in the starch batter.
  2. First fry at 165 °C ~10–12 minutes to cooked and pale gold; rest.
  3. Second fry at 175–180 °C to deep glassy crisp.
  4. Warm the sauce to bubbling; toss hot chicken to glaze thinly.
  5. Sesame over; serve instantly with radish and beer.

Chef tips

  • Double-fry is the whole secret — single-fried is another dish.
  • Sauce thin and hot — drowning softens the glass.
  • Potato starch fries crisper and lighter than flour alone.
