Rfissa

Morocco

The new-mother's stew: chicken braised with mountains of onion, lentils and fenugreek in ginger-turmeric-ras el hanout broth — ladled over shredded msemen/trid flatbread that drinks the sauce. Warming, restorative, ceremonial; fenugreek is the soul.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, jointed + 2 onions sliced
  • 1 cup lentils + 1 tbsp fenugreek (soaked overnight)
  • Ginger + turmeric + ras el hanout + saffron + smen; 5 msemen, shredded; coriander

Method

  1. Soak fenugreek overnight (tames bitterness).
  2. Sweat chicken + onions + spices + smen 10 min.
  3. Add lentils + fenugreek + water; simmer 75–90 min.
  4. Reduce broth rich; keep chicken warm.
  5. Shred msemen in a platter; ladle broth; chicken + lentils over; coriander.

Chef tips

  • Soak the fenugreek — unsoaked tastes harsh.
  • Shred bread bite-size — whole sheets won't drink the broth.
  • Serve immediately — the bread keeps absorbing.
