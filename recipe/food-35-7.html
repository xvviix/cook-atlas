Lyutenitsa

Bulgaria

Lyutenitsa is Bulgaria's national relish — flame-roasted red peppers and ripe tomatoes ground together with oil, garlic, sugar and salt, then simmered low until thick enough to stand a spoon. Despite the name (from lyut, 'hot/spicy'), most lyutenitsa is mild and sweet-smoky rather than fiery; heat comes only from optional chili. Bulgarian families still make huge autumn batches for winter jars, and it is eaten on bread, alongside grilled meats, or straight from the jar.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg red kapia peppers + 1 kg ripe tomatoes
  • 150 ml sunflower oil + 4 garlic cloves + 2 tbsp sugar + salt + chili to taste

Method

  1. Char the peppers (flame, grill or 220 °C oven) until blackened; steam covered, then peel and seed.
  2. Blanch, peel and roughly grind the tomatoes.
  3. Fry the pepper-tomato pulp in oil 10 minutes, then add sugar, salt and chili.
  4. Simmer low 40–60 minutes, stirring often, until thick and glossy.
  5. Blend coarse or smooth to taste; jar hot and seal (or refrigerate up to 2 weeks).

Chef tips

  • Roasting is the flavor — unroasted peppers give a pale imitation.
  • Stir often near the end — thick relish scorches easily.
  • Balance sugar and salt at the end — tomatoes vary wildly in sweetness.
  • For winter storage, sterilize jars properly or freeze in portions.
