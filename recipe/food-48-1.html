Mandi

Saudi Arabia

Mandi — Hadhramaut's gift to the Peninsula, perfected in Saudi Arabia: chicken or lamb dry-rubbed with mild hawaij (cardamom, cumin, turmeric, black lime — notably gentle, no chili heat), slow-roasted until falling apart, served over fragrant yellow rice finished with charcoal smoke (the dukhna: a glowing coal in oil, sealed under the lid). Delicate where kabsa is bold, mandi is about aroma over fire. The smoke finish is non-negotiable theater and flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg chicken or lamb + mandi hawaij + turmeric + black lime + ghee
  • 4 cups basmati + onion + raisins + saffron
  • Glowing charcoal + oil for smoking; daqqus tomato salsa to serve

Method

  1. Rub meat with hawaij, turmeric and salt; rest 2+ hours.
  2. Slow-roast covered until nearly falling apart; uncover to bronze.
  3. Cook basmati in light stock with onion, saffron and raisins.
  4. Smoke: coal in an oiled cup nestled in the rice; seal 5 minutes.
  5. Mound rice; crown with meat; serve with daqqus and yogurt.

Chef tips

  • Mild spice by design — mandi is aromatic, not hot.
  • Smoke at the end — the dukhna is the signature.
  • Bronze after braising — pale mandi meat looks unfinished.
  • Daqqus alongside — the fresh salsa cuts the richness.
