Pljeskavica

Serbia

The Serbian burger — but bigger, thinner and juicier than any bun-burger: a dinner-plate patty of seasoned beef-pork grilled over coals and served in lepinja with ajvar, urnebes (spicy cheese spread) and raw onions. Leskovac's grill festival crowns its masters.

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef + 500 g pork, ground
  • 1 onion, minced + 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp paprika + salt + pepper (+ cayenne for ljuta/pljeskavica)
  • Splash of sparkling water; oil for grilling
  • Lepinja + ajvar + urnebes + onions

Method

  1. Mix meats with onion, garlic and spices until tacky; rest chilled 2+ hours.
  2. With damp hands, pound into thin 20 cm patties.
  3. Grill over medium-high 5–7 minutes per side with a good char.
  4. Toast lepinja cut-side down on the grill.
  5. Build: ajvar on bread, patty, urnebes, onions; serve immediately.

Chef tips

  • Thin and wide is the style — thick patties are hamburgers, not pljeskavica.
  • Resting firms the mix so the giant patty holds together.
  • Stuff it (gurmanska) with cheese and ham for the indulgent version.
