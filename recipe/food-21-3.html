Lobio

Georgia

Georgia's walnut-bean stew: red kidney beans simmered to creamy, partly mashed, then enriched with fried onion, walnut-garlic paste, blue fenugreek, coriander and pomegranate juice. Served bubbling in clay pots with mchadi cornbread and pickles — humble, nutty, deeply savory.

Ingredients

  • 400 g dried red kidney beans, soaked overnight
  • 150 g walnuts, ground + 5 garlic + blue fenugreek + coriander + cayenne
  • 1 onion fried + tkemali/pomegranate juice + cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer beans with bay to very tender; reserve 500 ml liquid.
  2. Grind walnuts with garlic, spices and cilantro to a paste.
  3. Fry onion soft; bloom the walnut paste 1 minute.
  4. Add beans + liquid + sour juice; mash a quarter of the beans.
  5. Simmer 10 min to a thick stew; serve hot with mchadi.

Chef tips

  • Blue fenugreek (utskho suneli) is the signature — source it.
  • Partial mashing thickens without flour.
  • Sour juice at the end brightens the earthiness.
