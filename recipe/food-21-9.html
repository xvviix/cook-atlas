Georgia

Georgia's velvet chicken soup: rich broth thickened with a dry-toasted flour liaison and enriched off-boil with eggs beaten with lemon — silky, tangy, coriander-scented. The eggs must never boil, or silk turns to scramble.

Ingredients

  • 1 small chicken (~1.2 kg) + 2.5 l water
  • 1 onion fried in butter + 3 tbsp flour, dry-toasted
  • 3 eggs + juice of 1 lemon; garlic + coriander + salt + pepper

Method

  1. Simmer chicken 50–60 min; lift out; shred; strain broth.
  2. Fry onion golden; toast flour dry to nutty (no color).
  3. Whisk flour smooth with cold broth; simmer into the pot 8–10 min.
  4. Return chicken + garlic; bring just to a bare simmer.
  5. Stream in egg-lemon off-boil, stirring; heat to steaming — never boil. Serve with coriander.

Chef tips

  • Egg-lemon goes in off the boil — boiling scrambles it.
  • Toast flour dry first — raw flour tastes pasty.
  • Temper the eggs with hot broth for insurance.
