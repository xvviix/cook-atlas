Shawarma

Lebanon

Homemade chicken shawarma: thigh strips marinated overnight in yogurt (or oil-vinegar), garlic, lemon and shawarma spices — seared hard for charred edges, then wrapped in pita with toum (garlic whip), pickles and fries. The spit is theater; the marinade is the flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs, in strips
  • Marinade: yogurt + garlic + lemon + shawarma spice + paprika + cumin + cardamom + cinnamon + salt
  • To serve: pita + toum + pickles + fries + parsley

Method

  1. Marinate 4–24 hours refrigerated.
  2. Sear in a ripping-hot skillet in batches — char, don't steam.
  3. Rest 5 minutes; slice thin against the grain.
  4. Warm the pita; spread toum; layer chicken, pickles, fries.
  5. Roll tight; toast seam-down; serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Thighs, not breast — breast dries under high heat.
  • Small batches keep the sear; crowding boils.
  • Overnight marinade = depth; 30 minutes = surface only.
