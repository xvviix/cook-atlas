Sancocho

Colombia

Colombia's one-pot feast soup: chicken (or hen) and corn simmered with aliños, then plantain, yuca, potato and yam added in stages to a hearty, starchy broth — served with rice, avocado, lime and ají. Weekend and celebration food.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, pieces + 3 l broth/water
  • Aliños (onion-tomato-garlic-cumin base) + corn
  • Green plantain + yuca + potatoes + yam; cilantro + avocado + lime + ají

Method

  1. Simmer chicken + corn + aliños 30–35 min.
  2. Add plantain and yam; 15 min.
  3. Add yuca + potato; simmer 25–30 min to tender.
  4. Season; cilantro over.
  5. Serve with rice, avocado, lime and ají.

Chef tips

  • Stage the starches — together they turn to mixed mush.
  • Yuca must cook fully — undercooked yuca stays waxy.
  • Skim the foam for a clean broth.
