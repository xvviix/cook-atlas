Bambalouni

Tunisia

Bambalouni (bambaloni) are Tunisia's beach doughnuts: a simple yeasted dough stretched into thin rings or discs, fried until blistered and golden, then drowned in sugar while hot. Associated with Sidi Bou Said's seaside cafés, where they're eaten with mint tea overlooking the Mediterranean, they must be eaten within minutes — crisp, chewy, hot. The stretch-thin shaping is what keeps them light instead of bready.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + yeast + sugar + warm water + pinch of salt
  • Frying oil at 175 °C + plenty of sugar for dusting

Method

  1. Knead a soft dough; rise 1–1.5 hours until doubled.
  2. Divide into balls; rest 15 minutes.
  3. Stretch each thin — a disc with a hole, or a thin ring.
  4. Fry at 175 °C until puffed, blistered and gold both sides.
  5. Drain seconds; bury in sugar; eat immediately.

Chef tips

  • Stretch thin — thick bambalouni fry raw inside.
  • Sugar while hot — sugar slides off cold doughnuts.
  • Eat within minutes — they toughen as they cool.
  • Mint tea alongside — the Sidi Bou Said ritual.
