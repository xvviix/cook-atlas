Pebre

Chile

Chile's table salsa: tomato, onion, cilantro, garlic and ají verde chopped fresh with oil and vinegar — brighter and looser than pico de gallo. Lives on every asado table for bread-dunking and meat-topping; made fresh daily, never bottled (at home).

Ingredients

  • 3 tomatoes diced + 1 onion diced (soaked to mellow)
  • 1 bunch cilantro + garlic + ají verde/jalapeño
  • Oil + red wine vinegar + salt

Method

  1. Soak onion 10 min; drain (mellows the bite).
  2. Chop everything fresh and even.
  3. Dress with oil + vinegar + salt.
  4. Rest 15–30 min to marry.
  5. Serve with bread, sopaipillas, asado.

Chef tips

  • Fresh daily — day-old pebre fades.
  • Cilantro is mandatory — no cilantro, no pebre.
  • Soak the onion — raw bite bullies the rest.
