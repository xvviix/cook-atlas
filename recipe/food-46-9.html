Kalb el Louz

Algeria

Kalb el louz ('almond heart') is Algeria's Ramadan-night semolina cake: coarse semolina enriched with butter, sugar and yogurt, baked with an almond crowning each diamond, then flooded with orange-blossom syrup until molten-moist. Cousin to basbousa and makroudh's soft side, it must rest hours (ideally overnight) so the syrup penetrates to the base. Served with mint tea after iftar, it gleams like amber.

Ingredients

  • Cake: 500 g coarse semolina + 200 g sugar + 150 g butter + 250 g yogurt
  • Baking powder + vanilla + almonds for topping
  • Syrup: sugar + water + lemon + orange-blossom water

Method

  1. Mix semolina, sugar and baking powder; rub in butter; bind with yogurt.
  2. Rest the batter 1+ hour so grains hydrate.
  3. Press into a buttered pan; score diamonds; crown each with an almond.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 30–40 minutes until deep gold.
  5. Flood with warm syrup; rest overnight; re-cut and serve.

Chef tips

  • Coarse semolina — fine gives a cakey, not grainy, bite.
  • Rest the batter — unhydrated grains bake gritty.
  • Score before baking — cutting after shatters.
  • Overnight syrup rest — patience is an ingredient.
