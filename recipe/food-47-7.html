Oliebollen

Netherlands

Oliebollen — New Year's Eve oil balls: yeasted, beer-lightened batter studded with raisins, currants and apple, scooped with an ice-cream scoop into 180 °C oil and fried to craggy golden spheres, buried in powdered sugar and eaten by the dozen from street stalls (oliebollenkramen). Crisp-shelled, airy-centered, best within the hour. The batter should be thick enough to hold shape — thin batter fries into flat discs.

Ingredients

  • Batter: 500 g flour + yeast + warm milk + 2 eggs + sugar + salt + beer
  • Raisins + currants + diced apple + lemon zest
  • Oil at 180 °C + powdered sugar by the kilo

Method

  1. Soak dried fruit; make a thick yeasted batter; rise 1 hour.
  2. Fold in fruit and apple.
  3. Heat oil to 180 °C; scoop balls with an oiled scoop.
  4. Fry 4–5 minutes, turning, until deep gold.
  5. Drain; bury in powdered sugar; eat within the hour.

Chef tips

  • Thick batter — thin batter fries flat.
  • 180 °C steady — cool oil greases, hot oil burns raw middles.
  • Oiled scoop — batter releases cleanly.
  • Sugar just before eating — early sugar melts to glaze.
