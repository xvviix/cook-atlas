Jareesh

Saudi Arabia

Jareesh (jereesh) is Najd's ancient comfort porridge: crushed wheat simmered for hours with lamb or chicken, onion and cumin until it collapses into a thick, savory, spoonable mass — finished with a sizzle of samneh (ghee) and sometimes yogurt. Ramadan and winter food, humble and deeply nourishing, it resembles harees but keeps more texture. Long, slow, stirred often: undercooked jareesh is gritty, not creamy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups crushed wheat (jareesh) + 700 g lamb or chicken
  • Onion + cumin + black lime + salt + water/stock
  • Samneh + yogurt to finish

Method

  1. Brown meat with onion and cumin.
  2. Add wheat and stock; bring to a bare simmer.
  3. Cook low 2–3 hours, stirring often, until collapsed and thick.
  4. Shred the meat through; season (black lime, salt).
  5. Serve porridge-thick, crowned with sizzling samneh and yogurt.

Chef tips

  • Stir often — wheat porridge catches and scorches.
  • Low heat, long time — haste makes grit.
  • Porridge-thick, not soupy — it should mound.
  • Samneh sizzle to finish — the flavor crown.
