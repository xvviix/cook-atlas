Borscht

Russia

Russian red borscht: beef-and-bone broth loaded with beets, cabbage, potato and carrot — the beets sautéed separately with tomato and vinegar to lock their ruby color, added late so the soup glows rather than browns. Served with smetana and dill; better on day two.

Ingredients

  • 500 g bone-in beef + 3 l water
  • 3 beets grated + 1 onion + 1 carrot + tomato + vinegar + sugar (zazharka)
  • Potato + cabbage + garlic + bay; smetana + dill

Method

  1. Simmer beef 1–1.5 hrs, skimming; shred meat; strain.
  2. Sauté beets with onion, carrot, tomato, vinegar, sugar 10 min.
  3. Boil potatoes in broth 10 min; cabbage 10 min.
  4. Add zazharka + garlic; simmer 10 min — don't boil hard.
  5. Rest 20 min; serve with smetana and dill.

Chef tips

  • Vinegar with the beets locks the red — skip it, get brown.
  • Beets in late — long boiling dulls the color.
  • Day-two borscht is the real borscht.
