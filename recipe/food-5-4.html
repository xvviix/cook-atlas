Patatas Bravas

Spain

Madrid's essential tapa: crisp fried potato cubes napped with salsa brava (smoky paprika-tomato, with cayenne bite) and zigzags of alioli. Hot sauce on hot potatoes, eaten standing up.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg waxy potatoes, 2–3 cm cubes
  • Oil for frying + salt
  • Brava: 1 onion, 2 tbsp paprika (sweet + smoked), ½ tsp cayenne
  • 1 tbsp flour + 250 ml stock + 3 tbsp tomato purée
  • Alioli: mayo + garlic + lemon (quick version)

Method

  1. Soak the cubes 30 minutes, dry thoroughly — surface starch is the enemy of crispness.
  2. Make the brava: sweat onion, bloom paprika + cayenne 1 minute, stir in flour, then stock and tomato; simmer 10 minutes and blend smooth.
  3. Fry the potatoes at 175–180 °C until deep golden and crisp (double-fry for extra crunch).
  4. Drain, salt immediately, and spoon over generous hot brava.
  5. Finish with alioli zigzags and serve at once.

Chef tips

  • Wet potatoes spatter and steam — dry them like you mean it.
  • Fry in batches; crowding drops the oil temperature and breeds sogginess.
  • Make the sauce ahead — it reheats perfectly and tastes better rested.
