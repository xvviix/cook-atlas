Bramboráky

Czech Republic

Czech garlic potato pancakes: coarsely grated potato with egg, flour, heaps of garlic and marjoram — fried thin and lacy in shallow oil. Beer-hall sides and midnight snacks; ham, cheese or sauerkraut folded in are all legitimate.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, coarsely grated + squeezed
  • 2 eggs + 100 g flour + 6 garlic cloves + marjoram + salt + pepper
  • Oil for shallow-frying; (+ ham/cheese optional)

Method

  1. Grate + squeeze potatoes dry.
  2. Mix with eggs, flour, garlic, marjoram.
  3. Rest batter 15–30 min.
  4. Fry thin ladles 3 min per side to lacy gold.
  5. Drain; salt; serve hot with beer.

Chef tips

  • Thin = crisp; thick = soggy middles.
  • Garlic generously — timid garlic misses the point.
  • Serve instantly — they soften as they stand.
