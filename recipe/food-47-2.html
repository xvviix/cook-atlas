Poffertjes

Netherlands

Poffertjes are Holland's tiny, puffy griddle cakes — yeasted buckwheat-wheat batter cooked in the dimpled poffertjespan, flipped with a fork until golden, and served heaped with butter and powdered sugar (sometimes stroop). Fluffy, coin-small and dangerously poppable, they're the fairground and market treat of Dutch childhoods. The batter must be yeasted and bubbly; pancake-batter poffertjes are merely small pancakes.

Ingredients

  • Batter: 250 g flour + 250 g buckwheat flour + yeast + milk + 2 eggs + sugar
  • Butter for the pan
  • Butter + powdered sugar (+ stroop) to serve

Method

  1. Whisk a smooth yeasted batter; rise 45–60 minutes until bubbly.
  2. Heat the poffertjespan; butter each dimple.
  3. Pipe or spoon batter into dimples.
  4. Flip with a fork when set at the edges; brown both sides.
  5. Heap onto warm plates; crown with butter and snowfall sugar.

Chef tips

  • Yeasted batter, full rise — flat batter makes discs, not puffs.
  • Hot pan, quick flip — hesitation burns the bottoms.
  • Butter every round — dry dimples stick.
  • Serve instantly — poffertjes deflate as they cool.
