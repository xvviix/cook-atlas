Apfelstrudel

Austria

Viennese apple strudel is a feat of dough-craft: an elastic flour-water-oil dough rested, then hand-stretched over a floured cloth until transparent enough to read through — thin enough, legend says, to pull a love letter through. Rolled around cinnamon apples, butter-toasted breadcrumbs, rum raisins and walnuts, it bakes to a shattering, flaky log dusted with icing sugar and served with vanilla sauce or cream. The toasted breadcrumb layer is essential: it drinks the apple juices so the paper-thin pastry stays crisp.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 250 g flour + 130 ml lukewarm water + 2 tbsp oil + pinch of salt
  • Filling: 1 kg tart apples + 100 g toasted breadcrumbs + 80 g sugar + cinnamon + lemon
  • Rum raisins + walnuts + 100 g melted butter for brushing + icing sugar

Method

  1. Knead a smooth elastic dough 10 minutes; oil, cover and rest 30–45 minutes.
  2. Toast breadcrumbs in butter until golden; soak raisins in rum.
  3. Slice apples thin; toss with sugar, cinnamon, lemon, raisins and nuts.
  4. Stretch dough on a floured cloth to transparency; trim thick edges.
  5. Butter the sheet; scatter crumbs; spread apples; roll up using the cloth.
  6. Bake at 190 °C for 40–50 minutes, basting with butter; dust with icing sugar.

Chef tips

  • Rest the dough fully — unrested dough tears instead of stretching.
  • Stretch with the backs of your hands — fingertips poke holes.
  • Toasted crumbs are the moisture barrier — skip them and the base sogs.
  • Serve warm with vanilla sauce — cold strudel loses half its charm.
