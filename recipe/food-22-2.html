Lángos

Hungary

Hungary's fried-bread legend: yeasted potato (or plain) dough stretched thin and fried to puffed gold — brushed with garlic oil and loaded with sour cream and grated cheese. Crisp-blistered outside, chewy within; eat it hot over paper at the market.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 250 ml warm milk + yeast + salt + sugar (+ mashed potato optional)
  • Garlic oil: 5 garlic cloves + 100 ml oil
  • Sour cream + 200 g grated Trappista/Gouda; oil at 175 °C

Method

  1. Knead a soft dough; rise 1 hour to doubled.
  2. Divide into 6–8 balls; rest 15 min.
  3. Stretch each to 20 cm, edges thicker, center thin.
  4. Fry at 175 °C 1–2 min per side to puffed gold; drain.
  5. Brush garlic oil; spread sour cream; snow with cheese; eat instantly.

Chef tips

  • Thin centers puff and crisp; thick centers fry doughy.
  • Garlic oil first so it soaks; cream and cheese after.
  • Serve seconds from the oil — lángos dies lukewarm.
