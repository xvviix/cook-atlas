Plăcintă

Romania

Plăcintă cu brânză: Romania's cheese pie — hand-stretched (or puff/phyllo) pastry layered with salty telemea + fresh cow cheese, eggs and smântână — baked golden. Dobrogea's coiled version is the showpiece; eaten warm, any time of day.

Ingredients

  • Pastry: flour + egg + oil + water + vinegar (or puff/phyllo)
  • 500 g telemea + fresh cheese + eggs + smântână + dill
  • Butter for layering/brushing

Method

  1. Knead a soft elastic dough; rest.
  2. Mix cheeses + eggs + cream to thick-batter consistency.
  3. Stretch/roll sheets thin; butter each.
  4. Spread filling; roll/coil into the pan.
  5. Brush butter-egg-cream; bake 180 °C 30–40 min gold.

Chef tips

  • Rest the dough — relaxed dough stretches thin.
  • Salty + fresh cheese mix — all-telemea overpowers.
  • Butter every layer — dry layers turn leathery.
