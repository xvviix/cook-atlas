Štrukli

Croatia

Zagorje's beloved cheese parcels: strudel-thin hand-stretched dough spread with fresh cow's cheese, eggs and sour cream, rolled, cut with a plate rim, then boiled (the authentic way, with buttered crumbs) or baked golden with cream. Comfort in a dish.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 1 egg + oil + vinegar + lukewarm water; rest 30 min
  • Filling: 600 g fresh cheese + 3–4 eggs + sour cream + salt + melted butter
  • To finish: butter + cream (baked) or buttered breadcrumbs (boiled)

Method

  1. Knead a soft stretchy dough; rest oiled 30 minutes.
  2. Stretch paper-thin over a floured cloth; brush with butter.
  3. Spread the cheese filling; roll jelly-style using the cloth.
  4. Cut into pieces with a plate rim.
  5. Boil 10–15 min and serve with crumbs — or bake 200 °C 20–30 min with butter and cream.

Chef tips

  • Stretch, don't roll — translucency is the standard.
  • The plate-rim cut seals as it slices — knives drag.
  • Baked for crunch, boiled for tradition — both demand serving hot.
