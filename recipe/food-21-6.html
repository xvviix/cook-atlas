Badrijani Nigvzit

Georgia

Eggplant rolls with walnut-garlic paste: salted eggplant strips fried golden, spread with walnuts ground with garlic, cilantro, blue fenugreek, coriander and vinegar, rolled tight and crowned with pomegranate. The supra table's most elegant bite.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggplants, 5 mm lengthwise strips, salted 30 min
  • Paste: 200 g walnuts + garlic + cilantro + blue fenugreek + coriander + vinegar + salt + water
  • Oil for frying; pomegranate to garnish

Method

  1. Salt eggplant; rest 30 min; pat very dry.
  2. Fry strips golden both sides; drain on paper.
  3. Grind paste ingredients to a thick, spreadable cream.
  4. Spread each strip; roll tight; chill 1+ hour.
  5. Garnish with pomegranate; serve cool.

Chef tips

  • Dry slices fry golden; wet slices drink oil.
  • Chilling marries the flavors — serve cool, not warm.
  • Paste should spread, not crumble — add water by spoons.
