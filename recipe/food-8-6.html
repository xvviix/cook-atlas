Jiaozi

China

Northern China's boiled dumplings and Lunar New Year centerpiece: juicy pork-and-napa filling in wheat wrappers, sealed in crescents and boiled until they float. Mixed one direction for spring, dipped in black vinegar.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 300 g flour + 160 ml water + salt (or 40 round wrappers)
  • 250 g ground pork
  • 200 g napa cabbage, salted and squeezed dry
  • Scallion + ginger; soy + Shaoxing + sesame oil + white pepper
  • Dip: black vinegar + soy + chili oil

Method

  1. Rest the dough 30+ minutes until silky; roll 8–9 cm rounds, edges thin.
  2. Salt the cabbage 10 minutes, squeeze bone-dry, and mix with pork and seasonings — one direction until tacky.
  3. 1 tbsp filling per wrapper; wet the rim; fold and pleat shut, expelling air.
  4. Boil in plenty of water, stirring once to unstick; when they float, cook 2–3 minutes more.
  5. Lift out with a slotted spoon; serve steaming with dip.

Chef tips

  • One-direction mixing builds the juicy, springy bind — back-and-forth breaks it.
  • Wet filling bursts wrappers — the cabbage squeeze is sacred.
  • Freeze raw on floured trays, then bag; boil from frozen.
