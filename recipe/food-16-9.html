Cà phê sữa đá

Vietnam

Vietnamese iced coffee: dark robusta dripped slow through a phin filter directly onto sweetened condensed milk (a French-colonial legacy, born of no fresh milk in the tropics), stirred and poured over a tall glass of ice. Bitter, sweet, rocket fuel.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp coarsely ground Vietnamese robusta (Trung Nguyen-style)
  • 2–3 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • 120 ml water at 95 °C; tall glass of ice; phin filter

Method

  1. Add condensed milk to the glass; set the phin on top with coffee inside.
  2. Bloom with a splash of hot water 30 seconds; add the press plate.
  3. Pour the remaining water; lid on; drip 4–8 minutes.
  4. Stir the concentrate into the milk until uniform.
  5. Pour over ice; stir again; adjust milk to taste.

Chef tips

  • Slow drip = strong coffee; a flooding phin makes brown water.
  • Robusta's bitterness is the point — arabica tastes thin here.
  • Stir twice: once hot, once iced — milk sinks otherwise.
