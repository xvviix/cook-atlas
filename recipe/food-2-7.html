Pastitsio

Greece

Greek baked pasta ('Greek lasagna'): long macaroni layered with cinnamon-clove beef sauce and a thick nutmeg béchamel enriched with eggs and Kefalotyri cheese, baked until the top sets golden. A Sunday-table classic.

Ingredients

  • 500 g long pastitsio macaroni (or bucatini/penne)
  • 700 g minced beef; 2 red onions + 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste + 400 g tomatoes
  • 120 ml red wine
  • 2 bay leaves, cinnamon, clove, allspice
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper
  • Béchamel: 75 g butter, 75 g flour, 1 l milk, 2–3 eggs, 150 g grated Kefalotyri/Parmesan, nutmeg

Method

  1. Brown the beef with onion and garlic; add paste, wine, tomatoes and spices; simmer until very thick (30–40 minutes).
  2. Make the béchamel: roux + hot milk to a thick sauce; off the heat whisk in beaten eggs, most of the cheese and nutmeg.
  3. Boil the pasta very al dente, drain, and toss with a little butter and cheese.
  4. Layer in a 33×23 cm dish: pasta, then meat sauce, then béchamel smoothed level; top with remaining cheese.
  5. Bake at 175 °C (350 °F) 35–45 minutes until golden with set edges (center still slightly jiggly); rest 20+ minutes before slicing.

Chef tips

  • The meat sauce must be thick — watery sauce collapses the layers (stir in breadcrumbs if needed).
  • Room-temperature milk and eggs keep the béchamel lump-free.
  • Like moussaka, it slices cleanly only after a proper rest.
