Hortobágyi Palacsinta

Hungary

The savory crêpe invented for Expo '58: unsweetened palacsinta stuffed with minced veal pörkölt, ends tucked, baked under a paprika-sour cream sauce. Elegant, retro, and utterly Hungarian — the puszta on a plate, minus the puszta.

Ingredients

  • Crêpes: 4 eggs + 250 g flour + 500 ml milk + oil + salt (10–12)
  • Filling: 600 g veal pörkölt, meat minced + spoon of cream
  • Sauce: pörkölt juices + 500 ml sour cream + flour; parsley

Method

  1. Braise a veal pörkölt; lift meat; mince with a little cream.
  2. Fry thin unsweetened crêpes.
  3. Fill each with minced veal; tuck ends; roll; arrange in a buttered dish.
  4. Whisk juices with sour cream (+ flour); pour over.
  5. Bake 180 °C 10–12 min; parsley and extra cream to serve.

Chef tips

  • Unsweetened crêpes only — sugar would wreck the savory balance.
  • Tucked ends keep the filling in under the sauce.
  • Bake just to heat through — the components are already cooked.
