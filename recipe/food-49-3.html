Fanesca

Ecuador

Fanesca is Ecuador's once-a-year Easter soup — twelve grains and beans (the Apostles) with salt cod (bacalao, for Christ), pumpkin, peanut milk, cheese and cream, garnished with empanadas, avocado, egg and fried plantain. Made only during Holy Week, in vast pots, by families cooking together, it's arguably Ecuador's most symbolic dish. Desalting the cod properly (24+ hours, changed water) decides everything.

Ingredients

  • Bacalao, desalted + 12 grains/beans: lupini, favas, corn, peas, lentils, rice...
  • Pumpkin + peanut milk + cheese + cream + achiote + cumin
  • Empanadas + avocado + egg + plantain to garnish

Method

  1. Desalt cod 24 hours, changing water; poach; flake.
  2. Cook each grain/bean separately to tender.
  3. Simmer pumpkin into a base with achiote and cumin.
  4. Combine all with peanut milk; simmer to a thick potage.
  5. Enrich with cheese and cream; season.
  6. Serve loaded with all garnishes.

Chef tips

  • Desalt thoroughly — salty cod ruins the pot.
  • Cook grains separately — they finish at different times.
  • Thick potage, not broth — fanesca mounds.
  • Garnishes abundant — they're part of the dish.
