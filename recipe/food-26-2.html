Milanesa

Argentina

Milanesa a la napolitana (invented in Buenos Aires, not Naples): beef cutlets breaded and fried crisp, then topped like a pizza with ham, tomato sauce and mozzarella and broiled bubbling. Served with fries — Argentina's beloved Italo-porteño hybrid.

Ingredients

  • 4 beef cutlets (~150 g each), pounded even
  • Breading: eggs + parsley + garlic + breadcrumbs; rest breaded 1+ hr
  • Topping: ham + tomato sauce + mozzarella; oil at 170 °C; fries

Method

  1. Season cutlets; egg-dip; crumb firmly; chill 1+ hr (adhesion).
  2. Fry at 170 °C 4–6 min per side to deep gold; drain.
  3. Top each with ham, sauce, mozzarella.
  4. Broil 5–9 min to bubbling and spotted.
  5. Serve with fries and salad.

Chef tips

  • Chill after breading — the crust stays on.
  • Don't drown in sauce — soggy milanesa is tragedy.
  • Pound even — thick spots fry raw.
