Humita

Argentina

Northern Argentina's corn parcels: fresh corn grated to a coarse cream, cooked with onion, capsicum and paprika, enriched with cheese — wrapped in corn husks, tied, and steamed to set. Sweet, milky, earthy; unwrapped at the table like a gift.

Ingredients

  • 6 large corn cobs (husks reserved whole)
  • Onion + red capsicum + paprika + sugar
  • 100 ml milk + 100 g melting cheese + cornmeal if wet; string

Method

  1. Peel husks whole; rinse; cut kernels off.
  2. Process kernels coarse (not purée).
  3. Sauté onion-capsicum; paprika in; add corn + sugar; cook 10–15 min thick.
  4. Off-heat: milk + cheese; cool 15 min.
  5. Wrap 2–3 tbsp in doubled husks; tie; steam 45–50 min upright.

Chef tips

  • Coarse grind — purée turns gummy.
  • Thicken before wrapping — wet filling leaks.
  • Steam upright — lying parcels unfold.
