Bastilla

Morocco

Listed here as the seafood bastilla (pastilla's coastal sibling; see food-30-2 for chicken): shrimp, calamari and firm fish folded with vermicelli, mushrooms and charmoula-tomato sauce in buttered warqa — baked golden, crowned with shrimp. Savory, not sweet — no sugar dusting.

Ingredients

  • 900 g shrimp + 500 g calamari + 500 g firm fish
  • Vermicelli (soaked, cut) + black mushrooms + tomato-charmoula sauce
  • Warqa/filo + butter; cheese optional; parsley + lemon

Method

  1. Sear each seafood separately; reserve all juices.
  2. Cook vermicelli + mushrooms in the juices + tomato sauce to absorbed.
  3. Fold in seafood + herbs; season boldly.
  4. Assemble in buttered warqa; seal; egg-wash shine.
  5. Bake 180 °C 30–40 min to gold; garnish shrimp + parsley.

Chef tips

  • Sear separately — mixed seafood overcooks unevenly.
  • Reserve every drop of juice — it's the vermicelli's flavor.
  • Season boldly — bland filling sinks the dish.
