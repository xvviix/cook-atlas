Appeltaart

Netherlands

Dutch appeltaart is the tall, deep, lattice-topped apple pie — buttery shortcrust (often with a hint of lemon and cinnamon in the dough), packed with tart Goudreinette or Elstar apples, raisins, cinnamon and a breadcrumb or custard-powder layer that drinks the juices, finished with a woven lattice and served with lightly whipped cream. Deep-dish and generous, it's the centerpiece of Dutch coffee time (koffietijd). Tart apples only — sweet apples bake to mush without character.

Ingredients

  • Crust: 300 g flour + 200 g butter + 150 g sugar + egg + lemon zest + cinnamon
  • Filling: 1.5 kg tart apples + raisins + sugar + cinnamon + breadcrumbs
  • Egg wash + whipped cream to serve

Method

  1. Rub butter into flour; bind with sugar, egg and zest; chill 1 hour.
  2. Line a deep springform with two-thirds of the dough.
  3. Dust the base with breadcrumbs; pack in spiced apples and raisins.
  4. Weave the lattice; seal edges; egg-wash.
  5. Bake at 175–180 °C for 60–75 minutes until deep gold.
  6. Cool fully before slicing; serve with whipped cream.

Chef tips

  • Tart apples — Goudreinette ideal, Granny Smith fine.
  • Breadcrumb base — it drinks the juice flood.
  • Cool fully — hot appeltaart collapses when cut.
  • Deep form — appeltaart is tall by law of tradition.
