Sashimi

Japan

Raw fish sliced to order: the craft is entirely selection, temperature and knife work. Sushi-grade fish (frozen for parasite destruction), a single-bevel yanagiba, and single-pull cuts — hira-zukuri slices 6–8 mm thick.

Ingredients

  • Sushi-grade tuna, salmon or hamachi loin
  • Yanagiba or sharpest long knife; tweezers; chilled plates
  • Shredded daikon + shiso leaves; soy sauce; fresh wasabi

Method

  1. Thaw frozen fish overnight in the fridge — never at room temperature; keep everything cold until slicing.
  2. Pat dry (don't rinse); remove skin in one pull and pluck pin bones with tweezers.
  3. Square the loin; slice hira-zukuri at 45° in single pulls toward you, 6–8 mm thick.
  4. Wipe the blade between cuts; arrange in overlapping fans on chilled plates.
  5. Serve immediately with soy and wasabi on the side.

Chef tips

  • Single strokes only — sawing tears cells and dulls flavor and sheen.
  • Slice just before serving; pre-cut sashimi oxidizes within the hour.
  • When in doubt about provenance, don't serve it raw.
