Potjiekos

South Africa

Small-pot food over fire: meat browned in the oiled potjie, vegetables layered on top (hard below, soft above), a little wine/stock around the sides — lidded and left 2–4 hours on coals. Never stirred; the layers steam into each other while everyone talks around the fire.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg lamb/beef/chicken, chunks
  • Layered veg: onion + carrot + potato + butternut + beans + mushrooms
  • Stock + wine + potjie spice + herbs; rice/bread to serve

Method

  1. Heat potjie on coals; oil; brown meat; set aside.
  2. Soften onions; return meat.
  3. Layer veg hard-to-soft; season layers.
  4. Liquid around sides (not covering); lid on.
  5. Simmer 2–3 hrs untouched; serve with starch.

Chef tips

  • Never stir — stirring is potjie sacrilege.
  • Little liquid — steam does the work.
  • Manage coals — fierce heat burns the bottom.
