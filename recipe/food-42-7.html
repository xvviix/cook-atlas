Baghali Polo

Iran

Baghali polo — dill-and-fava rice — is springtime Persia on a plate: basmati layered with masses of fresh dill and tender fava beans, steamed with saffron and butter, traditionally served with lamb shank (mahicheh). The dill must be fresh and abundant — dried dill is a different, dusty dish — and the favas peeled twice (pod, then skin) for emerald tenderness. Elegant, fragrant and deceptively simple.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups basmati + 2 cups shelled favas (peeled) + 2 bunches fresh dill
  • Saffron + butter + salt
  • Lamb shanks braised with onion and turmeric to serve

Method

  1. Double-peel the favas: pod, blanch, slip the skins.
  2. Parboil rice; layer with dill and favas in the pot.
  3. Drizzle saffron and butter; steam under a towel-lidded lid 45 minutes.
  4. Braise lamb shanks separately until falling off the bone.
  5. Fluff gently; serve rice mounded with shanks and their juices.

Chef tips

  • Fresh dill in bulk — this is not the dish for dried.
  • Double-peel favas — skins are bitter and tough.
  • Towel lid again — Persian rice rules apply.
  • Gentle fluffing — favas crush under rough handling.
