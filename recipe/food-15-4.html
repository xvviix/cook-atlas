Mac and Cheese

United States

The blonde-roux stovetop classic, baked to finish: sharp cheddar for tang plus a melty partner (Monterey Jack), mustard powder and cayenne humming underneath, crowned with buttery panko and baked to bubbling gold. Creamy inside, crisp on top.

Ingredients

  • 500 g elbow macaroni, cooked 1 min shy
  • Roux: 60 g butter + 60 g flour, cooked blonde 1–2 min
  • 750 ml–1 l warm whole milk + 250 ml cream
  • 300 g sharp cheddar + 200 g Monterey Jack, hand-shredded
  • Mustard powder + garlic powder + cayenne + salt; panko + butter topping

Method

  1. Cook pasta shy of done; it finishes in the sauce and oven.
  2. Build the roux; whisk in warm milk gradually to a thick béchamel.
  3. Off the heat, melt in the cheeses a handful at a time; season.
  4. Fold in pasta; pour into a buttered dish; top with panko + butter + extra cheese.
  5. Bake at 190 °C 20–25 minutes to gold and bubbling; rest 10.

Chef tips

  • Hand-shredded cheese melts smooth; bagged shreds turn grainy.
  • Off-heat melting prevents the broken, oily sauce.
  • Mustard powder is the quiet essential — never skip it.
