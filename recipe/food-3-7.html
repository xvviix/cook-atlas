Arroz con Pollo

Peru

Peru's green chicken-and-rice: bone-in chicken seared then braised with cilantro-spinach purée, ají amarillo, beer (traditionally chicha de jora) and cumin, the rice cooking in the same pot until fluffy and emerald. Finished with peas, peppers and salsa criolla.

Ingredients

  • 700 g bone-in chicken thighs
  • 350 g long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 big bunch cilantro + handful of spinach, blended with 120 ml stock
  • 1 onion + 3 garlic cloves; 2–3 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 250 ml dark beer or chicha de jora; 500 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tsp cumin; 1 red pepper, carrots, peas
  • Oil, salt, pepper; salsa criolla to serve

Method

  1. Sear the seasoned chicken golden on both sides; set aside (keep the fond — it's the flavor base).
  2. Sweat onion, garlic, ají paste and cumin; stir in the green purée and cook until dark green.
  3. Add beer, reduce by half, then stock; return the chicken, cover and braise ~10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the rice, cover and cook on low ~20 minutes; scatter peppers, carrots and peas over the top for the last 5 minutes.
  5. Rest 10 minutes off the heat, fluff, return the chicken, and serve with salsa criolla.

Chef tips

  • Spinach in the purée keeps the rice vivid green — cilantro alone fades to khaki.
  • Rinse the rice so grains stay separate, and don't lift the lid while steaming.
  • The seared fond + beer reduction is the difference between good and great.
