Sopaipillas

Chile

Chile's pumpkin fry-bread: zapallo-kneaded dough rolled thin, pricked, cut in rounds and fried to puffed gold — eaten savory with pebre, or passed through chancaca syrup (sopaipillas pasadas) for rainy-day sweetness. Winter's smell in Chile.

Ingredients

  • 250 g cooked zapallo/pumpkin, mashed
  • 500 g flour + butter + salt + milk
  • Oil at 175 °C; (chancaca + cinnamon + orange for pasadas)

Method

  1. Knead pumpkin + flour + butter to a soft dough.
  2. Roll 5 mm; cut rounds; prick all over.
  3. Fry at 175 °C to puffed gold; drain.
  4. Serve hot with pebre — or simmer briefly in chancaca syrup.
  5. Eat the same day — they toughen overnight.

Chef tips

  • Prick well — unpricked sopaipillas balloon and burn.
  • Hot oil, quick fry — cool oil greases.
  • Zapallo gives color and sweetness — don't substitute plain.
