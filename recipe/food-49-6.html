Fritada

Ecuador

Fritada de chancho: pork braised in its own fat with garlic, cumin, onion and chicha/beer until tender, then fried down until the edges crisp — served with mote, tostado (toasted corn), llapingachos, plantain and curtido. Where hornado roasts, fritada confits-then-crisps: melting within, golden without. The two-stage cook (braise, then crisp in rendered fat) is the technique.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg pork shoulder, chunked + garlic + cumin + onion
  • Chicha/beer + water + salt
  • Mote + tostado + llapingachos + plantain + curtido to serve

Method

  1. Marinate pork with garlic, cumin and salt.
  2. Braise in chicha/water 1.5–2 hours until tender.
  3. Uncover; reduce; fry in rendered fat until crisp-edged.
  4. Serve piled with mote, tostado, llapingachos and curtido.

Chef tips

  • Two stages — braise tender, then crisp.
  • Render, don't add oil — the pork fries in its own fat.
  • Crisp edges, melting centers — that's fritada.
  • Curtido cuts richness — always serve it.
