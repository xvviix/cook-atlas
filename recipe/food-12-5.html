Ajvar

Serbia

'Serbian vegetarian caviar': sweet red roga/kapia peppers roasted, peeled and ground with eggplant, then cooked down with oil to a thick, smoky-sweet spread. Autumn's giant batch, jarred for winter — mild (blagi) or hot (ljuti).

Ingredients

  • 5 kg sweet red peppers (roga/kapia/shepherd) + 1 kg eggplant
  • 200–400 ml sunflower oil
  • Salt + sugar + splash of 9% vinegar
  • (Ljuti: + hot chiles)

Method

  1. Roast peppers and eggplant until collapsed and charred; steam covered 20 minutes; peel and seed.
  2. Drain well — even overnight — wet pulp makes thin ajvar.
  3. Grind to rustic-smooth.
  4. Cook down in hot oil 1–2 hours, stirring, until thick enough to part before the spoon.
  5. Season with salt, sugar and vinegar; jar hot and seal; or refrigerate up to weeks.

Chef tips

  • Roga-type peppers make thicker, sweeter ajvar than bell peppers.
  • Drainage decides texture — impatient ajvar stays soupy.
  • The spoon-parting test: the trail should hold before closing.
