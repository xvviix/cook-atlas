Tortilla Española

Spain

Spain's thick potato omelette: sliced potatoes (and onion — the eternal debate) gently confited in olive oil, mixed with beaten egg, and flipped to a golden cake. Served warm or at room temperature, jugosa (runny) or cuajada (set).

Ingredients

  • 3 medium waxy potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced (optional but classic)
  • 6 large eggs, beaten
  • 250 ml+ extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Confit potato and onion submerged in olive oil on low heat 20–30 minutes until tender but not browned; drain (save the oil).
  2. Mix with beaten eggs and salt; rest 15 minutes so the potato drinks up egg.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high; pour in the mixture and settle.
  4. Cook 4–5 minutes, tucking the edges with a spatula; flip onto a plate and slide back.
  5. Cook 2–4 minutes more (less for jugosa, more for cuajada); rest 10 minutes before serving.

Chef tips

  • Low, slow confit — frying crisps the potato and ruins the texture.
  • The rest before cooking binds everything; skipping it causes breakage.
  • Room-temperature tortilla beats fridge-cold every time.
