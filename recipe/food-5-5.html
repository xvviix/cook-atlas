Croquetas

Spain

Listed here as the classic croquetas de jamón: a firm, nutmeg-scented béchamel loaded with Serrano ham, chilled overnight, shaped into torpedos, breaded and fried to molten-centered gold. Spain's greatest tapa-technicians are judged on these.

Ingredients

  • 60 g butter + splash of oil; 1 small onion, minced
  • 80 g flour
  • 600 ml hot whole milk
  • 100 g Serrano ham, finely minced
  • Nutmeg, salt, white pepper
  • Breading: flour + beaten egg + fine breadcrumbs; oil for frying

Method

  1. Sweat the onion soft in butter-oil; stir in flour and cook 1–2 minutes past the raw smell.
  2. Whisk in hot milk gradually to a thick, paste-like béchamel that pulls from the pan.
  3. Fold in ham, nutmeg and seasoning; simmer briefly, then spread on a tray and chill 4+ hours, ideally overnight.
  4. Shape into torpedos; roll flour → egg → breadcrumbs, sealing completely.
  5. Fry at 175 °C in small batches 2–3 minutes until deep gold; drain and serve hot.

Chef tips

  • The chill is structural — soft béchamel explodes in the fryer.
  • Any gap in the breading becomes a leak; double-check the seal.
  • Fry few at a time; each addition drops the oil temperature.
