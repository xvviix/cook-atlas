Dulce de Leche

Argentina

Argentina's milk caramel: milk and sugar (plus a pinch of soda against curdling) simmered 2–3 hours, stirred patiently, to a deep amber, spoon-coating spread. The soul of alfajores, pancakes and everything sweet — homemade beats canned, always.

Ingredients

  • 1 l whole milk + 300 g sugar + ¼ tsp baking soda + vanilla

Method

  1. Combine milk, sugar, soda in a heavy wide pot.
  2. Bring to a bare simmer; stir to dissolve.
  3. Simmer 2–3 hrs low, stirring increasingly often.
  4. It's done at deep amber, coating the spoon thickly.
  5. Vanilla in; cool (it thickens); jar; refrigerate.

Chef tips

  • Low heat, no rushing — scorched milk is ruined milk.
  • Wide heavy pot — thin pots crystallize and burn.
  • Stir more as it thickens — the last 30 min need attention.
