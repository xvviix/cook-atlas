Tanjia

Morocco

Marrakech's bachelor stew: beef shank sealed in a clay urn with garlic, cumin, saffron, smen, preserved lemon and olive oil — buried in hammam ashes for 6–8 hours until collapsing. At home: low oven or coals. No water added; the meat makes its own liquor.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg beef shank/neck, large bone-in chunks
  • 8 garlic cloves + cumin + saffron + smen + preserved lemon + olive oil + salt
  • Parchment + string to seal the urn/pot

Method

  1. Load meat + all seasonings into the tanjia; mix.
  2. Seal with parchment tied tight (few steam slits).
  3. Bury in warm (not red-hot) ashes — or 140 °C oven — 6–8 hrs. Don't open.
  4. Shake once midway if accessible.
  5. Pour out tableside; serve with bread.

Chef tips

  • Warm ashes, not inferno — fierce heat burns before tenderizing.
  • Never open mid-cook — escaping steam stalls it.
  • Large bone-in chunks — small pieces disintegrate.
