Goulash

Czech Republic

Hovězí guláš: the Czech pub bowl — beef braised with an equal weight of dark-caramelized onions, paprika, caraway, marjoram and garlic, thickened (sometimes with a Pilsner splash) to a dense mahogany gravy. Served with knedlíky and raw onion rings; beer alongside is law.

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg beef chuck + 700 g onions, chopped
  • Lard + paprika + caraway + marjoram + garlic + tomato paste
  • Stock + Pilsner; flour to finish; knedlíky + onion rings

Method

  1. Caramelize onions dark gold in lard (20+ min).
  2. Paprika + caraway in; beef in; sear.
  3. Stock + beer; simmer 2 hrs to tender.
  4. Mash some onion into the sauce; thicken if needed.
  5. Serve with dumplings, onion rings, parsley, beer.

Chef tips

  • Dark onions = dark flavor — pale onions, pale guláš.
  • Marjoram at the end — its perfume fades with boiling.
  • Thick enough to mound — thin guláš is soup.
