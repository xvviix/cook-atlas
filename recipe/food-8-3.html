Kung Pao Chicken

China

Sichuan's gongbao jiding: wok-seared chicken with dried chilies, numbing peppercorns and peanuts in a lychee-profile sweet-sour-savory glaze. Fast, fiery, and balanced — nothing like gloopy takeout versions.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken thigh, 2 cm cubes
  • Marinade: Shaoxing + light soy + cornstarch
  • 8–12 dried facing-heaven chilies + 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • Sauce: black vinegar + sugar + soy + sesame oil + water + starch
  • Ginger + garlic + scallion whites; 80 g roasted peanuts; oil

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken 15–30 minutes; pre-mix the sauce — the wok waits for nothing.
  2. Sear the chicken hard in a smoking wok; remove.
  3. Bloom chilies and peppercorns in fresh oil seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add aromatics, return chicken, pour sauce around the rim; toss 30 seconds to a lustrous glaze.
  5. Kill the heat, fold in peanuts, and plate over rice.

Chef tips

  • Peppercorns go in whole and brief — burnt ones turn bitter, not numbing.
  • Peanuts last, always — early peanuts go soft.
  • Mise en place is mandatory; the cooking takes three minutes.
