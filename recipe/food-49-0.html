Ceviche

Ecuador

Ecuadorian ceviche breaks the raw-fish rule: shrimp (or fish, octopus, chochos) briefly poached, then marinated in lime and bitter-orange juice with red onion, tomato and cilantro — often with a spoon of ketchup and mustard whisked in, coastal-style — served cold with popcorn, plantain chips (chifles) and corn nuts. Soupier and tangier-sweet than Peruvian ceviche, it's beach food: bright, chilled, eaten with a spoon. The seafood must be just-cooked, never rubbery.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg shrimp, poached just pink + lime + bitter-orange juice
  • Red onion + tomato + cilantro + ají + salt
  • Ketchup + mustard (coastal style) + popcorn + chifles to serve

Method

  1. Poach shrimp 2–3 minutes until just pink; chill fast.
  2. Slice onion feather-thin; rinse to tame.
  3. Whisk citrus, ketchup, mustard and salt into leche de tigre-style marinade.
  4. Combine seafood, onion, tomato and cilantro; marinate chilled 30+ minutes.
  5. Serve ice-cold with popcorn, chifles and corn nuts.

Chef tips

  • Just-cooked seafood — overcooking makes erasers.
  • Chill everything — warm ceviche is a food-safety risk.
  • Bitter orange if possible — its perfume is coastal Ecuador.
  • Popcorn on top — the classic crunch.
