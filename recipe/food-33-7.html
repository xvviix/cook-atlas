Knedlíky

Czech Republic

Houskové knedlíky: Czech bread dumplings — yeasted dough studded with stale-roll cubes, shaped into loaves, boiled or steamed, sliced with thread. The sauce-mop under svíčková, guláš and vepřo knedlo zelo; spongy, mild, essential.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + yeast + 300 ml milk + 1 egg + salt
  • 150 g stale rolls, cubed

Method

  1. Activate yeast; knead a soft dough.
  2. Fold in bread cubes; rise 45–60 min.
  3. Shape 2 loaves; rise 15 min.
  4. Boil/steam 18–20 min (turn once if boiling).
  5. Slice hot with thread; serve with saucy mains.

Chef tips

  • Stale cubes hold shape — fresh bread dissolves.
  • Don't peek while steaming — they collapse.
  • Slice with thread — knives compress.
