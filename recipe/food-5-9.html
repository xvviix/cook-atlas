Gambas al Ajillo

Spain

Sizzling garlic shrimp in olive oil: a cazuela of fruity oil infused with slivered garlic and chili, shrimp flashed just until pink, finished with sherry and parsley — delivered still bubbling, with bread for the oil.

Ingredients

  • 700 g large shrimp, peeled (shells saved for the oil)
  • 12–16 garlic cloves (minced + sliced)
  • 120–250 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 dried chili or guindilla + pinch of flakes
  • 2 tbsp dry sherry or sherry vinegar
  • Parsley, flaky salt, crusty bread

Method

  1. Salt the shrimp 10 minutes ahead (with a pinch of baking soda for snap, optional).
  2. Gently warm the sliced garlic and chili in the oil until fragrant and just blond — never brown.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook 1–2 minutes per side until C-shaped and just opaque.
  4. Splash in the sherry, shower with parsley, and carry the bubbling cazuela straight to the table.
  5. Mop every drop of the garlic-chili oil with bread.

Chef tips

  • Garlic has two stages: gentle infusion first, fierce shrimp sear second.
  • C-shape = juicy; O-shape = overcooked and rubbery.
  • Have bread ready before the shrimp hit the pan — this waits for no one.
