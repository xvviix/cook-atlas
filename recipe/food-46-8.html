Dolma

Algeria

Algerian dolma (dolma batata, qraa) stuffs potatoes, zucchini, peppers and tomatoes with cinnamon-scented minced beef and rice, braised in a light tomato-chickpea broth until the vegetables surrender and the sauce thickens. Friday-family food, gentler and sweeter-spiced than Levantine versions — cinnamon and a pinch of sugar define it. Hollow generously but leave sturdy walls; over-thin vegetables collapse in the braise.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes + zucchini + peppers + tomatoes for stuffing
  • 500 g minced beef + ½ cup rice + onion + cinnamon + parsley
  • Tomato-chickpea broth + pinch of sugar + mint

Method

  1. Hollow vegetables, leaving 1 cm walls; salt the cavities.
  2. Mix raw mince with rice, onion, cinnamon and parsley.
  3. Stuff loosely (rice swells); cap with tomato slices.
  4. Nestle in a pot; pour over tomato-chickpea broth.
  5. Braise covered 45–55 minutes; rest; serve with sauce and mint.

Chef tips

  • Sturdy walls — paper-thin shells collapse.
  • Stuff loosely — swelling rice bursts tight cases.
  • Cinnamon + sugar pinch — the Algerian signature.
  • Rest before serving — the rice finishes setting.
