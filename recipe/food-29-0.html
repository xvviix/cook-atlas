Nasi Lemak

Malaysia

Malaysia's national dish: coconut-pandan rice with fiery-sweet sambal, crisp ikan bilis, roasted peanuts, cucumber and egg — traditionally wrapped in banana leaf. The sambal decides everything; the rice must be fragrant, never greasy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice + 1 cup coconut milk + water + pandan + ginger + salt
  • Sambal: dried chiles + shallot + garlic + belacan + tamarind + sugar
  • Ikan bilis + peanuts (fried crisp) + cucumber + eggs

Method

  1. Cook coconut rice with pandan; rest 10 min; fluff.
  2. Blend and fry the sambal low 15–20 min to deep red and oily (pecah minyak).
  3. Fry ikan bilis and peanuts crisp; boil eggs.
  4. Slice cucumber.
  5. Plate rice + sambal + bilis + peanuts + egg + cucumber (banana leaf if you can).

Chef tips

  • Pecah minyak (oil splitting) = sambal done; pale sambal is raw.
  • Balance sweet-sour-salty in the sambal before serving.
  • Crisp bilis at the last — they soften fast.
