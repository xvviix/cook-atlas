Knafeh Nabulsiyeh

Palestine

Nablus's gift to the dessert world: knafeh na'ameh made with the city's trademark white, firm, salty Nabulsi cheese (desalted through repeated soaking), encased in fine-ground semolina-kataifi dough tinted sunset-orange, baked in ghee and soaked in ater syrup. Nablus's sweet-shops bake it in giant round trays, and no Palestinian celebration is complete without it. Outside Palestine, a mozzarella-ricotta-feta blend approximates the salty-stretchy Nabulsi character.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fine knafeh dough (or blitzed kataifi) + 200 g ghee + orange knafeh dye
  • 700 g Nabulsi cheese, desalted (or mozzarella + ricotta + feta blend)
  • Ater: 400 g sugar + water + lemon + orange blossom; blitzed pistachios

Method

  1. Desalt Nabulsi cheese with repeated soakings until only faintly salty; shred and dry.
  2. Rub ghee and dye through the dough; press firmly into the pan.
  3. Spread cheese evenly; cap with remaining dough; press again.
  4. Bake at 190–200 °C until the edges are deep orange-gold and cheese bubbles.
  5. Flip; flood with warm ater; shower with pistachios; serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Desalting is the make-or-break step — taste the cheese before assembling.
  • Dry the cheese thoroughly — moisture is the enemy of crispness.
  • Serve the day it's made — reheated knafeh never fully recovers.
  • The orange tint is traditional Nablusi identity — embrace it.
