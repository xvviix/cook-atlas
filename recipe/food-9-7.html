Escargots

France

Escargots de Bourgogne: snails baked in their shells (or plates) under parsley-garlic butter until bubbling — the butter is the dish, the snail its vehicle, the baguette its destiny.

Ingredients

  • 24 canned snails + shells (or escargot plates)
  • 125 g soft butter + 4 garlic cloves + 1 shallot
  • Big handful of parsley (+ chives); salt, pepper
  • Splash of white wine; Dijon dab (optional); baguette

Method

  1. Rinse the snails and pat completely dry so the butter clings.
  2. Blitz butter with garlic, shallot, herbs, wine and seasoning to a smooth compound.
  3. Plug each shell with butter, add a snail, cap with more butter; arrange opening-up.
  4. Bake at 200 °C 8–12 minutes until furiously bubbling.
  5. Serve sizzling with baguette for the garlic butter.

Chef tips

  • Dry snails, always — wet ones repel the butter.
  • Don't ration the butter; it's the entire point.
  • Special plates or a bed of salt keep shells upright.
