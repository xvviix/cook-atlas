Halifax Donair

Canada

Halifax's official food: spiced beef (cayenne, oregano, garlic, onion powder) kneaded to a dense, sliceable loaf, roasted on a spit, shaved and crisped, then wrapped in pita with tomatoes, onions and a flood of sweet garlic-condensed-milk sauce. Invented in the 1970s for Atlantic Canadian tastes, it's messy, sweet-garlicky and fiercely loved — now with its own festival and a sibling dish, the donair egg roll.

Ingredients

  • Loaf: 1 kg ground beef + cayenne + oregano + garlic + onion powder + salt
  • Donair sauce: condensed milk + vinegar + garlic powder + sugar
  • Pita + tomatoes + onions (+ cheese for pizza-style)

Method

  1. Knead the spiced beef hard (stand mixer 5+ minutes) until dense and tacky.
  2. Form a tight loaf; roast at 175 °C to 70 °C+ internal; rest.
  3. Slice thin; crisp the slices on a hot griddle.
  4. Steam the pita over the meat.
  5. Load with meat, tomatoes and onions; drown in donair sauce; wrap.

Chef tips

  • Knead hard — the dense texture is the whole game.
  • Rest before slicing — hot loaf crumbles.
  • Crisp the shavings — floppy slices are sad slices.
  • Sauce generously — a dry donair is a tragedy.
