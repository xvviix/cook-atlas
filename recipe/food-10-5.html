Nasi Padang

Indonesia

Not one recipe but a Minangkabau feast architecture: steamed rice surrounded by small plates of rendang, gulai, ayam pop, dendeng, sambal ijo/merah, cassava leaves and perkedel — traditionally served all-at-once, and you pay for what you touch.

Ingredients

  • Steamed jasmine rice (slightly dry so curries don't pool)
  • Mains: beef rendang + ayam pop (coconut-poached chicken) + dendeng balado
  • Curries: gulai cubadak (jackfruit) + gulai tunjang (tendon)
  • Sambal ijo + sambal merah; cassava leaves in coconut milk; perkedel; fried shallots; lime

Method

  1. Prepare each component in advance — a Nasi Padang is fridge-stocked dishes, warmed to order.
  2. Warm everything gently in separate small pots, never the microwave.
  3. Mound rice on the plate; arrange 3–5 small dishes around and over it.
  4. Rendang, ayam pop and both sambals are the non-negotiables; the rest rotate.
  5. Shower with fried shallots; serve lime wedges for the rendang and dendeng.

Chef tips

  • Cook components across days — no one makes a full Padang spread in one session.
  • Gentle reheating in their own oils beats fresh-but-rushed.
  • Eat with the right hand, mixing rice with each curry as you go.
