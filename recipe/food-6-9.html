Gyoza

Japan

Pan-fried crescent dumplings: pork-and-cabbage filling in thin wheat wrappers, pleated shut and cooked fry-steam-fry for crisp lace bottoms and juicy centers. Served with soy-vinegar-chili dip.

Ingredients

  • 30 round gyoza wrappers
  • 250 g ground pork (80% lean)
  • 250 g napa cabbage, salted 10 min and squeezed bone-dry
  • 2 scallions + 2 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy + 1 tbsp sesame oil + pepper
  • Dip: 2:1 soy to rice vinegar + chili oil

Method

  1. Chill the mixed filling 10 minutes — cold filling seals cleaner.
  2. 1 tsp filling per wrapper; wet the edge, fold, and pleat 5–6 folds on one side; seal airtight.
  3. Film a nonstick pan with oil; stand gyoza flat-side down and fry 2–3 minutes to gold.
  4. Add 80 ml water, lid on instantly, steam 4–5 minutes until wrappers turn translucent.
  5. Uncover, re-crisp 30–60 seconds, invert onto a plate and serve with dip.

Chef tips

  • Squeeze the cabbage ruthlessly — wet filling bursts the wrappers.
  • Don't overfill; 1 teaspoon is the discipline.
  • A tight lid and a timer beat peeking — steam does the work.
