Pad Kra Pao

Thailand

Thai holy-basil stir-fry: garlic and chiles pounded, ground chicken (or pork) seared hard, glazed with oyster-fish-soy sauces and sugar, finished with a wilting handful of peppery holy basil — over rice with a crisp-edged fried egg. Fifteen minutes, maximum fire.

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground chicken/pork
  • Garlic + bird chiles pounded; shallot (optional)
  • Oyster + fish sauce + Golden Mountain/soy + dark soy + sugar
  • Big handful holy basil; rice + fried eggs + prik nam pla

Method

  1. Pound garlic + chiles; sear hard 30 seconds.
  2. Add meat; press and sear — brown, don't steam.
  3. Sauces + sugar + splash of stock; toss to glaze.
  4. Holy basil in; 15 seconds off-heat to wilt.
  5. Serve over rice with a crispy fried egg and prik nam pla.

Chef tips

  • Holy basil (kra pao), not Thai/Italian — peppery and distinct.
  • High heat throughout — grey steamed meat is failure.
  • Basil last, seconds only — it dies fast.
