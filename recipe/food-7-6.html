Mantı

Türkiye

Kayseri's tiny dumplings — tradition says forty fit on a spoon: pea-sized lamb parcels in pasta-like dough, boiled and served over garlic yogurt with brown butter, pul biber and mint. Labor-intensive, legendary.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 300 g flour + 2 eggs + 80 ml water + salt
  • Filling: 300 g lamb + grated onion + parsley + salt + pepper
  • Sauce 1: 400 g yogurt + 2 garlic cloves + salt
  • Sauce 2: butter + pul biber/Aleppo + dried mint
  • (Optional: caramelized tomato sauce)

Method

  1. Knead a firm smooth dough; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Roll very thin; cut 3 cm squares; pea of filling on each; pinch four corners together.
  3. Boil in salted water (or light broth) 8–10 minutes until they float and turn tender.
  4. Spread yogurt on the serving dish; ladle mantı over.
  5. Pour foaming spiced butter over; dust with sumac and mint; serve instantly.

Chef tips

  • Smaller is more skilled — and cooks more evenly.
  • Seal firmly; open mantı leak their filling into the pot.
  • Serve the second it's assembled — the dough drinks the yogurt fast.
