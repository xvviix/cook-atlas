Kimchi

Korea

Korea's soul ferment: napa cabbage salted to crisp-tender, coated leaf-by-leaf in a gochugaru-garlic-ginger-fish sauce paste with daikon and scallion, then lacto-fermented to tangy, funky, alive. Days for fresh crunch, weeks for deep sour — both correct, different dishes.

Ingredients

  • 1 napa cabbage, quartered; ¼ cup non-iodized salt
  • Paste: ¼ cup gochugaru + 6 garlic + ginger + 2 tbsp fish sauce + sugar + rice porridge (optional binder)
  • Daikon + carrot julienne + scallions

Method

  1. Salt the cabbage 4–6 hours (or 2-hour brine), turning; rinse 3+ times; drain.
  2. Mix the paste with daikon, carrot and scallion.
  3. Glove up; coat every leaf layer thoroughly.
  4. Pack tight into jars, pressing out air; leave headspace.
  5. Ferment room-temp 1–3 days (burp daily), then refrigerate; eat from day 3, peak at weeks.

Chef tips

  • Non-iodized salt only — iodine inhibits fermentation.
  • Rinse well or the finish is harshly salty.
  • Press and submerge — exposed cabbage molds instead of fermenting.
