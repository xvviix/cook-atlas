Tamales

Mexico

Tamales rojos de puerco: chile-braised pork wrapped in lard-whipped masa, folded in corn husks and steamed upright until the masa pulls clean from the husk. A two-day, all-hands ritual — the pork broth seasons both filling and dough.

Ingredients

  • Filling: 1 kg pork shoulder + guajillo/ancho chile sauce + garlic + cumin
  • Masa: 500 g masa harina + 250 g whipped lard + baking powder + salt + warm pork broth + ½ cup chile sauce
  • 30+ corn husks, soaked soft

Method

  1. Day 1: simmer pork with onion, garlic and bay until shreddable; save every drop of broth.
  2. Blend the chile sauce; simmer the shredded pork in it to a thick, juicy filling; cool.
  3. Whip lard fluffy; beat in masa harina, baking powder, salt, broth and chile sauce — it should float a tester in water.
  4. Spread masa on each husk, add filling, fold sides then bottom up.
  5. Stand upright in a steamer (not touching the water); steam 70–90 minutes; rest 15 before opening.

Chef tips

  • The float test never lies — sinking masa makes leaden tamales.
  • Reserve chile sauce for the masa — pale masa is bland masa.
  • Rest before judging; hot-from-steamer masa always seems soft.
