Mici

Romania

Mititei — Romania's skinless grilled sausages: beef-pork-lamb with garlic, thyme (cimbru), allspice and baking soda for the springy snap — kneaded with broth, rested overnight, rolled into fingers and grilled over fierce coals. Mustard and cold beer mandatory; May 1st runs on mici.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef + 500 g pork/lamb + suet/fat
  • 1 head garlic + thyme + allspice + coriander + pepper + salt
  • 1 tsp baking soda + 150 ml beef broth; mustard + bread + beer

Method

  1. Grind meats with fat; mix in garlic + spices + soda.
  2. Knead in broth 10 min to sticky and homogenous.
  3. Rest covered 12–24 hrs refrigerated.
  4. Roll 10 cm fingers; grill hot 3–5 min per side.
  5. Serve instantly with mustard, bread, beer.

Chef tips

  • Overnight rest develops flavor and bind — don't skip.
  • Fat is juiciness — lean mici turn to corks.
  • Hot grill, quick cook — slow grilling dries them.
