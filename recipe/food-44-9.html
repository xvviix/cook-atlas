Kepta Duona

Lithuania

Kepta duona — fried rye fingers rubbed with raw garlic — is Lithuania's essential beer snack: strips of dense dark rye fried in oil until crisp-edged and chewy, rubbed while hot with cut garlic cloves, salted and served with cheese-and-mayo dip (or simply more garlic). Bar food elevated to ritual, it takes 15 minutes and disappears faster. Dense, moist rye is essential — airy bread fries to croutons instead of chewy-crisp fingers.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf dense dark rye
  • Oil for frying + 4–6 garlic cloves + flaky salt
  • Dip: grated cheese + mayo + garlic + herbs

Method

  1. Cut rye into finger-thick strips.
  2. Fry in shallow oil until crisp-edged but still chewy inside.
  3. Drain; rub immediately and firmly with cut garlic.
  4. Salt while hot.
  5. Serve hot with cheese-mayo dip and cold beer.

Chef tips

  • Dense rye only — airy loaves fry to dry croutons.
  • Rub garlic while hot — cold bread won't take the flavor.
  • Chewy-crisp, not hard — over-frying kills the chew.
  • Serve instantly — the magic fades in 10 minutes.
