Banosh

Ukraine

Banosh is Hutsul comfort from the Carpathians: fine cornmeal stirred into a bubbling mix of sour cream (or smetana) and milk until thick and glossy, enriched with butter, then crowned with crispy salo cracklings (shkvarky) and salty bryndza cheese. Unlike Italian polenta, banosh's dairy base makes it tangy and lush. Stir constantly, serve immediately — banosh waits for no one and reheats poorly.

Ingredients

  • 200 g fine cornmeal + 500 ml sour cream + 250 ml milk
  • Butter + salt
  • Toppings: bryndza/feta + salo fried to cracklings + mushrooms (optional)

Method

  1. Bring sour cream and milk to a bare simmer with salt.
  2. Rain in the cornmeal, whisking constantly.
  3. Stir 15–20 minutes until thick, glossy and pulling from the sides.
  4. Beat in butter until lush.
  5. Fry salo to cracklings; crumble bryndza.
  6. Bowl up immediately; crown with cracklings and cheese.

Chef tips

  • Constant stirring — lumps form in seconds of neglect.
  • Fine cornmeal — coarse grinds stay gritty.
  • Serve instantly — banosh seizes as it stands.
  • Dairy base, not water — sour cream is what makes it banosh.
