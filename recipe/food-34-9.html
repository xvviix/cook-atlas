Vetkoek

South Africa

Fat-cake fried bread: risen yeast dough balls deep-fried to crisp-gold pillows — split and stuffed with curried mince (the classic) or jam and cheese. Township and farm staple; best eaten too hot, standing up.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + yeast + sugar + salt + 350 ml warm water
  • Filling: curried mince (beef + onion + curry + chutney) or jam + cheese
  • Oil at 170–175 °C

Method

  1. Knead a soft dough; rise 1 hr.
  2. Shape balls; proof 15 min.
  3. Fry at 170 °C, turning, to deep gold.
  4. Drain on rack.
  5. Split; stuff with hot mince; serve now.

Chef tips

  • Full proof = hollow pillows; under-proofed = doughy.
  • Steady oil temp — cool oil greases.
  • Stuff generously — skimpy vetkoek disappoints.
