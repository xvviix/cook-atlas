Udon

Japan

Thick, chewy wheat noodles in clear dashi broth — listed here as the classic kitsune udon with sweet fried-tofu pouches, narutomaki and scallions. Twenty minutes, five broth ingredients, infinite comfort.

Ingredients

  • 4 portions udon (frozen is excellent)
  • 1 l dashi (kombu + katsuobushi, or instant)
  • Seasoning: 2 tbsp soy (usukuchi ideal) + 2 tbsp mirin + 1 tbsp sugar + salt
  • Inari-age pouches, narutomaki slices, scallions, shichimi

Method

  1. Make dashi: steep kombu below boiling, remove, add katsuobushi, steep and strain.
  2. Season the dashi with soy, mirin, sugar and salt; hold at a steaming simmer.
  3. Boil the noodles in a separate pot (never in the broth) until just chewy; drain.
  4. Warm the toppings in the broth briefly.
  5. Divide noodles into deep bowls, ladle over broth, arrange toppings, finish with scallions and shichimi.

Chef tips

  • Noodles cook separately — their starch would cloud the crystal broth.
  • Frozen udon beats dried for chew; it needs only 2–3 minutes.
  • Serve instantly: udon swells and softens by the minute.
