Roti Canai

Malaysia

Mamak flying bread: an elastic flour-water-ghee dough rested in oil, stretched paper-thin (flipped spinning through the air, if you're showy), folded to trap air — griddled to flaky-crisp layers. Clapped between the palms to puff, torn and dunked in dhal and curry.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + 1 egg + 100 ml milk/water + 2 tbsp ghee + salt + sugar
  • Plenty of oil/ghee for resting and griddling; dhal + curry to serve

Method

  1. Knead smooth and elastic; ball; submerge in oil; rest 4+ hrs/overnight.
  2. Stretch each ball paper-thin on oiled steel (or flip!).
  3. Fan-fold / coil; rest 10 min; flatten to discs.
  4. Griddle medium 2–3 min per side to flaky gold.
  5. Clap between palms to separate layers; serve hot with dhal + curry.

Chef tips

  • Long oily rest = stretchable dough; short rest = tearing.
  • Thin is flaky; thick is bready — stretch bravely.
  • Clap while hot — cold roti won't puff.
