Pancit

Philippines

Pancit bihon: birthday-long-life noodles — rice sticks soaked pliable, tossed through a soy-chicken broth with chicken, shrimp, cabbage, carrot and beans until they drink it all dry. Finished with calamansi and a mountain of toppings; cutting the noodles cuts the luck (so they say).

Ingredients

  • 250 g bihon (rice sticks), soaked 10–15 min
  • Chicken + shrimp; cabbage + carrot + beans; garlic + onion
  • 750 ml chicken broth + soy + fish sauce + pepper; calamansi + fried garlic

Method

  1. Sear chicken and shrimp; set aside.
  2. Stir-fry veg crisp-tender; set aside.
  3. Simmer broth with soy and fish sauce.
  4. Add drained noodles; toss 5–7 min, adding broth until absorbed and supple.
  5. Return everything; calamansi over; serve with extra citrus.

Chef tips

  • Soak, never boil — boiled bihon turns to mush.
  • Add broth gradually; drowned noodles go gummy.
  • Serve immediately — bihon seizes as it stands.
