Placki ziemniaczane

Poland

Poland's potato pancakes: russets grated fine with onion, squeezed bone-dry, bound with egg and a spoon of flour, and shallow-fried to lacy, shattering crispness. Served with sour cream — or goulash ladled over for the Hungarian-style feast (placek po węgiersku).

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg russet potatoes, grated fine
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg + 2 tbsp flour + salt + pepper
  • Lard or oil for shallow-frying; sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Grate potato and onion; squeeze in a towel until truly dry (save the starch that settles — stir it back in).
  2. Mix quickly with egg, flour, salt and pepper — work fast before it greys.
  3. Shallow-fry thin ¼-cup scoops in hot fat 3–4 minutes per side to deep gold.
  4. Drain on a rack (not paper — steam softens); salt immediately.
  5. Serve at once with sour cream or applesauce.

Chef tips

  • Dryness is crispness — wet batter steams instead of frying.
  • Grate fine and fry thin; thick placki go gummy inside.
  • Serve instantly — potato pancakes die within minutes.
