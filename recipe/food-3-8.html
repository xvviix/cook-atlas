Rocoto Relleno

Peru

Arequipa's fiery showpiece: whole rocoto peppers (hotter than habaneros) tamed by blanching, stuffed with cumin-scented beef, peanuts, olives, raisins and egg, capped with cheese, and baked in a milk-egg custard — classically beside pastel de papa (potato gratin).

Ingredients

  • 8 large red rocotos
  • Sugar, salt, vinegar, lemon for blanching
  • 400 g minced or chopped beef; 2 onions + 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp ají panca paste; 1 tsp cumin + oregano
  • 50 g toasted peanuts, olives, raisins, 2 chopped hard eggs, parsley
  • 200 g queso fresco/paria for capping; 250 ml evaporated milk + 2 eggs (custard)
  • Gloves for handling the peppers

Method

  1. Wearing gloves, slice the pepper tops (keep as lids), scoop out seeds and veins completely.
  2. Blanch the peppers 2–3 times in fresh water with sugar, salt and a splash of vinegar — this tames (never removes) the heat.
  3. Brown the beef with onion, garlic, ají panca and spices; fold in peanuts, olives, raisins, egg and parsley.
  4. Stuff the peppers, cap each with a thick cheese slice (plus its lid, pinned), and nestle in a baking dish.
  5. Pour the milk-egg custard around (not inside), and bake at 175–180 °C 30–45 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve with pastel de papa or plain rice to balance the fire.

Chef tips

  • Blanching water must be changed between rounds or the heat just re-soaks.
  • Never touch face/eyes while handling rocotos — gloves are mandatory.
  • Make-ahead friendly: stuff a day ahead, then custard and bake to serve.
