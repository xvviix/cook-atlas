Mee Goreng

Malaysia

Mamak mee goreng: yellow noodles wok-tossed with sambal-chile-tamarind, ketchup, soy and curry powder — with shrimp, tofu, potato, sprouts and egg. Sweet-sour-spicy, brick-red, crowned with fried shallots and lime; the mamak stall's greatest noodle hit.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh yellow noodles, blanched
  • Sambal paste: dried chiles + shallot + garlic (+ peanuts blended in)
  • Shrimp + tofu + potato + sprouts + eggs; ketchup + soy + tamarind + curry powder; lime + shallots

Method

  1. Blend and fry the sambal fragrant.
  2. Sear shrimp + tofu; push aside; scramble eggs in the wok.
  3. Noodles in; sauces over; toss 2–3 min.
  4. Potato + sprouts; toss to just-wilted.
  5. Plate with fried shallots, lime and extra sambal. (2 portions max per batch.)

Chef tips

  • Small batches — mamak woks run volcanic; home stoves don't.
  • Blanch noodles first — raw mee taste alkaline.
  • Sauce balance before serving — sweet, sour, heat in thirds.
