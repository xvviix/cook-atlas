Bandeja Paisa

Colombia

Antioquia's mountain platter: red beans, rice, carne molida, chicharrón, chorizo, fried egg, sweet plantain, avocado and arepa — nine components, each cooked separately, united by hogao (tomato-onion sauce). Served on a literal tray; belts loosened in advance.

Ingredients

  • Red beans + rice + 400 g ground beef + pork belly + 4 chorizos
  • 4 eggs + 2 ripe plantains + 2 avocados + 4 small arepas
  • Hogao: tomato + onion + pepper + garlic + cumin + cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer beans tender; cook rice fluffy.
  2. Build the hogao; braise beans in some of it; season the beef with the rest.
  3. Render chicharrón crisp; brown chorizo; fry plantains gold.
  4. Fry eggs sunny-side; warm arepas.
  5. Assemble generously on trays; avocado + lime + hogao alongside.

Chef tips

  • Each component seasoned alone — the variety is the point.
  • Chicharrón: render low first, then crisp — impatience burns.
  • Serve everything hot at once — timing is the real recipe.
