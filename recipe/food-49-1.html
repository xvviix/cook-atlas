Locro de Papas

Ecuador

Locro de papas is the Andes in a bowl: potatoes simmered with onion, garlic, cumin and achiote until collapsing, enriched with milk and melting queso fresco into a thick, golden, silky soup — served with avocado slices, more cheese and ají. Humble, vegetarian-friendly and profoundly comforting, it's Quito's everyday soup. The potatoes must fully surrender — half-mashed locro is just potato soup.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg floury potatoes + onion + garlic + cumin + achiote
  • Milk + 300 g queso fresco + salt + pepper
  • Avocado + cilantro + ají to serve

Method

  1. Soften onion and garlic with cumin and achiote.
  2. Add potatoes and water; simmer 25–30 minutes until collapsing.
  3. Mash coarsely in the pot — thick, not puréed.
  4. Stir in milk and cheese until melted and silky.
  5. Serve with avocado, cheese, cilantro and ají.

Chef tips

  • Floury potatoes — waxy ones won't collapse.
  • Collapse fully — texture is the dish.
  • Achiote for gold — the Andean color.
  • Avocado on top — non-negotiable.
