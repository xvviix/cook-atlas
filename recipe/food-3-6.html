Papa a la Huancaína

Peru

Cold boiled potatoes napped with huancaína — a silky golden sauce of ají amarillo, queso fresco, evaporated milk and crackers — served on lettuce with egg and black olives. A staple starter from the Huancayo region, also a Christmas-table classic.

Ingredients

  • 6–8 yellow potatoes, boiled whole, peeled, sliced thick
  • Sauce: 2–3 ají amarillo (or 2–3 tbsp paste), ¼ onion + 2 garlic cloves
  • 300 g queso fresco
  • 250 ml evaporated milk
  • 4–5 saltine crackers (the classic thickener); 2–3 tbsp oil
  • To serve: lettuce leaves, hard eggs, black Peruvian olives

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes until just tender; cool completely, then slice — cold slices hold the sauce best.
  2. Sauté ají, onion and garlic in oil until soft and sweet, ~7 minutes.
  3. Blend with queso fresco, evaporated milk and crackers until completely smooth, streaming in oil to gloss; season.
  4. Chill the sauce 30 minutes to thicken; adjust with milk (thinner) or crackers (thicker).
  5. Line plates with lettuce, overlap potato slices, nap generously with sauce, and garnish with egg and olives.

Chef tips

  • The crackers are traditional, not a shortcut — they give the sauce its body.
  • Serve cold or room temperature; heat splits the cheese sauce.
  • Leftover sauce is superb on pasta, yuca or as a dip.
