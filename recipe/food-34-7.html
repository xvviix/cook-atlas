Pap

South Africa

Mieliepap: South Africa's maize-meal staple — stywe (stiff), slap (soft) or krummelpap (crumbly) by ratio. The braai's foundation under boerewors, chakalaka and sous; cooked low and long, stirred with a fork, served steaming.

Ingredients

  • 500 g maize meal + 1 l salted water (+ butter)
  • Sous/tomato-onion gravy + braai meats to serve

Method

  1. Boil salted water.
  2. Whisk in meal (or cold-paste first to avoid lumps).
  3. Lid on; lowest heat; 30–40 min, stirring every 10.
  4. Fork through for krummel, or smooth for stywe.
  5. Serve hot with sous + braai.

Chef tips

  • Low heat + heavy pot — thin pots scorch.
  • Cold-paste start prevents lumps.
  • Long cooking — raw pap tastes chalky.
