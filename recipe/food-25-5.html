Kare-Kare

Philippines

The fiesta peanut stew: oxtail (and tripe) simmered to silk in a golden annatto-peanut sauce thickened with ground toasted rice — with eggplant, sitaw, pechay and banana blossom. The sauce is deliberately mild; the bagoong (shrimp paste) alongside brings the salt and funk.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg oxtail (+ tripe optional)
  • Sauce: 1 cup unsweetened peanut butter + ¼ cup ground toasted rice + annatto water + sugar
  • Eggplant + sitaw + pechay + banana heart; bagoong to serve

Method

  1. Simmer oxtail 2–3 hrs (or pressure-cook) to fall-apart; reserve broth.
  2. Sauté onion-garlic; whisk in peanut butter, rice powder, annatto, broth.
  3. Simmer sauce 15 min to thick and glossy.
  4. Blanch veg separately; arrange over the stew (or simmer in).
  5. Serve with rice and bagoong — never mix the paste in.

Chef tips

  • Unsweetened peanut butter — sweetened throws the balance.
  • Toasted-rice powder thickens with nutty depth, not pastiness.
  • Bagoong on the side, always — stirred in, it bullies the peanuts.
