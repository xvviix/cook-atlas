Pašticada

Croatia

Dalmatia's queen of stews: beef marinated overnight in vinegar (larded with bacon and garlic), braised hours with root vegetables, red wine, Prošek, prunes, figs and apple — the vegetables then blended into a glossy sweet-sour sauce. Served over gnocchi at weddings and feasts.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef round/chuck, larded with pancetta + garlic slivers
  • Marinade: red wine vinegar + red wine, overnight
  • Onion + carrot + celeriac + parsley root; tomato paste; bay + rosemary
  • Prunes + figs + apple + Prošek; olive oil; gnocchi + Parmesan

Method

  1. Lard the beef; marinate overnight in vinegar and wine.
  2. Pat dry; brown hard on all sides; set aside.
  3. Caramelize onion and roots; add tomato paste; deglaze with marinade + wines.
  4. Braise covered 2.5–3 hours, adding fruit midway; balance sweet-sour.
  5. Slice the beef; blend the vegetables into sauce; serve over gnocchi.

Chef tips

  • The overnight vinegar marinade is the tenderness and the tang.
  • Sweet-sour balance at the end — plum jam or sugar vs. vinegar.
  • Low and long; pašticada can't be hurried.
