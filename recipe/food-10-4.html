Soto

Indonesia

Listed here as soto ayam: a golden turmeric chicken soup perfumed with lemongrass, galangal and lime leaf, ladled over rice noodles with shredded chicken, bean sprouts, boiled egg, fried shallots, lime and sambal. Indonesia's comfort bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken (~1 kg), simmered; rich stock reserved
  • Spice paste: shallots, garlic, candlenuts, turmeric, ginger, coriander, cumin, pepper
  • Lemongrass + galangal + lime leaves + salam
  • Rice vermicelli + bean sprouts + shredded cabbage
  • Boiled eggs, fried shallots, lime, sambal, celery leaves

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken with bruised aromatics 30–40 minutes; shred the meat, keep the stock.
  2. Blitz and fry the spice paste until dark, oily and fragrant; add to the stock.
  3. Simmer 15 minutes; season with salt, mushroom powder and a squeeze of lime.
  4. Place noodles, sprouts, cabbage and chicken in bowls; ladle over the golden broth.
  5. Top with egg, fried shallots, celery and lime; sambal on the side.

Chef tips

  • Fry the paste properly — raw paste makes a chalky, flat broth.
  • Shred, don't cube, the chicken — strands drink up the soup.
  • Lime and sambal go in at the table so each bowl tunes itself.
