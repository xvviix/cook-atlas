Pelmeni

Russia

Siberia's meat dumplings: beef-pork filling loosened with ice water and onion, wrapped teaspoon-by-teaspoon in thin egg dough, pinched into ear-shaped rings — boiled from frozen in salted water with bay, served with butter, smetana and vinegar. Made by the hundred; frozen on trays.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 400 g flour + 1 egg + 150 ml cold water + salt; rest 30 min
  • Filling: 300 g beef + 200 g pork + onion + garlic + 100 ml cold water + salt + pepper

Method

  1. Knead a firm smooth dough; rest 30 min.
  2. Mix meats with onion-garlic; beat in cold water to loose and juicy.
  3. Roll 2 mm thin; cut 7 cm rounds.
  4. 1 tsp filling each; half-moon, seal, join corners into rings.
  5. Boil in salted bay water 3–5 min from floating; butter + smetana + vinegar.

Chef tips

  • Thin dough, small dumplings — thick dough turns gummy.
  • Ice water in the filling = juicy centers.
  • Freeze on floured trays before bagging — or they fuse.
