Gudeg

Indonesia

Yogyakarta's sweet jackfruit stew: unripe jackfruit braised for hours in coconut milk with palm sugar, coriander, galangal and teak leaves (or tea) for its signature brown color — served with chicken, hard-boiled eggs, tofu and fiery krecek. Sweet, then hot.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg young green jackfruit (canned, rinsed)
  • 1.5 l coconut milk + coconut water
  • Spice paste: shallots, garlic, coriander, candlenuts, ginger, galangal
  • 80 g palm sugar + salam leaves + lemongrass + salt
  • Color: teak leaves (traditional) or a black tea bag; chicken + eggs + tofu to accompany

Method

  1. Blitz the spice paste; fry with salam, lemongrass and galangal until fragrant.
  2. Add jackfruit, chicken, coconut milk, palm sugar, salt and tea/teak leaves.
  3. Braise on the lowest heat 3–5 hours until the jackfruit is spoon-tender, brown and caramelized.
  4. Nestle in boiled eggs and fried tofu for the last hour so they drink the sauce.
  5. Serve with steamed rice and krecek (spicy beef-skin crackers) for contrast.

Chef tips

  • Low and long is mandatory — rushing leaves pale, squeaky jackfruit.
  • Tea stands in for teak leaves convincingly for the mahogany color.
  • Gudeg keeps beautifully; the flavor deepens overnight.
