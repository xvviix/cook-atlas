Nanaimo Bars

Canada

Named for Nanaimo, BC: a no-bake triple-decker — chocolate-crumb-coconut base, sunshine-yellow custard-buttercream middle, and a crackly dark chocolate top. The middle must be firm enough to slice but soft enough to bite — achieved by real custard powder, soft butter and proper chilling between layers. Cut with a hot knife for the patisserie-clean layers that make Nanaimo bars instantly recognizable.

Ingredients

  • Base: graham crumbs + cocoa + butter + sugar + egg + coconut + walnuts
  • Middle: butter + custard powder + cream + icing sugar (vanilla; mint/choc variants)
  • Top: 170 g dark chocolate + butter

Method

  1. Melt butter, sugar and cocoa; whisk in egg and cook to thicken.
  2. Stir in crumbs, coconut and nuts; press firmly into a lined pan; chill.
  3. Beat butter with custard powder, cream and icing sugar until fluffy.
  4. Spread over base; chill until firm (1+ hour).
  5. Pour over melted chocolate-butter; chill until just set.
  6. Slice with a hot dry knife; store chilled.

Chef tips

  • Chill between every layer — rushing bleeds the layers together.
  • Hot knife, wiped clean — cold knives crack the chocolate.
  • Real custard powder gives the classic yellow and flavor.
  • Cut small — they are intensely rich.
