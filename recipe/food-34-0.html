Bobotie

South Africa

South Africa's Cape Malay classic: curried mince with soaked bread, chutney, apricot jam and raisins — baked, then crowned with savory egg custard and bay leaves until just set. Served with yellow rice and sambals; sweet-savory comfort in a dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef mince + 2 onions
  • Curry + turmeric + cumin + ginger + garlic + vinegar + sugar
  • Bread soaked in milk + chutney + jam + raisins + almonds; custard: 3 eggs + milk; bay

Method

  1. Fry onion + spices; add mince; cook through.
  2. Mix in mashed soaked bread + chutney + jam + raisins.
  3. Bake 180 °C 1 hr (bain-marie optional).
  4. Pour over egg-milk; bay on top; bake 30 min to set.
  5. Serve with yellow rice + sambals.

Chef tips

  • Rest before serving — the custard firms.
  • Don't overbake the custard — rubbery tops disappoint.
  • Chutney + jam are essential — bobotie is sweet-savory.
