Shawarma

Saudi Arabia

Saudi shawarma — the Kingdom's late-night obsession, Jeddah-style: spit-roasted chicken shaved thin, rolled in paper-thin saj bread with garlic sauce (or tahini-yogurt), pickles and fries into a slim, crisp-griddled roll — smaller, tighter and simpler than Levantine versions, eaten two or three at a time. The saj wrap and the final griddle-press define it. (See food-45-1 for Syrian shawarma with toum.)

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken + shawarma spice + yogurt + lemon + garlic + oil
  • Saj bread (paper-thin) + garlic sauce + pickles + fries

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in spiced yogurt 4+ hours.
  2. Roast hot and shave thin (or griddle strips).
  3. Crisp shavings in their juices.
  4. Spread garlic sauce on saj; add chicken, pickles, fries.
  5. Roll slim and tight; griddle-press until crisp.
  6. Serve immediately, two per person minimum.

Chef tips

  • Saj, not pita — paper-thin is the Saudi way.
  • Slim rolls — fat wraps don't crisp.
  • Griddle-press — the seal and crunch in one.
  • Eat in multiples — one Saudi shawarma is a snack.
