Nasi Uduk

Indonesia

Jakarta's fragrant coconut rice: jasmine steamed with coconut cream, lemongrass, pandan, galangal and bay — served with omelette ribbons, crispy anchovies (teri), peanuts, fried tempeh, cucumber and sambal. Breakfast, lunch and celebration in one plate.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups jasmine rice + ¼ cup coconut cream + 1.5 cups water
  • Aromatics: lemongrass + pandan + bay + lime leaves + galangal; salt
  • Toppings: thin omelette strips, crispy teri anchovies, roasted peanuts, fried tempeh, fried shallots
  • Sambal + cucumber + krupuk/emping to serve

Method

  1. Rinse the rice; combine with coconut cream, water, salt and all aromatics.
  2. Cook (rice cooker or stovetop-steam) until fluffy; rest 10 minutes; remove aromatics and fluff.
  3. Fry omelette strips, crisp the anchovies, roast peanuts with a kecap manis glaze.
  4. Mound the rice; arrange toppings around it with sambal and cucumber.
  5. Serve warm — coconut rice firms and dulls as it cools.

Chef tips

  • Coconut cream + water beats thin milk — richness without sogginess.
  • Don't skip the rest — it finishes the grains evenly.
  • The plate needs crunch (teri, peanuts, krupuk) against the soft rice.
