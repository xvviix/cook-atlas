Ciorbă de burtă

Romania

Romania's legendary tripe soup — the hangover cure: beef tripe simmered hours with roots to tender strips in clear broth, dressed with a tempered smântână-yolk liaison, garlic and vinegar. Served with extra cream, vinegar and hot peppers at the table; silky, sour, garlicky.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pre-boiled beef tripe, in strips
  • Broth: beef bones + carrot + parsnip + onion + bay + peppercorns
  • Liaison: 350 g smântână + 4 yolks; garlic paste + vinegar; chiles + cream to serve

Method

  1. Simmer tripe + veg 2–3 hrs to tender; strain; keep strips.
  2. Caramelize grated carrot in oil; return to soup.
  3. Whisk yolks + cream; temper with hot ladles.
  4. Stir liaison + garlic + vinegar into the pot (no hard boil).
  5. Serve with cream, vinegar, pickled chiles alongside.

Chef tips

  • Temper the liaison — dumped cream curdles.
  • Garlic + vinegar at the end — their punch fades with boiling.
  • Tripe must be truly tender — rubbery tripe ruins it.
