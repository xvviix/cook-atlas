Som Tam

Thailand

Isaan's pounded green-papaya salad: garlic and chiles bruised in a clay mortar, long beans, tomato and dried shrimp pounded in, then papaya threads tossed with fish sauce, lime and palm sugar — hot, sour, salty, sweet, crunchy. No oil; all punch.

Ingredients

  • 1 green papaya, julienned + long beans + cherry tomatoes
  • Garlic + bird chiles + dried shrimp + peanuts
  • Fish sauce + lime + palm sugar

Method

  1. Julienne the papaya fine; soak crisp in ice water; drain.
  2. Pound garlic + chiles coarse in the mortar.
  3. Pound in beans, tomato, shrimp lightly.
  4. Add papaya + dressing; pound-toss to coat.
  5. Taste (hot-sour-salty-sweet); peanuts over; serve now.

Chef tips

  • Pound, don't pulverize — bruised, not mashed.
  • Balance all four tastes before serving.
  • Serve immediately — it weeps within minutes.
