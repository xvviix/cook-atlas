Ojja

Tunisia

Ojja is shakshouka's heartier Tunisian cousin: a thick, harissa-fired ragù of peppers and tomatoes studded with merguez sausage (or shrimp, or chicken livers), finished with eggs cracked straight into the bubbling sauce. Rustic, smoky and unapologetically spicy, it's scooped with baguette in homes and roadside grills across Tunisia. The merguez renders its paprika-red fat into the sauce — that orange slick on top is flavor, not grease to skim.

Ingredients

  • 400 g merguez (or shrimp/chicken livers)
  • 2 peppers + 800 g tomatoes + onion + garlic + 1–2 tbsp harissa
  • 4 eggs + cumin + caraway + olive oil + baguette

Method

  1. Brown the merguez in olive oil; lift out and slice.
  2. Soften onion and peppers in the rendered fat.
  3. Add garlic, harissa and spices; bloom; add tomatoes.
  4. Simmer 15 minutes until thick; return merguez.
  5. Crack in eggs; cover; poach to runny-yolk perfection.
  6. Serve bubbling with baguette and extra harissa.

Chef tips

  • Brown the merguez first — the fond flavors the whole pan.
  • Keep the rendered fat — it's liquid seasoning.
  • Thick sauce before eggs — same rule as shakshouka.
  • Caraway is the Tunisian signature — don't skip it.
