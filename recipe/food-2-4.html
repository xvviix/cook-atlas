Greek Salad

Greece

Horiatiki, the village salad: big chunks of tomato, cucumber, green pepper and red onion with Kalamata olives and a whole slab of block feta, dressed with nothing but olive oil, vinegar or lemon, sea salt and oregano. Authentic versions never contain lettuce.

Ingredients

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, cut in wedges
  • 1 English cucumber, thick half-moons
  • 1 green bell pepper, strips; ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • 150 g Kalamata olives
  • 200 g block feta in brine (one slab)
  • 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1–2 tsp dried Greek oregano, sea salt

Method

  1. Cut all vegetables into large rustic chunks and combine in a wide shallow bowl; scatter over the olives.
  2. Season with salt and half the oregano; pour over the oil and vinegar.
  3. Lay the feta slab whole on top (never crumbled), drizzle with more oil and the remaining oregano.
  4. Rest 15–30 minutes at room temperature so the juices mingle; serve with crusty bread for mopping.

Chef tips

  • Summer-ripe tomatoes and real block feta in brine make or break this salad.
  • No lettuce, no fancy dressing — restraint is the recipe.
  • The bread-mopped juice at the bottom is considered the best part.
