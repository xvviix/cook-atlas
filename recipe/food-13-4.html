Palak Paneer

India

Punjabi green curry: blanched-and-shocked spinach (for the emerald color) pureed and simmered with an onion-tomato masala, folded with soft paneer cubes and finished with cream and kasuri methi. Served with roti, naan or jeera rice.

Ingredients

  • 500 g spinach, blanched 2 min + ice-shocked
  • 250–300 g paneer, cubed (lightly fried, optional)
  • Masala: 1 onion + 1 tomato + ginger-garlic + green chile + cumin
  • Turmeric, coriander, Kashmiri chile, garam masala, kasuri methi
  • Cream or whisked yogurt + ghee

Method

  1. Blanch spinach, shock icy, and grind with green chile to a smooth puree.
  2. Brown onion in ghee; add ginger-garlic, spices and tomato; cook to a thick masala.
  3. Add the spinach puree; simmer 5 minutes — no longer, or green turns olive.
  4. Fold in paneer; warm through; finish with cream and crushed kasuri methi.
