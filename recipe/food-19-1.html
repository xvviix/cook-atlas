Bibimbap

Korea

'Mixed rice': seasoned namul vegetables, beef and a fried egg (or raw yolk) arranged over rice with gochujang — in the dolsot stone-pot version, crisped tableside to a crackling nurungji crust. Mix violently at the table; that verb is the dish's name.

Ingredients

  • Short-grain rice
  • Namul: spinach + bean sprouts + carrot + zucchini + mushrooms, each blanched/sautéed + sesame + garlic + salt
  • Beef strips (bulgogi-style) + fried eggs
  • Gochujang + sesame oil + sesame seeds; (dolsot pot for the crisp version)

Method

  1. Cook each vegetable separately; season each with sesame oil, garlic and salt.
  2. Sear the beef; fry the eggs.
  3. Dolsot: oil the screaming-hot stone pot; pack in rice; arrange toppings in wedges.
  4. Top with egg, gochujang and sesame; heat until it crackles.
  5. Mix everything tableside and eat from the sizzling pot.

Chef tips

  • Separate cooking keeps colors and flavors distinct — the rainbow is deliberate.
  • Sesame oil in the hot dolsot = the golden crust.
  • Mix at the table, not in the kitchen — theater and texture.
