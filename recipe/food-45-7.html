Mujaddara

Syria

Mujaddara — lentils and rice crowned with a tangle of deeply caramelized onions — is the Levant's humble masterpiece: cheap, vegan, one-pot, and so good it reportedly tempted Esau. Brown or green lentils parboiled, finished with basmati in onion-scented water with cumin, then mounded and buried under onions fried to mahogany crispness. Served with yogurt or fattoush, it proves three ingredients can be dinner-party food. The onions need a full 40 minutes — pale onions make pale mujaddara.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups brown/green lentils + 1.5 cups basmati
  • 4 large onions (half diced for the pot, half sliced for crisping)
  • Cumin + olive oil + salt + yogurt to serve

Method

  1. Parboil lentils 15 minutes; drain.
  2. Caramelize diced onions deeply golden, 20+ minutes.
  3. Add rice, lentils, cumin and water; simmer covered 20 minutes.
  4. Rest 10 minutes; fluff — grains distinct, lentils tender.
  5. Meanwhile fry sliced onions to mahogany crispness; drain.
  6. Mound on a platter; bury under crispy onions; serve with yogurt.

Chef tips

  • Brown/green lentils only — red lentils dissolve to mush.
  • Caramelize patiently — rushed onions taste raw-sweet.
  • Rest before fluffing — the grains finish steaming off heat.
  • Crisp the topping separately — pot onions and crisp onions are two jobs.
