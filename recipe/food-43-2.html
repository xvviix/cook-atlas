Holubtsi

Ukraine

Ukrainian stuffed cabbage: blanched leaves wrapped around pork-rice (or buckwheat, or mushroom for fasting) filling, nestled in a pot with a sweet-sour tomato-sour-cream sauce and braised until silky. Sour whole cabbage heads (kryzhavky/kysla kapusta) make the festive sour version; fresh cabbage the everyday one. Small, tight rolls in a single layer braise most evenly — and like all cabbage rolls, they're better reheated.

Ingredients

  • 1 large cabbage (or sour head) + 600 g pork mince + 1 cup rice, parboiled
  • Onion + carrot + garlic + dill + salt + pepper
  • Sauce: tomato + sour cream + stock + bay + a little sugar

Method

  1. Blanch cabbage leaves until pliable; trim thick ribs.
  2. Mix mince with parboiled rice, sautéed onion-carrot, garlic and dill.
  3. Roll small and tight, tucking the sides.
  4. Layer in a pot over kraut/carrot; pour over tomato-sour-cream sauce.
  5. Braise covered at 175 °C (or stovetop) 60–90 minutes.
  6. Rest; serve with extra sour cream and dill.

Chef tips

  • Parboil the rice — raw rice bursts the rolls.
  • Small and tight — fat rolls cook unevenly.
  • Trim the ribs — thick ribs crack when rolled.
  • Make ahead — reheated holubtsi are legendary.
