Żurek

Poland

Poland's sour rye soup: a 5–7-day fermented zakwas stirred into a smoky sausage-potato broth with marjoram and garlic, served with halved boiled eggs — at Easter, in a bread bowl. Sour, smoky, hearty: the Polish soul in a bowl.

Ingredients

  • Zakwas (start 5–7 days ahead): rye flour + water + garlic + bay + allspice + rye crust
  • 400 g biała kiełbasa + smoked bacon/boczek
  • Potatoes + onion + carrot + parsley root + leek
  • Marjoram + garlic + salt + pepper; sour cream; boiled eggs

Method

  1. Ferment the zakwas at room temperature 5–7 days until pleasantly, yogurty sour; strain.
  2. Simmer sausage and vegetables into a rich broth; lift out and slice the sausage.
  3. Add potatoes; cook tender.
  4. Stir in zakwas gradually — 250 ml first, then to your sourness — and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Finish with crushed garlic and marjoram; serve with egg halves and a cream swirl.

Chef tips

  • Taste the zakwas before committing — sourness varies wildly.
  • Add zakwas gradually; you can sour more, never less.
  • Marjoram + garlic go in at the end for maximum perfume.
