Falafel

Lebanon

Beirut's herb-green fritters: dried chickpeas soaked (never cooked, never canned) and ground with parsley, cilantro, onion, garlic, cumin and coriander — rested, scooped and fried at 175 °C to shattering green-gold. Stuffed into pita with tahini, pickles and mint.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dried chickpeas, soaked 12–24 hrs (NOT cooked)
  • Parsley + cilantro + 1 onion + garlic; cumin + coriander + pepper + salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder (just before frying); oil at 175 °C
  • Pita + tahini sauce + pickles + mint to serve

Method

  1. Soak chickpeas with a pinch of soda; drain very well.
  2. Grind with herbs, onion, garlic and spices to coarse sand — not paste.
  3. Rest the mix 30+ minutes (or overnight) to bind.
  4. Add baking powder; scoop balls/patties firmly.
  5. Fry at 175 °C 3–4 min to deep gold; stuff into pita immediately.

Chef tips

  • Dried-soaked only — canned chickpeas disintegrate in the oil.
  • Dry mix, coarse grind — wet paste falls apart.
  • Test-fry one; if it breaks, grind finer or chill the mix.
