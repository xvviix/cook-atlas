Sticky Toffee Pudding

England

The Lake District's gift to dessert: dark, moist date sponge steamed or baked, drowned in hot toffee sauce (butter, brown sugar, cream, treacle) and served with custard, cream or ice cream. The dates dissolve into the sponge for its fudgy depth; the sauce goes on hot and in quantity. Invented (or at least popularized) at Cartmel in the 1970s, now England's most-ordered pub dessert.

Ingredients

  • Sponge: 250 g dates + flour + butter + brown sugar + 2 eggs + baking soda + vanilla
  • Toffee sauce: butter + brown sugar + cream + treacle + salt
  • Custard/cream/ice cream to serve

Method

  1. Simmer dates in water with soda until collapsed; cool.
  2. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs; fold in flour and dates.
  3. Bake at 170–180 °C until springy (30–40 minutes).
  4. Simmer sauce ingredients until glossy and thick.
  5. Poke the hot sponge; drench with sauce.
  6. Serve warm with custard or ice cream and more sauce.

Chef tips

  • Dates dissolved — lumps of date are fine, chunks are not.
  • Sauce hot, sponge warm — temperature is the luxury.
  • Drench generously — restraint has no place here.
  • Dark sugar + treacle — pale sauce tastes thin.
