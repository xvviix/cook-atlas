Bigos

Poland

The hunter's stew and Poland's de facto national dish: sauerkraut and fresh cabbage braised with pork, beef, kiełbasa, bacon, dried mushrooms, prunes and red wine — simmered, cooled and reheated across days until the flavors fuse into something greater than the pot.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg sauerkraut (rinsed lightly) + ½ head fresh cabbage
  • 400 g pork + 400 g beef + 300 g kiełbasa + 150 g bacon
  • Dried porcini (soaked) + prunes + onion + garlic
  • Red wine + tomato paste + bay + allspice + marjoram + caraway + pepper

Method

  1. Simmer sauerkraut with bay, allspice and prunes 45–60 minutes.
  2. Brown all meats separately; soften onion; bloom tomato paste; deglaze with wine.
  3. Combine everything with mushrooms and their liquor; simmer 1+ hours.
  4. Cool overnight; reheat and simmer again — repeat for 2–3 days.
  5. Serve with rye bread and potatoes. It freezes superbly.

Chef tips

  • Days, not hours — reheating cycles are the recipe.
  • Don't rinse the kraut to death — its sourness is the backbone.
  • Prunes + wine + marjoram are the signature trio; skip none.
