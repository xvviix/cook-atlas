Slavonski kulen

Croatia

Slavonia's PGI-protected paprika salami: premium pork with back fat, sweet and hot paprika, garlic and curing salt — stuffed, cold-smoked over beech/oak for weeks, then matured 6+ months to a firm, brick-red, intensely aromatic sausage. A 7-month project, not a weekend one.

Ingredients

  • 2.5 kg pork shoulder + 0.5 kg back fat, coarsely minced
  • Sweet + hot paprika + garlic + salt + curing salt (Prague #2 for dry-cure) + wine
  • Natural casings; smoker + curing chamber (12–15 °C, 75–80% humidity)

Method

  1. Mince cold; mix with spices and wine to sticky; rest 24 hours chilled.
  2. Stuff firmly into casings, pricking air pockets; ferment 2–3 days cool.
  3. Cold-smoke 2–3 hours daily for 10–15 days total.
  4. Hang to mature 6+ months, wiping any bad mold.
  5. Slice thin; serve with bread, cheese and red wine.

Chef tips

  • Curing salt + temperature control are food-safety essentials — no improvising.
  • Cold smoke only — heat cooks instead of cures.
  • Buy authentic PGI kulen unless you're equipped to cure safely.
