Stollen

Germany

Dresden's Christmas loaf: rich yeasted dough loaded with rum-soaked raisins, candied peel and almonds — wrapped around a marzipan log, baked, then buttered and snowed with icing sugar. Rests 2–3 weeks to mature; sliced thin with coffee through Advent.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + yeast + 250 g butter + eggs + milk + sugar + lemon + spices
  • 250 g rum-soaked raisins + candied peel + almonds
  • 200 g marzipan log; butter + icing sugar for finishing

Method

  1. Soak fruit in rum overnight; drain.
  2. Knead enriched dough; rise 1 hr; knead in fruit + nuts.
  3. Roll oval; lay marzipan center; fold over; seal.
  4. Bake 170 °C 50–60 min to gold.
  5. Butter hot; snow with sugar twice; wrap; mature 2–3 weeks.

Chef tips

  • Mature it — fresh stollen is good, aged is transcendent.
  • Double-sugar — the snow seals moisture in.
  • Seal the fold well — escaping marzipan burns.
