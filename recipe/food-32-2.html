Completo

Chile

Completo Italiano: Chile's loaded hot dog — vienesa in a soft bun buried under diced tomato, mashed palta (avocado) and a snowfall of mayo in flag formation. Don't hold back: completos are gloriously excessive. Chacarero, dinámico and as variations continue the madness.

Ingredients

  • 8 vienesas (Chilean franks) + 8 soft buns
  • 4 tomatoes diced + 4 avocados mashed with salt (+ lime)
  • Generous mayo (homemade ideal)

Method

  1. Simmer vienesas 5 min (or grill).
  2. Toast buns lightly.
  3. Dice tomatoes + salt; mash avocados + salt.
  4. Assemble: sausage, tomato, palta, mayo avalanche.
  5. Serve immediately — soggy buns wait for no one.

Chef tips

  • Over-top aggressively — restraint is un-Chilean here.
  • Assemble to order — tomatoes sog the bun fast.
  • Salt the palta — bland avocado sinks it.
