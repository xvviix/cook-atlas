Harira

Morocco

Ramadan's sunset soup: tomatoes, lentils, chickpeas and small beef/lamb cubes simmered with celery, onion, ginger and turmeric — thickened with a tedouira (flour-water liaison) and finished with vermicelli, parsley and cilantro. Served with dates, chebakia and a squeeze of lemon to break the fast.

Ingredients

  • 250 g beef/lamb cubes + 180 g soaked chickpeas + 140 g lentils
  • Crushed tomatoes + paste + onion + celery + ginger + turmeric + pepper
  • Tedouira: flour + water; vermicelli + parsley + cilantro + lemon

Method

  1. Sweat onion + meat + celery + spices.
  2. Add tomatoes + paste + chickpeas + water; simmer 45–50 min.
  3. Add lentils; simmer 20 min more.
  4. Whisk in tedouira; simmer to velvety.
  5. Vermicelli + herbs 10 min; lemon to serve.

Chef tips

  • Tedouira whisked smooth — lumps ruin the silk.
  • Chickpeas soaked overnight — canned added at the end.
  • Herbs last — long boiling dulls them.
