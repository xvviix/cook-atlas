Yakitori

Japan

Charcoal-grilled chicken skewers from the izakaya: thigh meat (negima with scallion) seasoned shio (salt) or glazed with sweet-savory tare. Binchotan charcoal and late basting are the secrets.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken thighs, 2–3 cm cubes
  • 4–6 scallions, 3 cm pieces (whites for skewers, greens for sauce)
  • Tare: 180 ml soy + 100 ml mirin + 100 ml sake + 60 g sugar + ginger + garlic
  • Salt; soaked bamboo skewers

Method

  1. Simmer the tare 5–10 minutes until syrupy enough to coat a spoon; strain.
  2. Thread chicken-scallion-chicken; salt lightly and evenly.
  3. Grill over medium-high 4–5 minutes per side until nearly done.
  4. Brush with tare and grill 1–2 minutes more per side, basting once or twice to a lacquer.
  5. Serve immediately, extra tare on the side.

Chef tips

  • Thighs forgive; breasts punish — thighs stay juicy over fierce heat.
  • Baste late: tare sugar burns long before chicken cooks.
  • Soak bamboo skewers or they ignite over the coals.
