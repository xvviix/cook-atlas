Chole Bhature

India

Delhi's decadent duo: dark, tangy chickpea curry (boiled with tea for color, spiked with anardana and chole masala, finished with a ghee tadka) alongside bhature — puffy, blistered deep-fried leavened bread. Pickled onions and green chile essential.

Ingredients

  • Chole: 250 g chickpeas soaked overnight + tea bags + whole spices + baking soda
  • Masala: onion-tomato + ginger + chole masala + anardana/amchur + chile
  • Bhatura: 500 g maida + yogurt + semolina + baking powder/soda + salt + sugar; oil for frying
  • Ghee tadka; ginger juliennes; pickled onions + green chiles

Method

  1. Boil chickpeas with tea, whole spices and soda until butter-soft and dark; reserve the liquor.
  2. Braise with onion-tomato masala and chole masala; simmer 20 minutes to a thick gloss.
  3. Pour sizzling ghee over the top; garnish with ginger and coriander.
  4. Knead and rest the bhatura dough 2+ hours; roll ovals.
  5. Fry at 180 °C, pressing gently — they should balloon; drain and serve instantly with chole.
