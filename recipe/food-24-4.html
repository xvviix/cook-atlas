Tom Kha Gai

Thailand

Chicken in coconut-galangal soup: poached chicken and mushrooms in coconut milk infused with galangal, lemongrass and lime leaves — finished with lime, fish sauce and chiles. Creamy yet bright, rich yet soupy; galangal leads, coconut follows.

Ingredients

  • 400 ml coconut milk + 500 ml chicken stock
  • Galangal + lemongrass + lime leaves + shallot + Thai chiles
  • 350 g chicken thigh + mushrooms (+ tomato optional); fish sauce + lime + sugar; cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer aromatics in coconut + stock 10 min to infuse.
  2. Add chicken; poach gently 8–10 min.
  3. Add mushrooms (+ tomato); 3–4 min.
  4. Off-heat: fish sauce + lime + sugar to bright balance.
  5. Serve hot with jasmine rice; warn about the aromatics.

Chef tips

  • Gentle poach — boiling toughens chicken and splits coconut.
  • Lime off-heat, always.
  • Galangal by the thumb, not the sliver.
