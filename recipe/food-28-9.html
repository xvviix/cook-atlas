Medovik

Russia

The imperial honey layer cake: 8–12 caramel-honey biscuit layers baked thin and crisp — softened overnight between tangy smetana-cream frosting, cloaked in cake crumbs. Crisp layers + overnight rest = the melting, toffee-honey slice of Russian celebrations.

Ingredients

  • Layers: 200 g honey + 130 g butter + 100 g sugar + 3 eggs + 550 g flour + soda
  • Frosting: 500 g smetana + 400 ml cream + sugar + honey + vanilla
  • Crumbs from trimmings; berries optional

Method

  1. Boil honey-butter-sugar briefly; cool; beat in eggs.
  2. Add flour + soda to a sticky gingerbread-like dough.
  3. Roll thin; bake 175 °C 4–5 min per layer to gold; trim warm.
  4. Whip frosting to soft peaks.
  5. Stack with generous cream; crumb-coat; chill 8+ hrs/overnight.

Chef tips

  • Trim layers warm — cold layers shatter.
  • Overnight chill is structural — same-day medovik crunches.
  • Generous cream — skimpy layers stay dry.
