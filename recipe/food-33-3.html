Trdelník

Czech Republic

Prague's old-town spiral: sweet yeasted dough wrapped around a trdlo spit, buttered, rolled in cinnamon-walnut sugar and baked to caramelized coils — hollow, warm, and eaten walking across Charles Bridge. Ice-cream-filled cones are the tourist remix.

Ingredients

  • 480 g flour + yeast + milk + eggs + butter + sugar + salt
  • Coating: butter + cinnamon sugar + ground walnuts
  • Foil-wrapped rolling pins as spits

Method

  1. Knead a soft sweet dough; rise 1 hr.
  2. Roll strips; wrap overlapping on buttered spits.
  3. Butter; roll in cinnamon-walnut sugar.
  4. Bake 185–190 °C ~20 min, turning, to gold.
  5. Slide off warm; eat warm.

Chef tips

  • Overlap the wrap — gaps split open.
  • Turn while baking — even caramel all around.
  • Eat warm — cold trdelník turns chewy-tough.
