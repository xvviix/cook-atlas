Black Forest Cake

Germany

Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte: chocolate sponge soaked with kirsch, layered with kirsch cherries and billows of whipped cream — cloaked in cream, chocolate curls and whole cherries. In Germany, kirsch isn't optional — without it, it legally isn't Black Forest cake.

Ingredients

  • Chocolate sponge (6 eggs + cocoa + flour + butter)
  • 2 cups sour cherries + 100 ml kirsch + sugar
  • 750 ml heavy cream + powdered sugar + vanilla; chocolate curls + cherries

Method

  1. Bake the sponge; cool; split into 3 layers.
  2. Macerate cherries in kirsch + sugar.
  3. Soak each layer with kirsch syrup.
  4. Stack: cream + cherries × 2; cloak in cream.
  5. Curls + rosettes + cherries on top; chill 4+ hrs.

Chef tips

  • Kirsch generously — dry layers taste of nothing.
  • Stabilize the cream if holding — or whip to firm peaks and serve soon.
  • Chill before slicing — warm cake collapses.
