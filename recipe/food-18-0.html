Zagorski mlinci

Croatia

Zagorje's pasta-flatbread hybrid (PGI-protected): thin wheat sheets baked twice to crisp crackers, broken up, blanched soft, and drowned in roast turkey drippings. Croatia's Christmas-lunch legend — the drippings make it; dry mlinci are just crackers.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg flour + 2 eggs + salt + 400 ml cold water (store-bought mlinci fine)
  • 1 roast turkey with abundant drippings (or chicken + enriched stock)
  • Salted boiling water for blanching

Method

  1. Roast the turkey (1 hr/kg at 180 °C), basting; reserve all drippings.
  2. If homemade: roll dough 2 mm thin; bake both sides crisp at 165 °C; cool.
  3. Break mlinci into pieces; pour over salted boiling water; soak minutes; drain.
  4. Douse generously with hot turkey drippings; toss to drink.
  5. Serve immediately beside the carved turkey.

Chef tips

  • Generous drippings or nothing — stinginess makes cardboard.
  • Drain well before the drippings or they slide off wet.
  • Serve the instant they're dressed — mlinci seize as they cool.
