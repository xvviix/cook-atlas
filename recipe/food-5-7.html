Churros

Spain

Spain's fried-dough breakfast: star-nozzle piped dough (flour, water, salt) fried crisp outside and soft within, rolled in sugar and dunked in chocolate a la taza — thick enough to stand a spoon in.

Ingredients

  • 250 ml water + pinch of salt + 1 tbsp sugar
  • 150 g flour
  • (Rich version: + butter and eggs, choux-style)
  • 1 l oil for frying
  • Sugar (+ cinnamon) for coating
  • Chocolate: 500 ml milk + 200 g dark chocolate + 1 tbsp cornstarch

Method

  1. Boil water with salt (and butter/sugar if using); dump in the flour and beat to a smooth ball that leaves the pan; cool slightly, beat in eggs if using.
  2. Pipe 10 cm lengths through a star tip onto parchment or straight into 180 °C oil.
  3. Fry 2–3 minutes, turning, until deep golden; drain and roll in sugar while hot.
  4. For the chocolate: simmer milk, whisk in chopped chocolate and cornstarch slurry until thick and glossy.
  5. Serve churros immediately with the chocolate for dunking.

Chef tips

  • Smooth, air-free dough matters — trapped air pockets can burst in hot oil.
  • Star tip isn't decoration: the ridges are what go shatteringly crisp.
  • Churros die within minutes — fry last, serve first.
