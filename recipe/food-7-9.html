Künefe

Türkiye

Hatay's molten cheese dessert: shredded kadayif crisped in butter around stretchy unsalted cheese, drenched in syrup tableside and buried in pistachios. Served blazing in its copper pan — a cold künefe is a failed künefe.

Ingredients

  • 250 g kadayif, shredded
  • 100 g butter, melted
  • 200 g unsalted melting cheese (dil/hatay or mozzarella)
  • Syrup: 300 g sugar + 300 ml water + 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Crushed pistachios; clotted cream (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the syrup 10 minutes with lemon; cool completely.
  2. Toss kadayif with butter until every strand glistens; press half firmly into a small pan.
  3. Layer the cheese edge-to-edge; cap with remaining kadayif and press hard.
  4. Fry on low-medium until deep gold; flip via plate and brown the second side.
  5. Pour cold syrup over the screaming-hot künefe (it should sizzle); pistachios; serve in the pan instantly.

Chef tips

  • Press firmly at every stage — loose kadayif falls apart on flipping.
  • Unsalted cheese only; salty cheese + sweet syrup clashes.
  • Time dessert to the second — this cannot wait.
