Feijoada

Brazil

Brazil's national stew: black beans slow-simmered with pork shoulder, ribs, bacon, sausage and smoked cuts into a thick, smoky, spoon-coating pot — served Wednesday-and-Saturday style with rice, collards (couve), farofa, orange slices and caipirinha. A feast, not a side.

Ingredients

  • 500 g dried black beans, soaked overnight
  • 800 g pork shoulder + 300 g ribs + 200 g bacon + 300 g paio/linguiça + smoked hock
  • 2 onions + garlic + bay + smoked paprika
  • To serve: rice + couve + farofa + orange + hot sauce

Method

  1. Simmer beans with bay 45–60 minutes until nearly tender.
  2. Brown all pork cuts in batches; soften onion and garlic in the fat.
  3. Combine with beans and just enough liquid; simmer 1.5–2 hours to melting and thick.
  4. Lift meats; slice; return to the pot; adjust salt and vinegar.
  5. Serve with rice, garlicky collards, farofa, orange and cachaça.

Chef tips

  • Low, slow and barely covered — feijoada thickens, never boils thin.
  • Orange slices cut the richness — they're structural, not decorative.
  • Better the next day; make it ahead of the party.
