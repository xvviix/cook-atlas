Žemaičių blynai

Lithuania

Samogitian pancakes from Lithuania's west: ovals of cooked-potato dough (mashed potato bound with egg and flour) wrapped around savory minced-meat filling, fried golden and served with sour cream. Unlike raw-grated potato pancakes, the cooked-potato shell fries up tender and golden rather than lacy — closer to a croquette than a latke. The filling goes in cooked; the fry just bronzes the shell and heats through.

Ingredients

  • Shell: 1 kg mashed potato (cold) + 2 eggs + 150 g flour + salt
  • Filling: 400 g cooked mince + onion + garlic + pepper + dill
  • Butter-oil for frying + sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Cook the mince with onion and garlic; season and cool.
  2. Mix cold mash with eggs, flour and salt to a soft dough.
  3. Flatten ovals in floured hands; fill; seal and shape.
  4. Fry in butter-oil over medium heat until golden both sides.
  5. Serve hot with sour cream.

Chef tips

  • Cold mash only — warm mash turns gluey.
  • Cooked filling — the fry only heats through.
  • Floured hands — the dough is soft by design.
  • Medium heat — fierce heat burns before heating through.
