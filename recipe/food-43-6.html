Salo

Ukraine

Salo — cured pork back fat — is Ukraine's iconic zakuska: thick slabs salted (or brined) with garlic, pepper and bay for days, sliced thin and eaten on rye with garlic, pickles and horseradish — or ground with garlic into a spread (zatsyorka). Silky, clean-tasting and garlicky when good, it pairs with borscht, horilka and black bread. The fat must come from a well-fed pig (thick back fat, pinkish-white); thin or yellow fat makes poor salo.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg thick pork back fat with skin
  • Cure: coarse salt + garlic + cracked pepper + bay + paprika (optional)

Method

  1. Score the skin lightly; rub generously with salt, garlic and spices.
  2. Pack in more salt; cure refrigerated 3–5 days, turning.
  3. Brush off excess salt; (optionally cold-smoke or paprika-coat).
  4. Freeze 1 hour for clean slicing; slice thin.
  5. Serve on rye with garlic, pickles and mustard.

Chef tips

  • Thick, white-pink fat only — thin or yellow fat fails.
  • Salt generously — salo can't really over-cure in dry salt.
  • Slice thin and serve cold — thick warm slices feel greasy.
  • Freeze briefly for slicing — room-temp salo tears.
