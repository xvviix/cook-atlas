Rouladen

Germany

Sunday-dinner beef rolls: thin top-round slices spread with sharp mustard, layered with bacon, onion and dill pickle — rolled, seared and braised in red-wine beef gravy to fork-tender. Served with red cabbage and dumplings, gravy mandatory.

Ingredients

  • 8 thin top-round slices (~1 kg)
  • German mustard + 8 bacon slices + onion + 4 dill pickles
  • Braise: red wine + beef stock + mirepoix; cornstarch gravy

Method

  1. Pound slices even; mustard each.
  2. Bacon + onion + pickle on each; fold sides; roll tight; skewer/tie.
  3. Sear all over; remove.
  4. Sweat mirepoix; wine + stock in; braise 1.5–2 hrs.
  5. Strain; thicken to gravy; slice rolls; serve with cabbage + dumplings.

Chef tips

  • Roll tight and secure — loose rolls unravel in the braise.
  • Sear before braising — color is flavor.
  • Low slow braise — hurrying toughens the round.
