Stroopwafel

Netherlands

Gouda's gift to coffee breaks: two thin, crisp cinnamon waffle cookies sandwiching molten caramel stroop — best eaten resting atop a hot cup so the steam softens the syrup. The waffles bake in a shallow pizzelle-style iron, are split horizontally while hot (the make-or-break skill), spread with butter-sugar-cinnamon syrup, and pressed together. Warm, chewy-crisp stroopwafels from a market stall bear little resemblance to packaged ones.

Ingredients

  • Waffles: 500 g flour + yeast + butter + sugar + 2 eggs + cinnamon + milk
  • Stroop: 250 g brown sugar + butter + treacle/molasses + cinnamon
  • Waffle/pizzelle iron + sharp knife or wire for splitting

Method

  1. Knead an enriched dough; rise 1 hour.
  2. Simmer stroop ingredients to a thick, spreadable syrup; keep warm.
  3. Press small dough balls in the hot iron until crisp and gold.
  4. Split each waffle horizontally immediately (wire or sharp knife).
  5. Spread warm stroop; press halves together.
  6. Serve warm — or lay over coffee to re-melt.

Chef tips

  • Split while hot — cooled waffles crack instead of splitting.
  • Keep the stroop warm — cold syrup tears the waffles.
  • Thin press — thick waffles won't split cleanly.
  • Serve over coffee — the ritual is half the flavor.
