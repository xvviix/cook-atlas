Moqueca

Brazil

Bahia's coconut fish stew: firm white fish and shrimp layered with peppers, tomato and onion, simmered in coconut milk and finished with dendê (red palm oil), lime and cilantro — served bubbling with rice, farofa and pirão. The dendê is non-negotiable.

Ingredients

  • 700 g firm white fish + 400 g shrimp, lime-marinated
  • 1 onion + 2 peppers + 3 tomatoes, sliced; garlic + malagueta chile
  • 400 ml coconut milk + 2–3 tbsp dendê oil
  • Cilantro + lime; rice + farofa to serve

Method

  1. Marinate seafood in lime, salt and pepper 15–20 minutes.
  2. Layer half the vegetables in a clay pot; add seafood; top with remaining veg.
  3. Pour over coconut milk; simmer covered 15–20 minutes — gentle, never boiling.
  4. Drizzle dendê over; rest 5 minutes.
  5. Shower with cilantro; serve with rice, farofa, pirão and lime.

Chef tips

  • Dendê defines Bahiana moqueca — without it, it's another stew.
  • Gentle simmer only — boiling toughens fish and splits coconut milk.
  • Layer, don't stir — the strata steam each other.
