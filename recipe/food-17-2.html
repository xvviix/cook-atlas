Churrasco

Brazil

Southern Brazil's barbecue ritual: salt-crusted cuts (picanha, fraldinha, costela, chicken hearts, queijo coalho) grilled over embers on long skewers and carved tableside in rodízio style — with farofa, vinaigrette salsa and chimichurri alongside. Fire, salt, patience.

Ingredients

  • Mixed cuts: picanha + skirt/flank + short ribs + chicken hearts + queijo coalho
  • Coarse salt; (garlic-lime baste for chicken, optional)
  • Charcoal + long skewers
  • Sides: farofa + vinaigrette + chimichurri + grilled pineapple with cinnamon

Method

  1. Salt the beef generously; keep chicken and cheese for later rounds.
  2. Grill over mature embers (no licking flames) on skewers, turning steadily.
  3. Carve thin slices tableside; return the roast to crisp again — the rodízio rhythm.
  4. Serve each round with farofa, vinaigrette and chimichurri.
  5. Finish with cinnamon grilled pineapple to reset the palate.

Chef tips

  • Embers, not flames — flaming fat soots the meat bitter.
  • Salt early on beef; salt draws then reabsorbs into a crust.
  • Pace the rounds — churrasco is a marathon with meat.
