Sisig

Philippines

Pampanga's sizzling legend: boiled-then-grilled pork mask (face) and belly chopped fine with liver, onion and chile — finished on a screaming-hot cast-iron plate with calamansi, liquid seasoning and (optionally) egg and mayo. Arrives still crackling; mix tableside.

Ingredients

  • 700 g pork belly + 500 g pig face (maskara); (all-belly works)
  • 100 g chicken liver + onion + siling labuyo + calamansi
  • Liquid seasoning/soy + butter; egg + mayo optional; sizzling plate

Method

  1. Boil pork 40–60 min to tender; drain and dry.
  2. Grill or fry to charred and crisp.
  3. Chop fine; toss with liver, onion, chile, calamansi, seasoning.
  4. Heat the sizzling plate with butter until smoking.
  5. Pile sisig on; crack egg over (optional); serve crackling with rice + beer.

Chef tips

  • Dry before crisping — wet pork steams, never chars.
  • Chop fine — chunky sisig eats like grilled pork, not sisig.
  • The plate must scream — lukewarm iron, no sizzle.
