Rechta

Algeria

Rechta is Algiers' celebratory hand-cut noodle dish: fine fresh noodles (rolled from semolina dough, cut hair-thin) steamed over a fragrant chicken, chickpea, turnip and zucchini broth scented with cinnamon, then dressed with the broth and crowned with the chicken. Eid and wedding food, lighter and more aromatic than couscous, the noodles steamed — never boiled — so they stay silky and separate. Cinnamon is the signature note.

Ingredients

  • Noodles: 500 g fine semolina + salt + water (or fine fresh egg noodles)
  • 1 chicken, jointed + chickpeas + turnips + zucchini + onion
  • Cinnamon + ginger + turmeric + butter/smen + coriander

Method

  1. Roll semolina dough thin; cut hair-fine noodles; flour lightly.
  2. Simmer chicken with chickpeas, onion, cinnamon and ginger 30 minutes.
  3. Add vegetables; simmer until tender.
  4. Steam the noodles (in a couscoussier or colander) 15–20 minutes.
  5. Dress noodles with butter and ladles of broth.
  6. Crown with chicken and vegetables; serve broth alongside.

Chef tips

  • Steam, never boil — boiled rechta turns gummy.
  • Hair-fine cut — thick noodles steam doughy.
  • Cinnamon leads — it's the Algiers signature.
  • Dress with broth at serving — dry noodles seize.
