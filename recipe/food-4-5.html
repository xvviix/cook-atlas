Polvo à Lagareiro

Portugal

'Olive-mill worker's octopus': a whole octopus simmered until tender, then roasted in a flood of olive oil with smashed garlic, bay and 'punched' roast potatoes (batatas a murro), showered with cilantro. Minimal ingredients, maximal olive oil.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole octopus (1.5–2 kg), frozen-then-thawed (freezing tenderizes)
  • 1 kg small potatoes, skin on
  • 200–300 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 onion (quartered for the pot) + 6–8 smashed garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves, peppercorns; coarse salt, pepper; bunch of cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer the octopus in unsalted water (salt toughens it) with onion and bay 40–60 minutes until a skewer slides in easily; drain.
  2. Boil the potatoes in well-salted water until tender; drain, then smash each lightly with a fist so it splits open.
  3. Lay the octopus in an earthenware dish, surround with potatoes, and douse everything generously with olive oil, garlic and bay.
  4. Roast at 200 °C (400 °F) 20–30 minutes, basting once, until the edges crisp and the potatoes drink up the oil.
  5. Shower with cilantro and serve family-style with bread for the oil.

Chef tips

  • Buy frozen octopus deliberately — freeze-thaw breaks down tough fibers better than beating.
  • No salt in the poaching water; season only after cooking.
  • The leftover garlic-chili oil is liquid gold — plan bread accordingly.
