Vepřo knedlo zelo

Czech Republic

The holy trinity on one plate: garlic-and-caraway roast pork (krkovice) with pan gravy, steamed bread dumplings, and sweet-sour braised sauerkraut with bacon and onion. Czech comfort in its purest form; the gravy must touch all three.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork neck/shoulder + garlic + caraway + salt
  • Kraut: 750 g sauerkraut + bacon + onion + sugar + vinegar + caraway
  • Knedlíky + pan gravy (roux + drippings + stock)

Method

  1. Rub pork with garlic + caraway + salt; rest.
  2. Roast 160 °C ~1.5 hrs, basting, to tender with crust.
  3. Braise kraut with bacon-onion + sweet-sour balance 30–40 min.
  4. Steam/boil knedlíky; slice.
  5. Plate all three; gravy over everything.

Chef tips

  • Caraway, not cumin — the defining Czech spice.
  • Sweet-sour kraut balanced — neither candy nor pickle.
  • Gravy over all three — dry dumplings are tragedy.
