Dolmades

Greece

Vine leaves wrapped around herbed rice (dolmades yalantzi, served cool with lemon and olive oil) or a meat-and-rice mixture (served warm, often with avgolemono sauce). Neat rolling and gentle weighted cooking give intact, tender parcels.

Ingredients

  • 50–60 brined vine leaves, rinsed (plus torn ones for lining)
  • 250 g long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 150 ml olive oil, divided
  • Juice of 2–3 lemons
  • Dill, mint, parsley — generous handfuls, chopped
  • Salt, pepper; 500 ml warm water/stock
  • (Meat version: add 500 g minced beef/pork to the filling)

Method

  1. Parboil the rice 7 minutes with half the lemon juice; cool, then mix with sautéed onion, herbs, half the oil and seasoning (plus raw mince for the meat version).
  2. Lay each leaf shiny-side down, snip the stem, place 1 tsp filling near the stem end, fold sides in and roll snugly — not tight, rice swells.
  3. Line a wide pot with torn leaves, pack the rolls seam-down in layers, and pour over water/stock, remaining oil and lemon.
  4. Weight with an inverted heatproof plate, cover, and simmer gently 40–60 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed.
  5. Cool in the pot; serve rice dolmades at room temperature with lemon and oil, meat ones warm.

Chef tips

  • Roll firmly but leave room — overstuffed rolls burst.
  • The plate weight stops the rolls unraveling during simmering.
  • Fresh spring vine leaves (blanched) beat jarred ones when available.
