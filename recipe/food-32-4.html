Curanto

Chile

Chiloé's earth-oven feast: shellfish, pork, chicken, chorizo, potatoes, chapaleles and milcao layered over hot stones in a pit, covered with nalca leaves and sacks — steamed 1–3 hours by earth and fire. At home: curanto en olla (layered pot). Communal, ritual, unforgettable.

Ingredients

  • Mussels + clams + pork ribs + chicken + chorizo
  • Potatoes + corn + chapaleles (potato-flour dumplings) + milcao
  • Nalca/cabbage leaves; white wine; pebre to serve

Method

  1. Pit: heat stones red-hot in fire 1–2 hrs.
  2. Layer: shellfish, meats, veg, dumplings — nalca between layers.
  3. Cover with leaves + sacks; steam 1–3 hrs.
  4. (Olla version: layer in a pot with wine; lid; simmer 1 hr.)
  5. Uncover tableside; serve with pebre + white wine.

Chef tips

  • Layers + leaves trap the steam — gaps leak flavor.
  • Don't peek — each opening costs heat.
  • Shellfish on the bottom near stones — they need the fiercest heat.
