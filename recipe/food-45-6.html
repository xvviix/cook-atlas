Fatteh

Syria

Syrian fattet hummus layers crunch and cream: toasted pita softened with chickpea broth, warm whole chickpeas, and a cool blanket of tahini-yogurt with garlic, lemon and cumin — finished with pine nuts sizzled in samneh and pomegranate seeds. Damascus's beloved breakfast (fatteh variations also run to chicken, tongue and makdous), it must be assembled at the last second so the pita keeps its bite. (See food-37-6 and food-38-3 for Palestinian and Egyptian fatteh.)

Ingredients

  • 4 pitas, toasted crisp + 3 cups cooked chickpeas + their broth
  • Yogurt blanket: 2 cups yogurt + tahini + garlic + lemon + cumin
  • Pine nuts + samneh + pomegranate + parsley to finish

Method

  1. Toast pita deeply golden; break into shards.
  2. Warm chickpeas in their broth until steaming.
  3. Whisk yogurt with tahini, garlic, lemon, cumin and salt.
  4. Layer: pita, hot broth to moisten, chickpeas.
  5. Blanket with yogurt; crown with samneh-fried pine nuts and pomegranate.
  6. Serve immediately.

Chef tips

  • Assemble last-minute — standing fatteh turns to porridge.
  • Hot chickpeas, cool yogurt — the contrast is the dish.
  • Deep-toast the pita — pale pita dissolves.
  • Broth to moisten, not drown — soggy fatteh is a failure.
