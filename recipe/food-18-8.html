Brudet

Croatia

The fishermen's stew of the Adriatic: mixed rockfish layered over onion-tomato-garlic in olive oil, covered with wine and vinegar, simmered without stirring (shake the pot!) until the fish yields to the broth. Served over polenta, with the broth as the prize.

Ingredients

  • 1.2–1.5 kg mixed firm white fish (monkfish, bream, eel, scorpionfish)
  • 2 onions + garlic + tomatoes/paste + olive oil
  • White (or red) wine + wine vinegar + bay + parsley
  • Polenta or crusty bread

Method

  1. Soften onion in generous olive oil; add tomato and garlic.
  2. Lay fish over the base; add wine, vinegar and water to barely cover.
  3. Simmer covered 30–40 minutes — shake the pot, never stir.
  4. Adjust salt, vinegar and oil at the end.
  5. Serve over soft polenta with parsley and bread for the broth.

Chef tips

  • Never stir — the spoon breaks the fish to mush; shake instead.
  • Mixed fish build the depth; single-fish brudet falls flat.
  • Vinegar brightens and once preserved it — don't fear it.
