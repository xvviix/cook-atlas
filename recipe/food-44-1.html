Kugelis

Lithuania

Kugelis (bulvių plokštainis) is Lithuania's bacon-studded potato pudding: raw grated potatoes mixed with egg, milk, onion and crisped bacon, baked low and slow in a deep dish until the top bronzes and the center sets custardy — served in squares with sour cream and more spirgai (bacon bits). The Lithuanian answer to kugel, it's Sabbath-comforting, crowd-feeding and better with crispy edges, so a wide shallow dish beats a deep one. Grate fine, bake patiently.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg potatoes, grated fine + 2 onions, grated
  • 300 g smoked bacon, crisped + 4 eggs + 250 ml milk
  • Butter for the dish + salt + pepper + marjoram; sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Crisp the bacon; reserve fat; grate potatoes and onions fine.
  2. Whisk eggs with milk, salt, pepper and marjoram.
  3. Combine everything (plus bacon fat) quickly — before greying.
  4. Pour into a buttered dish; dot with butter.
  5. Bake at 175–180 °C for 60–90 minutes until bronzed and set.
  6. Rest 10 minutes; cut squares; serve with sour cream and spirgai.

Chef tips

  • Speed after grating — grey batter bakes grey.
  • Shallow dish, crisp edges — deep dishes steam the center.
  • Bacon fat in the mix — it's the flavor carrier.
  • Set center, bronzed top — jiggle means more time.
