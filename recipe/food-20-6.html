Manakish

Lebanon

Lebanon's breakfast flatbread: soft yeasted dough dimpled and spread edge-to-edge with za'atar whisked into olive oil, baked blazing-hot until puffed and spotted — eaten folded, hot from the saj, with cucumber, tomato and mint. Also: cheese (jebneh) and lahm bi ajeen versions.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + yeast + sugar + salt + 60 ml olive oil + 300 ml warm water
  • Topping: 4 tbsp za'atar + 80 ml olive oil (paste consistency)
  • Cucumber + tomato + mint to serve

Method

  1. Knead a soft, slightly tacky dough; rise 1 hour.
  2. Divide into 6 balls; rest 15 minutes.
  3. Stretch to 20 cm rounds; dimple with fingertips.
  4. Spread za'atar paste to the edges.
  5. Bake at 250 °C (stone/steel) 6–8 min to puffed and golden; fold and eat hot.

Chef tips

  • Blazing oven + stone = puff and char; cool ovens make crackers.
  • Paste to the very edge — bare rims burn.
  • Eat within minutes — manakish waits for no one.
