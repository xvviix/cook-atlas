Baguette

France

The Parisian icon: lean poolish dough, an open holey crumb and a blistered crackling crust — earned by fermentation, gentle shaping, scoring, and aggressive steam. Best within hours of the oven.

Ingredients

  • Poolish (night before): 175 g flour + 175 g water + 1 g yeast
  • Dough: 175 g flour + 90 g cold water + 7 g salt + all the poolish
  • (×2–3 for 3–4 baguettes); rice flour for the couche

Method

  1. Build the poolish the night before; use at peak bubbles.
  2. Mix to shaggy; autolyse 20 minutes; knead to smooth. Bulk 1.5–2 hours with folds.
  3. Divide, pre-shape rectangles, bench-rest 20 minutes.
  4. Shape to 55–65 cm with tapered ends — firm but gas-preserving.
  5. Proof seam-up on a floured couche 45–75 minutes; preheat stone + steam pan at 250 °C.
  6. Flip onto the peel; score 4–5 overlapping slashes at 30°; load, steam hard 10–15 minutes, then vent to deep gold (22–28 min total).

Chef tips

  • Steam is non-negotiable — dry heat gives a pale leathery shell.
  • Shape with a light hand; the holes are preserved gas, not created air.
  • Baguettes stale in hours — bake the morning you serve.
