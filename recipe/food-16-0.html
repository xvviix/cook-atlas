Phở

Vietnam

Phở bò: Hanoi's aromatic beef noodle soup — a crystal broth of parboiled bones simmered with charred onion and ginger plus toasted star anise, cinnamon, clove, fennel and coriander seed, ladled boiling over flat rice noodles and raw eye-of-round that cooks in the bowl. Herbs, lime and chile at the table.

Ingredients

  • 2.5 kg beef bones (marrow + knuckle), parboiled and rinsed
  • Charred: 2 onions + 100 g ginger
  • Toasted spices: star anise, cinnamon, cloves, fennel, coriander seed, cardamom (in a bag)
  • Fish sauce + rock sugar + salt; 500 g flat bánh phở noodles
  • Toppings: raw eye-of-round shaved thin + brisket + onion + scallion + cilantro; herbs + bean sprouts + lime + chile

Method

  1. Parboil bones 10 minutes; rinse — this is the clarity secret.
  2. Char onion and ginger under the broiler; toast spices fragrant.
  3. Simmer everything at the barest bubble 3–6 hours, skimming; season with fish sauce, rock sugar and salt.
  4. Blanch noodles into deep bowls; lay raw beef over; ladle rolling-boiling broth to cook it.
  5. Serve with the herb plate; hoisin and sriracha on the side (never in the pot).

Chef tips

  • A rolling boil clouds the broth — the barest simmer, always.
  • Boiling-hot broth at service cooks the raw beef — lukewarm fails.
  • Taste the broth plain first; it should sing before garnishes.
