Tafelspitz

Austria

Emperor Franz Joseph's favorite dish: prime boiled beef (the tri-tip-like 'tafelspitz' cut) simmered with marrow bones and root vegetables into crystal broth and melting meat, served with roast potatoes, creamed spinach and the famous trio of sauces — apple-horseradish (Apfelkren), chive-sour cream, and bread sauce. Elegant in its restraint, it proves boiling can be luxury. The marrow spread on toast (Mark) served from the same pot is the insider's bonus.

Ingredients

  • 1.5–2 kg tafelspitz/tri-tip + 1 kg marrow + soup bones
  • Carrot + celeriac + leek + onion + parsley + bay + peppercorns + lovage
  • Sauces: apple + horseradish; sour cream + chives; bread sauce; roast potatoes + spinach

Method

  1. Cover bones with cold water; bring up slowly, skimming diligently for clear broth.
  2. Add the beef and aromatics; simmer (never boil) 2–2.5 hours until tender.
  3. Poach marrow bones alongside; scoop marrow for toast.
  4. Rest the meat in hot broth 15 minutes; slice across the grain.
  5. Serve in broth with vegetables, potatoes, spinach and all three sauces.

Chef tips

  • Start cold, stay at a bare simmer — boiling clouds the broth and toughens meat.
  • Skim patiently in the first 30 minutes — clarity is decided early.
  • Slice across the grain and serve in broth — exposed slices dry in minutes.
  • Freshly grated horseradish only — jarred kren is a shadow of it.
