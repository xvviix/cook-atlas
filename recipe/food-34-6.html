Koeksisters

South Africa

Afrikaner syrup braids: plaited dough fried gold and plunged hissing into ice-cold lemon-ginger syrup — crisp outside, syrup-drenched within. Hot pastry + cold syrup is the physics; (Cape Malay koesisters are the spiced, coconut-rolled cousins.)

Ingredients

  • Dough: flour + baking powder + egg + butter + milk
  • Syrup: sugar + water + lemon + ginger + cinnamon (chilled overnight)
  • Oil at 175–180 °C

Method

  1. Make syrup a day ahead; chill hard.
  2. Knead dough; rest 30 min.
  3. Roll; cut strips; braid; pinch ends.
  4. Fry gold in batches.
  5. Plunge hot into cold syrup 30 sec per side; drain on rack.

Chef tips

  • Ice-cold syrup — warm syrup makes soggy koeksisters.
  • Pinch ends tight — braids unravel in oil.
  • Small batches keep oil hot and syrup cold.
