Paella

Spain

Paella Valenciana from Valencia: saffron bomba rice cooked in a wide shallow pan with chicken, rabbit, flat green beans and garrofó beans — no seafood, no chorizo in the orthodox recipe. The rice is never stirred; the prize is the socarrat, the caramelized crust on the bottom.

Ingredients

  • 500 g bomba rice
  • 800 g chicken + rabbit pieces, bone-in
  • 200 g flat green beans (ferraúra) + 150 g garrofó or broad beans
  • 2 grated tomatoes + 1 tsp smoked pimentón
  • Pinch of saffron threads
  • 1.5 l water or light stock
  • 100 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • Rosemary sprig, salt, lemon wedges

Method

  1. Brown the meat deeply in olive oil over a strong flame; push aside.
  2. Add beans, then grated tomato and pimentón; fry briefly into a dark sofrito (don't burn the paprika).
  3. Pour in the water, boil 20 minutes to build the broth; add saffron and salt — it should taste slightly over-salted.
  4. Scatter the rice in a cross, spread level, and do not stir again; cook ~18 minutes on decreasing heat.
  5. For the socarrat: raise to high heat 90 seconds–2 minutes, listening for crackling; pull at the first frankly burnt smell.
  6. Cover with a cloth, rest 5 minutes, and serve with lemon.

Chef tips

  • Stirring after the rice goes in = risotto, not paella.
  • The socarrat window is about 20 seconds — nose and ears beat timers.
  • Bomba rice forgives timing; long-grain rice does not — use bomba.
