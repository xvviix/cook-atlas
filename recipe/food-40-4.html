Tourtière

Canada

French Canada's Christmas pie: spiced ground pork (sometimes veal or beef) simmered with onion, garlic, potato, cinnamon, clove and allspice, baked in a double flaky crust and served hot with ketchup, fruit ketchup or chutney. The potato — mashed into the filling — is the quiet genius: it binds without heaviness. Every Quebec family claims the true recipe; all agree it must be fragrant, juicy and eaten on Christmas Eve (réveillon).

Ingredients

  • Double pie crust (butter pastry)
  • 750 g ground pork (+ veal/beef optional) + 1 large potato
  • Onion + garlic + cinnamon + clove + allspice + savory + stock + egg wash

Method

  1. Simmer pork with onion, garlic, potato and spices in stock 20–25 minutes.
  2. Mash the potato into the meat to bind; cool the filling completely.
  3. Line a pie plate; fill generously; cap, crimp and vent.
  4. Egg-wash; bake at 200 °C for 40–50 minutes until deep gold.
  5. Rest 15 minutes; serve with fruit ketchup or sharp chutney.

Chef tips

  • Cool the filling first — hot filling melts the crust raw.
  • The potato binds — skip it and the slices crumble.
  • Spice boldly but balanced — clove should whisper, not shout.
  • Rest before slicing — molten filling floods the plate otherwise.
