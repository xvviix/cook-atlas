Bánh mì

Vietnam

Saigon's Franco-Vietnamese sandwich: a shattering rice-flour baguette spread with pâté and mayo, layered with grilled pork (thịt nướng) or cold cuts, quick-pickled daikon and carrot, cucumber, cilantro and chiles. Crunch, funk, acid, heat — in one hand.

Ingredients

  • 4 crisp baguettes (light, rice-flour style ideal)
  • 500 g pork shoulder: shallot + garlic + fish sauce + soy + sugar, grilled/charred
  • Quick pickles: daikon + carrot + salt-squeeze + vinegar + sugar + water, 1+ hours
  • Pâté + mayo; cucumber + cilantro + jalapeño

Method

  1. Salt-squeeze the julienned veg; pickle 1+ hours (overnight better).
  2. Marinate pork; roast hot and broil to charred edges; slice thin.
  3. Toast baguettes; spread pâté one side, mayo the other.
  4. Layer pork, pickles, cucumber, cilantro and chiles generously.
  5. Press, halve on the diagonal, and eat immediately — the crunch is fleeting.

Chef tips

  • The bread makes or breaks it — shattering crust, cottony middle.
  • Pickle juice on the bread is flavor, not sogginess — but assemble to order.
  • Pâté is traditional and transformative — don't skip it.
