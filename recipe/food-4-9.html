Sardinhas Assadas

Portugal

Summer in Portugal on a plate — especially Lisbon's Santo António festival: whole fresh sardines salted and grilled over white-hot charcoal until the skin blisters, served over boiled potatoes with roasted-pepper salad, olive oil and bread. Nothing but salt, fire and timing.

Ingredients

  • 12 very fresh whole sardines, scaled and gutted (heads on)
  • Coarse sea salt (generous)
  • Olive oil + lemon wedges to serve
  • Classic sides: boiled potatoes, roasted red/green peppers with garlic + vinegar
  • Rustic bread + simple tomato-onion salad

Method

  1. Rinse and pat the sardines bone-dry; salt generously inside and out 15–20 minutes ahead.
  2. Build a charcoal fire to white-hot coals (~230–250 °C at the grate); oil the bars well.
  3. Grill 3–4 minutes on the first side without touching — they release when ready.
  4. Flip once; grill 2–3 minutes more until opaque at the backbone but still juicy.
  5. Plate over warm potatoes with the pepper salad; drizzle with olive oil and serve with lemon and bread.

Chef tips

  • Freshness is everything — sardines deteriorate within hours; buy the morning of grilling.
  • Never move them early; premature flipping tears the skin.
  • Eat with your hands, Lisbon-style: the potatoes soaked in smoky fish oil are the prize.
