Tikvenik

Bulgaria

Tikvenik is banitsa's sweet autumn sibling — phyllo sheets rolled around grated pumpkin with sugar, crushed walnuts, cinnamon and cloves, coiled into a pan and baked until crisp and caramelized. Traditionally made when pumpkins ripen in fall (and for Christmas Eve, where it stands in for the savory banitsa), it fills the house with warm spice as it bakes. Served dusted with powdered sugar, it is equally at home as dessert or with afternoon coffee.

Ingredients

  • 400 g thin phyllo + 1 kg pumpkin, peeled and grated
  • 100 g sugar + 100 g walnuts, chopped + 1 tsp cinnamon + pinch of cloves + 120 ml oil/butter

Method

  1. Grate the pumpkin; toss with sugar, walnuts, cinnamon and cloves.
  2. Brush phyllo sheets with oil; spread filling and roll into long logs.
  3. Coil the rolls into a greased round pan; brush the top with oil/butter.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 35–45 minutes until crisp and deeply golden.
  5. Cool slightly; dust with powdered sugar and serve warm or at room temperature.

Chef tips

  • Squeeze very watery pumpkin lightly — flooding filling makes soggy rolls.
  • Brush every sheet — dry phyllo layers stay pale and papery.
  • Serve the same day — the crisp shell softens overnight.
  • A spoon of yogurt on the side balances the sweetness beautifully.
