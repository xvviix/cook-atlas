Baklava

Türkiye

The Gaziantep pistachio icon: forty whisper-thin yufka layers, clarified butter, and vivid green Antep pistachios under a light lemon syrup. Lighter and less spiced than the Greek walnut-honey version — crunch first, perfume second.

Ingredients

  • 450 g phyllo (yufka if available)
  • 300 g+ clarified butter/ghee, warm
  • 450 g raw pistachios, finely ground (+ extra for dusting)
  • Syrup: 400 g sugar + 250 ml water + lemon peel + 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Make the syrup first and chill it completely — cold syrup, hot pastry.
  2. Butter the dish; lay 10 buttered sheets; scatter a third of the nuts; repeat; crown with 10 buttered sheets.
  3. Cut into diamonds/squares with a sharp knife before baking.
  4. Bake at 160–175 °C 55–65 minutes until deeply golden and audibly crisp.
  5. Pour the cold syrup over the hot pastry; rest 4+ hours; dust with pistachio.

Chef tips

  • Clarified butter fries crisper than whole butter — milk solids soften layers.
  • Underbaking leaves a chewy core; push for deep gold everywhere.
  • One must be hot, the other cold — two hots make mush.
