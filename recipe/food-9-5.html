Crêpes

France

Paper-thin pancakes from Brittany: a rested, silky batter swirled in a buttered pan, flipped in seconds, and stacked for sweet (sucrées) or savory filling. The first one belongs to the cook.

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour + 4 eggs + 500 ml milk + 50 g melted butter
  • Pinch of salt (+ 2 tbsp sugar + vanilla for sweet)
  • Butter for the pan

Method

  1. Whisk or blend to a lump-free, thin-cream batter.
  2. Rest 30–60 minutes refrigerated (overnight is best) — hydration prevents tearing.
  3. Heat a buttered crêpe pan over medium; pour 60 ml batter and swirl instantly to coat thin.
  4. Cook 1–2 minutes until edges lift and the base spots gold; flip 30 seconds.
  5. Stack between parchment; fill and serve warm.

Chef tips

  • Resting is structural, not optional — unrested batter tears.
  • Thin pour + fast swirl = lace edges; thick pour = pancakes.
  • Control heat between crêpes — the pan keeps getting hotter.
