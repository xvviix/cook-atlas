Nasi Goreng

Indonesia

Indonesia's national fried rice: day-old jasmine tossed with a chile-shallot-garlic belachan (shrimp paste) base and kecap manis — the thick sweet soy that gives the dark caramel gloss. Crowned with a fried egg and prawn crackers (krupuk).

Ingredients

  • 400 g day-old jasmine rice
  • 3 tbsp kecap manis + 1 tbsp light soy/fish sauce
  • Base paste: 2 shallots + 3 garlic + 1–2 red chiles + 1 tsp belachan
  • Chicken/shrimp + greens (chye sim, beans)
  • 4 fried eggs; cucumber + tomato + krupuk to serve

Method

  1. Pound or blitz the base paste; fry in hot oil until deeply fragrant.
  2. Add protein and sear; add rice and toss 2–3 minutes until every grain is hot and separate.
  3. Drizzle kecap manis around the hot wok; toss to a dark even gloss.
  4. Wilt the greens through with white pepper; adjust sweet-salty balance.
  5. Plate with a runny fried egg on top, cucumber, tomato and krupuk.

Chef tips

  • Kecap manis is the whole identity — light soy alone makes a different dish.
  • Day-old dry rice fries; fresh rice steams to mush.
  • Sweet needs salt: balance the kecap with soy, fish sauce or salt.
