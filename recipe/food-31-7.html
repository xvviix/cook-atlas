Tochitură

Romania

Moldavia's pork feast: slow-braised pork, ribs and smoked sausage in wine-tomato-garlic — served over mămăligă with brânză, a fried egg and mujdei (garlic sauce). Hearty winter Christmas fare; pickles cut the richness.

Ingredients

  • 700 g pork + ribs + 300 g smoked sausage
  • Wine + tomato + garlic + thyme + bay
  • Mămăligă + brânză/feta + fried eggs + mujdei + pickles

Method

  1. Brown pork + ribs; add sausage.
  2. Wine + tomato + garlic + herbs in; braise 1.5–2 hrs.
  3. Make mămăligă; fry eggs; mix mujdei.
  4. Plate polenta + cheese + meat + egg.
  5. Mujdei over; pickles alongside.

Chef tips

  • Braise to spoon-tender — chewy tochitură disappoints.
  • Mujdei is mandatory — garlic sauce makes it.
  • Pickles balance the fat — don't skip.
