Maftoul

Palestine

Maftoul — 'rolled' — is Palestinian hand-rolled couscous: coarse bulgur-and-flour pearls, traditionally rolled by hand on a garbal sieve, steamed over a rich chicken, chickpea and onion broth until each pearl swells with flavor. Rustic and deeply comforting, it is winter food and celebration food at once, always served with the chicken on top, chickpeas strewn over and broth on the side. Store-bought maftoul (often labeled pearl couscous) makes it weeknight-feasible.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, portioned + 2 cups maftoul (Palestinian couscous)
  • 2 cans chickpeas + 3 onions + tomatoes + cumin + cardamom + allspice + cinnamon
  • Butter + parsley + lemon + yogurt to serve

Method

  1. Simmer chicken with onion and spices 30 minutes into a rich broth; lift out and brown.
  2. Toast the maftoul in butter with garlic and spices until fragrant.
  3. Add hot broth; simmer covered 15–18 minutes until tender and swollen.
  4. Warm chickpeas separately in buttered, spiced broth.
  5. Mound maftoul on a platter; arrange chicken over; spoon chickpeas around.
  6. Serve with broth, lemon, parsley and yogurt alongside.

Chef tips

  • Toast the maftoul first — untoasted pearls taste flat and starchy.
  • Brown the poached chicken — pale boiled chicken looks and tastes sad.
  • Keep extra broth on the table — maftoul keeps drinking as it sits.
  • Lemon at the end lifts the whole dish.
