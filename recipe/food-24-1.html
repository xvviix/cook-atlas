Tom Yum

Thailand

Tom yum goong: the hot-sour shrimp soup — shrimp-shell stock infused with lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves and chiles, sharpened with lime and fish sauce, enriched with nam prik pao. Clear (nam sai) or creamy with evaporated milk (nam khon); aromatics stay in the bowl, uneaten.

Ingredients

  • 500 g head-on shrimp (+ shells for stock)
  • Lemongrass + galangal + lime leaves + shallot + bird chiles
  • Mushrooms + tomato; fish sauce + lime + nam prik pao + sugar (+ evaporated milk optional)

Method

  1. Simmer shells 10 min; strain to a rich stock.
  2. Boil aromatics in the stock 5 min to fragrant.
  3. Stir in nam prik pao + fish sauce + sugar.
  4. Add mushrooms, tomato, then shrimp; cook 3 min to just pink.
  5. Off-heat: lime juice in; cilantro over; serve blazing hot.

Chef tips

  • Lime goes in off-heat — boiling turns it bitter.
  • Galangal, not ginger — different perfume entirely.
  • Shrimp seconds overcook — pull at just-pink.
