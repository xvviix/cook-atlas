Linzer Torte

Austria

From Linz, with a recipe dating to 1653 — among the oldest known cake recipes in the world: a buttery shortcrust made with toasted ground hazelnuts (or almonds), cinnamon and cloves, filled with sharp red currant or raspberry jam and finished with the signature lattice. Rich, spiced and jewel-topped, it keeps for weeks and improves with age as the jam softens the crumb. Austria's Christmas cookie (Linzer Augen) is its miniature descendant.

Ingredients

  • Crust: 250 g flour + 200 g toasted ground hazelnuts + 200 g butter + 150 g sugar
  • 2 eggs + cinnamon + cloves + lemon zest + pinch of salt
  • 300 g red currant or raspberry jam + sliced almonds (optional)

Method

  1. Toast and grind the hazelnuts; rub butter into flour, nuts, sugar and spices.
  2. Bind with eggs; chill the dough at least 1 hour (it is very buttery).
  3. Press two-thirds into a tart tin; spread jam thickly edge to edge.
  4. Roll remaining dough; cut strips and lay the classic lattice; chill again.
  5. Bake at 175 °C for 35–45 minutes until deep gold.
  6. Cool fully; rest a day before serving for the flavors to meld.

Chef tips

  • Toast the nuts — untoasted nuts taste flat and pale.
  • Chill the lattice strips — warm buttery strips stretch and tear.
  • Red currant is the classic — its sharpness cuts the rich crust.
  • It improves for days — bake ahead of any celebration.
