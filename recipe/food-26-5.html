Provoleta

Argentina

The asado starter: a thick disc of aged provolone grilled in a provoletera (or cast-iron skillet) to charred outside and molten middle — bloomed with oregano and chile flakes off-heat. Scooped onto crusty bread while it stretches; gone before the meat lands.

Ingredients

  • 1 disc aged provolone, 2–2.5 cm thick (250–300 g)
  • Olive oil + dried oregano + chile flakes + pepper
  • Crusty bread; cast-iron skillet/provoletera

Method

  1. Heat the skillet on the grill to medium-high.
  2. Oil the cheese lightly; sear 2–3 min to charred and bubbling.
  3. Flip once (fragile!); char the second side.
  4. Off-heat: oregano + chile over; rest 1 min.
  5. Serve in the pan with bread — eat while molten.

Chef tips

  • Aged provolone holds; fresh mozzarella melts to soup.
  • One confident flip — hesitation breaks it.
  • Serve instantly — provoleta seizes as it cools.
