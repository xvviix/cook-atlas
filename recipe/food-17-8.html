Pastel

Brazil

The feira (market) classic: whisper-thin cachaça-spiked dough — the spirit fries out for extra blisters — folded over cheese, beef, chicken, heart-of-palm or pizza fillings, sealed with a fork and fried to puffed, bubbly gold. Eaten with caldo de cana (sugarcane juice).

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + 2 tbsp cachaça + salt + warm water + oil; rest 30 min
  • Fillings: mozzarella / seasoned beef + olives + egg / chicken / heart-of-palm / pizza
  • Oil at 175 °C for frying

Method

  1. Knead a smooth elastic dough; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Roll pasta-thin; cut rectangles.
  3. Fill one half sparingly with cool filling; fold; fork-seal all edges.
  4. Fry at 175 °C 1–2 minutes per side to puffed and blistered gold.
  5. Drain upright; serve screaming hot with caldo de cana.

Chef tips

  • Cachaça = blisters and shatter — the authentic crispness trick.
  • Pasta-thin rolling; thick pastel fries doughy.
  • Cool filling only — steam bursts the seal.
