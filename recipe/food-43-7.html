Kholodets

Ukraine

Kholodets (studenets/dragli) is Ukraine's festive jellied meat: pork trotters, hocks and chicken simmered for 5–6 hours until the broth is collagen-rich enough to set firm without gelatin, studded with meat, garlic, carrot and egg, chilled overnight into a trembling mold. Served with fierce horseradish (khrin) or mustard, it anchors Christmas and Easter tables. The test: a spoonful of hot broth chilled on a saucer should set — if not, simmer on.

Ingredients

  • Pork trotters + hock + ½ chicken + onion + carrot + bay + peppercorns
  • Garlic + salt + parsley + boiled eggs to garnish
  • Horseradish (khrin) + mustard to serve

Method

  1. Cover meats with cold water; simmer 5–6 hours, skimming, until meat falls off.
  2. Add vegetables and spices for the final hour.
  3. Lift out; pick all meat; strain the broth; season boldly with garlic and salt.
  4. Arrange meat, carrot and egg in molds; ladle over broth.
  5. Chill overnight until firmly set.
  6. Unmold (warm dip); serve with horseradish and mustard.

Chef tips

  • No gelatin needed — trotters supply it; test-set a spoonful.
  • Skim diligently — clear broth makes jewel-like kholodets.
  • Season boldly — cold dulls salt perception.
  • Chill overnight — rushed kholodets weeps and wobbles.
