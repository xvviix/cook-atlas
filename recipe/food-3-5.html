Causa Rellena

Peru

A striking cold layered terrine: lime- and ají amarillo-seasoned mashed yellow potato sandwiching avocado and chicken/tuna salad, unmolded and garnished with egg and olives. Served chilled as an appetizer — the potato must be waxy yellow (papa amarilla or Yukon Gold).

Ingredients

  • 1 kg yellow waxy potatoes (papa amarilla / Yukon Gold)
  • 2–3 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 3–4 tbsp lime juice + 60 ml oil
  • 300 g shredded chicken or tuna + 3–4 tbsp mayo + diced onion
  • 2 ripe avocados, sliced
  • Salt, pepper
  • To garnish: hard eggs, black olives, lettuce

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes whole until tender; peel while warm and rice or mash until perfectly smooth; cool completely.
  2. Knead in ají paste, lime, oil and salt until vivid yellow and pliable; chill 30 minutes.
  3. Mix the chicken/tuna with mayo, onion, lime, salt and pepper.
  4. In a ring mold or cling-lined dish layer: half the potato, avocado slices, chicken salad, remaining potato; press gently and chill 1+ hours.
  5. Unmold, garnish with egg, olives and lettuce; slice with a hot knife.

Chef tips

  • Rice the potatoes (never food-process) and season only when cool — warm potato turns gluey.
  • Waxy yellow potatoes hold the structure; floury ones crumble.
  • Assemble ahead — causa slices cleanest after a full chill.
