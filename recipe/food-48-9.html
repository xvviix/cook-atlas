Kunafa

Saudi Arabia

Saudi kunafa — the Kingdom's celebration dessert, often in cream-filled (bi qishta) style: crisp buttered kataifi top and bottom around molten qishta cream (or sweet cheese), baked gold, flipped and drowned in rose-orange-blossom atr, crowned with pistachios. Served hot so the cream flows. (See food-37-3 and food-37-7 for Palestinian knafeh styles.)

Ingredients

  • 500 g kataifi + 200 g ghee
  • Qishta: milk + cream + cornstarch + mastic/rose (or sweet cheese)
  • Atr: sugar + water + lemon + rose + orange blossom; pistachios

Method

  1. Make qishta: simmer milk-cream with starch until thick; cool.
  2. Work ghee through kataifi; press half firmly into the pan.
  3. Spread qishta edge to edge; cap with remaining pastry.
  4. Bake at 190–200 °C until deep gold.
  5. Flip; flood with warm atr; scatter pistachios; serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Press firmly — loose pastry crumbles on the flip.
  • Qishta thick — thin cream leaks and sogs.
  • Serve hot — the flowing center is the magic.
  • Atr warm, kunafa hot — then eat immediately.
