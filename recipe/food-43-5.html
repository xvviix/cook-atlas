Pampushky

Ukraine

Pampushky are Ukraine's garlic bread rolls — pillowy yeasted buns baked touching in a pan, then drenched while hot with garlic oil (chopped garlic pounded with oil, salt and dill or parsley). Served obligatorily with borscht, they tear apart steaming and fragrant. The garlic dressing goes on hot rolls so it soaks in rather than sitting on top; sweet versions (with cherry or poppy) exist for dessert tables.

Ingredients

  • Buns: 500 g flour + yeast + warm milk + 1 egg + butter + sugar + salt
  • Garlic oil: 1 head garlic + 80 ml oil + salt + dill/parsley
  • Egg wash + extra dill to finish

Method

  1. Knead a soft enriched dough; rise 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Shape small balls; nestle touching in a buttered pan; proof 30 minutes.
  3. Egg-wash; bake at 180 °C for 20–25 minutes until gold.
  4. Pound garlic with salt; stir into oil with dill.
  5. Drench the hot rolls generously; rest 5 minutes; serve with borscht.

Chef tips

  • Dress while hot — cold rolls won't absorb the garlic oil.
  • Nestle touching — they steam each other soft.
  • Pound the garlic with salt — it melts into the oil.
  • Serve with borscht — the pairing is sacred.
