Humitas

Chile

Chilean humitas: fresh choclo corn ground with basil, mixed with onion sofrito (and ají, tomato, cheese in the fuller version) — wrapped in husks and boiled/steamed. Sweeter and basil-heavy vs. Argentina's; served with ensalada chilena and sugar-or-salt to taste.

Ingredients

  • 8 ears fresh choclo + husks reserved
  • Onion + garlic + (ají + tomato + cheese optional)
  • Basil galore + milk + salt + sugar; string for tying

Method

  1. Husk carefully; blanch husks pliable.
  2. Grind corn + basil coarse-creamy.
  3. Sweat onion (+ peppers); mix into corn + milk; season.
  4. Wrap spoonfuls in husks; tie.
  5. Boil 20–25 min (or steam 1 hr); drain; serve with salad.

Chef tips

  • Fresh corn only — old corn turns starchy.
  • Basil is the signature — be generous.
  • Tie firmly — loose humitas flood.
