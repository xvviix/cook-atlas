Kajmak

Serbia

Balkan clotted cream: milk simmered low until a thick cream crust forms, skimmed off in layers, salted and rested. Eaten young and milky (mladi) within days or aged (stari) to a pungent cheese-like depth — the soulmate of grilled meats and warm lepinja.

Ingredients

  • 2 l full-fat milk (unpasteurized ideal) + 250 ml double cream
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat milk in the widest pot to steaming (never boiling); stir in the cream.
  2. Hold at the barest simmer 1–1.5 hours without stirring until a thick skin forms.
  3. Lift the crust into a shallow dish; salt lightly; let a new crust form; repeat.
  4. Rest chilled overnight; serve at room temperature.
  5. Young: eat within days. Aged: keep salted and cool for weeks of deepening flavor.

Chef tips

  • Wide pot, no stirring — surface area and patience make the crust.
  • Salt each layer — it preserves as well as seasons.
  • Serve at room temperature; fridge-cold kajmak tastes muted.
