Kafta

Lebanon

Lebanese grilled kebabs: beef (or beef-lamb) kneaded with onion, parsley and seven spices, molded onto skewers and grilled over medium heat to charred and juicy. Served with hummus, tabbouleh and grilled onion-tomato — or simmered skillet-style.

Ingredients

  • 700 g ground beef (15–20% fat) + 200 g lamb (optional)
  • 1 onion, very finely chopped + 1 cup parsley + garlic
  • 2 tsp seven spices/allspice + salt + pepper + cayenne

Method

  1. Mix meat with onion, parsley and spices; knead 2–3 minutes to tacky.
  2. Mold firmly onto (soaked) skewers in long ovals; chill 20 minutes.
  3. Grill over medium heat 8–10 min, turning every 2 minutes, to 70 °C+.
  4. Rest 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with pita, hummus, grilled onion and tomato.

Chef tips

  • Knead to tacky — loose mix slides off skewers.
  • Chill before grilling — firm kafta clings.
  • Medium heat and frequent turns — fierce heat chars raw middles.
