Couscous

Algeria

Algerian couscous — Friday's sacred dish, often with lamb, seven vegetables and chickpeas in a ras-el-hanout-scented broth: fine semolina rolled with oil and salted water, steamed three times in a couscoussier with rolling and aerating between each pass, until every grain stands separate and fluffy. Algeria's versions range from savory lamb to sweet seffa with raisins and cinnamon. The triple steam is the craft; instant couscous is a different grain entirely. (See food-41-0 for Tunisian couscous.)

Ingredients

  • 500 g fine couscous + olive oil + smen/butter
  • 1 kg lamb + onion + tomato + chickpeas + ras el hanout + paprika
  • Carrots + turnips + zucchini + pumpkin + cabbage + coriander

Method

  1. Brown lamb with onion, tomato and spices; add water and chickpeas; simmer.
  2. Roll couscous with oil and salted water; rest 10 minutes.
  3. Steam 20 min (1st); roll with water, break lumps; steam 20 min (2nd).
  4. Add vegetables to broth by hardness; roll grains again.
  5. Steam 15 min (3rd); butter until glistening and separate.
  6. Mound grains; crown with meat and vegetables; serve broth alongside.

Chef tips

  • Three steams, three rollings — no shortcuts to fluffy.
  • Break every lump — lumps steam into dumplings.
  • Vegetables by hardness — zucchini last, carrots first.
  • Broth on the side — couscous drinks at the table.
