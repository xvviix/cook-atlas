Butter Chicken

India

Murgh makhani from Delhi: charred yogurt-marinated chicken folded into a silken tomato-cashew-butter-cream gravy perfumed with kasuri methi. Restaurant gloss comes from straining the gravy and finishing with cold butter.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 700 g thighs + yogurt + ginger-garlic + tandoori masala + lemon
  • Gravy: 500 g tomatoes + 2 onions + 15 cashews + ginger + garlic + butter
  • Spices: Kashmiri chile, garam masala, kasuri methi, cardamom, cinnamon
  • 100 ml cream + 50 g cold butter; sugar + salt to balance

Method

  1. Marinate chicken 2+ hours; grill/broil to charred edges; set aside.
  2. Simmer tomatoes, onions, cashews, ginger and garlic until collapsing; cool and blend silk-smooth; strain.
  3. Temper whole spices in butter; add the strained gravy with ground spices; simmer to a gloss.
  4. Fold in chicken; simmer 5 minutes; crush in kasuri methi.
  5. Finish with cream and cold butter off the heat; serve with naan and basmati.
