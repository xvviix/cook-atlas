Šaltibarščiai

Lithuania

Lithuania's shocking-pink summer soup: grated cooked beets swirled into cold kefir with cucumber, dill, scallions and hard-boiled eggs — served ice-cold, always with hot boiled potatoes on the side (never in the soup). Refreshing, tangy, probiotic and ready in 20 minutes plus chilling, it's the taste of a Lithuanian July. The color should be vivid magenta: pale šaltibarščiai means too little beet or too much kefir.

Ingredients

  • 500 g cooked beets, grated + 1 liter cold kefir
  • 2 cucumbers + 4 eggs, boiled + dill + scallions
  • Salt + lemon/vinegar + hot boiled potatoes to serve

Method

  1. Grate the cooked beets into a large bowl.
  2. Add diced cucumber, scallions and most of the dill.
  3. Pour over cold kefir; stir to vivid pink.
  4. Season with salt and a squeeze of lemon; chill 1–2 hours.
  5. Boil potatoes separately; serve the soup ice-cold with eggs, dill and hot potatoes.

Chef tips

  • Ice-cold soup, hot potatoes — the contrast is the tradition.
  • Potatoes beside, never in — in-soup potatoes turn the pink muddy.
  • Chill before serving — the flavors marry in the cold.
  • Vivid pink or bust — adjust beet-to-kefir ratio boldly.
