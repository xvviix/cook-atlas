Peameal Bacon

Canada

Toronto's signature sandwich filling: lean pork loin wet-cured in sweet-salty brine, rolled in golden cornmeal (originally ground peameal), then sliced thick and griddled. Juicier and leaner than strip bacon, it piles onto a soft kaiser roll with honey mustard and pickles at the St. Lawrence Market — the Carousel Bakery version is world-famous. Curing takes 4–5 days; cooking takes minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 kg pork loin + cure: kosher salt + pink cure #1 + sugar + maple + pickling spice
  • Cornmeal for rolling
  • Kaiser rolls + honey mustard + pickles to serve

Method

  1. Brine the loin refrigerated 4–5 days, turning daily; test-fry a slice for salt.
  2. Soak briefly if too salty; pat dry; roll thickly in cornmeal.
  3. Roast whole to 63 °C OR slice thick (1 cm) and griddle.
  4. Griddle slices 2–3 minutes per side until the cornmeal crisps.
  5. Pile onto rolls with mustard and pickles; serve hot.

Chef tips

  • Pink cure #1 is essential for safety in a multi-day brine.
  • Test-fry before committing — brines vary; soaking rescues over-salt.
  • Thick slices — thin peameal dries in the pan.
  • Crisp the cornmeal — pale cornmeal is gummy.
