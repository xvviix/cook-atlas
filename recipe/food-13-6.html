Samosa

India

Punjabi aloo-matar samosa: ajwain-scented crisp pastry around cumin-ginger potatoes and peas — sealed tight, rested, and fried low-and-slow to a blistered, shattering gold. Served with imli and mint chutneys.

Ingredients

  • Pastry: 500 g maida + ajwain + salt + 100 g oil rubbed to crumbs + water; rest 30 min
  • Filling: 4 potatoes boiled + peas + cumin + ginger + green chile + coriander + amchur + garam masala
  • Flour-water glue; oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Rub oil into flour to breadcrumbs; knead a firm dough; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Temper cumin, ginger and chile; fold through potatoes, peas and spices; cool completely.
  3. Roll ovals, halve, form cones, glue the seam; fill firmly and seal the top.
  4. Rest shaped samosas 30–45 minutes (drying prevents blisters bursting).
  5. Fry at 150–160 °C 12–15 minutes to deep gold; drain; serve with chutneys.
