Borscht

Ukraine

Ukraine's UNESCO-recognized icon: a ruby beet soup built on pork-rib or beef broth with cabbage, potatoes, carrots and beans, soured with vinegar or tomato, finished with garlic pounded with lard (zazharka) and dill — served with sour cream and garlic pampushky. Every region, every grandmother differs; all agree it must be sweet-sour, garnet-red and better on day two. The beets go in two stages — some simmered for body, some sautéed with vinegar to lock the color.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork ribs/beef + 1 liter water + 3 beets + ½ cabbage
  • Potatoes + carrots + onion + beans + tomato paste + vinegar
  • Garlic + dill + bay + sour cream + pampushky to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the meat 1+ hour into a rich broth; skim well.
  2. Add potatoes and beans; simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Sauté beets with vinegar and tomato (color lock); soften carrots and onion separately.
  4. Add cabbage; simmer 10 minutes; stir in the beet zazharka.
  5. Pound garlic with salt and a little lard; stir in with dill off the heat.
  6. Rest 20+ minutes; serve with sour cream and pampushky.

Chef tips

  • Vinegar with the beets locks the ruby color — skip it and borscht browns.
  • Garlic off the heat — boiled garlic turns harsh.
  • Better on day two — the flavors marry overnight.
  • Sweet-sour balance is personal — adjust vinegar and sugar at the end.
