Gored Gored

Ethiopia

Gored gored is kitfo's cubed cousin: pristine raw beef cut into bite-size cubes and glossed with warm niter kibbeh, mitmita, berbere and awaze — served raw or flash-seared rare, with injera and ayib. Where kitfo is minced and butter-mashed, gored gored celebrates the cube: clean beef flavor ignited by spice. Like all raw-beef dishes, it demands impeccable sourcing and is cubed at the last moment.

Ingredients

  • 500–900 g very fresh tenderloin/sirloin
  • Niter kibbeh + mitmita + berbere + awaze + salt
  • Injera + ayib + extra awaze to serve

Method

  1. Chill the beef well; sterilize board and knife.
  2. Cube into 1.5–2 cm pieces at the last moment.
  3. Toss with mitmita, berbere, awaze and salt.
  4. Gloss with warm niter kibbeh a spoonful at a time.
  5. Serve immediately raw, or flash-sear 1–2 minutes for rare.

Chef tips

  • Cube at the last moment — pre-cut beef oxidizes and risks contamination.
  • Tenderloin or sirloin only — tough cuts can't be eaten raw.
  • Warm (not hot) butter — hot butter cooks the cubes grey.
  • When in doubt, sear — rare gored gored pleases everyone.
