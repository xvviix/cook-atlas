Rendang

Indonesia

The Minangkabau slow-cooked icon: beef braised hours in spiced coconut milk until the liquid evaporates and the meat fries in its own oil to a dark, caramelized, intensely spiced finish. A 'wet' rendang is kalio; true rendang is dry and keeps for weeks.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shin/chuck, large cubes
  • 800 ml full-fat coconut milk
  • Spice paste: onion, garlic, ginger, galangal, lemongrass, red chiles
  • Whole spices: star anise, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, bay/salam
  • Salt; toasted coconut (kerisik, optional)

Method

  1. Brown the beef in batches; set aside. Fry the spice paste until dark and oily.
  2. Return beef with coconut milk and whole spices; simmer gently.
  3. Cook low 3–4 hours (stovetop) or 6–8 hours (slow cooker low), stirring as it thickens.
  4. When the oil separates and the sauce clings dark and dry, fry the meat in it 10–15 minutes to caramelize.
  5. Rest; serve with steamed rice. Better on days 2–3.

Chef tips

  • Patience is the recipe — the dry, dark finish can't be rushed.
  • Large cubes survive the hours; small pieces shred to floss.
  • Stir more as it dries — the sugary coconut base catches fast at the end.
