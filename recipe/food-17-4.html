Coxinha

Brazil

The teardrop croquette of every Brazilian party: chicken-broth dough wrapped around shredded chicken and catupiry cream cheese, shaped into drumsticks, breaded and fried to gold. Crisp shell, molten heart.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 ml chicken broth + butter + 250 g flour (panade-style)
  • Filling: 500 g poached shredded chicken + onion + garlic + parsley + 200 g catupiry
  • Egg + breadcrumbs; oil at 180 °C

Method

  1. Poach chicken with aromatics; shred; reserve the broth for the dough.
  2. Boil broth with butter; dump in flour; beat to a smooth ball; knead warm until silky.
  3. Sauté onion-garlic; toss with chicken and catupiry; cool.
  4. Flatten dough disks; fill; pinch up into teardrops with sealed necks.
  5. Bread egg-then-crumbs; chill 1 hour; fry to deep gold; drain.

Chef tips

  • Knead the dough while warm — cold dough cracks when shaped.
  • Chill before frying or the teardrops slump in the oil.
  • Seal the neck firmly — leaks become oil sponges.
