Somlói Galuska

Hungary

Hungary's rum-soaked trifle (invented 1950s, Brussels Expo-famous): cubes of plain, cocoa and walnut sponge layered with vanilla custard, rum syrup, raisins and walnuts — chilled, scooped into dumplings and drowned in chocolate sauce and whipped cream.

Ingredients

  • 3 small sponges: plain + cocoa + walnut (2 eggs each)
  • Custard: 500 ml milk + 3 yolks + sugar + starch + vanilla
  • Rum syrup + rum raisins + walnuts; chocolate sauce + whipped cream

Method

  1. Bake the three sponges; cool; cut into cubes.
  2. Cook a thick vanilla custard; cool with film on surface.
  3. Make rum syrup; plump the raisins in it.
  4. Layer sponge, syrup, custard, raisins, walnuts; press; chill 2+ hrs.
  5. Scoop dumpling-quotas into bowls; chocolate sauce + cream over.

Chef tips

  • Chill firmly — warm somlói collapses when scooped.
  • Rum syrup generously — dry sponge defeats the dish.
  • Rustic scoops are authentic — no neat slices.
