Currywurst

Germany

Berlin's 1949 street legend: grilled bratwurst sliced into coins and drowned in warm curry ketchup — bloomed with curry powder, paprika, Worcestershire and a sweet-sour kick — dusted with more curry. Eaten standing with fries (Pommes rot-weiß) and a tiny wooden fork.

Ingredients

  • 4–6 bratwurst (or Bockwurst)
  • Sauce: ketchup + onion + curry powder + paprika + Worcestershire + vinegar + sugar
  • Extra curry for dusting; fries + roll

Method

  1. Sweat onion; bloom curry + paprika 30 seconds.
  2. Add ketchup + seasonings; simmer 10–15 min thick; blend smooth.
  3. Grill the sausages to snappy.
  4. Slice into coins; sauce generously over.
  5. Dust with curry; serve with fries and a fork.

Chef tips

  • Bloom the curry in oil — raw powder tastes dusty.
  • Sauce served warm — cold sauce on hot sausage disappoints.
  • Generous dusting — the yellow top is the signature.
