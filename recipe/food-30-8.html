Zaalouk

Morocco

Morocco's smoky cooked salad: flame-charred (or pan-softened) eggplant mashed with tomatoes, garlic, cumin, paprika and olive oil into a silky dip — brightened with lemon and cilantro. Served warm or room-temp with khobz; part of every salad spread.

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant + 3 tomatoes, peeled + diced
  • 3 garlic cloves + 2 tsp cumin + paprika + chile + olive oil
  • Cilantro + parsley + lemon; khobz to serve

Method

  1. Char eggplant whole over flame 15 min (or cube + salt + pan-soften).
  2. Peel; chop; squeeze out water.
  3. Fry garlic + tomatoes to a paste.
  4. Add eggplant + spices + oil; mash and simmer 10–15 min thick.
  5. Lemon + herbs; serve warm or room-temp with bread.

Chef tips

  • Char = smoke; skipping it loses half the flavor.
  • Cook out the water — soupy zaalouk disappoints.
  • Lemon at the end — it lifts the earthiness.
