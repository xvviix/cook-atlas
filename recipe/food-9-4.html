Ratatouille

France

Nice's Provençal vegetable confit: eggplant, zucchini, peppers and tomatoes, each sautéed separately then married in a slow tomato-herb braise. Rustic chunks or elegant tian slices — summer in a pot.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggplants + 3 zucchini + 2 peppers, in chunks
  • 5 ripe tomatoes, chopped; 2 onions + 4 garlic cloves
  • Thyme + bay + basil; generous extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant 30 minutes; rinse and pat dry.
  2. Sauté each vegetable separately in olive oil until golden; set aside — this preserves identity.
  3. Sweat onion and garlic; add tomatoes and herbs; simmer 10 minutes into a sauce.
  4. Return all vegetables; cover and confit on the lowest heat 15–20 minutes.
  5. Fold in basil and a thread of oil; rest 15 minutes; serve hot, warm or room-temp.

Chef tips

  • Separate sautés prevent the dreaded uniform mush.
  • Peak-summer vegetables only — winter ratatouille is a rumor.
  • Room-temperature, day-old ratatouille may be its finest form.
