Bolón de Verde

Ecuador

Bolón de verde — the coast's mighty green-plantain balls: boiled or fried green plantains mashed with butter and salt, stuffed with cheese and/or chicharrón, formed into orange-size balls and fried gold — served for breakfast with fried egg, coffee and ají. Dense, savory, sustaining: one bolón fuels a morning. Mash while hot and season boldly; cold plantains turn gluey and bland.

Ingredients

  • 4 green plantains + butter + salt
  • Stuffing: queso fresco + chicharrón
  • Oil for frying + fried egg + ají + coffee to serve

Method

  1. Boil or fry green plantains until tender.
  2. Mash hot with butter and salt until smooth.
  3. Wrap portions around cheese and chicharrón; form balls.
  4. Fry until crisp and gold all over.
  5. Serve hot with fried egg and ají.

Chef tips

  • Mash hot — cold plantains go gluey.
  • Green, not ripe — ripe plantains make dessert.
  • Big balls — bolón means generous.
  • Breakfast serving — egg and coffee mandatory.
