Pkhali

Georgia

Georgia's jeweled vegetable pâtés: cooked vegetables (spinach, beet, beans) squeezed dry and pounded with walnuts, garlic, cilantro, fenugreek and vinegar — shaped into balls with pomegranate-seed wells. A trio of colors on every feast table.

Ingredients

  • 500 g vegetable (spinach/beets/beans), cooked
  • 150 g walnuts + 3 garlic + onion + cilantro + coriander + blue fenugreek + vinegar + salt
  • Pomegranate seeds + walnut oil to finish

Method

  1. Cook the vegetable; cool; squeeze out ALL water — critical.
  2. Grind walnuts with garlic, herbs, spices and vinegar to a coarse paste.
  3. Chop the vegetable fine; knead together with the paste.
  4. Shape balls with wet hands; press a well; fill with pomegranate.
  5. Chill 30+ min (ideally overnight); serve cool.

Chef tips

  • Squeeze ruthlessly — wet pkhali weeps and splits.
  • Coarse paste, not butter — texture is wanted.
  • Overnight rest deepens everything.
