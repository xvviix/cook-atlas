Pão de queijo

Brazil

Minas Gerais' addictive cheese bread: tapioca-starch dough scalded with hot milk and oil, mixed with eggs and sharp cheese, baked to crisp-shell puffs with stretchy, almost-hollow cheesy middles. Naturally gluten-free; dangerous warm.

Ingredients

  • 250 g tapioca starch (polvilho)
  • 160 ml milk + 80 ml oil + salt, boiled
  • 1–2 eggs + 100 g mozzarella/Parmesan mix (+ pinch of cayenne, optional)

Method

  1. Boil milk, oil and salt; pour over the tapioca; stir to a shaggy mass; cool 5 minutes.
  2. Beat in eggs then cheeses to a sticky, scoopable dough.
  3. Scoop balls onto a lined sheet; wet-finger any spikes.
  4. Bake at 200 °C 18–22 minutes to puffed and deep gold.
  5. Cool 5 minutes (molten centers!) and serve warm.

Chef tips

  • Sticky dough is correct — floury hands, not more flour.
  • Real queijo minas if you can; Parmesan-mozzarella if you can't.
  • Eat warm — they deflate and chew tough as they cool.
