Barszcz czerwony

Poland

Poland's Christmas Eve clear beet soup: a ruby consommé of beet zakwas (fermented 5–10 days) married to a dried-mushroom vegetable broth — served crystal-clear in cups with uszka (tiny mushroom dumplings), or alongside krokiety. Never boiled, or the color dies.

Ingredients

  • Zakwas (5–10 days ahead): beets + garlic + salt + lukewarm water, fermented
  • Broth: beets + carrot + parsley root + leek + celery + dried porcini + bay + allspice
  • Butter; salt + pepper; (uszka or cooked beans to serve)

Method

  1. Ferment sliced beets with garlic and salted lukewarm water 5–10 days; strain the ruby zakwas.
  2. Simmer the vegetables and dried mushrooms 45–60 minutes; strain to a clear broth.
  3. Marry zakwas into broth off the boil — heat to steaming, never boiling.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and a knob of butter; adjust sourness with more zakwas.
  5. Serve clear in cups with uszka floating.

Chef tips

  • Boiling kills the color — steaming is the maximum heat.
  • Start the zakwas a week before Christmas Eve, not the day before.
  • Clear is the standard — strain meticulously.
