Pączki

Poland

Fat Thursday's glory: rich brioche-like doughnuts spiked with spirytus (so they drink less oil), fried to gold, filled with rose jam, plum powidła or custard, and veiled in glaze or powdered sugar. Pillowy, citrusy, gone by noon.

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour + 7 g yeast + warm milk
  • 6 yolks + 80 g sugar + 80 g butter + rum/spirytus + lemon/orange zest + salt
  • Rose jam/powidła/custard; oil for frying (175 °C); glaze or powdered sugar

Method

  1. Activate yeast in warm milk; knead a soft enriched dough; rise to doubled.
  2. Roll 2 cm; cut rounds; proof 30–40 minutes until puffy.
  3. Fry at a steady 175 °C ~2 minutes per side to deep gold.
  4. Drain; fill with jam via piping bag while warm.
  5. Glaze or dust with sugar; eat the same day.

Chef tips

  • Spirytus/rum in the dough genuinely reduces oil absorption.
  • Steady 175 °C — cool oil makes greasy pączki, hot oil burns raw middles.
  • Pączki stale fast — fry the morning of the feast.
