Laksa

Malaysia

Curry laksa: prawn-shell stock enriched with laksa paste and coconut milk — ladled over rice noodles with prawns, tofu puffs, bean sprouts, egg and herbs. Rich, spicy, creamy; a squeeze of lime cuts through. (Asam laksa, the sour tamarind-fish cousin, is a separate pilgrimage.)

Ingredients

  • Stock: prawn shells + water, simmered 20–60 min
  • 3–4 tbsp laksa/curry paste + 400 ml coconut milk
  • Rice noodles + prawns + tofu puffs + sprouts + egg + laksa leaf; lime + sambal

Method

  1. Fry shells red; simmer to a rich stock; strain.
  2. Fry paste fragrant; add stock + coconut milk; simmer.
  3. Season fish sauce + sugar + tamarind to spicy-creamy balance.
  4. Blanch noodles into bowls; poach prawns in broth.
  5. Ladle over; top tofu, sprouts, egg, herbs; lime + sambal alongside.

Chef tips

  • Shell stock is the depth — water-only laksa tastes thin.
  • Poach prawns in the broth at the end — seconds only.
  • Noodles in bowls, broth over — boiled-together noodles bloat.
