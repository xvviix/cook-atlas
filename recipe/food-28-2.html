Beef Stroganoff

Russia

Count Stroganov's 19th-century classic: seared beef strips folded through a Dijon-Worcestershire mushroom sauce enriched with sour cream off the boil — served over egg noodles or mash. Fast, creamy, elegant; the sour cream must never boil or it splits.

Ingredients

  • 700 g sirloin, 5 mm strips
  • Onion + 350 g mushrooms, deeply browned + garlic
  • Flour + 500 ml beef stock + wine; Dijon + Worcestershire + 160 ml sour cream; noodles

Method

  1. Sear beef in batches 30 sec per side; set aside (keep pink).
  2. Caramelize onion + mushrooms 10–15 min; garlic; flour.
  3. Deglaze with wine; add stock; simmer to coat.
  4. Off-heat: whisk in sour cream + Dijon + Worcestershire.
  5. Return beef 3 min to warm through; serve over noodles.

Chef tips

  • Sear fast, batches small — grey beef is overcooked beef.
  • Sour cream off the boil — boiling splits it.
  • Beef returns last and briefly — it keeps cooking.
