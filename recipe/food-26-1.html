Empanadas

Argentina

Salta-style baked empanadas: tallow-enriched dough wrapped around juicy knife-cut beef with onion, egg, olive, cumin and paprika — sealed with the braided repulgue that codes the filling, egg-washed and baked golden. The filling must be cold or the seal fails.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 450 g flour + 55 g tallow/butter + warm water + salt
  • Filling: 500 g hand-cut beef + onion + 2 eggs + olives + cumin + paprika
  • Egg wash; ( Salteña: + potato)

Method

  1. Cook the filling with cumin-paprika; cool completely (overnight ideal).
  2. Knead a pliable dough with warm water; rest 30 min.
  3. Roll 12 cm discs; 2 tbsp cold filling each.
  4. Seal: fold, press out air, braid the repulgue (13 folds = Tucumán pride).
  5. Egg-wash; bake 200 °C 18–22 min to amber.

Chef tips

  • Cold filling only — steam bursts warm-filled seals.
  • Two tablespoons feels stingy and is correct.
  • Repulgue pattern identifies the filling — braid beef, fork-press ham-cheese.
