Picanha

Brazil

The queen of churrasco: top sirloin cap with its fat layer (gordura) intact, cut with the grain into horseshoe steaks, seasoned with nothing but sal grosso, and grilled over charcoal fat-side to render and crisp. Sliced thin against the grain, still mooing.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole picanha (~1.5 kg) with 1 cm+ fat cap
  • Coarse rock salt (sal grosso) — nothing else
  • Charcoal grill + skewers (or grill pan)

Method

  1. Trim the fat cap to ~1 cm; remove silver skin underneath.
  2. Cut with the grain into 5 cm horseshoe steaks; skewer in C-shapes.
  3. Salt generously — including the fat — just before grilling.
  4. Grill over fierce charcoal 3–5 min per side, fat crisped and golden.
  5. Rest; slice thin against the grain; serve with farofa and vinaigrette.

Chef tips

  • With the grain first, against the grain to serve — the two-cut rule.
  • Salt the fat cap too — it bastes as it renders.
  • Medium-rare or don't bother; well-done picanha is a tragedy.
