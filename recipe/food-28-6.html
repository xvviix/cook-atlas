Pirozhki

Russia

Hand-pie buns: soft yeasted dough wrapped around braised cabbage (or beef, or egg-green onion) — sealed into boats, egg-washed and baked golden. Lunchbox, picnic and soup-side staples; best warm, excellent cold, gone by evening.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 550 g flour + yeast + milk + water + egg + oil + sugar + salt; rise 1 hr
  • Cabbage filling: cabbage + sauerkraut + onion, braised soft + egg wash

Method

  1. Braise the filling to soft and savory; cool completely.
  2. Knead a soft dough; rise to doubled.
  3. Divide into 60 g pieces; flatten to ovals.
  4. Fill; seal firmly into boats; proof 20 min.
  5. Egg-wash; bake 185 °C 20–25 min to gold.

Chef tips

  • Cool filling — warm filling melts the dough.
  • Seal firmly — burst pirozhki leak.
  • Proof before baking — dense buns disappoint.
