Apple Pie

United States

The all-American icon: a tart-sweet apple duo (Granny Smith + Honeycrisp) in cinnamon-spice under a hand-woven lattice of all-butter crust — baked until the juices bubble thick through the lattice and the crust turns deep gold. Cool fully or it runs.

Ingredients

  • Crust: 320 g flour + 225 g cold butter + sugar + salt + ice water (2 discs)
  • Filling: 6–7 apples mixed tart-sweet + sugars + flour + cinnamon + nutmeg + lemon + vanilla
  • Egg wash + coarse sugar

Method

  1. Make the dough with pea-size butter bits; chill 1+ hours.
  2. Toss sliced apples with sugars, spices, flour and lemon; rest 15 minutes.
  3. Line the dish; mound the filling high (it shrinks); weave the lattice; crimp.
  4. Chill 20 minutes; egg-wash and sugar the top.
  5. Bake at 200 °C 20 min, then 190 °C 35–45 min until bubbling and deep gold; cool 3–4 hours.

Chef tips

  • Mixed apples = balanced flavor and texture; single-variety pies taste flat.
  • Bubble means done — pale, quiet pies have raw flour and runny centers.
  • Foil the edges if they brown before the center finishes.
