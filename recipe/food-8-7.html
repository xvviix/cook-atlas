Char Siu

China

Cantonese 'fork-roast' BBQ pork: marbled shoulder marinated 24–48 hours in hoisin-five-spice-shaoxing, roasted on a rack and finished with a maltose-honey glaze to sticky, charred-edged glory.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork shoulder, in thick strips
  • Marinade: hoisin + soy + Shaoxing + five-spice + red bean curd + sugar + honey + garlic
  • Glaze: honey/maltose + marinade liquid
  • Water-filled tray under the rack

Method

  1. Marinate 24–48 hours refrigerated, turning once — under-marinating is the home cook's sin.
  2. Roast on a rack over a water tray at 200–220 °C 15 minutes.
  3. Flip, brush with marinade; roast 10 minutes more.
  4. Brush with honey glaze and blast at 240 °C+ 3–5 minutes per side for charred edges.
  5. Rest, slice against the grain, and drizzle with boiled leftover marinade.

Chef tips

  • Fatty shoulder only — lean loin dries to chalk.
  • The water tray catches drips before they smoke the oven.
  • Charred edges are the goal, not a mistake.
