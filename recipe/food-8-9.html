Fried Rice

China

Listed here as the benchmark Yangzhou fried rice: day-old jasmine grains (ideally egg-coated gold), char siu, shrimp, peas and scallion, tossed over maximum heat for wok hei. One portion at a time on a home stove.

Ingredients

  • 400 g day-old jasmine rice, clumps broken
  • 2 eggs (1 to coat the rice, 1+ scrambled)
  • 100 g char siu + 100 g shrimp
  • Peas + diced carrot; scallions
  • Light soy (edge-poured) + salt + white pepper + Shaoxing; smoking-hot oil

Method

  1. Break every clump; toss the cold rice with beaten egg and salt until each grain glistens.
  2. Screaming wok, thin oil: scramble egg, remove; sear shrimp and char siu, remove.
  3. Fry the rice 2–3 minutes, pressing and tossing until grains dance and toast.
  4. Pour soy around the rim so it sears before touching rice; toss through.
  5. Return everything + peas + scallions; 30-second final toss; serve smoking.

Chef tips

  • Cold, dry, day-old rice — fresh rice steams to mush. No exceptions.
  • Crowding kills wok hei; cook single portions.
  • Sauce on the rim, never the center — the sizzle is the seasoning.
