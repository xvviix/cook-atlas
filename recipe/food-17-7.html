Brigadeiro

Brazil

Brazil's party truffle: condensed milk, butter and cocoa stirred patiently until the mixture pulls from the pan (ponto de brigadeiro), cooled, rolled and drowned in chocolate sprinkles. Three ingredients, one technique, infinite parties.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (395 g) sweetened condensed milk + 2 tbsp butter + 3–5 tbsp cocoa
  • Butter for hands and plate; chocolate sprinkles (granulado)

Method

  1. Combine everything in a heavy pan over medium heat.
  2. Stir constantly 10–15 minutes until thick enough that the spoon leaves a 3-second valley (ponto).
  3. Spread on a buttered plate; cool completely (room temp, then fridge).
  4. Butter your palms; roll small balls; coat in sprinkles.
  5. Nestle in paper cups; serve at room temperature.

Chef tips

  • Ponto is everything — undercooked won't roll, overcooked turns to toffee.
  • Constant stirring, no walking away — condensed milk scorches in a minute.
  • Butter your hands generously or wear the brigadeiro home.
