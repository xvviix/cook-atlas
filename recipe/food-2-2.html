Gyro

Greece

Greece's beloved street wrap: seasoned meat (traditionally pork or chicken stacked and roasted on a vertical spit, shaved to order) tucked into warm pita with tzatziki, tomato, onion and often fries. At home the same flavors are achieved with marinated strips, a compact oven-roasted loaf, or spiced mince.

Ingredients

  • 900 g pork strips or chicken thighs (or 500 g spiced minced beef/lamb)
  • Marinade: 60 ml olive oil, lemon juice, 2 tbsp yogurt, 4 garlic cloves, 2 tsp oregano, 1 tsp each paprika, cumin, salt, pepper
  • 4–6 Greek-style pita breads
  • Tzatziki (see Tzatziki card)
  • Tomato, red onion, lettuce, fries, feta (optional)

Method

  1. Marinate the meat 2+ hours (overnight best) in the oil-lemon-yogurt-spice mixture.
  2. Cook hot and fast: grill, sear in a cast-iron pan, or roast at 220 °C until browned at the edges and cooked through; rest, then slice thin.
  3. (Mince version: mix with spices + a little water, form a compact loaf, roast, chill, slice thin, then crisp slices in a hot pan.)
  4. Warm the pitas directly over the flame or in a dry pan until soft and puffy.
  5. Assemble: tzatziki on the pita, then meat, tomato, onion, fries; roll tight in paper and serve immediately.

Chef tips

  • The vertical spit is traditional, but fierce heat + thin slicing gives the same crisp-juicy contrast at home.
  • Warm pita is non-negotiable — cold pita cracks and dulls the whole wrap.
  • Serve fries inside the wrap, Greek street-style, not on the side.
