Peka

Croatia

Dalmatia's bell-roast: lamb and veal with potatoes and vegetables sealed under a metal dome (čripnja) buried in embers — steaming and roasting simultaneously for fall-apart meat and dripping-rich potatoes. At home: a Dutch oven standing in for the bell.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg lamb shoulder + 1 kg veal, in 250 g chunks
  • 1.5 kg potatoes + onions + peppers + tomatoes + carrot
  • Lard/olive oil + white wine + rosemary + bay; salt + pepper

Method

  1. Build a hardwood-ember fire (or heat oven to 180 °C with a Dutch oven).
  2. Salt the meat chunks; layer with vegetables, herbs, lard and wine in the peka/Dutch oven.
  3. Cover with the bell and bury in embers (or lid on, into the oven).
  4. Cook ~45 minutes; turn the meat; cook 45–60 more to collapsing.
  5. Serve hot with bread to mop the juices and white wine.

Chef tips

  • Big chunks only — small pieces dry out under the bell.
  • Embers around and on top; direct flame scorches.
  • The mid-cook turn evens the roast — don't skip it.
