Boeuf Bourguignon

France

The Julia Child classic: beef braised low and slow in red wine with bacon, carrots, pearl onions and mushrooms until it surrenders. Three hours of oven time for the definitive French comfort stew.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg braising beef, large chunks
  • 1 bottle+ red wine (Burgundy ideal) + beef stock
  • 150 g bacon lardons
  • 3 carrots + 2 onions + garlic + tomato paste + flour
  • Bouquet garni; 250 g pearl onions; 400 g mushrooms; parsley

Method

  1. (Optional: marinate the beef in wine with herbs overnight.) Pat dry and sear in batches to a deep crust.
  2. Render bacon; soften carrots and onions; stir in flour and tomato paste 1 minute.
  3. Add beef, wine and stock to barely cover; bring to a bare simmer.
  4. Braise covered at 160 °C 2.5–3 hours until yielding.
  5. Brown the pearl onions and mushrooms separately; stir in for the last 30 minutes; reduce, skim, and serve over mash or potatoes.

Chef tips

  • Batch-sear with space — crowded beef boils grey.
  • Flour early (on the veg) thickens the flood of wine.
  • Skim the fat and serve day two for peak silk.
