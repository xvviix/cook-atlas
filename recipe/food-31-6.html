Cozonac

Romania

Romania's festive sweet bread — Christmas and Easter tables demand it: rich lemon-vanilla dough rolled around walnut-cocoa-rum filling (plus rahat and raisins), braided, proofed and baked to a glossy swirled loaf. Sliced to show the spiral; stale slices make legendary French toast.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 1 kg flour + yeast + milk + 5 yolks + butter + sugar + lemon + vanilla
  • Filling: 300 g walnuts + cocoa + whipped whites + sugar + rum + raisins (+ rahat)

Method

  1. Knead a soft enriched dough; rise 1 hr warm.
  2. Whip whites; fold in walnuts + cocoa + sugar + rum.
  3. Roll dough; spread filling; roll logs; braid two.
  4. Proof in the tin 30 min; egg-wash; sugar + nuts.
  5. Bake 180 °C 50–60 min; cool on side.

Chef tips

  • Warm draft-free rising — cold kitchens stall it.
  • Braid, don't just roll — the swirl is the show.
  • Cool before slicing — hot crumb gums.
