Cornish Pasty

England

The PGI-protected Cornish pasty: D-shaped shortcrust (half butter, half lard) crimped along the side, filled with raw beef skirt, potato, swede and onion — seasoned with salt and pepper only — baked until the filling steams itself tender inside. Miners' lunch, engineered to eat by the crimp with dirty hands. Raw filling, chunky dice, side crimp, no carrots: the protected rules.

Ingredients

  • Pastry: 500 g flour + 125 g butter + 125 g lard + cold water + salt
  • Filling: 400 g beef skirt + potato + swede + onion, all diced
  • Salt + lots of pepper + egg wash + butter knob

Method

  1. Rub fats into flour; bind with cold water; chill 1 hour.
  2. Dice everything chunky and even; season boldly.
  3. Roll rounds; fill one half generously; dot with butter.
  4. Fold; crimp the side edge firmly.
  5. Egg-wash; bake at 170–180 °C for 45–55 minutes.
  6. Cool 15 minutes (the filling is molten).

Chef tips

  • Raw filling — it cooks inside, steaming tender.
  • Side crimp — top crimp isn't Cornish.
  • No carrots — PGI rules forbid them.
  • Chunky dice — mince makes it a different pasty.
