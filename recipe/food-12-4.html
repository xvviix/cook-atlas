Gibanica

Serbia

Serbia's shower-of-cheese phyllo pie: crumpled layers soaked in an egg-sour cream custard with feta and cottage cheese, lifted with sparkling water, baked to a puffed golden slab. Served warm with yogurt — breakfast, side or feast food.

Ingredients

  • 500 g phyllo, thawed
  • 500 g feta + 250 g cottage cheese/ricotta
  • 5–6 eggs + 200 ml sour cream + 200 ml sparkling water
  • 100 ml oil + melted butter; salt

Method

  1. Whisk eggs, sour cream, oil and salt; fold in the cheeses.
  2. Butter the pan; lay a phyllo base; then crumple sheets dipped in custard into free layers.
  3. Pour over remaining custard and the sparkling water; settle into every pocket.
  4. Bake at 180–200 °C 40–60 minutes until puffed and deep gold.
  5. Cool 10 minutes; cut squares; serve warm with yogurt.

Chef tips

  • Crumpled, not flat, layers — the air pockets are the texture.
  • Sparkling water is the traditional lift — don't substitute still.
  • Phyllo dries in minutes — keep it under a damp towel while you work.
