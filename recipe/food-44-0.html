Cepelinai

Lithuania

Lithuania's national dish — zeppelin-shaped giants of potato dough (raw grated plus cooked mashed, bound with the starch settled from the grating liquid) stuffed with minced pork, boiled and drowned in bacon-sour-cream sauce (spirgai). Named for the airships they resemble, cepelinai are the ultimate Lithuanian comfort: labor-intensive, enormous, and worth every minute. The dough greys on contact with air, so everything moves fast once the potatoes are grated.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 2 kg raw potatoes + 500 g cooked mashed + reserved potato starch + salt
  • Filling: 500 g minced pork + onion + garlic + salt + pepper
  • Sauce: smoked bacon + onion + sour cream + dill

Method

  1. Grate raw potatoes fine; squeeze through cloth, reserving the liquid.
  2. Let the liquid settle; pour off water; keep the white starch.
  3. Mix raw, mashed, starch and salt to a pliable dough — work fast.
  4. Season the mince; flatten dough ovals in your palm; fill and seal into zeppelins.
  5. Lower into simmering salted water; cook 20–25 minutes until floating plus 10.
  6. Fry bacon with onion; stir in sour cream; drown the cepelinai and serve.

Chef tips

  • Work fast after grating — grey dough is oxidized dough (lemon juice helps).
  • Seal completely — leaking filling clouds the water and hollows the dumpling.
  • Simmer, never boil — agitation bursts the giants.
  • Wet hands shape smoothly — dry hands stick and tear.
