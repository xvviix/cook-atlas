Mutabbaq

Saudi Arabia

Mutabbaq (murtabak) is the Hejazi stuffed griddle pancake: paper-thin dough stretched by hand, filled with spiced minced meat, egg, tomato, onion and greens (cooks pour beaten egg over the filling so it sets into a frittata layer), folded into a square and griddled in ghee until crisp. Sweet versions (banana, honey, cheese) follow the savory. Thin dough, generous filling, fierce griddle.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + salt + oil + water (rested until elastic)
  • Filling: 400 g mince + onion + tomato + leek/greens + cumin + pepper
  • 2–3 eggs poured over + ghee for griddling + lemon

Method

  1. Knead a soft dough; rest oiled 2+ hours until very stretchy.
  2. Brown mince with onion, tomato and spices; cool.
  3. Stretch dough paper-thin; spread filling; pour over beaten egg.
  4. Fold into a tight square parcel.
  5. Griddle in ghee until crisp and gold both sides.
  6. Cut into squares; serve with lemon.

Chef tips

  • Elastic dough — long rest or it tears.
  • Egg over the filling — it binds everything into layers.
  • Hot griddle, enough ghee — steaming makes it leathery.
  • Cool filling first — hot filling melts the thin dough.
