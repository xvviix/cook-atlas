Khinkali

Georgia

Georgia's soup dumplings: beef-pork filling juiced with onion and water, pleated accordion-style into dough purses with a twisted topknot handle, boiled until they float. Eaten by hand — bite, slurp the broth, then devour; the tough topknot is left on the plate.

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500 g flour + salt + 250 ml warm water; rest 30 min
  • Filling: 500 g beef-pork + onion + cilantro + garlic + 120 ml water + salt + pepper + chile

Method

  1. Knead a firm, elastic dough; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Mix meat with onion, cilantro and seasonings; beat in water until absorbed.
  3. Roll 12 cm rounds, edges thinner; fill 1.5 tbsp; pleat ~19 folds to a topknot.
  4. Boil in salted water 8–10 min until floating + 2–4 min.
  5. Serve peppered; eat by hand — slurp first, leave the knot.

Chef tips

  • Water in the filling = the soup; dry filling = dry dumpling.
  • Seal firmly — a leak loses the broth to the pot.
  • Eat hot with hands; forks puncture and waste the juice.
