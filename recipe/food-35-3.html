Kebapche

Bulgaria

Kebapche (plural kebapcheta) is Bulgaria's beloved grilled minced-meat sausage — a simple mix of pork and beef seasoned almost exclusively with cumin, salt and pepper, formed into plump finger-length logs and grilled over coals until charred outside and juicy within. The official TasteAtlas recipe, published with Bulgaria's Ministry of Tourism, lists just four ingredients, proving that great kebapche is about meat quality and technique rather than a long spice list. Served with chips or bread, lyutenitsa and a cold beer, it is the undisputed king of the Bulgarian barbecue.

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced pork + 300 g minced beef (60/40 ratio, not too lean)
  • 1–2 tsp ground cumin + salt + black pepper (garlic optional)
  • Splash of beer (optional, for juiciness) + oil for the grill

Method

  1. Mix the meats with cumin, salt and pepper (plus beer if using) until tacky and homogenous.
  2. Rest the mixture refrigerated at least 1 hour, ideally overnight, to firm up and meld.
  3. With wet hands, roll into oblong sausages about 12–15 cm long and 3 cm thick.
  4. Grill over medium-high coals 7–10 minutes, turning often for even char.
  5. Rest 3 minutes so juices redistribute; serve hot with lyutenitsa, fries and beer.

Chef tips

  • Fat is juiciness — lean mince makes dry, crumbly kebapche.
  • Wet hands prevent sticking and give a smooth, crack-free surface.
  • Turn often rather than once — even browning on all sides is the goal.
  • Don't press with the spatula — squeezing forces out the juices you worked to keep.
