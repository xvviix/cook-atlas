Chakalaka

South Africa

The braai's fiery relish: onion, peppers, carrot and baked beans simmered with curry, chile and tomato — sweetened with Mrs Ball's chutney. Hot or cold, with pap, boerewors or bread; every family tunes the heat.

Ingredients

  • Onion + garlic + ginger + green pepper + carrot
  • Baked beans + tomato + curry + chile
  • Mrs Ball's chutney + oil + salt; coriander

Method

  1. Soften onion + garlic + ginger + peppers.
  2. Bloom curry + chile.
  3. Add carrot + tomato + beans; simmer 15–20 min.
  4. Stir in chutney; adjust heat.
  5. Serve hot or cold with pap + braai.

Chef tips

  • Chutney balances the heat — don't skip.
  • Simmer to meld — rushed chakalaka tastes raw.
  • Better next day — make ahead.
