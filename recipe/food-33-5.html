Kulajda

Czech Republic

South Bohemia's dill-mushroom soup: forest mushrooms and potato in a vinegar-sugar balanced cream broth loaded with fresh dill — crowned with a poached egg. Sweet-sour-creamy-herbaceous all at once; the egg yolk melting in finishes it.

Ingredients

  • Dried porcini (soaked, liquor saved) + fresh mushrooms
  • Potatoes + roux + broth + cream
  • Vinegar + sugar + 3 tbsp fresh dill; poached eggs

Method

  1. Simmer potatoes + mushrooms in broth 15 min.
  2. Whisk in a blonde roux; simmer to thicken.
  3. Cream + vinegar + sugar to sweet-sour balance.
  4. Dill in last; season.
  5. Serve with a poached egg per bowl + dill.

Chef tips

  • Balance vinegar-sugar carefully — harmony, not pucker.
  • Dill leaves only — stems turn bitter.
  • Porcini liquor is gold — strain and use it.
