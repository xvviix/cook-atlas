Japchae

Korea

The royal-party noodle: springy sweet-potato starch noodles (dangmyeon) tossed with separately cooked beef, spinach, carrot, onion, mushrooms and egg ribbons in soy-sesame-sugar. Served warm or room-temp at every Korean feast — janchi food.

Ingredients

  • 250 g dangmyeon (sweet-potato glass noodles)
  • 150 g beef strips + shiitake, marinated in soy + sugar + sesame
  • Spinach + carrot + onion + mushrooms; jidan egg ribbons
  • Sauce: soy + sugar + sesame oil + garlic; sesame seeds

Method

  1. Marinate beef and shiitake; blanch spinach and squeeze dry.
  2. Boil noodles 5–6 minutes; rinse; cut with scissors; toss in seasoned soy-sesame.
  3. Stir-fry each vegetable separately to crisp-tender; sear beef.
  4. Make thin omelettes; slice into golden ribbons.
  5. Toss everything with sauce; crown with egg and sesame.

Chef tips

  • Separate cooking = distinct colors and textures, not a brown tangle.
  • Scissor-cut the noodles — full-length dangmyeon is unservable.
  • Sesame oil coats the noodles against clumping — don't skimp.
