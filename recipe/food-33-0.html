Svíčková

Czech Republic

Svíčková na smetaně — the Czech national dish: bacon-larded beef braised over carrot, parsley root, celeriac and onion — the vegetables blended silky with cream, mustard, lemon and a whisper of sugar. Served with houskové knedlíky, whipped cream, cranberries and lemon. Sunday's crown.

Ingredients

  • 1.3 kg beef (false tenderloin/chuck) + bacon for larding
  • Carrot + parsley root + celeriac + onion + apple
  • Allspice + bay + thyme; roux + cream + mustard + lemon + sugar; knedlíky

Method

  1. Lard beef with bacon strips; sear all over.
  2. Braise on roots + spices 2–3 hrs to tender.
  3. Lift beef; blend veg with liquor smooth.
  4. Roux + purée + cream + mustard + lemon + sugar; simmer silky.
  5. Slice beef; sauce over; dumplings + cream + cranberry + lemon.

Chef tips

  • Larding keeps lean beef juicy — don't skip.
  • Blend the sauce velvet-smooth — lumps are failure.
  • Sweet-sour balance at the end — taste and adjust.
