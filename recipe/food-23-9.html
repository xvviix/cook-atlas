Buñuelos

Colombia

Colombia's Christmas cheese fritters (Encanto-famous): queso costeño grated into yuca starch/cornstarch with egg and a touch of sugar — rolled into balls that puff, spin and bob in the oil to crisp gold with molten-chewy hearts. Start the oil cooler; raise it as they fry.

Ingredients

  • 125 g salty white cheese (queso costeño/cotija/feta), finely grated
  • 100 g cornstarch + 25 g yuca starch + 1 tsp baking powder + 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 egg + splash of milk; oil starting ~150 °C, finishing 175 °C

Method

  1. Grate cheese superfine; mix with starches, powder and sugar.
  2. Bind with egg + milk to a smooth play-dough.
  3. Roll tight, crack-free balls.
  4. Fry starting in cooler oil; raise heat as they puff and float.
  5. Turn to even gold; drain; serve warm.

Chef tips

  • Crack-free balls — cracks split and leak cheese.
  • Cool-start oil lets them puff before the crust sets.
  • Fine grating melts smooth — coarse chunks burst through.
