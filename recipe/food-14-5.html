Borscht

Poland

The file lists borscht under Poland (it's shared across Eastern Europe): here the hearty Ukrainian-style beef-and-beet soup with cabbage, potatoes and tomato — sweet-sour balanced with vinegar and sugar, finished with dill and sour cream.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg meaty beef bones + water for stock
  • 3 beets grated (+ 1 held back for the finish)
  • Cabbage shredded + potatoes + carrots + onion + tomato paste + garlic
  • Vinegar + sugar; bay; salt + pepper; dill + sour cream + rye

Method

  1. Simmer bones 1+ hours to a rich stock; strain.
  2. Sweat onion, carrot and tomato paste; add beets and a splash of vinegar (locks the ruby color).
  3. Add stock, cabbage and potatoes; simmer 25–30 minutes.
  4. Stir in the reserved fresh beet off the boil for a color burst; balance vinegar-sugar-salt.
  5. Serve with dill, sour cream and rye. Better tomorrow.

Chef tips

  • Vinegar early keeps beets ruby; without acid they brown.
  • The last-minute raw beet is the color insurance.
  • Sweet-sour-salt in balance — taste three times before serving.
