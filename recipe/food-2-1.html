Souvlaki

Greece

Skewers of marinated pork (shoulder or tenderloin), grilled over charcoal until juicy with charred edges. The marinade is simple — olive oil, lemon or wine vinegar, garlic, oregano — and the meat is served on the skewer or wrapped in pita with tzatziki, tomato and onion.

Ingredients

  • 900 g pork shoulder or tenderloin, cut in bite-sized cubes
  • 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon (or 3 tbsp white wine vinegar)
  • 3–6 garlic cloves, minced; 1 grated onion (optional)
  • 1 tbsp dried Greek oregano + 1 tsp rosemary
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika, salt, pepper
  • To serve: pita, tzatziki, tomato, onion, fries, lemon wedges

Method

  1. Whisk the marinade (oil, lemon/vinegar, garlic, onion, herbs, spices) and coat the pork thoroughly.
  2. Marinate covered and refrigerated at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.
  3. Thread onto skewers (soak wooden ones 30 minutes); let come toward room temperature 20 minutes.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal 10–14 minutes, turning often, until cooked through with charred edges (internal ~70 °C).
  5. Rest 5 minutes; finish with olive oil, oregano and lemon (ladolemono) and serve with pita and tzatziki.

Chef tips

  • Pork shoulder stays juicier than lean loin over fierce heat.
  • Don't crowd skewers — space lets each piece char instead of steam.
  • Overnight marinating transforms both flavor and tenderness.
