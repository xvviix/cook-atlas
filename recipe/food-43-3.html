Deruny

Ukraine

Ukrainian potato pancakes: raw potatoes and onion grated to a wet slurry (never squeezed dry), bound with egg and a spoon of flour, fried in shallow oil into lacy, golden-crisp fritters with creamy centers — served with sour cream (or mushroom sauce, or salmon for festive tables). Grating fine and frying immediately prevents the grey oxidation that haunts potato pancakes; a little lemon or onion juice in the mix buys time. Crisp edges, tender middles, eaten hot.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg starchy potatoes + 1 onion, grated fine together
  • 2 eggs + 2–3 tbsp flour + salt + pepper
  • Oil for shallow-frying + sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Grate potatoes with the onion (the onion slows greying).
  2. Mix immediately with eggs, flour, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat 5 mm oil until shimmering; drop spoonfuls and flatten.
  4. Fry 3–4 minutes per side until deep gold and crisp.
  5. Drain on a rack; serve instantly with sour cream.

Chef tips

  • Fry immediately — the batter greys within minutes.
  • Fine grate for lacy edges — coarse grate for chunky middles.
  • Hot oil, uncrowded pan — steaming makes soggy deruny.
  • Rack, not paper — paper traps steam and softens the crust.
