Mandu

Korea

Listed here as kimchi-pork mandu: juicy pork, chopped kimchi, tofu, glass noodles and chives in thin wheat wrappers — pan-fried (gun-mandu), steamed (jjin-mandu) or boiled in soup (mandu-guk). Seollal (Lunar New Year) food and everyday freezer hero.

Ingredients

  • 40 round mandu wrappers
  • 400 g pork + 2 cups squeezed-chopped kimchi + crumbled tofu + chopped dangmyeon + chives + onion
  • Soy + sesame oil + garlic + ginger + sugar + pepper
  • Dip: soy + vinegar + chile

Method

  1. Squeeze the kimchi dry; mix all filling ingredients tacky.
  2. Fill 1 tbsp per wrapper; water the edge; half-moon and crimp tight.
  3. Pan-fry: brown bottoms, add water, lid-steam 8–10 min, re-crisp. (Or steam 10 min.)
  4. Serve hot with dip. Freeze raw on trays for later.

Chef tips

  • Squeeze the kimchi bone-dry — wet filling bursts wrappers.
  • Don't overfill — 1 tablespoon is the discipline.
  • Tight crimps survive boiling; loose ones open in the pot.
