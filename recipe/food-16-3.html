Gỏi cuốn

Vietnam

Saigon's fresh spring rolls: shrimp and pork belly with vermicelli, lettuce and herbs rolled in translucent rice paper — the shrimp placed to show through like stained glass. Served with hoisin-peanut sauce, never fried, never heated.

Ingredients

  • Rice paper rounds (22 cm) + vermicelli + lettuce leaves
  • Shrimp (butterflied) + pork belly slices, boiled
  • Mint + cilantro + Thai basil + chives; cucumber (optional)
  • Sauce: hoisin + peanut butter + garlic + water/coconut milk + crushed peanuts + chile

Method

  1. Boil pork belly; poach shrimp; slice everything bite-ready.
  2. Simmer the sauce to a thick, glossy dip; top with peanuts.
  3. Dip rice paper 2 seconds in warm water — it keeps softening on the board.
  4. Layer lettuce, noodles, herbs, pork; roll once, lay shrimp cut-side down, roll tight.
  5. Serve immediately with sauce — rice paper toughens within the hour.

Chef tips

  • Two-second dip only — oversoaked paper tears and gums.
  • Roll tight with tucked sides; loose rolls fall apart mid-dip.
  • Make and eat in the same hour — these don't wait or reheat.
