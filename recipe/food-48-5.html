Saleeg

Saudi Arabia

Saleeg is Hejaz's beloved creamy rice — Taif's pride: short-grain rice cooked in chicken stock until it breaks down risotto-soft, enriched with milk and ghee into a silky porridge, served with roasted chicken and daqqus. Comfort food elevated to celebration dish, eaten with spoons from a shared platter. The rice must overcook by every other standard — distinct grains are a failure here.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups short-grain (Egyptian) rice + 1 chicken + stock
  • 2 cups milk + 100 g ghee + mastic + cardamom + salt
  • Daqqus + lemon to serve

Method

  1. Poach chicken in aromatics until tender; reserve rich stock.
  2. Roast the chicken to bronze while rice cooks.
  3. Simmer rice in stock, stirring, until breaking down (30+ minutes).
  4. Stir in hot milk and ghee; cook to silky porridge.
  5. Plate rice; crown with chicken; drizzle ghee; serve with daqqus.

Chef tips

  • Short-grain only — basmati can't break down right.
  • Overcook deliberately — this is porridge, not pilaf.
  • Hot milk in — cold milk seizes the starch.
  • Ghee generously — richness is the point.
