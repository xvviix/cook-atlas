Tteokbokki

Korea

Seoul street food #1: chewy cylinder rice cakes (garaetteok) simmered in a glossy gochujang-gochugaru anchovy broth with fish cakes, cabbage and boiled eggs — sweet, spicy, sticky. Cheese, ramyeon and dumplings crash the party as beloved variations.

Ingredients

  • 500 g cylinder rice cakes (soaked if refrigerated-hard)
  • Sauce: 3 tbsp gochujang + 1 tbsp gochugaru + soy + sugar + garlic
  • 750 ml anchovy-kelp stock (or water + stock cube)
  • Fish cakes + cabbage + scallions + boiled eggs; sesame oil + seeds

Method

  1. Simmer the stock; whisk in gochujang, gochugaru, soy, sugar and garlic.
  2. Add rice cakes; simmer 8–10 minutes, stirring, until chewy-tender.
  3. Fold in fish cakes, cabbage and eggs; simmer to a clinging gloss.
  4. Finish with sesame oil and seeds; serve bubbling.

Chef tips

  • Soak hard rice cakes or they cook chalky-centered.
  • Stir while simmering — starch sinks and scorches fast.
  • Sauce thickens as it stands; serve immediately or loosen.
