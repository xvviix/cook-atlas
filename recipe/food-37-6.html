Fatteh

Palestine

Palestinian fattet djaj layers textures like no other dish: fried pita chips softened with hot chicken broth, fluffy rice, shredded poached chicken, and a cool blanket of garlic-lemon-tahini yogurt — finished with pine nuts sizzling in samneh (clarified butter). Assembled at the last second so crunch meets cream in every bite, it is celebration food across the Levant. The contrast — hot/cool, crisp/soft, rich/bright — is the entire point.

Ingredients

  • ½ chicken, poached + 1 liter yogurt + 2 pitas
  • Rice + 4 garlic cloves + tahini + lemon + salt + pepper
  • Pine nuts + samneh/butter for finishing

Method

  1. Poach the chicken with aromatics; shred; keep the broth boiling hot.
  2. Fry pita squares until deeply golden; drain and salt.
  3. Cook fluffy rice; whisk yogurt with garlic, tahini, lemon and salt.
  4. Layer: pita, hot broth to soften, rice, chicken.
  5. Blanket with yogurt; crown with pine nuts fried in foaming samneh.
  6. Serve immediately — fatteh waits for no one.

Chef tips

  • Assemble at the last minute — standing fatteh turns uniformly soggy.
  • Hot broth, cool yogurt — the temperature contrast is essential.
  • Thick yogurt only — thin yogurt soaks the bread to mush.
  • Brown the pine nuts in the samneh until foaming and fragrant.
