Kapsalon

Netherlands

Rotterdam's 2003 invention — kapsalon ('hair salon,' named for the barber who ordered it): fries layered with shawarma or döner, blanketed in Gouda and grilled until molten, then topped with salad (lettuce, tomato, cucumber, onion) and flooded with garlic sauce and sambal. Served in an aluminum tray, eaten with a fork, it's Dutch multicultural comfort in a box — now exported worldwide. Melt the cheese over meat-and-fries before the cold toppings go on.

Ingredients

  • Fries (double-fried) + 400 g shawarma/döner meat
  • 200 g Gouda, grated + garlic sauce + sambal
  • Lettuce + tomato + cucumber + red onion

Method

  1. Crisp the fries; cook the meat hot.
  2. Layer fries then meat in an oven tray.
  3. Blanket with Gouda; grill until molten and spotted.
  4. Top with chopped salad.
  5. Flood with garlic sauce and sambal; serve in the tray.

Chef tips

  • Melt cheese before salad — hot-cold layers are the architecture.
  • Gouda that melts — young, not aged crystalline.
  • Sauce generously — dry kapsalon is a tragedy.
  • Eat from the tray — plating is pretension.
