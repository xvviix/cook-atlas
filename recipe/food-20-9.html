Baklava

Lebanon

Lebanese baklawa: pistachio-filled phyllo layered with clarified butter — lighter and less sweet than its neighbors — cut before baking, baked to shattering gold, then drowned in cool orange-blossom syrup. Hot pastry + cool syrup = crisp layers that drink, never sog.

Ingredients

  • 450 g phyllo (thawed) + 250 g clarified butter/ghee, melted
  • 400 g pistachios + 100 g sugar (+ saffron/cardamom)
  • Syrup: 300 g sugar + 300 ml water + lemon + orange-blossom water

Method

  1. Make the syrup first; cool completely.
  2. Layer 8–10 buttered phyllo sheets; spread pistachio-sugar; repeat; top with 8–10 sheets.
  3. Cut into diamonds before baking.
  4. Bake at 175 °C 35–45 min to deep, even gold.
  5. Pour cool syrup over hot baklawa; rest 1+ hour; garnish pistachio.

Chef tips

  • Cut before baking — cut after shatters.
  • Cool syrup + hot pastry is the crispness rule.
  • Keep phyllo under a damp towel — it dries in minutes.
