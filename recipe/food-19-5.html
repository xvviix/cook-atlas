Samgyeopsal

Korea

Korean BBQ's centerpiece: thick unmarinated pork belly grilled tableside, snipped with scissors, and wrapped in lettuce/perilla with ssamjang, garlic, kimchi and peppers — eaten in one balanced bite. The wrap (ssam) is the etiquette and the joy.

Ingredients

  • 800 g thick-cut pork belly (fresh, unmarinated)
  • Lettuce + perilla leaves; ssamjang; salted sesame oil
  • Garlic + green chile + kimchi + pickled radish + scallion salad
  • Table grill + scissors + tongs

Method

  1. Heat the grill medium-high; no oil — the belly provides.
  2. Grill thick slices 2–3 minutes per side to crisp gold; snip bite-size.
  3. Grill kimchi and garlic alongside in the rendered fat.
  4. Build ssam: leaf + pork + ssamjang + garlic + kimchi — one bite only.
  5. Serve with rice, doenjang-jjigae and cold beer/soju.

Chef tips

  • One bite per wrap — overstuffing is the tourist mistake.
  • Perilla + lettuce together is the classic double-leaf wrap.
  • Let the fat render slowly; rushed belly stays flabby.
