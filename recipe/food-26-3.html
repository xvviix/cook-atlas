Choripán

Argentina

The asado's opening act and street icon: Argentine chorizo butterflied (mariposa) and grilled, tucked into crusty bread with chimichurri and salsa criolla. Eaten standing up, dripping, while the main cuts still cook — the appetizer that threatens to upstage dinner.

Ingredients

  • 4 Argentine-style chorizos (pork, mild, paprika)
  • 4 crusty rolls/baguette lengths + oil
  • Chimichurri + salsa criolla (tomato + onion + pepper + vinegar)

Method

  1. Make chimichurri hours ahead (overnight better).
  2. Butterfly chorizos; grill cut-side down to char; flip; finish ~15 min.
  3. Toast rolls on the grill with oil.
  4. Chimichurri both sides of bread; chorizo in; salsa over.
  5. Serve immediately, napkins mandatory.

Chef tips

  • Butterflying crisps the inside — whole links stay soft.
  • Toast the bread — untoasted goes soggy instantly.
  • Chimichurri on both sides — stinginess is a sin.
