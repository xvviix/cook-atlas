Cendol

Malaysia

The green-jewel iced dessert: pandan cendol jelly pressed worm-like into ice water — layered over shaved ice with santan (salted coconut milk) and dark gula melaka syrup. Optional red beans, corn, grass jelly, durian; the salt in the coconut against the smoky palm sugar is the magic.

Ingredients

  • Jelly: rice flour + mung-bean flour + pandan juice + salt, pressed into ice water
  • Gula melaka syrup: palm sugar + water + pandan, simmered thick
  • Santan: coconut milk + pinch of salt, chilled; shaved ice (+ beans/corn)

Method

  1. Simmer gula melaka with pandan to a thick syrup; strain; cool.
  2. Cook flours + pandan juice to a thick green paste.
  3. Press through a colander into ice water; chill the worms.
  4. Salt the coconut milk; chill.
  5. Layer jelly + ice; syrup over; santan over; extras; serve instantly.

Chef tips

  • Salt the santan — unsalted coconut tastes flat.
  • Thick syrup — thin syrup vanishes into the ice.
  • Serve instantly — cendol melts into soup within minutes.
