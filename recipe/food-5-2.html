Gazpacho

Spain

Gazpacho Andaluz: Andalusia's ice-cold 'liquid salad' of ripe tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, garlic, sherry vinegar and olive oil, body from stale bread. No cooking — just blending and chilling. Made for 40 °C summers.

Ingredients

  • 6–7 ripe tomatoes (~1 kg)
  • 1 cucumber, peeled
  • 1 green Italian/Cubanelle pepper
  • ½ small onion + 1 garlic clove
  • 50 g stale white bread, soaked
  • 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1–2 tbsp sherry vinegar (Jerez)
  • Salt; ice-cold water to thin

Method

  1. Soak the bread, squeeze dry, and add to the blender with roughly chopped vegetables and garlic.
  2. Blend until completely smooth, then stream in the olive oil and vinegar with the motor running.
  3. Season boldly with salt; thin with ice water to a drinkable-soup consistency.
  4. Chill at least 2 hours — gazpacho must be served ice-cold.
  5. Serve with diced toppings: egg, croutons, pepper, cucumber, and a thread of oil.

Chef tips

  • Peak-season tomatoes make or break it — winter tomatoes give pink water.
  • Strain through a sieve for a silky restaurant texture.
  • Vinegar last and to taste; you can add, never remove.
