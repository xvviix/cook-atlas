Biryani

India

Listed here as Hyderabadi chicken dum biryani: yogurt-spiced marinated chicken layered with 70%-cooked basmati, saffron milk, fried onions, mint and ghee — sealed and steam-cooked (dum) so the grains finish in spiced vapor. Served with mirchi ka salan and raita.

Ingredients

  • 750 g chicken + yogurt + ginger-garlic + biryani masala + chile + lemon
  • 500 g aged basmati, soaked
  • Whole spices: bay, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, shah jeera
  • Saffron in warm milk; 3 crisp-fried onions; mint + coriander; ghee; kewra/rose water

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken 2+ hours (overnight best).
  2. Boil rice with whole spices to 70–80% — the grain must break to a white core; drain.
  3. Layer chicken then rice in a heavy pot; crown with onions, herbs, saffron milk, ghee and kewra.
  4. Seal with foil + lid (or dough); dum on the lowest flame 25–35 minutes.
  5. Rest sealed 10 minutes; open at the table; serve with salan and raita.
