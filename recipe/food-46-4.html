Karantika

Algeria

Karantika (garantita, calentica) is Oran's beloved chickpea-flan street food: a thin batter of chickpea flour, water, oil, cumin and egg baked blazing-hot until puffed, bronzed and custardy within — served in baguette sandwiches dusted with cumin and harissa. Spanish legacy (calentita) turned Algerian icon, it's crispy-edged, creamy-centered and costs almost nothing. High heat and a preheated oiled pan give the puff and the crust.

Ingredients

  • 250 g chickpea flour + 1 liter water + 2 eggs
  • 100 ml oil + cumin + salt + pepper
  • Baguette + harissa to serve

Method

  1. Whisk flour with water lump-free; rest 1+ hour (overnight best).
  2. Whisk in eggs, oil, cumin and salt.
  3. Heat an oiled pan in a 220–240 °C oven until smoking.
  4. Pour batter thin; bake 25–35 minutes until puffed and bronzed.
  5. Dust with cumin; stuff into baguette with harissa; eat hot.

Chef tips

  • Rest the batter — hydrated flour bakes custardy, not gritty.
  • Smoking-hot pan — the sizzle sets the crust.
  • Thin layer — thick karantika turns stodgy.
  • Eat hot — it deflates and firms as it cools.
